Method 1: Using Apps

a) Select a Reputable App:

  • Explore the app store and choose a "fake call" app with positive user reviews and high ratings.
  • Consider apps that offer advanced features such as caller ID spoofing, custom wallpapers, and voice customization.

b) Configure the App's Settings:

  • Customize the app to match your preferences. Choose a realistic caller name, photo, and wallpaper to enhance the authenticity of the call.
  • Select a unique ringtone to avoid confusion with actual incoming calls.
  • Adjust the call duration to accommodate your needs and avoid suspicion.

c) Rehearse and Practice:

  • Familiarize yourself with the app's features and controls. Practice making fake calls to ensure smooth execution and natural behavior.
  • Pay attention to your tone of voice, body language, and facial expressions to create a believable performance.

d) Stay Updated:

  • Regularly check for app updates to stay abreast of new features and bug fixes. This will ensure seamless performance and prevent any glitches that may disrupt your plans.

Method 2: Pretending You Received a Call

a) Be Accessible:

  • Keep your phone within easy reach to respond promptly to the imaginary call.
  • Ensure that the phone is not on silent or vibrate mode to prevent any unintended distractions.

b) Simulate an Incoming Call:

  • Notice an incoming call on your phone screen, as if it were an actual call.
  • Quickly silence the ringer to avoid drawing attention to the fact that it's not a genuine incoming call.

c) Answer Confidently:

  • Answer the "call" as you would a real phone call. Maintain a natural and sincere tone of voice.
  • Address the caller by name and engage in a realistic conversation.

d) Enact Body Language:

  • Use appropriate body language to enhance the illusion of an ongoing phone conversation.
  • Raise your eyebrows for surprise, nod in agreement, or glance at the screen as if reading a message.

e) End the Call Gracefully:

  • Politely end the conversation when necessary. Apologize to the person you're with for the interruption and explain that it was an important call.

Method 3: Pre-planning an Escape Route

a) Arrange a Friend's Call:

  • Reach out to a trusted friend and ask them to call you at a specific time.
  • Explain your situation and request their assistance in providing an excuse to get away.

b) Set a "Ringtone" Alarm:

  • Create an alarm on your phone that sounds like a ringtone. Set the alarm to go off at the desired time to simulate an incoming call.
  • To ensure privacy, name the alarm something inconspicuous, such as "Appointment Reminder."

c) Record a Voicemail Message:

  • Record a voicemail message on another phone. The message should sound like a genuine caller leaving a message for you.
  • Save the voicemail and play it back on your phone when you need an excuse to step away.
