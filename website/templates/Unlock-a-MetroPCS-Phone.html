How to Unlock Your MetroPCS Phone

If you've decided to switch carriers or simply want to use your MetroPCS phone on another network, unlocking it is essential. Here's a comprehensive guide on how to unlock your MetroPCS phone using various methods:

Method 1: Contact MetroPCS

Steps:

  1. Call Customer Service: Dial 1-888-863-8768 or visit a MetroPCS store.
  2. Verification: Provide your phone number, account holder name, account PIN, and email address.
  3. Unlock Code Request: MetroPCS will initiate the unlocking process and send an email with an unlock code within 2-3 business days.
  4. Unlock Process: Follow the instructions in the email to unlock your phone.

Eligibility:

  • Your phone must have been active on the MetroPCS network for 180 consecutive days.
  • If your phone is a CDMA device, this method is not available.

Method 2: Use the Device Unlock App

Availability:

  • This method is only compatible with specific phone models, including LG Leon LTE, LG G Stylo, and Kyocera Hydro Elite.

Steps:

  1. Device Unlock App: Open the "Device Unlock" app on your phone.
  2. Permanent Unlock: Select "Permanent Unlock" and allow the process to complete.
  3. Phone Restart: Restart your phone to complete the unlocking process.

Method 3: Third-Party Unlock Services

Steps:

  1. Service Selection: Research and choose a reputable unlock service provider online.
  2. IMEI Number: Provide your phone's IMEI number, which can be found by dialing *#06#.
  3. Payment Information: Pay for the unlocking service using your preferred payment method.
  4. Unlock Code: Within a specified timeframe, you will receive an email containing the unlock code.
  5. Unlock Instructions: Follow the detailed instructions provided with the code to unlock your phone.

Eligibility:

  • This method is available for both GSM and CDMA MetroPCS phones.
  • Ensure your phone is not blacklisted or under contract with another carrier.

Important Considerations

CDMA Compatibility: CDMA technology is not compatible with unlocking services. If your MetroPCS phone uses CDMA, it cannot be unlocked by MetroPCS or third-party services.

Blacklisting: If your phone has been reported lost or stolen, it may be blacklisted, making it ineligible for unlocking.

Unlocking Fees: Third-party unlock services typically charge a fee for their services. The cost can vary depending on your phone model and service provider.

Warranty: Unlocking your phone may void any remaining warranty.
