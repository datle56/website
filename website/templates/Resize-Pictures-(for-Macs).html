Unlock the Power of Preview: Easy Photo Editing on Mac - Resizing and Cropping Made Simple


Image Editing: Resize and Crop Like a Pro with Preview

Whether you're a seasoned graphic artist or a newbie navigating the digital realm, resizing and cropping images are crucial skills. Preview, a versatile image editor on macOS, offers an effortless and beginner-friendly approach to these tasks, empowering you to enhance your photos and designs with precision. Discover the step-by-step methods below to master the art of image manipulation in Preview.


Method 1: Resizing an Image in Preview

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Open the Image: Drag and drop the image file onto the Preview app icon to open it.
  2. Access Edit Mode: Click the "Edit" button located on the toolbar at the top of the Preview window.
  3. Choose Adjustment Size: From the "Tools" menu, select "Adjust Size."
  4. Modify Resolution: Adjust the resolution (measured in pixels per inch) to suit your intended use. For printing purposes, higher resolutions are preferred. For web usage, lower resolutions are suitable.
  5. Set Dimensions: Enter the desired width and height of the image in the corresponding fields.
  6. Ensure Proportional Scaling (Optional): Tick the "Scale proportionally" box to maintain the image's original aspect ratio during resizing.
  7. Confirm Changes: Click "OK" to view the resized image.

Method 2: Cropping an Image in Preview

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Enter Edit Mode: Click the "Edit" button to enter Edit Mode.
  2. Select Rectangular Selection Tool: Click on the "Rectangular Selection" icon located in the toolbar. It resembles a rectangle with dashed lines.
  3. Outline Cropping Area: Drag the mouse cursor over the image to draw a rectangle around the portion you wish to keep.
  4. Execute Crop: Once the desired cropping area is selected, click the "Crop" button located in the toolbar. This will remove all parts of the image that fall outside the selection.
  5. Resize Cropped Area (Optional): If necessary, you can further resize the cropped image by adjusting its width and height using the resizing handles.
  6. Save Changes: Press "Command + S" to save the cropped image.

Conclusion:

By utilizing these methods in Preview, you can effortlessly resize or crop your images to meet your specific requirements. Whether you need to adjust the resolution for printing or create a custom crop for social media, Preview's user-friendly interface and precise tools make the process a breeze. Experiment with different settings to achieve the desired size and composition for your images, and enjoy the convenience of editing them natively on your Mac.
