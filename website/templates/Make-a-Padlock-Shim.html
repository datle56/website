Easy Padlock Opening: A Comprehensive Guide Using an Aluminum Can Shim

Introduction

Losing a padlock key can be a frustrating experience, but it doesn't have to be a reason to break the lock or spend money on a locksmith. With the simple technique described below, you can easily create an improvised shim from an aluminum can and unlock the padlock in mere minutes.

Materials Required:

  • Aluminum can (any size)
  • Scissors or tin snips
  • Needle-nose pliers

Step-by-Step Instructions:

1. Prepare the Aluminum Can:

  • Remove both the top and bottom of the can using scissors or tin snips.
  • Cut a vertical line down the side of the can from top to bottom.
  • Open the can along the cut line to create a flat sheet of aluminum.

2. Create Long Strips:

  • Cut the sheet of aluminum into two long rectangular strips of equal width.

3. Divide into Squares:

  • Cut each strip into squares measuring approximately 1.5-2 inches on each side.

4. Create M-Shaped Shims:

  • Mark each square in half lengthwise.
  • Cut an M-shape on one half of the square, ensuring that the uncut portion is at least 0.5 inches wide. This will form the handle of the shim.

5. Fold and Crimp the Handle:

  • Fold the uncut half of the square down to create the handle.
  • Use needle-nose pliers to bend the two outside flaps of the M-shape over the handle.
  • Crimp the handle securely to hold the flaps in place.

6. Shape the Shim:

  • Use your pliers to gently shape the shim to fit the contours of the padlock shackle.

Using the Shim to Open the Padlock:

1. Insert the Shim:

  • Carefully insert the shim into the space between the shackle and the lock mechanism. Ensure that the M-shaped opening is facing the shackle's locking bar.

2. Pull Side to Side:

  • While holding the handle, pull the tabs of the shim from side to side. Apply gentle but firm pressure and listen for a clicking sound.

3. Unlock:

  • Continue pulling the tabs until the padlock shackle unlocks. The lock should open easily.

Tips for Success:

  • Use multiple shims simultaneously if attempting to open a small lock.
  • Be cautious of sharp edges on the aluminum can.
  • Avoid using this technique on illegal locks.
