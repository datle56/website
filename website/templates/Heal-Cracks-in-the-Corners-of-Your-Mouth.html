Home Remedies for Cracked Mouth Corners

Cracked mouth corners, also known as angular cheilitis, can be an uncomfortable and unsightly issue. Fortunately, there are several effective home remedies that can help soothe and heal these cracks.

Topical Applications

  • Petroleum Jelly: This classic remedy creates a protective barrier on the skin, preventing saliva from irritating the cracks. Apply a small amount directly to the affected area several times a day.
  • Coconut Oil: Coconut oil is deeply moisturizing and contains antibacterial and anti-inflammatory properties. Massage a small amount into the cracks to help soothe and promote healing.
  • Vitamin E and Shea Butter Lip Balm: Lip balms containing these ingredients provide deep hydration and repair damaged skin. Apply generously throughout the day, especially before bed.
  • Honey: Honey's antibacterial and humectant properties make it an effective moisturizer and wound healer. Apply a small amount to the cracks and leave on for 15-20 minutes before rinsing.

Dietary Changes

  • Iron-Rich Foods: Iron deficiency can contribute to cracked mouth corners. Include iron-rich foods such as spinach, red meat, and pumpkin seeds in your diet to boost iron levels.
  • Vitamin B-Rich Foods: Vitamin B is essential for skin health and fighting infections. Consume foods like salmon, eggs, and nutritional yeast to support your skin's recovery.
  • Zinc-Rich Foods: Zinc deficiency can worsen cracking. Include zinc-rich foods such as beef, shellfish, and fortified cereals in your meals.
  • Yogurt: Yogurt contains probiotics that help fight yeast and bacterial infections, which can be underlying causes of cracked mouth corners. Incorporate yogurt into your daily diet.

Medical Treatment

In some cases, home remedies may not be sufficient to resolve cracked mouth corners. Medical treatment may be necessary if:

  • Yeast Infection: If the cracks are accompanied by itching, redness, and a white or yellow coating, a yeast infection may be the cause. Your doctor may prescribe an anti-fungal cream.
  • Bacterial Infection: Bacterial infections can lead to inflammation and swelling. A topical steroid cream may be prescribed to reduce the inflammation and pain.
  • Severe Bacterial Infection: In severe cases, oral antibiotics may be necessary to clear the infection.
  • Dental Prosthesis Refitting: Ill-fitting dentures or braces can cause excess saliva and dryness, leading to cracked mouth corners. If you wear dentures or braces, have them refitted by your dentist.
  • Underlying Condition Check: If home treatments and common medical treatments fail to resolve the issue, consult a doctor. There may be an underlying health condition, such as a vitamin deficiency or autoimmune disease, that requires specific treatment.

Conclusion:

Cracked mouth corners, also known as angular cheilitis, can be an uncomfortable and unsightly condition. While it's often caused by mild factors such as dehydration or nutrient deficiencies, it can also be a sign of underlying health issues. By following these home remedies, dietary changes, and medical treatments, you can effectively alleviate the symptoms and prevent future occurrences of cracked mouth corners. Consult a healthcare professional if home treatments are ineffective or if you suspect a more serious condition is present to ensure prompt and appropriate treatment.
