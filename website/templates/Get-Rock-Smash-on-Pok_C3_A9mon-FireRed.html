How to Obtain Rock Smash in Pokémon FireRed

Step 1: Embark on a Voyage to One Island

Once you have triumphed over the gym leader on Cinnabar Island, your journey leads you to the enigmatic One Island. Board the boat docked at Cinnabar Island's harbor and embark on a nautical expedition towards this uncharted destination.

Step 2: Navigate the Beaches of Tranquility

Upon arriving on One Island, venture towards the easternmost shores. A soothing melody of waves crashing against the sandy beach beckons you to explore further. Summon your surfing prowess and glide northward along the coastline.

Step 3: Discover the Hidden Cave

As you continue your aquatic journey, a second sandy expanse will emerge before you. Disembark from your watery steed and traverse the tall grass, your eyes scanning the horizon for a telltale cave entrance. Immerse yourself in the dimly lit interior of Ember Spa, where the gentle sound of dripping water fills the air.

Step 4: Seek Wisdom from the Cave's Dweller

Within the cavern's depths, amidst cascading waterfalls, a man stands in solitude. Engage in a dialogue with this enigmatic figure, and he will bestow upon you HM06, Rock Smash. This coveted technical machine grants your Pokémon the ability to shatter small, crumbling boulders that obstruct your path.

Additional Notes:

  • Rock Smash is an essential tool for navigating certain areas of the game, such as Victory Road.
  • The cave on One Island is also home to other valuable items, including a Fire Stone and a Thunder Stone.
  • Be aware that Rock Smash can also be used to destroy rocks in various locations, revealing hidden items or opening up new areas.
  • Once you have obtained Rock Smash, you can use it by selecting it from your Pokémon's "Moves" menu and targeting a nearby boulder.

Conclusion:

Congratulations! By following these simple steps, you have successfully acquired the coveted Rock Smash HM in Pokémon FireRed. This powerful move not only obliterates pesky boulders and opens new paths, but also provides your Pokémon with a valuable attack against Rock and Ice-type opponents. With Rock Smash in your arsenal, you are now well-equipped to traverse the treacherous landscapes and overcome the formidable challenges that lie ahead in your Pokémon journey.
