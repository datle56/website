How to Grow Dreadlocks Naturally: A Comprehensive Guide

Method 1: Let Your Hair Lock Naturally (Freeform Dreadlocks)

Step 1: Embrace the Journey

  • Cease getting regular haircuts and allow your natural hair to grow uninhibited.

Step 2: Shape Your Locks

  • As your hair grows, encourage it to form locks by rolling them clockwise and securing them with rubber bands. Focus on shaping and defining the desired dreadlock pattern.

Step 3: Regular Cleansing

  • Wash your hair regularly with a gentle shampoo to remove dirt and oils. Avoid using conditioner as it may impede the locking process.

Step 4: Maintenance

  • Roll your dreadlocks frequently to maintain their shape and prevent them from unraveling.

Method 2: Backcombing Straight or Wavy Hair (Starter Locks)

Step 1: Prepare Your Hair

  • Refrain from using conditioner for a week before backcombing to enhance grip.
  • Divide your hair into sections and braid each section for two days to create friction.

Step 2: Section and Backcomb

  • After unbraiding, separate individual strands.
  • Grab a cluster of hair and backcomb it thoroughly from tip to root using a fine-toothed comb. Repeat this process for all clusters.

Step 3: Locking

  • Palm roll the backcombed locks to smooth out the cuticles and create a uniform shape. Secure them with rubber bands to maintain their structure.

Step 4: Protection

  • Wear a bandana or shower cap to safeguard your locks during the initial stages of locking.

Method 3: Twisting Curly or Kinky Hair (Interlocking)

Step 1: Length Requirements

  • Allow your hair to grow to a minimum of 3 inches before starting the twisting process.

Step 2: Cleansing and Drying

  • Thoroughly rinse your hair with shampoo and allow it to dry completely.

Step 3: Twisting

  • Divide your hair into sections and twist each cluster clockwise from the roots to the tips. Secure the twists with rubber bands.

Step 4: Palm Rolling

  • Palm roll your dreadlocks every other day to tighten and lock them. Ensure that the rolls are firm and even.

Method 4: Using a Hairstylist (Professional Dreadlocks)

Step 1: Seek Expertise

  • Consult with a hairstylist specializing in natural dreadlocks. They will assess your hair type and recommend the most suitable method for you.

Step 2: Time Allocation

  • Book an appointment for approximately 6 hours, as the dreadlocking process can be time-consuming.

Step 3: Post-Appointment Care

  • Wash your hair once a week with shampoo only. Avoid using conditioner.

Step 4: Shaping and Rolling

  • Roll your dreadlocks to refine their shape and maintain uniformity.

Step 5: Follow-Up Appointments

  • Schedule follow-up appointments if your hair fails to lock naturally. The stylist can provide additional assistance to ensure successful dreadlock formation.
