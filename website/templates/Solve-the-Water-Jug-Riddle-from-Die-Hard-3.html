Understanding the Riddle

Imagine yourself facing a seemingly perplexing puzzle: you're tasked with filling a 5-gallon jug with precisely 4 gallons of water. You're equipped with two empty containers – one with a capacity of 3 gallons and the other holding 5 gallons. At first glance, it may appear like an impossible feat, but with some careful planning and strategic moves, you can unravel this riddle.

Hints for Solving the Riddle

  • Break down the task: Your objective is to transfer 4 gallons of water into the 5-gallon jug.
  • Remember, you have two jugs at your disposal, along with an abundant supply of water.
  • Approach it as a mathematical problem: You'll be adding and subtracting 3's and 5's to reach the desired quantity.

Steps to the Solution

You can approach this riddle by employing different strategies, and here are two potential solutions:

Solution 1

  1. Fill to the brim: Start by filling the 5-gallon jug to its maximum capacity.

  2. Transfer to 3-gallon jug: Pour water from the 5-gallon jug into the 3-gallon jug until it's filled.

  3. Empty the 3-gallon jug: Once the 3-gallon jug is full, empty its contents back into the 5-gallon jug.

  4. Partial fill of 5-gallon jug: Using the water remaining in the 5-gallon jug, fill the 3-gallon jug again, but this time stop when there's only 1 gallon left in the 5-gallon jug.

  5. Refill the 5-gallon jug: Fill the 5-gallon jug to capacity once more.

  6. Transfer again: Fill the 3-gallon jug from the 5-gallon jug, leaving 1 gallon of space in the larger container.

  7. Complete the transfer: Pour the remaining water from the 3-gallon jug into the 5-gallon jug, leaving you with exactly 4 gallons in the latter.

Solution 2

  1. Initial transfer: Fill the 3-gallon jug and pour its contents into the 5-gallon jug.

  2. Double the water: Fill the 3-gallon jug again and add it to the water already in the 5-gallon jug.

  3. Partial fill: Using the 3-gallon jug, fill the 5-gallon jug as much as possible, leaving some water behind in the 3-gallon jug.

  4. Empty the 5-gallon jug: Dump out the water from the 5-gallon jug.

  5. Transfer the leftover water: Pour the remaining water from the 3-gallon jug into the 5-gallon jug.

  6. Final fill: Fill the 3-gallon jug completely and pour its contents into the 5-gallon jug, giving you 4 gallons.
