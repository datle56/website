Method 1: Routine Polishing

Materials:

  • Baking soda
  • Dish soap
  • Warm water
  • Soft-bristled toothbrush
  • Soft cloth
  • Jeweler's cloth (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Soak the gold: Prepare a solution by dissolving 1 tablespoon of baking soda and a drop of dish soap in a bowl of warm water. Submerge the gold item in the solution for a few minutes to loosen dirt and grime.

  2. Gently scrub: Use a soft-bristled toothbrush to gently scrub the gold in the solution. Be extremely careful not to scratch or damage the gold's surface. Avoid using harsh materials like steel wool or abrasive cleaners that can cause damage.

  3. Rinse and dry: Thoroughly rinse the gold under running lukewarm water to remove the cleaning solution and any remaining dirt. Pat the gold dry with a soft, absorbent cloth to remove excess moisture.

  4. Final polishing: If desired, further polish the gold using a clean jeweler's cloth. This will remove any remaining debris and restore the gold's luster.

Method 2: Removing Scratches

Materials:

  • Dish soap
  • Water
  • Ammonia (1 part)
  • Water (6 parts)
  • Tissues

Instructions:

  1. Clean the gold: Begin by cleaning the gold with a mild solution of dish soap and water. Remove any dirt or surface grime that could interfere with the scratch removal process.

  2. Prepare the ammonia solution: Carefully mix one part ammonia with six parts water in a small container. It's crucial to ensure proper ventilation as ammonia fumes can be irritating.

  3. Soak the gold: Submerge the gold item in the ammonia solution for a maximum of one minute. Monitor the gold closely during this time to avoid any potential damage.

  4. Remove and dry: Remove the gold from the solution and immediately blot it dry with a tissue. If the scratches persist, repeat the soaking process as necessary.

Method 3: Protecting Gold

Tips:

  • Avoid strenuous activities: Wear delicate gold pieces sparingly and avoid wearing them during strenuous activities such as sports or heavy lifting, as this can cause damage or scratches.

  • Protect from cosmetics: Avoid applying lotions, perfumes, or other cosmetics directly onto or near gold jewelry. These products can contain chemicals that may damage the gold's surface or cause discoloration.

  • Be mindful of surroundings: Pay attention to your surroundings and be cautious not to bump or scrape gold jewelry against hard surfaces or other objects.

  • Store carefully: When not wearing gold, store it in a soft, lined jewelry box or pouch to protect it from dust and scratches. Avoid storing gold pieces together, as they can rub against each other and cause damage.
