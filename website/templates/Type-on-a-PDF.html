Comprehensive Guide to Adding Text to PDF Documents

PC or Mac

Adobe Acrobat DC

Adobe Acrobat DC is a powerful PDF editing suite that offers multiple options for adding text to documents.

  • Tool Menu:
    • Click on the "Tools" menu in the top toolbar.
    • Select "Fill & Sign" from the dropdown menu.
  • Signature Creation:
    • Click either "Add signature" or "Add initials" to create your own digital signature.
    • Follow the prompts to create your unique signature.
    • Once created, you can apply your signature to the document using the "Sign" tool.
  • Text Addition and Editing:
    • Select the "Text" tool from the "Fill & Sign" toolbar.
    • Click on the point where you want to add text.
    • Enter the desired text and customize it using the options at the top (font, size, color, alignment, etc.).

Preview (Mac Only)

Preview, Apple's built-in PDF viewer and editor, offers a simple method for adding text.

  • Pencil Icon:
    • Click on the pencil icon located in the top-right corner of the Preview toolbar.
  • Text Box Insertion:
    • Click on the "T" icon to insert a text box.
    • The text box will appear on the document where you clicked.
  • Text Customization:
    • Type your desired text into the text box.
    • Adjust the font, size, color, and alignment using the options provided in the toolbar at the top.

Mobile Devices

iPhone/iPad

Markup

Markup is Apple's annotation tool that can also be used to add text to PDFs.

  • Pencil Icon:
    • Tap the pencil icon in the bottom-left corner of the screen.
  • Text Addition:
    • Tap the "+" icon on the bottom toolbar.
    • Select "Text" from the options.
  • Text Customization:
    • Type your desired text into the text field.
    • Tap anywhere outside the text field to save your changes.

Android

Acrobat Reader DC

  • Pencil Icon:
    • Tap the pencil icon in the bottom-right corner of the screen.
  • Fill & Sign:
    • Tap on "Fill & Sign" in the bottom toolbar.
  • Text Tool:
    • Tap on the text tool from the options on the right.
    • Click on the point where you want to add text.
  • Signature Creation:
    • Tap on "Create Signature" to create your own digital signature.
    • Follow the prompts to create your unique signature.

Tips for Adding Text

  • Font Customization: Adjust the font size, color, and alignment to match the existing text or your preferences.
  • Signature Addition: Add a digital signature to PDFs that require it, ensuring the authenticity of your documents.
  • Annotation and Highlighting: Use the same methods to add annotations or highlight key points in your documents.
  • Saving Changes: Remember to save your edited PDF using the "Save" or "Save As" option to retain your changes.
