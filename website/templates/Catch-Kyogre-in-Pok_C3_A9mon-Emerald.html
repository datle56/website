How to Catch Kyogre in Pokémon Emerald: A Comprehensive Guide

1. Preparation

As Kyogre is a legendary Pokémon, meticulous preparation is essential for a successful capture. Before embarking on this quest, ensure that you have:

  • Defeated the Elite Four and Champion, demonstrating your Pokémon's prowess.
  • Trained your Pokémon to at least Level 70, providing them with sufficient strength and endurance.
  • Stocked up on an ample supply of Hyper Potions, Revives, Net Balls, and Timer Balls:
    • Hyper Potions: To keep your Pokémon's health replenished.
    • Revives: To revive fainted Pokémon.
    • Net Balls: Effective for catching Pokémon underwater.
    • Timer Balls: Increase in effectiveness the longer the battle continues.

2. Locating Kyogre

Kyogre's whereabouts are not fixed, requiring players to investigate its potential locations. To pinpoint Kyogre's presence, proceed to the Weather Institute situated on Route 119. Engage in conversation with the Scientist within to acquire information regarding Kyogre's current whereabouts.

Based on the information obtained from the Scientist, navigate to the route experiencing unusual rainfall. This anomaly is a telltale sign of Kyogre's presence beneath the ocean's surface.

3. Underwater Exploration

Once you have identified the route where Kyogre is lurking, it's time to take the plunge. Surf across the watery expanse, keeping an eagle eye out for a dark spot. This inconspicuous spot marks the entrance to an underwater cave, where Kyogre resides.

Dive into the dark spot to enter the cave, which serves as Kyogre's underwater sanctuary. Swim through the entrance located at the rear of the cave, leading you to the central chamber where Kyogre awaits your challenge.

4. Entering the Marine Cave

The Marine Cave is Kyogre's lair, a vast and labyrinthine underwater expanse. Before venturing further, it is prudent to save your game, ensuring you can reload if the encounter takes an unexpected turn.

5. The Moment of Truth: Battling Kyogre

With preparations complete, the time has come to confront Kyogre. Engage Kyogre in battle, employing a strategic approach to weaken it and increase your chances of capture.

  • Utilize Grass and Electric Attacks: Kyogre is vulnerable to Grass and Electric attacks. Capitalize on this weakness by using Pokémon with these move sets to inflict significant damage.
  • Net Balls at Low Health: As Kyogre's health dwindles, switch to Net Balls for an enhanced chance of capture. These specialized balls are effective for capturing underwater Pokémon.
  • Timer Balls Over Time: After 30 turns have elapsed in the battle, switch to Timer Balls. These balls gain increased effectiveness as the battle progresses, making them a valuable asset in protracted encounters.

6. Additional Tips

  • Be aware of Kyogre's ability to restore its health. Do not be dissuaded, and persist in your efforts.
  • If you run out of Pokémon or Kyogre gets knocked out, do not despair. Reload your game and resume your pursuit.
  • Refrain from using the Master Ball on Kyogre. This coveted item is invaluable for capturing other legendary Pokémon later in your journey.
