Folding Underwear Made Simple: A Comprehensive Guide

Panties

  1. Step 1: Spread the panties flat on a surface, facing upwards.
  2. Step 2: Bring the left side towards the center, creating a straight line.
  3. Step 3: Fold the right side over the left side, aligning the edges.
  4. Step 4: Lift the bottom edge of the panties and fold it upwards until it meets the waistband.
  5. Step 5: With the waistband facing down, turn the panties over to conceal it.

Thongs

  1. Step 1: Lay the thong flat on a surface, facing upwards.
  2. Step 2: Fold the waistband in half three times, forming a narrow, elongated shape.
  3. Step 3: Bring the crotch area upwards towards the waistband.
  4. Step 4: Turn the thong over so that the waistband, now folded and tucked, is visible.

Briefs

  1. Step 1: Spread the briefs flat on a surface, facing upwards.
  2. Step 2: Fold the left side of the briefs towards the center, creating a straight line.
  3. Step 3: Fold the right side over the left side, aligning the edges.
  4. Step 4: Bring the crotch area upwards towards the waistband.
  5. Step 5: With the waistband facing down, turn the briefs over to conceal it.

Boxers

  1. Step 1: Lay the boxers flat on a surface, facing upwards.
  2. Step 2: Fold the boxers in half from left to right, aligning the side seams.
  3. Step 3: Rotate the boxers 90 degrees to the left.
  4. Step 4: Fold the top of the boxers down to meet the bottom edge.
  5. Step 5: Fold the boxers from left to right, creating a neat and compact rectangle.
