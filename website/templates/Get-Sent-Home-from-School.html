Method 1: Claiming an Embarrassing Condition

  • Step 1: Choose a Common Embarrassing Issue

    Select a condition that is common enough to be relatable, such as diarrhea or gas. This will make your claim more believable.

  • Step 2: Visit the Bathroom Multiple Times

    Create a semblance of urgency by visiting the bathroom several times before approaching your teacher. This will reinforce the idea that you are genuinely experiencing discomfort.

  • Step 3: Privately Confess Your Condition

    Approach your teacher in private to avoid embarrassment in front of classmates. Express your discomfort and embarrassment without going into excessive detail.

  • Step 4: Stick to Your Story

    Be consistent with your story even if questioned. Do not change your symptoms or claim that you are feeling better. Maintaining a consistent narrative will increase your credibility.

Method 2: Faking a Fever

    Request to use a thermometer to confirm your temperature. If possible, subtly manipulate the thermometer to raise your body temperature. This can be done by rubbing the thermometer against your skin or placing it in warm water.

  • Step 3: Consistently Reporting Symptoms

    Repeat your symptoms and express discomfort throughout the nurse's or office worker's examination. Be consistent with your story and avoid overreacting.

    If you are sent home from school, resist the urge to celebrate excessively. This could arouse suspicion and reveal your deception. Maintain a subdued demeanor to support your claim.

Conclusion: Weighing the Consequences
