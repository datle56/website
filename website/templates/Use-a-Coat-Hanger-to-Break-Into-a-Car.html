Unlock Post Door Locks with a Coat Hanger

Materials:

  • Sturdy wire coat hanger

Instructions:

  1. Straighten the Hanger: Unbend the coat hanger completely, removing any curves or bends. Ensure it is straight and sturdy.

  2. Create a "V" Shape: At one end of the straightened hanger, bend the wire at a roughly 90-degree angle to create a "V" shape. This will serve as the hook for unlocking the door.

  3. Pry Open a Window: Identify a slightly open or partially closed window on the car. Use the V-shaped end of the hanger to gently pry open the gap between the window and the frame. Insert the hanger into this gap.

  4. Hook the Lock Post: Guide the hanger through the gap until you locate the lock post, which is a small protrusion that secures the door lock. Carefully hook the "V" shape around the post.

  5. Pull or Push to Unlock: Gently pull or push the hooked hanger to move the lock post. If pulling doesn't unlock the door, try pushing it instead. Repeat this process until the door unlocks.

Push or Pull Slide Locks with a Coat Hanger

Materials:

  • Straightened wire coat hanger

Instructions:

  1. Straighten and Bend the Hanger: Unbend the coat hanger completely, leaving only a small hook at one end. This hook will be used to push or pull the lock slide.

  2. Create a Hook or Ledge: Bend the other end of the straightened hanger to form a hook or a ledge. This will provide a surface for pushing or pulling against the lock slide.

  3. Slide into the Gap: Gently insert the hanger between the car door and the frame. Use the hook or ledge to slide along the surface of the door until it reaches the lock slide.

  4. Locate the Slide: It may be necessary to have a helper locate the exact position of the lock slide for easier access.

  5. Push or Pull: Use the hook or ledge to push or pull the lock slide. Continue applying pressure until the door unlocks.

Make a Slim Jim from a Coat Hanger

Materials:

  • Wire coat hanger

Instructions:

  1. Straighten Except for Hook: Unbend the coat hanger completely, leaving only a small hook at one end. This hook will be used to lift the lock bar.

  2. Slide Between Window and Weather Stripping: Insert the straightened hanger into the narrow gap between the car window and the rubber weather stripping around the window frame.

  3. Turn the Hanger: Rotate the hanger so that the hook faces the interior of the car.

  4. Move Side to Side: Slowly move the hanger side to side to search for the lock bar, which is a metal rod that connects the door lock to the locking mechanism.

  5. Pull or Lift: When the hook locates the lock bar, gently pull or lift it up or back to release the door lock.

Tips:

  • Wear Gloves: To prevent any cuts or punctures, wear gloves while using the coat hanger to avoid hurting your hands.
  • Exercise Caution: Be gentle and avoid excessive force when using the coat hanger. Damaging the window or other components of the car could be costly.
  • Experiment with Angles: Try different angles and bend the hanger as needed to reach the lock components effectively.
  • Persistence: Don't give up if it doesn't work initially. It may take some time and effort to successfully unlock the door.
