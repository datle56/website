Part 1: Finding Molecular Mass

1. Gather Materials:

  • Pencil and paper for notes and calculations
  • Periodic table for referencing atomic weights
  • Calculator for performing mathematical operations

2. Identify the Elements in the Compound:

  • Determine which chemical elements are present in the compound. This information can be obtained from the chemical formula.

3. Determine the Number of Atoms of Each Element:

  • Examine the subscripts in the chemical formula. The subscripts indicate the number of atoms of each element present in a single molecule of the compound. If no subscript is present, assume there is one atom of that element.

4. Find the Atomic Weights of the Elements:

  • Locate the atomic weights of the identified elements in the periodic table. Atomic weights represent the average mass of all naturally occurring isotopes of an element. They are typically expressed in atomic mass units (amu).

5. Calculate the Molecular Mass:

  • Multiply the number of atoms of each element by its corresponding atomic weight.
  • Sum the products obtained for each element to determine the molecular mass of the compound. The molecular mass is expressed in grams per mole (g/mol).

Example:

To find the molecular mass of carbon dioxide (CO2):

  • Carbon (C): 1 atom x 12.011 amu/atom = 12.011 amu
  • Oxygen (O): 2 atoms x 16.000 amu/atom = 32.000 amu
  • Molecular Mass = 12.011 amu + 32.000 amu = 44.011 g/mol

Part 2: Converting Grams to Moles

1. Use the Formula:

  • The formula for converting grams of a compound to moles is:

moles = grams of compound / molar mass of compound

2. Plug in the Values:

  • Replace "grams of compound" with the actual weight of the compound.
  • Replace "molar mass of compound" with the molecular mass calculated in Part 1.

3. Calculate the Number of Moles:

  • Perform the division operation to obtain the number of moles. The result represents the amount of compound present in the sample.

Example:

To convert 2 grams of sodium chloride (NaCl) to moles:

  • Molecular Mass of NaCl: 22.99 g/mol + 35.45 g/mol = 58.44 g/mol
  • Formula: moles = 2 g / 58.44 g/mol
  • Moles = 0.0342 moles
