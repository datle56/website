How to Catch the Regi Trio in Pokémon Emerald Without Cheats: A Comprehensive Guide

The Regi Trio, consisting of Regirock, Regice, and Registeel, are legendary Pokémon available in Pokémon Emerald. These formidable foes offer a thrilling challenge for trainers seeking to complete their Pokédex. However, unlike other legendary encounters, obtaining the Regi Trio requires a series of intricate puzzles and precise actions. This guide will provide a detailed walkthrough of how to catch the Regi Trio in Pokémon Emerald without resorting to cheats.

Step 1: Assembling the Key Pokémon

Before embarking on this quest, you will need to acquire two specific Pokémon: Relicanth and Wailord.

  • Relicanth: This fossil Pokémon can be found underwater near Sootopolis City. Use Dive to explore the ocean floor and search for a Relicanth fossil. Once you have obtained the fossil, revive it at the Sootopolis City Museum into a Relicanth.

  • Wailord: Wailord can be encountered near Dewford Town. Catch a Wailmer and level it up to level 40 to evolve it into Wailord. Alternatively, you can search for a wild Wailord in the area.

Step 2: Locating the Caves

Once you have Relicanth and Wailord in your party, fly to Pacifidlog Town. From there, use Surf to reach the entrance to the cave where the Regi Trio's tablets are located.

Step 3: Unlocking Regirock's Cave

Inside the cave, you will encounter three tablets corresponding to the Regi Trio. To unlock Regirock's cave, you will need to use Dive with Wailord and Relicanth in your party. This will activate a mechanism that allows you to use Dig with a Pokémon that knows the move.

Step 4: Capturing Regirock

After using Dig, fly to Lavaridge Town and enter Regirock's tomb in the desert. Use Rock Smash to break down the wall and encounter Regirock at level 40. Be prepared for a challenging battle, as Regirock is a formidable opponent.

Step 5: Apprehending Regice

To catch Regice, fly to Petalburg City or Dewford Town and make your way to Route 105. Once there, you will find Regice's cave. Follow the walls clockwise inside the cave until you reach a locked door. Use Flash to open the door and confront Regice at level 40.

Step 6: Subduing Registeel

Finally, to capture Registeel, fly to Fortree City and enter Registeel's cave on Route 120. Use Flash to open the door and encounter Registeel at level 40. Registeel is known for its high defense, so be prepared for a protracted battle.

Step 7: Claiming Your Triumph

Congratulations on catching the Regi Trio! These legendary Pokémon will serve as formidable additions to your team. Additionally, you can transfer them to the Diamond, Pearl, or Platinum versions of the game to encounter Regigigas, the colossal leader of the Regi Trio.
