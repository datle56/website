How to Connect a New Fire TV Stick Remote to Your TV

Whether you've purchased a new Fire TV Stick or your existing remote is malfunctioning, connecting a new remote is a straightforward process. Here's a detailed guide on how to do it:

Method 1: Power Cycling

This method works in most cases and is suitable for any type of Fire TV Stick remote.

  1. Disconnect Power: Unplug the Fire TV Stick from the power outlet.

  2. Remove Batteries: Slide open the battery cover on the back of the remote and remove the batteries.

  3. Reinsert Batteries: After 10 seconds, insert fresh batteries into the remote. Ensure they are properly aligned.

  4. Power On: Plug the Fire TV Stick back into the power outlet and turn on your TV.

  5. Re-Pair Remote: Press and hold the Home button (the one with the house icon) on the remote for 10 seconds. The LED indicator on the remote should start blinking.

  6. Success: The remote will automatically pair with the Fire TV Stick.

Method 2: Using Settings (for Additional Remotes)

This method is useful if you want to pair an additional remote to your Fire TV Stick.

  1. Access Settings: Using your existing remote, go to Settings.

  2. Select Controllers & Bluetooth Devices: Navigate to Controllers & Bluetooth Devices.

  3. Choose Amazon Fire TV Remotes: Click on Amazon Fire TV Remotes.

  4. Add New Remote: Select Add New Remote.

  5. Pair Remote: Press and hold the Home button on the new remote for 10 seconds. The LED indicator on the remote should start blinking.

  6. Success: The new remote will be added to the list of paired remotes.

Method 3: Using Your TV Remote (HDMI-CEC)

If your TV supports High-Definition Multimedia Interface (HDMI) Consumer Electronics Control (CEC), you can use your TV remote to control your Fire TV Stick.

  1. HDMI Connection: Connect the Fire TV Stick to an HDMI port on your TV.

  2. Enable HDMI-CEC: Go to your TV's settings and enable HDMI-CEC. The specific steps may vary depending on your TV model.

  3. Control Fire TV Stick: Use your TV remote to navigate and control the Fire TV Stick interface. Press the power button on your TV remote to turn on or off both the TV and Fire TV Stick.

Tips and Troubleshooting

  • You can pair up to seven remotes with your Fire TV Stick.
  • If you have a voice-enabled remote, hold down the Alexa button for 10 seconds instead of the Home button to troubleshoot pairing issues.
  • Not all remote models are compatible with all Fire TV Sticks. Check the Fire TV Stick compatibility page for more information.
  • If you're experiencing connection problems, try replacing the batteries in the remote or unplugging and replugging the Fire TV Stick.
