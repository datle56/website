Unlocking God Human in Blox Fruits: A Comprehensive Guide

Prerequisites:

Before embarking on the journey to obtain God Human, players must meet the following requirements:

  • Fighting Style Mastery: Achieve 400 mastery in each of the following fighting styles:

    • Dragon Talon
    • Superhuman
    • Death Step
    • Electic Claw
    • Sharkman Karate

  • Materials Collection: Gather the following resources:

    • $5,000,000 Beli
    • 5,000 Fragments
    • 20 Fish Tails
    • 20 Magma Ore
    • 10 Dragon Scales
    • 10 Mystic Droplets

Finding the Ancient Monk:

Once the prerequisites have been fulfilled, players can embark on the quest to locate the Ancient Monk, the gatekeeper of God Human. The monk resides on Turtle Island, hidden within a tree behind the Musketeer Pirates.

Purchasing God Human:

After finding the Ancient Monk, engage in conversation and select the option to purchase God Human for $5,000,000 Beli. Upon payment, the monk will bestow upon you the ancient technique.

Using God Human:

God Human grants players three unique abilities, each with distinct characteristics:

  • Soaring Beast (Z):

    • Dash forward with incredible speed, dealing massive damage to nearby enemies with a powerful punch.

  • Heaven and Earth (X):

    • Unleash a gust of wind that deals damage to enemies.
    • Hold X to create a whirlwind that deals continuous damage.

  • Sixth Realm Gun (C):

    • Dash forward with lightning speed, punching and knocking back enemies.
    • Hold C to charge the attack for increased damage.

Benefits of God Human:

  • Enhanced Fighting Prowess: God Human's abilities significantly boost a player's combat effectiveness, allowing them to dominate in PvP and PvE encounters.
  • Versatility: With three distinct abilities, God Human offers players versatility in combat, enabling them to adapt to different situations.
  • High Degree of Control: The abilities of God Human provide a high degree of control over combat, allowing players to precisely target enemies and execute powerful combos.

Tips for Mastering God Human:

  • Practice using the abilities in various combat scenarios to develop proficiency.
  • Experiment with different combinations of abilities to discover optimal attack strategies.
  • Leverage the mobility and range of Soaring Beast to outmaneuver opponents.
  • Utilize Heaven and Earth's wind effects to control enemy movement and create combos.
  • Time the release of Sixth Realm Gun carefully to maximize damage and stun effects.
