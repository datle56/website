Eliminate the Shortcut Virus from Windows Effortlessly with These Simple Steps


How to Remove a Stubborn USB Virus: Two Effective Methods

Are you tired of that pesky USB virus that refuses to go away? Don't give up hope just yet! This comprehensive guide will equip you with two proven methods to save your USB drive from the clutches of this digital menace.

First, we'll introduce you to the UsbFix Antimalware Tool, a powerful weapon against USB viruses. This free tool will scan your drive, identify the infection, and neutralize it. Alternatively, if you prefer a more hands-on approach, we have a step-by-step guide using the Command Line.

Get ready to say goodbye to USB viruses and reclaim your precious data!


Method 1: Using UsbFix Antimalware Tool

Step 1: Prevent the Virus from Running

Install Autorun Exterminator, a free tool that prevents malware from exploiting Autorun functionality. This should be done before connecting your infected USB drive to your computer.

Step 2: Download and Run UsbFix

  1. Visit the UsbFix website (https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/download/usb-fix/) and click "Download Now."
  2. Install the tool and launch it.
  3. Select your USB drive from the dropdown menu and click "Scan."

Step 3: Fix any Detected Virus

UsbFix will scan your USB drive for viruses and malware. If any infections are found, follow the on-screen instructions to remove them. The tool may also prompt you to restart your computer to complete the cleaning process.

Method 2: Using the Command Line

Step 1: Prevent Auto-Run Again

Install Autorun Exterminator to prevent the virus from running automatically as a precaution.

Step 2: Find the USB Drive Letter

  1. Connect your infected USB drive to your computer.
  2. Open File Explorer and navigate to "This PC."
  3. Right-click on your USB drive and select "Properties."
  4. Note the drive letter assigned to your USB drive, usually something like "E:\" or "F:\".

Step 3: Open Command Prompt as Administrator

  1. Press "Windows Key + X" and select "Command Prompt (Admin)."
  2. In the User Account Control window, click "Yes" to grant administrative privileges.

Step 4: Delete Shortcut Links

  1. Type the following command and press Enter: del *lnk

This will delete all *.lnk (shortcut) files from your USB drive.

Step 5: Restore Hidden Files

  1. Type the following command and press Enter, replacing "DRIVELETTER" with the USB drive letter: attrib -h -r -s /s /dDRIVELETTER:\*.*

This command will unhide and restore any hidden files on your USB drive.

Step 6: Format the USB Drive

  1. After scanning your computer for malware using another tool such as Malwarebytes or Windows Defender, reconnect your USB drive.
  2. Right-click on the USB drive in File Explorer and select "Format."
  3. Select a file system (e.g., FAT32, exFAT) and click "Start."

Formatting the USB drive will erase all data and ensure that any remaining infection is removed.


Conclusion:

Battling USB viruses can be a headache, but with these two effective methods, you'll be able to swiftly eliminate the infection and safeguard your devices. If you encounter any further issues, don't hesitate to consult your antivirus provider or a trusted IT professional. Remember, vigilance is key to maintaining the health of your systems, so stay alert to any suspicious behavior and keep your software updated. By following the steps outlined above, you'll be able to confidently connect to unknown USB drives without fear of compromising your precious data.
