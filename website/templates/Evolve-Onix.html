How to Evolve Onix into Steelix: A Comprehensive Guide

Introduction

Onix, the Rock/Ground Pokémon, can undergo a remarkable transformation when exposed to a specific magnetic field and a metal coating. By acquiring these elements and initiating a trade with a compatible Pokémon game, you can witness the evolution of Onix into the mighty Steelix.

Materials You'll Need:

1. Onix: The first step is to find and capture an Onix. Explore caves, mountains, and tunnels within the Pokémon world to locate this rugged creature.

2. Metal Coat: This held item is crucial for triggering Onix's evolution. Different Pokémon games offer various methods to obtain the Metal Coat, such as finding it as a held item on wild Pokémon like Magnemite or receiving it as a reward.

Steps to Evolve Onix into Steelix:

1. Equip Onix with the Metal Coat: Once you have both Onix and the Metal Coat, navigate to Onix's item menu and equip the Metal Coat. This will enhance Onix's magnetic receptivity and prepare it for evolution.

2. Initiate a Trade (for most games): In most Pokémon games, Onix requires trade to evolve. Connect with a trading partner who also owns a copy of the same game or a compatible version.

3. Initiate the Trading Process: Connect your game with your trading partner's and initiate a trade. Select Onix as the Pokémon you wish to trade.

4. Transfer Onix to Your Partner: Confirm the trade to transfer Onix to your trading partner. This action will trigger the evolution process.

5. Receive Steelix: Once the trade is complete, your trading partner will receive Onix. However, a magnetic field surge will activate during the transfer, causing Onix to evolve into Steelix.

6. Get Steelix Back: Have your trading partner trade the newly evolved Steelix back to you. The evolution process is now complete, and Steelix will become a permanent addition to your Pokémon team.

Additional Information:

  • Regional Restrictions: In Pokémon Ruby, Sapphire, and Emerald, Onix cannot be captured in the wild. You will need to trade for Onix from a compatible game.
  • Game Exceptions: In Pokémon Let's Go Pikachu and Let's Go Eevee, Onix cannot evolve into Steelix.
  • Mega Evolution: In Pokémon Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold, and SoulSilver, Steelix can Mega Evolve into Mega Steelix. To do this, equip Steelix with a Steelixite held item and ensure your trainer is wearing a Mega Ring.

Conclusion

Evolving Onix into Steelix is an exciting and rewarding experience that enhances your Pokémon team's strength and diversity. By following these steps and acquiring the necessary materials, you can unleash the potential of this formidable creature and add the mighty Steelix to your collection.
