Changing Your Minecraft Nickname

In the ever-evolving world of Minecraft, players often seek to change their in-game nicknames, whether to reflect a new identity, avoid confusion with similar names, or simply reinvigorate their gaming experience. While the process may vary depending on the version of Minecraft you play, this comprehensive guide will walk you through the steps required to change your nickname in both Java Edition and Bedrock Edition:

Java Edition

  1. Navigate to the Official Minecraft Website: Begin by visiting the official Minecraft website at https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/login.

  2. Log In: Enter your Minecraft account credentials and click the "Log In" button.

  3. Access Your Profile: Once you've successfully logged in, locate and click on the "My Games" option from the menu on the left-hand side of the screen.

  4. Select "Minecraft: Java Edition": Underneath the "My Games" section, find and click on the "Minecraft: Java Edition" option.

  5. Edit Your Profile Name: This will display your current Minecraft username. Click on the "Profile Name" field to edit it.

  6. Enter New Username: Type in your desired new username and ensure that it complies with the Minecraft naming guidelines.

  7. Confirm Changes: Once satisfied with your new username, click on the "Change Profile Name" button to apply the change.

Bedrock Edition

  1. Visit the Xbox Website: For Bedrock Edition, head over to the official Xbox website at https://social.xbox.com/changegamertag.

  2. Log In with Microsoft Account: You will need to log in with your Microsoft account, which is linked to your Minecraft Bedrock Edition account.

  3. Enter New Gamertag: In the field provided, type in your desired new gamertag. Note that your gamertag will also be used as your Minecraft Bedrock Edition nickname.

  4. Check Availability: To ensure that the gamertag is not already taken, click on the "Check availability" button. If the gamertag is available, a green checkmark will appear next to it.

  5. Claim Gamertag: If the gamertag is available, click on the "Claim it" button to confirm the change. Your new gamertag will be applied to both your Xbox account and your Minecraft Bedrock Edition account.

Important Considerations

  • Java Edition: You are only allowed to change your Minecraft Java Edition username once every 30 days. This restriction is in place to prevent abuse and maintain a level of security.
  • Bedrock Edition: Changing your gamertag on the Xbox website will also update your Minecraft Bedrock Edition nickname. This means that the changes will be reflected across all platforms where you access Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
  • Name Guidelines: When choosing a new username or gamertag, ensure that it adheres to the Minecraft naming guidelines. This includes avoiding inappropriate language, hate speech, and copyrighted material.
  • Cost: Changing your Minecraft Java Edition username or Xbox gamertag is free of charge.
