Break Free from the Wolf's Curse: The End of Lycanthropy

As the moon's ethereal glow casts its spell upon the night, a tale unfolds of ancient curses and their elusive cure. Lycanthropy, the affliction that transforms mortals into bloodthirsty beasts, has haunted legends for centuries. But fear no more, for in the realm of Skyrim, there glimmer paths to reclaim your humanity.


Curing Lycanthropy through Companions

Step 1: Embark on the Companions Questline

To initiate the path to curing lycanthropy through the Companions, it is imperative to embark on the main questline of the Companions faction. This entails completing a series of missions that delve into the ancient history and traditions of the Companions.

Step 2: Seek Guidance from Farkas or Vilkas

Once you have gained standing within the Companions, you will be granted access to the Jorrvaskr living quarters. There, you can approach Farkas or Vilkas, two influential members of the faction, to inquire about work.

Step 3: Accept the Purity Mission

Upon expressing your desire to rid yourself of lycanthropy, Farkas or Vilkas will assign you the "Purity" mission. This mission serves as a test of your commitment to the Companions and your willingness to conquer the beast within.

Step 4: Retrieve a Glenmoril Witch Head

The first task in the Purity mission is to obtain a Glenmoril witch head. These heads are relics of ancient witches who possessed the knowledge to cure lycanthropy. To acquire one, you must venture into the dangerous Glenmoril Coven, located in the Rift.

Step 5: Journey to Ysgramor's Tomb

With the Glenmoril witch head in hand, travel to Ysgramor's Tomb, an ancient burial site located near Winterhold. This tomb is said to be the resting place of Ysgramor, the legendary founder of Skyrim.

Step 6: Confront the Quest Giver

Inside Ysgramor's Tomb, you will encounter the quest giver, either Farkas or Vilkas, depending on who you initially spoke to. They will reveal that the Glenmoril witch head alone is not enough to cure lycanthropy. Instead, you must confront and defeat a powerful wolf spirit that resides within the tomb.

Step 7: Slay the Wolf Spirit

The wolf spirit is a formidable opponent, possessing great strength and ferocity. To overcome it, you must engage in a fierce battle, utilizing all of your skills and abilities. Once the wolf spirit is vanquished, you will gain a piece of its essence.

Step 8: Repeat for the Other Brother

If you accepted the mission from Farkas, you must complete the same process with Vilkas, and vice versa. This step ensures that both brothers have witnessed your determination and strength.

Step 9: Cure Yourself with the Flame of the Harbinger

With both pieces of the wolf spirit's essence in your possession, return to Jorrvaskr. Here, you will find the Flame of the Harbinger, a sacred fire that holds the power to cure lycanthropy. By interacting with the Flame of the Harbinger, you will be cleansed of the beast within and permanently cured of lycanthropy.

Curing Lycanthropy through Dawnguard

Step 1: Acquire the Dawnguard DLC

To utilize the Dawnguard method of curing lycanthropy, you must first purchase the Dawnguard downloadable content (DLC) for Skyrim. This DLC introduces a new faction known as the Dawnguard, who are tasked with hunting and eliminating vampires.

Step 2: Complete the "Awakening" Quest

After installing the Dawnguard DLC, you must complete the "Awakening" quest. This quest will introduce you to the Dawnguard faction and their mission.

Step 3: Venture to Castle Volkihar

Once you have completed the "Awakening" quest, travel to Castle Volkihar, located in the far eastern reaches of Skyrim. This castle serves as the stronghold of the vampire lord, Harkon.

Step 4: Embrace Vampirism

Upon entering Castle Volkihar, you will be presented with an opportunity to become a vampire lord yourself. Lord Harkon will offer you the Embrace, a ritual that will transform you into a vampire. If you accept his offer, you will gain access to the full power and abilities of a vampire lord.

Step 5: Alternatively, Become a Vampire Lord through Serana

During the "Chasing Echoes" or "Kindred Judgement" quests, you may encounter Serana, a powerful vampire who becomes your companion. By asking Serana to turn you into a vampire lord during these quests, you can bypass the Embrace ritual with Lord Harkon.

Step 6: Seek a Cure from Serana

Regardless of how you became a vampire lord, return to Castle Volkihar and speak with Serana after completing the "Chasing Echoes" or "Kindred Judgement" quests. She will offer you a final cure for lycanthropy. This cure involves a ritual that will strip you of your vampiric powers as well as your lycanthropy.

By undergoing either the Companions or Dawnguard method, you can permanently cure yourself of lycanthropy and shed the burden of the beast within. Both paths offer their own unique challenges and rewards, allowing you to choose the one that best suits your playstyle and preferences.


Conclusion: The Path to Redemption

Breaking free from the shackles of lycanthropy can be a transformative journey, offering a chance for redemption and renewal. Whether you choose the Companions' ancient ritual or the Dawnguard's forbidden pact, each path presents unique obstacles and rewards.

By embracing the companionship of loyal comrades or immersing yourself in the realm of the undead, you can reclaim your humanity. As you shed your wolf-like instincts, you will not only be cured of the curse but also gain a profound understanding of your own nature and the darkness that lies within.

Remember, the choice you make is not a destination but a testament to the strength of your will and the bonds you forge along the way. May your journey be filled with both peril and redemption, and may you emerge from the shadows as a beacon of hope in the face of the unknown.
