Making Meat Pies: A Comprehensive Guide

Indulge in the comforting and flavorful experience of homemade meat pies, a culinary delight that tantalizes taste buds and warms the soul. This comprehensive guide will empower you to create delectable meat pies from scratch, whether you prefer individual bite-sized pockets or a hearty whole pie.

Ingredients:

Crust:

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup shortening or unsalted butter, cold and cut into small pieces
  • 1/2 cup cold water

Filling:

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup chopped carrots
  • 1 cup chopped celery
  • 1 cup chopped green bell pepper
  • 3 tablespoons margarine or unsalted butter
  • 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 3 cups beef broth
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1/4 teaspoon dried sage
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Instructions:

Making the Crust:

  1. Dry Ingredients: In a large bowl, whisk together the flour and salt.
  2. Cut in Butter: Add the cold butter pieces to the flour mixture. Use a pastry cutter or two knives to cut the butter into the flour until it resembles coarse crumbs.
  3. Add Water: Slowly pour in the cold water while mixing with a fork or your fingers. Add just enough water until the dough comes together and forms a ball. Do not overmix.

Making the Filling:

  1. Brown the Beef: Heat the margarine or butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and cook until browned, breaking up any large clumps.
  2. Add Vegetables: Add the onion, garlic, carrots, celery, and green bell pepper to the skillet. Sauté for 5-7 minutes until softened.
  3. Make a Roux: Sprinkle the flour over the vegetables and cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly.
  4. Add Broth and Seasonings: Gradually whisk in the beef broth. Bring to a simmer and cook for 5-7 minutes, or until the sauce has thickened. Season with thyme, sage, salt, and pepper to taste.

Assembling Individual Meat Pies:

  1. Cut and Roll the Dough: Divide the dough into 6 equal pieces and roll each piece into a circle about 6 inches in diameter.
  2. Fill the Pockets: Place a generous scoop of the filling onto one half of each dough circle.
  3. Close the Pockets: Fold the dough over the filling and press the edges together with a fork.
  4. Cut Slits: Use a sharp knife to make a few slits in the top of each pocket to allow steam to escape.
  5. Bake: Place the pockets on a greased baking sheet and bake at 425 degrees Fahrenheit for 20-25 minutes, or until golden brown.

Assembling a Whole Meat Pie:

  1. Line the Pan: Roll out the remaining dough into a circle large enough to fit a 9-inch pie plate. Transfer the dough to the pie plate and trim the edges.
  2. Pour in the Filling: Pour the meat filling into the prepared pie crust.
  3. Cover the Pie: Roll out the remaining dough into another circle and place it over the filling. Trim and crimp the edges.
  4. Make Vents: Cut a few slits in the top of the pie crust for steam to escape.
  5. Bake: Bake the pie at 375 degrees Fahrenheit for 45-50 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbling.

Creative Variations:

  • Meat Selection: Experiment with different types of ground meat, such as pork, turkey, or lamb.
  • Sweet and Savory: Add raisins, dried apricots, or apples to the filling for a sweet and savory twist.
  • Spice It Up: Enhance the flavor with a pinch of cayenne pepper, cumin, or paprika.
  • Tex-Mex Twist: Add refried beans, corn, and shredded cheddar cheese to the filling for a Mexican-inspired meat pie.
  • Vegetarian Delight: Substitute the ground meat with cooked lentils, chickpeas, or black beans for a vegetarian option.
