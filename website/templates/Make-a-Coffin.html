Part 1: Creating Templates

To embark on the intricate process of constructing a coffin, it is imperative to meticulously craft templates that will guide the cutting and assembly stages. This foundational step lays the groundwork for a well-executed project.

  1. Gather Materials:

    • Shelf paper: A versatile and easily accessible material that will serve as the canvas for drawing the templates.
    • Plywood: Sturdy and durable, plywood will form the skeletal structure of the coffin. Its thickness should be carefully selected based on the desired sturdiness.
    • Wood screws: Essential for securing the various coffin components together, wood screws offer reliable strength and durability.
    • Wood glue: A trusty adhesive, wood glue will supplement the screws in ensuring a крепкий bond between the parts.
    • Measuring tools: Essential for accuracy, measuring tools such as rulers, tape measures, and T-squares will ensure precise dimensions.

  2. Draw Center Lines on Large Paper:

    • On a large sheet of shelf paper, draw perpendicular center lines that intersect at a right angle. These lines will serve as the central axis for the coffin shape.

  3. Create the Outline of the Coffin Shape:

    • Reference vintage coffin designs or draw freehand to create the outline of the coffin. Start from the intersection of the center lines and sketch the form, ensuring symmetry on both sides.

  4. Trace the Outline onto Another Paper to Make a Smaller Template for the Base:

    • Transfer the coffin shape outline onto another sheet of shelf paper. This smaller template will be used to cut the base of the coffin.

  5. Create Templates for the Sides Using Strips of Paper:

    • Cut long strips of shelf paper that are as wide as the desired height of the coffin sides.
    • Place the strips perpendicular to the center lines on the original template, ensuring they extend beyond the coffin outline.
    • Trace the coffin shape onto the strips, connecting the top and bottom edges. These strips will form the templates for the side panels.

Part 2: Cutting Pieces

With the templates in hand, the next stage involves meticulously cutting the plywood pieces that will constitute the coffin's structure. Accuracy and precision are paramount at this stage to ensure a smooth assembly process.

  1. Trace Templates onto Plywood:

    • Securely fasten the templates to the plywood sheets using masking tape or clamps.
    • Carefully trace the outlines of the templates onto the plywood, ensuring accuracy and paying close attention to the grain direction for optimal strength.

  2. Cut the Base with a Saw:

    • Using a circular saw or a jigsaw, carefully cut the base of the coffin along the traced outline. Ensure a clean and precise cut for a secure fit.

  3. Cut Side Pieces by Tracing Templates and Using a Saw:

    • Transfer the side panel templates onto the plywood and secure them.
    • Carefully cut the side pieces along the traced outlines, again ensuring precision and accuracy.

  4. Cut the Lid Outline onto Plywood and Cut It Out with a Saw:

    • Trace the coffin lid outline onto plywood and secure it.
    • Using a circular saw or a jigsaw, carefully cut the lid along the traced outline. Ensure a clean cut for a smooth fit.

  5. Angle the Edges of Side Pieces Using a Saw to Fit Them Together:

    • Measure and mark the angles at which the side pieces will meet at the corners of the coffin.
    • Using a miter saw or a hand saw with a miter box, carefully cut the edges of the side pieces at the marked angles. These angled cuts will allow the pieces to fit together snugly at the corners.

Part 3: Assembling and Finishing

The culmination of the coffin construction process involves meticulously assembling the various components and adding the final touches to create a functional and aesthetically pleasing piece.

  1. Dry-fit Side Pieces Around the Base:

    • Place the base on a flat surface and dry-fit the side pieces around it to ensure a proper fit. Adjust the angles of the side pieces as needed to achieve a snug fit.

  2. Attach Sides with Screws and Wood Glue:

    • Apply a generous amount of wood glue to the edges of the side pieces where they meet the base.
    • Secure the side pieces to the base using wood screws, ensuring they are evenly spaced and countersunk below the surface of the wood.

  3. Add a Lid by Securing It with Screws or a Hinge:

    • Position the lid on the coffin and align it carefully.
    • Secure the lid to the coffin using wood screws or, for a more sophisticated touch, install a hinge to allow for easy opening and closing.

  4. Customize the Coffin with Paint or Fabric if Desired:

    • To add a personal touch or match the coffin's surroundings, consider embellishing it with paint or fabric.
    • Paint the coffin in desired colors and designs using brushes or spray paint. Alternatively, you can cover the coffin with fabric using a stapler or upholstery tacks.
