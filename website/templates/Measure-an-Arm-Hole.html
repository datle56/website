Unlock the Secrets of Armhole Measurements: Essential Guide for Perfect-Fitting Garments

When it comes to sewing, achieving the perfect fit can be a daunting task. One crucial measurement that often gets overlooked is the armhole, yet it plays a vital role in ensuring comfort and a flattering silhouette. In this comprehensive guide, we'll delve into three foolproof methods for measuring your armhole, empowering you with the knowledge to create garments that drape effortlessly over your shoulders.


Method 1: Measuring Your Armhole

Step 1: Raise Your Arm

Raise your arm perpendicularly to your body, parallel to the floor, as if you're signaling "stop." Keep your arm relaxed and slightly bent at the elbow.

Step 2: Wrap the Tape Measure

Take a flexible tape measure and place the zero end at the top of your shoulder, where your neck meets your shoulder. Wrap the tape measure around your armpit, keeping it taut but not too tight.

Step 3: Complete the Measurement

Continue wrapping the tape measure over your shoulder and back to the starting point at the top of your shoulder. This will give you the full measurement of your armhole.

Method 2: Estimating Armhole Measurement from a Shirt

Step 1: Choose a Well-Fitting Shirt

Select a shirt that fits you comfortably, especially around the armholes. Lay the shirt flat on a surface.

Step 2: Measure the Armhole Seam

Locate the seam that curves around the armhole on the shirt. Use the tape measure to measure the length of this seam from one end to the other, both on the front and back of the shirt.

Step 3: Calculate the Armhole Depth

The armhole depth is half of the seam measurement. Divide the front and back seam measurements by 2 to get the front and back armhole depths, respectively.

Step 4: Estimate the Armhole Measurement

Add the front and back armhole depths together to estimate your approximate armhole measurement.

Method 3: Measuring a Pattern Armhole

Step 1: Locate the Armhole Stitching Line

Find the curved line on the pattern piece that represents the stitching line for the armhole. This line should be located between the shoulder line and the side seam.

Step 2: Measure the Armhole Curve

Place the tape measure along the armhole stitching line on the front and back pattern pieces. Measure the curve of the line from one end to the other.

Step 3: Calculate the Armhole Measurement

Add the front and back armhole measurements together. This will give you the total armhole measurement for the pattern piece.

Additional Tips:

  • For a more accurate measurement, use a flexible tape measure that conforms to your body.
  • If you have difficulty raising your arm, you can have someone assist you with the measurement.
  • If you are using Method 2, ensure that the shirt you use is a good fit for you, especially in the shoulder and armhole areas.
  • When using Method 3, check that the armhole curve on the pattern piece aligns well with your natural armhole shape.

Conclusion:

Determining your armhole measurement accurately is crucial for achieving a well-tailored fit in your garments. The three methods outlined in this guide provide reliable techniques to obtain this measurement, whether you're measuring your own body, estimating from an existing shirt, or using a pattern. By carefully following the steps for each method, you can ensure that your armholes align seamlessly with your body, resulting in comfortable and flattering garments that enhance your overall style.
