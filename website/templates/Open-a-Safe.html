Comprehensive Guide to Safe-Opening

3-Wheel Combination Safe:

  1. Turn the dial left three times: Rotate the dial counterclockwise, passing the first number three times. Allow the dial to stop on the first number.
  2. Turn the dial right twice: Rotate the dial clockwise, passing the second number two times. Allow the dial to stop on the second number.
  3. Turn the dial left once: Rotate the dial counterclockwise, passing the third number one time. Allow the dial to stop on the third number.
  4. Turn the dial right to open: Rotate the dial clockwise until you hear a click or the bolt retracts, indicating that the safe is open.

4-Wheel Combination Safe:

  1. Turn the dial right four times: Rotate the dial clockwise, passing the first number four times. Allow the dial to stop on the first number.
  2. Turn the dial left three times: Rotate the dial counterclockwise, passing the second number three times. Allow the dial to stop on the second number.
  3. Turn the dial right twice: Rotate the dial clockwise, passing the third number two times. Allow the dial to stop on the third number.
  4. Turn the dial left once: Rotate the dial counterclockwise, passing the fourth number one time. Allow the dial to stop on the fourth number.
  5. Turn the dial right to open: Rotate the dial clockwise until you hear a click or the bolt retracts, indicating that the safe is open.

Digital Safes:

  • Enter the code: Use the keypad to input the correct numeric code. The code must be entered exactly as programmed into the safe.

Swipe Card Safes:

  • Swipe the card: Insert the authorized swipe card into the designated slot.
  • Enter the code: After swiping the card, input the correct numeric code on the keypad.

Key-Lock Safes:

  • Insert the key: Insert the correct key into the keyhole.
  • Turn the key left to open: Rotate the key 90 degrees counterclockwise to unlock the safe and open the door.

Using a Locksmith:

In the event that the combination is lost or the safe is jammed, it is recommended to contact a reputable locksmith. Here's how to proceed:

  1. Provide documentation: Have documentation of ownership and identification ready to prove your identity and right to access the safe.
  2. Schedule an appointment: Contact a locksmith and schedule an appointment for them to come to your location.
  3. Opening and reset: The locksmith will employ specialized techniques to open the safe without damaging it. They may also reset the combination or replace components if necessary.

Conclusion:

By following these quick and easy steps, you can safely open various types of safes, ensuring the security of your valuables. Whether you need to access a 3-wheel, 4-wheel, digital, key-lock, or swipe card safe, these instructions will guide you through the process. If you encounter any difficulties or lose the combination, do not hesitate to contact a professional locksmith for assistance. Remember, proper safe opening techniques help prevent damage to the safe and its contents, keeping your valuables secure and accessible whenever you need them.
