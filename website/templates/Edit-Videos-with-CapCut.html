Getting Started with CapCut: A Comprehensive Guide

1. Download and Installation

  • Head to the Apple App Store (for iOS devices) or Google Play Store (for Android devices) and search for CapCut.
  • Download and install the free app.

2. Importing Media

  • Once CapCut is installed, open the app and tap on the "New Project" button.
  • Select the videos or photos you want to edit from your phone's library or cloud storage.
  • Tap on the "Add" button to import the selected media into a new CapCut project.

3. Familiarizing with the Editing Interface

Preview Panel: * Located at the top of the screen, it displays the video you're editing. * Use the play and pause buttons to control playback.

Timeline: * Situated below the Preview Panel, it shows the sequence of clips in your video. * Each clip is represented as a separate segment on the timeline.

Toolbar: * Located at the bottom of the screen, it houses various editing tools and features. * Use icons such as Trim, Join, Split, Speed, Reverse, Audio, Filters, Text, and Stickers to perform different editing tasks.

4. Basic Editing

Trim Clips: * Select the clip you want to trim on the timeline. * Drag the gray handles at the beginning or end of the clip to shorten it.

Join Clips: * Select multiple clips on the timeline. * Tap on the "Join" icon in the Toolbar to merge them into a single clip.

Split Clips: * Select the clip you want to split on the timeline. * Tap on the "Split" icon in the Toolbar to divide it into two or more parts.

5. Advanced Edits

Adjust Speed: * Select the clip you want to adjust the speed for on the timeline. * Tap on the "Speed" icon in the Toolbar. * Choose "Normal" speed editing to change the speed uniformly or "Curve" speed editing to vary the speed throughout the clip.

Reverse Clips: * Select the clip you want to reverse on the timeline. * Tap on the "Reverse" icon in the Toolbar to play it backward.

Add Background Audio: * Tap on the "Audio" icon in the Toolbar. * Select "Music" to browse and add background music. * Select "Sound Effects" to add sound effects. * Select "Voiceover" to record and add your own voice narration.

Enhance with Filters, Text, and Stickers: * Tap on the "Filters" icon in the Toolbar to add visual filters to your video. * Tap on the "Text" icon to add text overlays. * Tap on the "Stickers" icon to add animated stickers or images.

Undo Edits: * If you're not satisfied with an edit, simply tap on the "Undo" arrow in the top-left corner of the screen. * This action will revert the changes made to the selected clip.
