Crafting Bread with Yeast: A Time-Honored Tradition

Ingredients:

  • 1 package active dry yeast (2 1/4 teaspoons)
  • 2.25 cups warm water (105-115°F)
  • 6.5 cups all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
  • 3 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon kosher salt
  • 2 tablespoons canola oil

Instructions:

  1. Activate the Yeast: In the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with a dough hook, dissolve the yeast in warm water. Let it sit for 5-10 minutes, or until foamy. This indicates that the yeast is active and ready to use.

  2. Create the Dough: Add the sugar, salt, oil, and 3 cups of flour to the yeast mixture. Mix on low speed until a dough forms.

  3. Knead the Dough: Increase the speed to medium and knead for 8-10 minutes, or until the dough becomes smooth and elastic. If it's too sticky, gradually add more flour 1 tablespoon at a time. If it's too dry, add water 1 tablespoon at a time.

  4. First Rise: Grease a large bowl. Form the dough into a ball and place it in the bowl. Cover it with a damp cloth or plastic wrap and let it rise in a warm place for 1-2 hours, or until doubled in size.

  5. Divide, Shape, and Second Rise: Punch down the dough and divide it into two equal loaves. Shape the loaves into desired shapes, such as boules or loaves. Place them on a greased baking sheet and cover them with a damp cloth or plastic wrap. Let them rise for 30-45 minutes, or until nearly doubled in size.

  6. Baking: Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Brush the loaves with water or an egg wash. Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the bread sounds hollow when tapped.

Quick Bread: A Convenient Treat

Ingredients:

  • 2.25 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1.5 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1.5 cups milk or buttermilk
  • 6 tablespoons canola oil

Instructions:

  1. Prepare the Oven and Pan: Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour a 9x5-inch loaf pan.

  2. Combine Dry Ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.

  3. Mix Wet Ingredients: In a separate bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, and oil.

  4. Combine Ingredients: Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir only until smooth. Do not overmix.

  5. Bake: Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and bake for 40-45 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

Tips for Bread Making Success:

  • Proofing is Key: Let the dough rise until it's doubled in size. This ensures a light and airy texture.

  • Customization: Experiment with different ingredients to create personalized loaves. Add grated cheese, herbs, spices, nuts, or dried fruit.

  • Troubleshooting Over-Proofing: If the bread collapses in the oven, it may have been over-proofed. Try letting it rise for less time next time.

  • Storing Bread: Bread can be stored at room temperature for 2-3 days. For longer storage, wrap the bread in plastic wrap or freeze it for up to 3 months.
