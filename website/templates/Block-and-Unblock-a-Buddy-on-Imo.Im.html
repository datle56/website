Unveiling the Secrets: A Comprehensive Guide to Blocking and Unblocking Contacts on Imo Messenger


Navigating the Maze of Blocking and Unblocking on Imo: A Comprehensive Guide

Navigating the social landscape can be tricky, especially when it comes to managing unwanted interactions. Imo, a popular messaging platform, offers users the ability to block and unblock contacts, empowering them to control their digital circle. This guide delves into the ins and outs of blocking and unblocking on Imo, providing a comprehensive resource for managing your contacts effectively. Whether you seek to filter out unwanted messages or mend a broken connection, this step-by-step walkthrough will guide you through the process seamlessly.


Blocking on Mobile:

  1. Open the imo app: Locate the imo app icon on your mobile device's home screen or app drawer and tap on it to launch the application.

  2. Navigate to "Contacts": Once the imo app is open, navigate to the "Contacts" section by tapping on the corresponding tab at the bottom of the screen (Android) or the top of the screen (iOS).

  3. Locate the contact you wish to block: Scroll through the list of contacts or use the search bar to find the individual you want to block.

  4. Tap on the contact's name: Once you have found the contact you want to block, tap on their name to access their profile page.

  5. Initiate the blocking process: Depending on your device's operating system, you will either see a "Delete" option (Android) or a name card with additional options like "Delete contact" (iOS). Tap on either option to proceed.

  6. Confirm deletion: A confirmation pop-up will appear, asking you to confirm that you want to delete the contact. Tap on "Delete" or "OK" to complete this step.

  7. Switch on the "Block" option: After deleting the contact, you will be presented with a "Block" switch or toggle. Slide the switch to the on position to block the selected contact.

Unblocking on Mobile:

  1. Open the imo app: Follow step 1 from the blocking instructions to launch the imo app.

  2. Access the application menu: For iOS devices, tap on the three horizontal lines icon in the top-left corner of the screen to reveal the menu. For Android devices, tap on the three dots icon in the bottom-left corner to open the menu.

  3. Select "Settings": Scroll through the menu options and tap on "Settings" to access the app's settings screen.

  4. Locate "Blocked contacts": Navigate to the "Privacy and Security" section of the settings menu. Under that section, you will find an option for "Blocked contacts." Tap on it to view the list of blocked individuals.

  5. Find and unblock the desired contact: Find the contact you wish to unblock in the list of blocked contacts. Tap on their name to access their blocked contact profile page.

  6. Initiate the unblocking process: On the blocked contact's profile page, you will see an "Unblock" button or option. Tap on it to initiate the unblocking process.

  7. Confirm unblocking: A confirmation pop-up may appear, asking you to confirm that you want to unblock the contact. Tap on "Unblock" or "OK" to complete the process.

Blocking on Desktop:

  1. Open the imo app: Launch the imo desktop application by clicking on its shortcut or searching for it in your computer's program list.

  2. Navigate to "Contacts": Once the imo desktop app is open, click on the "Contacts" tab or list located on the left-hand sidebar of the application window.

  3. Identify the contact you wish to block: Scroll through the contact list or use the search bar to find the person you want to block.

  4. Right-click on the contact: Once you have found the contact you want to block, right-click on their name in the contact list.

  5. Select "Remove from Contacts": A context menu will appear. Select the "Remove from Contacts" option to initiate the contact removal process.

  6. Confirm removal: A confirmation dialog box will pop up, asking you to confirm that you want to remove the contact. Click on "Remove" to complete this step.

  7. Click "Block" in their profile page: After removing the contact, click on their profile name in the contact list. On their profile page, you will see a "Block" button. Click on it to block the selected contact.

Unblocking on Desktop:

  1. Open the imo app: Follow step 1 from the blocking instructions to launch the imo app.

  2. Navigate to the "imo" tab: Click on the "imo" tab located in the top-left corner of the application window.

  3. Select "Blocked Users": From the drop-down menu that appears, select the "Blocked Users" option.

  4. Locate the contact you wish to unblock: Find the contact you want to unblock in the list of blocked users. Click on their name to access their blocked user profile page.

  5. Click "Unblock": On the blocked user's profile page, you will see an "Unblock" button. Click on it to initiate the unblocking process.

  6. Confirm unblocking: A confirmation dialog box will appear, asking you to confirm that you want to unblock the contact. Click on "Unblock" again to complete the process.


Conclusion:

Blocking and unblocking contacts on imo is a straightforward process that can be performed on both mobile and desktop devices. By following these simple steps, you can manage your contacts and maintain the privacy and security of your conversations. Whether you need to temporarily restrict communication or permanently remove someone from your contact list, imo provides user-friendly options to help you manage your interactions efficiently.
