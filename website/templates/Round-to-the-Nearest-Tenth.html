Rounding to the Nearest Tenth: A Comprehensive Guide

Introduction

Rounding numbers is an essential skill in mathematics, statistics, and everyday life. Rounding to the nearest tenth involves approximating a decimal number to the nearest tenth of a unit. This guide will provide a step-by-step procedure, along with special cases and examples, to help you master the art of rounding.

Step 1: Identify the Tenths Place

The first step in rounding to the nearest tenth is to locate the tenths place in the decimal number. This is the digit immediately to the right of the decimal point. For example, in the number 3.24, the digit 2 is in the tenths place.

Step 2: Check the Hundredths Place

The next step is to examine the digit to the right of the tenths place. This is known as the hundredths place. In the number 3.24, the digit 4 is in the hundredths place.

Step 3: Rounding Rules

Based on the value of the digit in the hundredths place, follow these rounding rules:

  • If the hundredths place is 5 or greater, round up the tenths place digit by adding 1.
  • If the hundredths place is 4 or less, round down the tenths place digit by keeping it the same.

For example:

  • Round 3.24 to the nearest tenth:
    • Hundredths place is 4, so round down.
    • Rounded number: 3.2
  • Round 3.25 to the nearest tenth:
    • Hundredths place is 5, so round up.
    • Rounded number: 3.3

Special Cases

  • Zero in the Tenths Place: If the tenths place is a zero, keep the zero in your rounded answer.
  • Negative Numbers: Round negative numbers using the same process, but be sure to keep the minus sign.
  • Long Numbers: If the number is long, find the tenths place and round using the same rules.
  • No Hundredths Place: If the number does not have a hundredths place, it is already rounded to the nearest tenth.

Examples

  • Round 5.74 to the nearest tenth: 5.7
  • Round -2.85 to the nearest tenth: -2.9
  • Round 123.456 to the nearest tenth: 123.5
  • Round 0.2 to the nearest tenth: 0.2

Conclusion

Rounding to the nearest tenth is a straightforward process that requires an understanding of the tenths and hundredths place. By following the steps outlined above, you can confidently round decimal numbers with accuracy and ease.
