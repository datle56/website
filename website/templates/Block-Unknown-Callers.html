Tired of Annoying Robo-Calls and Suspicious Texts? Here's Your Solution!

In the digital age, it can be frustrating to deal with the constant barrage of unsolicited calls and messages that disrupt our daily routines. If you're an iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, or other Android phone user, you'll be thrilled to discover that there's a simple solution to this problem.

Get ready to say goodbye to unwanted interruptions as we guide you through a step-by-step process to effectively block anonymous calls and suspicious messages. Whether you're using an iPhone or any other Android phone, we've got you covered!


Method for iPhone:

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap on "Do Not Disturb."
  3. Toggle the "Do Not Disturb" switch to the "On" position.
  4. Tap on "Allow Calls From."
  5. Select "All Contacts" to only receive calls from saved contacts.

Additional options:

  • You can also choose to allow calls from specific groups or individuals by selecting them from the "Contacts" list.
  • You can set a schedule for Do Not Disturb to automatically turn on during certain times, such as at night or during meetings.
  • You can also enable "Emergency Override," which allows calls from emergency numbers to come through even when Do Not Disturb is turned on.

Method for Samsung Galaxy:

  1. Open the Phone app.
  2. Tap on the "Menu" icon in the top-right corner.
  3. Go to "Settings."
  4. Tap on "Block numbers."
  5. Toggle the "Block anonymous calls" switch to the "On" position.

Additional options:

  • You can also choose to block calls from specific numbers or contacts by adding them to the blocklist.
  • You can set up a schedule for call blocking to automatically block calls during certain times.
  • You can also enable "Call Forwarding" to send blocked calls to another number or voicemail.

Method for Other Android Phones:

  1. Download the "Should I Answer" app from the Google Play Store.
  2. Open the app and go to "Settings."
  3. Scroll down to "Block INCOMING calls from" and toggle the "Hidden numbers" switch to the "On" position.

Additional options:

  • The "Should I Answer" app also offers other call blocking features, such as the ability to block calls from specific area codes or countries.
  • You can also enable "Caller ID Lookup" to identify unknown callers and block them based on their reputation.
  • You can set up a schedule for call blocking to automatically block calls during certain times or when your device is in a specific location.

Conclusion:

By implementing these methods, you can effectively silence unwanted calls and safeguard your privacy. Whether you're using an iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, or another Android device, there's a simple solution to block anonymous callers. Remember to regularly review your settings to ensure maximum protection against spam and unwanted disturbances. By taking control of your call experiences, you can enhance your productivity and create a more peaceful digital environment.
