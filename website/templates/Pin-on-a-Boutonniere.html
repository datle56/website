Master the Art of Boutonniere Pinning with These 4 Simple Techniques


How to Pin a Boutonniere or Corsage with Grace and Style

Whether you're attending a formal event or a casual gathering, adding a touch of floral flair can elevate your attire and make a lasting impression. Pinning a boutonniere or corsage correctly ensures they stay in place and complement your outfit harmoniously. From the classic lapel placement to the intricate dress hold, this comprehensive guide will empower you to accessorize with botanical elegance and confidence.


Method 1: Lapel

Step 1: Positioning

  • Carefully place the boutonniere on the left lapel, slightly above the heart.
  • Position the flower on the widest part of the lapel, ensuring it aligns with the top button of your suit jacket.

Step 2: Securing with Pins

  • Take a sturdy pin and insert it from the back of the lapel, approximately 1 inch below the flower.
  • Gently pierce the boutonniere stem with the pin, guiding it through the stem and back into the lapel.
  • The pin should create a hidden stitch that securely holds the boutonniere in place.

Step 3: Additional Security

  • If necessary, insert a second pin alongside the first to provide extra stability.
  • Ensure both pins are positioned at an angle to prevent the boutonniere from shifting or falling off.

Method 2: Dress Shirt

Step 1: Location Selection

  • Choose a spot on the upper left side of your dress shirt, where there is a pocket if possible.
  • The pocket will provide additional support and prevent the boutonniere from drooping.

Step 2: Piercing the Fabric

  • Pinch the fabric at the chosen location and gently insert a straight pin through the layers.
  • Hold the pin perpendicular to the fabric to ensure a secure hold.

Step 3: Inserting the Boutonniere

  • Take the boutonniere stem and carefully insert it between the fabric and the pin.
  • Position the flower facing toward you so that it is properly displayed.

Step 4: Securing

  • Push the pin through the other side of the fabric, ensuring it pierces through both layers.
  • The boutonniere should be held flat against the shirt, with a secure connection that prevents it from moving.

Method 3: Dress

Step 1: Safety Precaution

  • For safety, gently lift the dress fabric away from your skin before inserting any pins.

Step 2: Position and Placement

  • Position the corsage below the left shoulder, slightly across from the armpit.
  • Ensure it is placed at an appropriate height and angle to complement your dress.

Step 3: Pinning Horizontally

  • Hold a long, sturdy pin horizontally and insert it from the side closest to your arm.
  • Pierce through the dress fabric and the corsage stem, ensuring a secure connection.

Step 4: Double Pinning

  • Insert the pin a second time, approximately 2 inches from the first pin.
  • Guide the pin through the fabric and corsage stem again, creating a triangular hold that effectively secures the corsage.

Method 4: Buttonhole

Step 1: Preparing the Buttonhole

  • Carefully open the buttonhole on the left lapel of your suit jacket.
  • Locate the small latch located behind the buttonhole opening.

Step 2: Sliding the Boutonniere

  • Take the boutonniere stem and gently slide it through the buttonhole opening.
  • Guide the stem behind the latch, ensuring it is securely held in place.

Step 3: Adjusting and Presenting

  • Carefully push the flower down into the buttonhole so that only the flower is visible from the front.
  • The boutonniere should be positioned at an angle that complements the lapel and suit jacket.

Conclusion:

Wearing a boutonniere or corsage is a timeless tradition that adds a touch of elegance to any formal occasion. With these four simple methods, you can confidently affix your floral adornment to your attire. Whether you choose to secure it to your lapel, dress shirt, dress, or buttonhole, each method ensures a secure and stylish hold that will complement your outfit and make you stand out from the crowd.
