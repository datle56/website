Calculating the Surface Area of Rectangular and Cylindrical Boxes

Rectangular Boxes

The surface area of a rectangular box can be calculated in two ways:

Method 1: Summing the Areas of the Sides

  • Calculate the area of each of the four sides of the box:
    • Length × Width (for the two opposite sides)
    • Height × Width (for the two opposite sides)
  • Sum the areas of all four sides:
    • Surface Area = Area of Length Sides + Area of Width Sides + Area of Height Sides

Method 2: Using the Simplified Formula

  • Utilize the simplified formula: Surface Area = 2lw + 2lh + 2wh
    • where:
      • l is the length of the box
      • h is the height of the box
      • w is the width of the box

Cylindrical Boxes (Tubes)

The surface area of a cylindrical box (tube) can be calculated using the following steps:

Step 1: Calculate the Base Area

  • Determine the area of one of the circular bases:
    • Area of Base = πr²
    • where:
      • r is the radius of the base

Step 2: Calculate the Circumference

  • Determine the circumference of the base:
    • Circumference = 2πr
    • where:
      • r is the radius of the base

Step 3: Calculate the Height-Circumference Area

  • Multiply the height of the cylinder by its circumference:
    • Height-Circumference Area = hC
    • where:
      • h is the height of the cylinder
      • C is the circumference

Step 4: Calculate the Surface Area

  • Sum the area of the two circular bases and the height-circumference area:
    • Surface Area = 2B + hC
    • where:
      • B is the area of the base
      • h is the height of the cylinder
      • C is the circumference

Conclusion:
