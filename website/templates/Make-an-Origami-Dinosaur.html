Unleash Your Inner Origami Master: A Step-by-Step Guide to Crafting a Majestic Dinosaur


Embark on an Origami Adventure with Two Legendary Creations:

Prepare to unleash your inner origami master with this captivating guide that will unveil the secrets to crafting two awe-inspiring prehistoric giants: the majestic T-Rex and the soaring Pterosaur! These elegant paper creations will transport you back in time, igniting your imagination and challenging your folding prowess.

From the formidable jaws of the T-Rex to the graceful wings of the Pterosaur, every intricate fold will reveal a captivating story of these magnificent creatures. As you meticulously follow the step-by-step instructions, you'll witness the transformation of simple paper into lifelike beings that will captivate and inspire.

Whether you're a budding origami enthusiast or a seasoned paper artist, these beginner-friendly and intermediate-level models promise an unforgettable origami experience. Grab your folding paper and prepare to embark on a thrilling prehistoric adventure!


Beginner's Pureland T-Rex

Step 1: Prepare the Paper Begin with a square sheet of origami paper. Fold it in half diagonally from corner to corner, pressing firmly to create a crisp crease. Unfold the paper and repeat the diagonal fold in the opposite direction.

Step 2: Form the Preliminary Base Re-fold the paper along one of the original diagonal creases. Now, fold the upper right and left edges towards the center crease, meeting at the central point.

Step 3: Shape the Head and Neck Crease the lower left side of the paper upwards, aligning it with the center crease. Next, bring the top point of the paper downwards to meet the intersection of the previous creases, forming the head and neck of the T-Rex.

Step 4: Define the Quadrilaterals Fold out the inner corners of the upper quadrilaterals, creating two smaller quadrilaterals on either side of the neck.

Step 5: Fold and Unfold the Top Flap Lift the top flap, which represents the head, and fold it upwards along the existing crease. Then, unfold the flap partially, creating a small triangular flap on top.

Step 6: Shape the Tail Fold the lowest point of the diamond shape, which will become the tail, upwards to meet the base of the neck.

Step 7: Form the Legs Fold the inner flaps outwards, creating two legs for the T-Rex.

Step 8: Finish the Body Unfold the back flap, which forms the body, and bring it downwards, aligning it with the bottom edge of the model.

Step 9: Pleat and Fold Inwards Pleat the top and bottom sections of the body inwards, creating a more defined shape.

Step 10: Create the Head and Tail Folds Make two mountain folds on either side of the head, defining its shape. Valley fold the head downwards, giving it a more downward angle.

Step 11: Position the Model Rotate the model so that the tail rests on a surface, giving the T-Rex a more imposing stance.

Intermediate Pterosaur

Step 1: Prepare and Pre-crease the Paper Start with a square sheet of origami paper. Fold it in half horizontally and vertically, pressing firmly to create four sharp creases.

Step 2: Fold the Bottom Point Bring the bottom point of the paper upwards to the center of the model, forming a triangle.

Step 3: Fold the Bottom Edge Fold the bottom edge of the model upwards to meet the center line, forming a smaller triangle on top of the larger one.

Step 4: Fold the Front Layer Fold a small portion of the front layer upwards, creating a rectangular flap.

Step 5: Pre-crease for 3D Model Pre-crease the model in half vertically, creating a center line. Make two additional pre-creases in the upper section, parallel to the center line.

Step 6: Pre-crease the Top Tip Pre-crease the top tip of the model twice, creating two parallel lines.

Step 7: Add Dimension Fold along the pre-creases to add dimension to the model. Fold the top point downwards, bringing it below the top crease.

Step 8: Make Reverse Folds Make an inside reverse fold and an outside reverse fold on the right side of the model.

Step 9: Valley Fold the Upper Portion Valley fold the upper portion of the model downwards, creating the head of the pterosaur.

Step 10: Valley Fold in Half Valley fold the model horizontally in half, bringing the wings together.

Step 11: Separate Wings and Head Valley fold the wings apart and press the lower body tip inwards, creating a more defined shape.

Step 12: Shape the Wings and Body Make straight folds and curved folds on the wings and body to refine their shape.

Step 13: Define the Head Fold down the top point of the head to create the beak.
