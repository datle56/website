Unlock the Power of Voice Memos: Convert Them to Text with Documents by Readdle

Tired of manually transcribing your valuable voice memos? Discover the magic of Documents by Readdle, a game-changer that seamlessly converts your spoken words into written text. Dive into this comprehensive guide and learn how to effortlessly convert your audio recordings into editable documents, saving you countless hours of tedious typing.


Method 1: Using Documents by Readdle

Step 1: Save the Voice Memo on Your Phone

  • Locate the Voice Memo app on your iOS device.
  • Open the app and select the voice memo you wish to convert.
  • Tap the "Share" button (an arrow pointing upwards out of a square).
  • Choose "Save to Files" from the share options.
  • Select a folder on your device to save the voice memo.

Step 2: Download and Install Documents by Readdle

  • Visit the App Store on your iOS device.
  • Search for "Documents by Readdle" and select the app from the search results.
  • Tap "Get" to download and install the app.

Step 3: Open Documents and Navigate to Converter

  • Once Documents by Readdle is installed, open the app.
  • Tap the "More" tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  • Scroll down and select "Converter" from the list of tools.

Step 4: Import the Voice Memo

  • In the Converter tool, tap the "Import" button in the top right corner of the screen.
  • Navigate to the folder where you saved the voice memo in Step 1.
  • Select the voice memo file and tap "Open."

Step 5: Choose the Output Format

  • Once the voice memo is imported, you will see a list of available output formats.
  • For conversion to text, select "Text (.txt)" from the list.
  • Other available formats include PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, and more.

Step 6: Convert the Voice Memo

  • Tap the "Convert" button at the bottom of the screen.
  • The conversion process will begin. The time it takes to convert will vary depending on the length and complexity of the voice memo.

Step 7: View and Save the Converted Text

  • Once the conversion is complete, the converted text will appear in a new document.
  • You can view, edit, and share the text from within Documents by Readdle.
  • To save the text file to your device or cloud storage, tap the "Share" button and select the desired option.

Conclusion:

For those seeking a quick and efficient way to convert their Voice Memos into MP3 files, Documents by Readdle offers a convenient solution that requires minimal technical expertise. By following these simple steps, users can effortlessly transform their audio recordings into a format that is widely compatible with various devices and media players. Whether you're looking to share your recordings with others, archive them for future reference, or edit them in a preferred audio editing software, Documents by Readdle provides an accessible and reliable method to convert your invaluable audio content into a standard MP3 format.
