Choosing a Protective Cup

Measuring Your Waist:

  • Use a measuring tape to determine your waist size around the area where the cup will sit.
  • Alternatively, consult the tag on a pair of pants that fit comfortably.

Selecting a Cup Size:

  • Refer to the manufacturers sizing chart to determine the appropriate cup size based on your waist measurement.
  • Different brands use slightly different sizing conventions, so consulting the chart is essential for an accurate fit.

Choosing a Cup Shape:

  • Crescent moon cups: These cups provide a wider coverage area, offering better protection. They are suitable for athletes with a larger frame or those who require maximum protection.
  • Form-fitting cups: These cups are designed to hug the body more closely, providing a snug fit. They are ideal for athletes with a smaller frame or those who prioritize mobility.

Considering Material and Lining:

  • Hard cups: Made of sturdy materials like polycarbonate, these cups provide maximum protection against impact.
  • Soft cups: Constructed from more flexible materials, these cups offer a more comfortable fit, but may not provide the same level of protection as hard cups.
  • Gel lining: For those with sensitive skin, a cup with a thicker gel lining can provide added comfort and reduce irritation.

Selecting a Supporter

Jock Strap:

  • Features elastic bands that wrap around the legs and a pouch to hold the cup.
  • Suitable for sports that require a lot of movement, such as hockey, baseball, and basketball.
  • Provides a secure fit and allows for quick adjustments.

Compression Shorts:

  • Tight-fitting underwear with a built-in slot to hold the cup.
  • Ideal for sports with less movement, such as cycling, weightlifting, and running.
  • Provides a comfortable and secure fit for extended periods of time.

Impact Shorts:

  • Compression shorts with built-in padding for additional protection.
  • Suitable for high-impact sports like snowboarding, skating, and motocross.
  • Offer maximum protection against hard falls and collisions.

Wearing Your Cup Properly:

  • Slide the cup securely into the opening of the supporter.
  • Adjust the cup until your genitals are fully and comfortably protected.
  • Perform movements to ensure the cup stays in place, such as taking a few steps and doing lunges.
  • Wash the supporter and clean the cup thoroughly after every use to maintain hygiene and prevent bacterial growth.

Conclusion: Arm Yourself with Protection

Choosing the right athletic cup and supporter is essential for safeguarding your most vulnerable areas during sports and recreational activities. By following these guidelines, you can confidently select a protective system that fits your body, prevents painful injuries, and allows you to perform at your best. Remember, a properly fitted cup empowers you to engage in physical activities with peace of mind and avoid the dreaded "cup check" after every impact. So, invest in quality protection and let the fear of injury fade away, leaving you to focus solely on the exhilaration of the game.
