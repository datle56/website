Method 1: Prying Open a Window

Assessment and Preparation:

  • Inspect the Window: Determine if the window is operable and lacks any physical barriers like locks or grilles.
  • Examine the Paint Seal: Check if paint has adhered to the window frame, preventing it from opening.

Procedure:

  • Loosen Dried Paint: If paint has sealed the window shut, use a sharp utility knife or razor blade to carefully cut away any excess.
  • Insert a Wedge: Gently insert a thin but sturdy wedge or putty knife between the edge of the window and the window frame.
  • Break the Seal: Place a block of wood on the edge of the wedge and tap it lightly with a hammer. This impact force helps break the paint seal.
  • Pry Open the Window: Once the seal is broken, apply force to the window using your hands or a pry bar. Gently push or pry the window open.

Method 2: Lubricating a Stuck Window

Identification of Cause:

  • Friction: Friction between the window and the frame can cause the window to stick.
  • Moisture: Excess moisture in the window frame can lead to swelling, making it difficult to open.

Lubrication Techniques:

  • Candle Wax: Rub the length of a candle along the top and sides of the window channel where it slides within the frame. The wax provides lubrication, reducing friction.
  • Blow Dry or Dehumidify: Use a hair dryer or a dehumidifier to remove any excess moisture from the window frame. This will help reduce swelling.
  • Widen the Channel: Use a wood block and a hammer to gently tap on the inside of the window frame, widening the channel slightly. This allows the window to slide more freely.
  • Spray Lubricant: Apply a spray lubricant, such as WD-40, along the window's edge and the channel. This provides a long-lasting lubrication effect.
  • Open and Close Repetitively: Repeatedly slide the window open and closed. This action helps distribute the lubricant and loosen up the window.

Method 3: Removing a Window Sash

Safety Considerations:

  • Wear Safety Glasses: Protect your eyes from flying debris during the removal process.
  • Ensure Proper Support: If the window is large or heavy, get assistance or support it with blocks or stands before removing the sash.

Removal Steps:

  • Remove Stopper Nails: Locate the window stops that hold the sash in place and remove them using a screwdriver or crowbar.
  • Unscrew Hardware: Check for screws or latches that secure the sash to the window frame and remove them.
  • Tilt and Lift Lower Sash: Tilt the top of the lower window inward. Identify and release the ropes connecting the bottom sash to the pulleys inside the window frame.
  • Sand Edges: Sand any rough edges or obstacles on the sash to ensure smooth removal.
  • Remove Upper Sash: Slide the upper sash down to release the ropes and proceed to remove it following the same steps as the lower sash.
  • Sand Track: Once both sashes are removed, sand the track inside the window frame to smooth out any irregularities.
  • Reinstall Sashes: Reverse the removal process to reinstall the window sashes.
