Master the Craft of Effortlessly Downloading YouTube Videos on Your Mac: A Step-by-Step Guide


Unlock the Secrets of YouTube: Effortlessly Download Your Favorite Videos

Get ready to bid farewell to buffering and hello to seamless video enjoyment, all at your fingertips!


Method 1: Using VLC Media Player

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Install VLC Media Player:
    • Visit the official VLC website (https://www.videolan.org/).
    • Click on the "Download VLC" button and select the appropriate installer for your operating system.
    • Run the installer and follow the on-screen instructions to complete the installation.
  2. Open VLC Media Player:
    • Locate the installed VLC icon and double-click on it to launch the application.
  3. Copy YouTube Video URL:
    • Open YouTube and navigate to the video you want to download.
    • Click the "Share" button below the video and copy the URL from the address bar of your browser.
  4. Paste URL into VLC:
    • In VLC Media Player, navigate to the "File" menu and select "Open Network" (or press Ctrl+N).
    • In the "Open Media" window, paste the copied YouTube URL into the "Network URL" field.
    • Click "Open" to start streaming the video in VLC.
  5. Save YouTube Video:
    • Right-click on the video window in VLC and select "Video" from the context menu.
    • Hover over "Save" and click on "Save As File."
    • In the "Save File" window, browse to the desired location on your computer and enter a filename for the video.
    • Click "Save" to download the YouTube video to your local drive.

Method 2: Using an Online Converter

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Choose an Online Converter:
    • Visit websites like 9xBuddy (https://9xbuddy.com/), Y2Mate (https://y2mate.com/), or KeepVid (https://keepvid.com/).
  2. Paste YouTube Video URL:
    • In the designated field on the converter website, paste the YouTube video URL that you want to download.
  3. Select Video Quality:
    • Most converters offer a range of video quality options. Choose the desired quality from the available options. Higher quality videos will generally have a larger file size.
  4. Download YouTube Video:
    • Click on the "Download" button on the converter website.
    • Wait for the website to process your request and prepare the video for download.
    • Once ready, click on the provided download link to save the YouTube video to your computer.

Method 4: Recording the Screen with QuickTime

Note: This method involves recording the video as it plays on your screen, which can result in lower video quality than the other methods.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Open YouTube Video:
    • Open YouTube and navigate to the video you want to record.
  2. Open QuickTime:
    • On a Mac, open Finder and navigate to the "Applications" folder.
    • Double-click on the "QuickTime Player" icon to launch the application.
  3. New Screen Recording:
    • In QuickTime, click on the "File" menu and select "New Screen Recording."
  4. Select Microphone:
    • In the settings window, click on the arrow next to "Microphone" and select "Internal Microphone" to record the video's sound.
  5. Select Recording Area:
    • Drag the crosshairs on the screen to select the video area you want to record.
  6. Start and Stop Recording:
    • Click on the "Record" button in QuickTime to start recording the video.
    • Press "Command + Shift + Esc" on a Mac to stop the recording.
    • Alternatively, you can click on the "Stop Recording" button in QuickTime.
  7. Save Screen Recording:
    • After stopping the recording, QuickTime will ask you to save the screen recording as a video file.
    • Choose a desired location on your computer and enter a filename for the video.
    • Click "Save" to export the screen recording to your local drive.
