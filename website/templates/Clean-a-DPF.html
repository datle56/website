Solving DPF Problems

Diesel particulate filters (DPFs) play a crucial role in reducing air pollution from diesel engines. However, they can sometimes run into issues that require attention. If you notice the DPF warning symbol illuminated on your dashboard, it's time to take action.

Active Regeneration

  • High-Speed Driving: Take your vehicle for a 15-minute drive at speeds exceeding 40 mph. This initiates the active regeneration process, which burns off accumulated soot from the DPF.
  • Maintain Engine Speed: Aim to keep the engine speed around 2500 rpm during the active regeneration phase.

Cleaning Additives

  • DPF Cleaning Additive: Purchase a specialized DPF cleaning additive designed to break down ash and soot.
  • Fuel Tank Addition: Carefully pour the additive into your vehicle's fuel tank.
  • Drive and Monitor: Drive for 15-30 minutes after adding the additive. The additive will work its magic as you drive.
  • Regular Use: To maintain the health of your DPF, it's recommended to use DPF cleaning additives every 3-6 months.

Maintenance Tips

  • Mechanic Consultation: If the DPF warning symbol persists despite active regeneration or additive use, consult a qualified mechanic to diagnose the underlying issue.
  • Fuel Quality: Always use high-quality diesel fuel to help keep your DPF clean and functioning correctly.
  • DPF Tampering: It's illegal and dangerous to remove or tamper with the DPF. Leave this to the professionals!
