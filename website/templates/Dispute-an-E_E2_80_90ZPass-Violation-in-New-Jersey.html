Navigating Toll Maze: Demystifying Incorrect Violations in New Jersey

Ever received a pesky toll violation despite having an active E-ZPass account? It's like finding a mystery letter in your mailbox, leaving you baffled and frustrated. But fear not, intrepid drivers! This guide will unravel the enigma of toll violations in New Jersey, empowering you with the knowledge to dispute and prevent these unwelcome surprises. From submitting disputes to understanding the finer points of E-ZPass, we've got you covered. So, buckle up and let's embark on this toll-trotting adventure together!


How to Handle Incorrect Toll Violations in New Jersey

Receiving a toll violation from NJ E-ZPass despite having an active account can be a frustrating experience. However, you don't have to worry, as there are clear steps you can take to dispute it effectively.

Submitting a Dispute

To initiate a dispute, follow these steps:

  • Online Dispute: Visit the NJ E-ZPass website: https://www.ezpassnj.com/violations/violationinquiry.do
    • Enter your license plate number and violation number.
    • Select "View Violation."
    • You will be presented with the details of the violation and the option to pay or submit a dispute.
  • Call Centers:
    • Customer Service Line: 1-888-288-6865
    • Violation Processing Center: 1-973-368-1425

Preventing Future Violations

To avoid future incorrect toll violations, it's essential to adhere to the following guidelines:

  • Payment and License Accuracy: Ensure that your E-ZPass account has a valid payment method and license plate number registered.
  • Vehicle Restriction: Use your E-ZPass tag only in the registered vehicle.
  • Tag Placement: Install the E-ZPass tag behind or next to the rearview mirror for optimal signal reception.
  • Tag Maintenance: If your E-ZPass tag is over 3 years old, request a new one to ensure proper functioning.

Understanding the Violation

Before disputing the violation, it's important to fully understand the issue:

  • Photo Verification: Examine the photo on the violation notice to confirm that it's your vehicle and E-ZPass tag.
  • Violation Type: Identify the specific type of violation, such as unpaid tolls, improperly mounted tag, or incorrect account details.
  • E-ZPass Statement Review: Scrutinize your itemized E-ZPass statement to identify any suspicious deductions or discrepancies.
  • Account Standing: Verify that your E-ZPass account is in good standing by contacting the service center at 1-888-288-6865 or visiting the website: https://www.ezpassnj.com/

Dispute Tips

  • If the violation is due to a towed vehicle, remove your E-ZPass tag before towing to avoid additional fees.
  • For violations exceeding $120, you can set up a payment plan to manage the balance.
  • Gather documentation to support your dispute, such as bank statements showing E-ZPass payments or proof of vehicle repairs that may have unmounted the E-ZPass tag.
  • Remain patient and persistent throughout the dispute process. It may take some time to resolve the issue, but it's important to follow up regularly and provide necessary evidence.

By following these guidelines, you can effectively dispute incorrect toll violations in New Jersey and ensure that you're not held liable for charges that are not your responsibility.


Conclusion:

Handling incorrect toll violations in New Jersey can be stressful, but by following the steps outlined in this guide, you can effectively resolve the issue. Remember to stay proactive by ensuring your E-ZPass account is accurate, your tag is properly installed, and you understand your violation notices. If you encounter any further difficulties or have additional questions, don't hesitate to contact the NJ E-ZPass customer support team for assistance. By addressing incorrect violations promptly, you can avoid unnecessary penalties and maintain a clear driving record.
