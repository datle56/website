Method 1: Using a Mobile Device (Expanded)

Step 1: Deplete Your Lives

Launch the Candy Crush app and play until you exhaust all your lives. Once you have no lives remaining, proceed to the next step.

Step 2: Force Close the App

Completely close the Candy Crush app by swiping it up or pressing the "X" button in the multitasking menu. This ensures that the game will not continue running in the background.

Step 3: Adjust Device Time

Access your phone's settings and navigate to the time and date settings. Manually set the time forward by three hours. This will trick the game into thinking that enough time has passed for you to earn new lives.

Step 4: Wait and Restart

Wait for a couple of minutes to allow the time change to take effect. Then, restart the Candy Crush app.

Step 5: Check for Extra Lives

Upon restarting Candy Crush, you should notice that you have gained extra lives. This time hack effectively rewinds the internal game timer, granting you additional plays.

Step 6: Reset Time to Automatic

Once you have played with your extra lives, remember to change your phone's time back to the automatic setting. This prevents potential issues with other apps that rely on the accurate time.

Method 2: Using a Computer (Expanded)

Step 1: Have One Life Remaining

Play Candy Crush until you have only one life left. This step is crucial, as losing your last life is the trigger for the time manipulation technique.

Step 2: Open Two Browser Tabs

In your web browser, open two separate tabs. Load Candy Crush in both tabs. The purpose of this is to have two instances of the game running simultaneously.

Step 3: Alternate Gameplay

Start playing Candy Crush in the first tab. If you lose your last life, quickly switch to the second tab and continue playing the game.

Step 4: Tab Switching Strategy

Keep alternating between the two tabs to continue playing. Close the tab in which you lose a life before the life counter reaches zero. By doing this, you can effectively reset the life counter and keep playing indefinitely.
