Method 1: Covering Your Head Completely

Folded Turban

  1. Choose a triangular scarf in a desired color or pattern.
  2. Fold the scarf in half diagonally to create a triangle.
  3. Place the folded scarf on your head with the center point on your forehead.
  4. Bring the ends around the back of your head and cross them.
  5. Return the ends to the front and tuck them under the scarf.
  6. Adjust the scarf as needed for comfort and coverage.

Top Knot

  1. Gather your hair into a ponytail at the crown of your head.
  2. Secure the ponytail with a hair tie.
  3. Take a scarf and drape it around your head, bringing the ends together at the nape of your neck.
  4. Tie the ends of the scarf securely and tuck them under the knot of your ponytail.
  5. Adjust the knot and scarf for desired coverage and style.

Back Knot

  1. Place a scarf around the back of your neck, bringing the ends together.
  2. Tie the ends of the scarf with a hair tie to create a knot.
  3. Adjust the knot to the desired tightness and position.
  4. Bring the scarf forward to cover your head and secure the ends underneath the knot.

Vintage Look

  1. Choose a square scarf with a vintage or elegant pattern.
  2. Place the scarf diagonally on your head, with one corner extended to form a point at the back.
  3. Fold the remaining corners of the scarf around your head and tie them under your chin.
  4. Adjust the scarf to create a snug and stylish fit.

Method 2: Tying a Decorative Scarf

Headband

  1. Choose a long, skinny scarf in a contrasting color or pattern.
  2. Place the scarf around your head with the ends behind your hairline.
  3. Bring the ends together at the top of your head and tie them securely.
  4. Adjust the headband to the desired tightness and position.

Skinny Turban

  1. Use a long, thin scarf in a complementary color or print.
  2. Place the scarf on your head with the middle behind your hairline.
  3. Cross the ends of the scarf over each other at the nape of your neck.
  4. Wrap the ends around your head, pulling them tight.
  5. Tuck the ends of the scarf securely under the wrap.

Bow

  1. Fold a square scarf into a triangle.
  2. Create a long band by folding one edge of the triangle down.
  3. Tie the band around your neck, bringing the ends together at the top of your head.
  4. Form a bow with the ends of the band and secure it with a knot.
  5. Adjust the bow and scarf for a flattering and stylish look.

Method 3: Folding a Hijab

Simple Wrap

  1. Choose a rectangular hijab in the desired color or material.
  2. Place the hijab across your forehead with the edge at the level of your eyebrows.
  3. Wrap the ends around the back of your head and bring them to the front.
  4. Cross the ends and tie them securely under your chin.

Secured Look

  1. Pin the hijab under your chin to secure it in place.
  2. Wrap the long end of the hijab around your head, starting at the nape of your neck.
  3. Secure the end with a pin at the desired position.
  4. Adjust the hijab for comfort and coverage.

Breezy Look

  1. Place the hijab on your head with one end draped over your shoulder.
  2. Wrap the other end around itself, creating a knot or tuck.
  3. Adjust the hijab for a comfortable and stylish fit.
