How to Join the Elite British Special Air Service (SAS): A Comprehensive Guide

Eligibility Requirements:

  • Active Military Service:
    • Join the British Army, Navy, or Air Force and complete 39 months of service.
  • SAS Reserves:
    • Serve in the SAS Reserves for a minimum of 18 months.

Age and Health Criteria:

  • Be a physically healthy male between 18 and 32 years old.
  • Possess a strong baseline level of fitness and cardiovascular endurance.

Selection Process:

The SAS selection process is renowned for its rigor and extreme challenges, designed to test both physical and mental fortitude. It consists of seven distinct phases:

Phase 1: Screening

  • Medical Examination: A thorough medical test to assess overall physical health.
  • Battle Fitness Test:
    • 2.5 km run to be completed within 15 minutes.
    • 10.5 minutes of strenuous individual exercises.

Phase 2: Special Forces Briefing Course

  • Comprehensive knowledge and suitability tests to evaluate cognitive abilities and personal motivation.

Phase 3: Fitness and Navigation

  • Endurance Hikes and Runs: Extended hikes and runs over various terrain.
  • Map and Compass Navigation: Advanced navigation skills using maps and compasses.
  • "Fan Dance": A 24 km hike involving repeated loops and directional changes.
  • "Long Drag": A grueling 64 km hike with a 25 kg pack to be completed within 20 hours.

Phase 4: Initial Continuation Training

  • Combat skills training including weapons handling, tactical drills, and close-quarters combat.
  • Parachute training to develop airborne capabilities.
  • Signaling skills to facilitate communication in challenging environments.

Phase 5: Jungle Training

  • Survival techniques in dense, humid jungle environments.
  • Advanced navigation, combat drills, and boat handling.

Phase 6: Escape and Evasion

  • Stealth tactics for avoiding detection and capture.
  • Evasion skills for living off the land and resisting interrogation.

Phase 7: Continuation Training

  • Specialized training in advanced special operations skills, such as counterterrorism, hostage rescue, and surveillance.

Preparation for Selection:

  • Physical Training: Engage in daily exercise with emphasis on cardiovascular fitness and strength building.
  • Mental Resilience: Develop mental toughness and a relentless commitment to overcome challenges.
  • Inner Drive: Cultivate an unwavering inner drive to excel and achieve extraordinary results.

Additional Considerations:

  • Successful completion of each selection phase is mandatory to advance to the next.
  • Only a very small percentage of candidates make it through the entire process.
  • Joining the SAS is a highly selective and challenging endeavor, requiring exceptional physical and mental capabilities.
  • It is essential to approach the selection process with determination, humility, and a willingness to push your limits to the utmost.

Conclusion:

Joining the SAS is an incredibly demanding endeavor reserved for the elite few who possess unwavering physical fitness, mental fortitude, and an unwavering commitment to service. The rigorous selection process tests not only your physical limits but also your character and ability to adapt to extreme challenges.
