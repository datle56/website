Rehash fond sexual experiences from the beginning of your relationship. Talk about what you loved and what made you spark. This can reignite the passion you once shared and bring back the fire that brought you together.

Plan a special and spontaneous date to create new memories that will add fuel to your relationship. Try a new restaurant, attend a concert, or engage in an activity that gets his dopamine flowing. Show him affection throughout the day with unexpected hugs, kisses, or simply holding his hand. These small gestures can build intimacy and remind him that you love and appreciate him.

11. Compliment Him: Boosting Confidence

Acknowledge his positive qualities and let him know how much you admire him. This can boost his confidence and make him feel valued. Compliment his physical appearance, his intelligence, or his kindness.

If you're concerned about the lack of intimacy, don't bottle it up. Approach him with empathy and honesty. Encourage him to share his perspective and work together to find a solution that reignites the spark in your relationship.
