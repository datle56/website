How to Play Presidents and Assholes

Introduction: Presidents and Assholes is a hilarious and strategic card game that combines luck, skill, and a healthy dose of social sabotage. It's perfect for a group of friends who enjoy a bit of competition and a lot of laughs.

Setup:

  • Gather at least 4 players: The game can be played with as few as 4 people, but it's more fun with a larger group.
  • Choose comfortable and uncomfortable seats: Designate one seat as the "Asshole" seat, which should be the most uncomfortable chair or spot in the room.
  • Designate a dealer: The dealer will deal the cards and start the game.
  • Deal all the cards evenly: Each player receives an equal number of cards.
  • Rank the cards: Aces are the highest-ranked cards, followed by Kings, Queens, Jacks, and so on, with 4s being the lowest-ranked cards.

Gameplay:

  • The player to the dealer's left starts: The player to the left of the dealer plays a single card or set of cards (two or more cards of the same rank, such as a pair of Jacks).
  • To beat the previous play, you must play a higher-ranked card or set of cards: For example, if the previous player played a single Queen, you must play a King or higher.
  • If you have a "2," play it to clear the table: The "2" is a special card that wipes out all other cards on the table and starts a new round.
  • You can use "3s" as wild cards: "3s" can be played as any other card or to clear the table if you play two "3s."
  • If you can't play a card, drink a beer: If you can't follow the rules or beat the previous play, you must drink a beer (or any other designated beverage).

Winning:

  • The first player to get rid of all their cards becomes the President: The President sits in the most comfortable seat and enjoys the admiration of their fellow players.
  • Other players receive a social rank based on when they finish: The second player to finish is the Vice President, and so on.
  • The last player to finish is the Asshole: The Asshole sits in the uncomfortable seat and endures the mockery of the other players.

Next Round:

  • Players switch seats based on their rank: The President takes the best seat, the Vice President takes the second-best seat, and so on. The Asshole gets the worst seat.
  • The Asshole deals the cards and gives their highest card to the President: The Asshole shuffles the deck and deals out the cards to the players. Once the cards are dealt, the Asshole gives their highest card to the President.
  • The President starts the round by playing a card or set of cards: The President leads off the new round, kicking off the competition once again.
  • Players of higher rank can order those of lower rank to drink at any time: For example, the President can order the Vice President to drink a beer, and the Vice President can order the Citizen to drink.

Tips for Winning:

  • Don't get greedy: It's tempting to hold onto your high-ranked cards, but it's better to play them early and get rid of them first.
  • Be strategic: Pay attention to what cards other players are playing and plan your own moves accordingly.
  • Use your "3s" wisely: "3s" are powerful cards that can help you clear the table or outsmart your opponents.
  • Don't be afraid to drink: Drinking is a part of the game, so don't be embarrassed to take a few sips if you can't play a card.
  • Have fun: Above all else, Presidents and Assholes should be enjoyed by everyone playing it. Embrace the chaos and laughter, and let the shenanigans ensue!

Conclusion:

Presidents and Assholes is a hilarious and chaotic drinking game that's perfect for social gatherings and dorm room shenanigans.
