Convert Your Playlist Effortlessly: A Comprehensive Guide

Introduction

Step 1: Assemble Your Playlist in Spotify

The first step is to create a playlist on Spotify, the starting point for your conversion adventure. Simply select the songs you wish to convert and add them to a designated playlist, conveniently labeled "YouTube" or "Convert" for easy identification later on.

Step 2: Engage the Power of Soundiiz

Now it's time to enlist the aid of Soundiiz, a versatile online platform that serves as a bridge between various music streaming services. Visit their website at soundiiz.com and create an account. This is where the magic happens, as you connect your Spotify and YouTube accounts, paving the way for a seamless transfer.

Once the accounts are linked, select the Spotify playlist you want to convert and hit the "Convert" button. Let Soundiiz know that YouTube is your chosen destination platform. Finally, click "Save Configuration" and "Confirm Tracklist" to initiate the process.

Step 3: Install Software for Seamless Downloading

To complete the conversion process, you'll need software capable of downloading the converted files from YouTube. Depending on your operating system, here are the tools to equip yourself with:

Windows Users:

  • Acquire youtube-dl.exe and place it in your User folder for easy access.
  • Download FFmpeg and extract the EXE files into your User folder, ready for action.

Mac Users:

  • Utilize the power of the Terminal to install Homebrew, a package manager that simplifies software installation.
  • With Homebrew on board, summoning youtube-dl and FFmpeg becomes as simple as a command, readily available for your conversion needs.

Step 4: Download and Convert Your Playlist

Prepare to witness the culmination of your efforts as you embark on the final step of downloading the converted songs.

  • Launch Command Prompt (Windows) or Terminal (Mac) and enter the following command, being sure to replace "playlistAddress" with the URL of your YouTube playlist:

youtube-dl --extract-audio --audio-format mp3 playlistAddress

  • Hit Enter and watch as the process unfolds. The download and conversion will commence, bringing your playlist closer to its new home on YouTube.
  • Upon completion, the MP3 files will be nestled comfortably in your User folder, ready to be imported into YouTube and enjoyed wherever your musical journey leads.
