Introducing the Ultimate Guide to Liquid Castile Soap: Making and Melting

Tired of the harsh chemicals lurking in your cleaning and personal care products? It's time to embrace the natural goodness of liquid castile soap. Whether you're a DIY enthusiast or prefer a streamlined approach, we've got you covered with two foolproof methods: making it from scratch and melting soap bars. Let's dive in and empower you with the knowledge and skills to create your own liquid castile soap haven!


Method 1: Creating Liquid Castile Soap from Scratch

Gather Ingredients:

  • 40 fl. oz. extra virgin olive oil (or a combination of vegetable oils such as coconut, almond, or jojoba)
  • 11.5 oz. potassium hydroxide lye flakes
  • 15 fl. oz. distilled water

Safety Precautions:

  • Wear protective gloves, eye goggles, and a mask to avoid contact with lye and fumes.
  • Ventilate your workspace well to prevent the inhalation of lye vapors.

Instructions:

1. Dissolve Lye in Water:

  • In a large, non-metallic bowl, slowly add the lye flakes to the distilled water while stirring constantly. The mixture will heat up and emit fumes, so ventilate your space accordingly.
  • Allow the mixture to cool to room temperature before proceeding.

2. Combine Oil and Lye Solution:

  • Pour the olive oil into a slow cooker or large pot.
  • Slowly and carefully pour the lye solution into the oil while stirring continuously with a wooden spoon or immersion blender.

3. Blend and Heat:

  • Use an immersion blender to mix the oil and lye together until they reach a thick, custardy consistency known as "trace."
  • Cover the soap and cook it on high for 3 hours, stirring every 30 minutes.

4. Test Clarity:

  • After 3 hours, check the soap by taking a small sample and diluting it with water. If the mixture is clear and gel-like, it is ready.

5. Dilute with Hot Water:

  • Add 16 fl. oz. of hot distilled water to the soap mixture and stir well.

6. Liquefy and Store:

  • Cover the soap mixture and leave it in a warm place (such as a turned-off oven) for 8 hours or overnight.
  • Transfer the liquid soap to airtight containers for storage.

Method 2: Transforming Castile Soap Bars into Liquid

Gather Ingredients:

  • Castile soap bars (1-2 bars)
  • 2-3 cups of boiling water

Instructions:

1. Grate Soap:

  • Use a cheese grater to grate the castile soap bars into a slow cooker or large pot.

2. Add Boiling Water:

  • Pour boiling water over the grated soap and cover the slow cooker.

3. Heat and Stir:

  • Set the slow cooker to high for 1 hour.
  • Use a whisk or immersion blender to mix the liquid soap as it melts.

4. Cool and Transfer:

  • Remove the pot from the slow cooker and let it cool for 1 hour.
  • Use a ladle or funnel to transfer the liquid soap to containers for storage.

Tips for Both Methods:

  • Use high-quality oils for the best results.
  • Adjust the amount of water in the liquid soap according to your desired consistency.
  • Allow the liquid soap to age for a few days before using it to improve its lather and effectiveness.
  • Store the liquid soap in airtight containers at room temperature for up to 6 months.

Conclusion:

With these two methods, you can now easily create your own versatile liquid castile soap. Whether you prefer the hands-on approach of making it from scratch or the convenience of melting soap bars, these methods offer a cost-effective and sustainable way to incorporate natural and effective cleaning solutions into your home. By using castile soap, you not only minimize the use of harsh chemicals but also contribute to a healthier environment. So, experiment with different oils and fragrances to create customized mixtures that meet your specific needs and preferences. Enjoy the versatility and benefits of liquid castile soap, knowing that you are taking a positive step towards a more eco-conscious and sustainable lifestyle.
