Mind-Reading Math Tricks

  • "End With 3" Trick:

This trick involves a series of mathematical operations that magically lead to the number 3. Here's how it works:

  1. Ask someone to choose any number.
  2. Instruct them to multiply their chosen number by 2.
  3. Next, have them add 5 to the result.
  4. Divide the previous answer by their original chosen number.
  5. Finally, ask them to subtract 7 from the result.

Regardless of the original number chosen, the final answer will always be 3.

  • "Divide by Half" Trick:

This trick involves a clever combination of addition and subtraction to determine half of a chosen number. Here's the process:

  1. Ask someone to choose a number that is even.
  2. You choose an even number as well.
  3. Add your chosen numbers together.
  4. Divide the sum by 2.
  5. Finally, subtract the original number chosen by the first person from the result.

The final answer will be half of the chosen even number.

  • "Lucky Number 13" Trick:

This trick appears as a stroke of luck, but it's actually a mathematical formula that always results in the number 13. Here's how to perform it:

  1. Ask someone to choose any number.
  2. Instruct them to multiply the chosen number by 9.
  3. Next, have them add up the individual digits of the result.
  4. Add 4 to the sum of the digits.

The final result will always be the number 13.

Simple Number Tricks

  • Multiply by 6 Trick:

This simple trick involves multiplying any even number by 6. The result will always end in the same number that was multiplied by 6.

  • Repeat, Add, Divide Trick:

This trick involves repeating any number in your head three times, adding the individual digits together, and then dividing by 3. The result will always be 37.

  • 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8 Trick:

This trick involves a series of mathematical operations that produce a surprising result. Here's the sequence:

  1. Choose a number between 1 and 6.
  2. Multiply the chosen number by 9.
  3. Multiply the result by 111.
  4. Multiply the result once more by 1001.
  5. Finally, divide the final result by 7.

The resulting number will contain the digits 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, and 8.

Memorizing Pi with Sentence

Memorizing the first several digits of Pi can be challenging, but this sentence trick can help:

  • "How I wish I could calculate Pi."

Count the number of letters in each word of the sentence:

  • How = 3
  • I = 1
  • wish = 4
  • I = 1
  • could = 5
  • calculate = 9
  • Pi = 2

These numbers correspond to the first 7 digits of Pi: 3.141592
