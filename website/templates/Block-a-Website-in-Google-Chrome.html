Block Access to Unwanted Websites with Chrome's Built-In Features


Take Back Control: The Ultimate Guide to Blocking Distracting Websites

Are you tired of spending countless hours mindlessly scrolling through social media or falling into rabbit holes on distracting websites? It's time to reclaim your productivity and focus! This comprehensive guide will empower you with three foolproof methods to block distracting websites on your computer, Android device, or iPhone/iPad.

We'll explore the step-by-step process of using popular website blocking extensions and apps to say goodbye to distractions and hello to a more productive and enjoyable online experience. Whether you're looking to boost your work efficiency or simply reduce screen time, this guide has got you covered!


Method 1: Using a Computer

Step 1: Install the Block Site Extension for Chrome

  • Open the Chrome Web Store.
  • Search for "Block Site."
  • Click the "Add to Chrome" button.
  • Confirm the installation by clicking "Add extension."

Step 2: Block the Desired Website

  • Click on the Block Site icon in the Chrome toolbar.
  • A pop-up window will appear.
  • Enter the URL of the website you wish to block in the text field.

Step 3: Add the Site to the Blocked List

  • Click the "Add Item" button.
  • The website will be added to the blocked list.
  • To verify, you can click the "View Blocked Items" button in the Block Site pop-up window.

Method 2: Using an Android Device

Step 1: Download the Block Site App

  • Open the Google Play Store.
  • Search for "Block Site."
  • Select the official Block Site app.
  • Tap the "Install" button.

Step 2: Enable Accessibility for the App

  • Go to your device Settings.
  • Scroll down and tap on "Accessibility."
  • Under "Installed Apps," locate and toggle on "Block Site."

Step 3: Block the Website

  • Launch the Block Site app.
  • Input the website address you want to block in the provided field.
  • Tap the blue plus button.
  • The website will be added to the blocked list.

Step 4: Verify the Blocked List

  • You can view the blocked websites by tapping the "Blocked Sites" tab in the app.

Method 3: Using an iPhone or iPad

Step 1: Enable Screen Time

  • Go to "Settings" on your device.
  • Scroll down and tap on "Screen Time."
  • If Screen Time is not enabled, toggle it on and create a passcode.

Step 2: Set Content & Privacy Restrictions

  • Tap on "Content & Privacy Restrictions."
  • Input your Screen Time passcode.
  • Tap on "Web Content."

Step 3: Limit Adult Websites

  • Select "Limit Adult Websites."
  • A list of pre-defined categories will appear.
  • Choose "Never Allow" from the options.

Step 4: Block the Website

  • Tap on the "Add Website" button.
  • Enter the URL of the website you want to block.
  • Click "Done."
  • The website will be added to the Never Allow list.

Step 5: Verify the Blocked List

  • To view the blocked websites, go to "Settings" > "Screen Time" > "Content & Privacy Restrictions" > "Web Content."
  • Under "Never Allow," you will find the list of blocked websites.

Conclusion:

By utilizing these simple steps outlined in the three methods, you can effectively block websites on various devices, providing peace of mind and control over your online presence. Whether you're aiming to enhance productivity, protect children from harmful content, or simply reduce distractions, these methods empower you to customize your browsing experience and maintain a healthier relationship with the vastness of the internet.
