Method 1: Mustard Oil

  • Materials:
    • Warm mustard oil
  • Steps:
    • Apply warm mustard oil evenly to dry hair, ensuring thorough distribution.
    • Massage the oil into the scalp, focusing on the roots.
    • Cover the hair with a shower cap or wrap it in a warm towel.
    • Leave the oil on for at least 2 hours or overnight for optimal darkening effects.
    • Rinse the hair thoroughly with a mild shampoo and lukewarm water.
    • Caution: Avoid contact with skin and clothing, as mustard oil can stain.

Method 2: Amla Powder

  • Materials:
    • Amla powder
    • Coconut oil
    • Water
    • Conditioner
  • Steps:
    • Create a paste by mixing amla powder with coconut oil, water, and a small amount of conditioner until it reaches a smooth consistency.
    • Apply the paste evenly to dry hair, distributing it thoroughly from roots to tips.
    • Cover the hair with a shower cap or wrap it in a warm towel.
    • Leave the paste on for 30-90 minutes, depending on the desired darkening effect.
    • Rinse the hair with lukewarm water.
    • Repeat the treatment weekly to gradually darken the hair.

Method 3: Henna Powder

  • Materials:
    • Henna powder
    • Boiling water
    • Lemon juice
    • Other optional ingredients (e.g., honey, olive oil)
  • Steps:
    • Mix henna powder with boiling water until it forms a thick paste. Add lemon juice to create a darker shade, or other optional ingredients for additional benefits.
    • Apply the henna paste to dry hair, ensuring even distribution.
    • Cover the hair with plastic wrap or a shower cap.
    • Leave the henna on for several hours or overnight, depending on the desired intensity.
    • Rinse out the paste with lukewarm water and shampoo the hair to remove any remaining residue.
    • Note: Henna is a permanent dye that cannot be lightened. Once applied, it will gradually darken and develop over time.

Method 4: Walnut Shells

  • Materials:
    • Walnut shells
    • Water
  • Steps:
    • Crush walnut shells into small pieces and boil them in water for approximately 30 minutes.
    • Strain the water and allow it to cool.
    • Apply the walnut shell water to dry hair, distributing it evenly.
    • Cover the hair with a shower cap or wrap it in a warm towel.
    • Leave the walnut shell water on for 1 hour or longer.
    • Rinse the hair with lukewarm water and shampoo to remove any remaining residue.

Method 5: Conditioner

  • Materials:
    • Shampoo and conditioner for brunette hair
    • Cocoa powder (optional)
  • Steps:
    • Use shampoo and conditioner specifically designed for brunette hair to enhance and darken the color.
    • Wash and condition the hair regularly as part of your normal hair care routine.
    • For an additional darkening effect, add cocoa powder to the shampoo before washing.

Method 6: Black Tea

  • Materials:
    • Strong black tea
  • Steps:
    • Brew a strong pot of black tea and allow it to cool.
    • Pour the black tea over dry hair or soak the hair in a bowl of tea for 15 minutes.
    • Rinse the hair with lukewarm water.
    • Repeat the treatment daily for 2 weeks or until you achieve the desired level of darkening.

Method 7: Coffee

  • Materials:
    • Strong brewed coffee
  • Steps:
    • Brew strong coffee and let it cool.
    • Pour the coffee over dry hair or dip the hair into a bowl of coffee.
    • Leave the coffee on for several minutes, depending on the desired darkening effect.
    • Rinse and wash the hair with lukewarm water.
    • Repeat the treatment multiple times to gradually darken the hair further.
