Step-by-Step Guide to Lacing Classic, Bar-Style, and Loop-Back AJ1s Perfectly


Lace Up Your Style: A Comprehensive Guide to Elevate Your Footwear

Explore the art of lacing your shoes with this comprehensive guide. Whether you prefer the classic simplicity of traditional lacing or seek a more intricate and artistic flair, we've got you covered. Join us as we delve into the captivating world of laces, where form meets function, and your footwear becomes a canvas for self-expression.


Method 1: Classic Lacing

Step 1: Thread the Laces

  • Retrieve two laces of appropriate length for your shoe size.
  • Thread each lace through the lowest eyelets, ensuring the laces are equal in length on both sides.

Step 2: Alternate Lacing

  • Hold the left side lace and pass it under the right side eyelet.
  • Bring the lace up through the next eyelet on the right side.
  • Repeat this step with the right side lace, passing it under the left side eyelet and up through the next eyelet on the left side.

Step 3: Pull and Cross

  • After lacing each side, pull the laces straight up to eliminate slack.
  • Cross the laces over each other, forming an 'X' shape.

Step 4: Continue Lacing

  • Repeat steps 2 and 3, alternating sides and ensuring the laces remain taut.
  • Continue lacing up the shoe, passing through all eyelets except the top two.

Step 5: Finish

  • Leave the top two eyelets unlaced.
  • Tie the loose ends of the laces together or tuck them inside the shoe.

Method 2: Bar Lacing

Step 1: Create the Bar

  • Retrieve a single lace and thread it through the lowest eyelets from the outside.
  • Form a straight bar across the bottom of the shoe, extending the laces on both sides to equal lengths.

Step 2: Vertical and Horizontal Lacing

  • Hold the right side lace and pass it vertically through the next eyelet on the right side.
  • Cross the lace horizontally over to the left side and insert it through the next available eyelet.

Step 3: Skip and Lace

  • On the left side, skip one eyelet and lace the left side lace vertically through the next eyelet.
  • Repeat steps 2 and 3, alternating sides and skipping one eyelet on each side as you lace upwards.

Step 4: Finish

  • Leave the last two eyelets unlaced.
  • Tie the excess lace into a factory knot, a simple overhand knot.
  • Tuck the knot under the tongue of the shoe for a clean and secure finish.

Method 3: Loop-Back Lacing

Step 1: Thread and Cross

  • Thread both laces through the lowest eyelets from the outside.
  • Cross the right side lace over the left side lace.

Step 2: Loop and Lace

  • Wrap the right side lace around the back of the shoe and lace it vertically through the next eyelet on the right side.
  • Lace both laces vertically through the next eyelets on each side.

Step 3: Repeat

  • Repeat steps 2 and 3, looping the right side lace around the back each time and then lacing both laces through the next eyelets.

Step 4: Finish

  • Reach the desired lacing height and leave the top loops unlaced.
  • Secure the excess lace by tying, tucking, or leaving it alone, depending on your preference.

Conclusion:

Now that you're equipped with these three versatile lacing methods, you can confidently elevate your footwear with stylish and functional lacework. Whether you prefer the traditional elegance of classic lacing, the athletic versatility of bar lacing, or the unique comfort of loop-back lacing, you can customize your footwear to suit your style and needs. Remember to experiment with different techniques and lace colors to find the perfect combination that complements your looks and enhances your footwear's performance. With a little practice, you'll master the art of lacing and become a master of shoe aesthetics and comfort.
