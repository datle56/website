Easily Set the Default Audio Track in VLC Media Player with Visual Step-by-Step Guide


Unveiling the Secrets: How to Change Audio and Subtitle Languages in VLC

Are you yearning to immerse yourself in international cinema or expand your cultural horizons through foreign films? Mastering the art of changing audio and subtitle languages in VLC is your key to unlocking a world of entertainment. Whether you're a novice or a seasoned VLC user, we've crafted a comprehensive guide that will empower you to effortlessly navigate language barriers and elevate your viewing experience.

In this detailed tutorial, we'll explore two foolproof methods for customizing your audio and subtitle settings in VLC. The first method offers a straightforward approach for beginners, while the advanced method caters to those seeking more granular control over their language preferences. Embrace the challenge and become a maestro of language customization with our step-by-step guide!


Method 1: Simple Settings

Step 1: Access Preferences

  • Launch VLC media player.
  • Click the "Tools" menu at the top of the window.
  • Select "Preferences" from the drop-down menu.

Step 2: Enable Simple Mode

  • In the "Preferences" window, navigate to the bottom and ensure that "Simple" is selected. This simplifies the settings interface, making it easier to configure audio and subtitle preferences.

Step 3: Set Audio Language

  • Click the "Audio" tab in the "Simple Preferences" window.
  • Locate the "Audio Language" field.
  • Enter the preferred audio language code. For example, "eng" for English, "fre" for French, or "esp" for Spanish.

Step 4: Set Subtitle Language (Optional)

  • If you wish to set a preferred subtitle language, click the "Subtitle" tab.
  • Locate the "Subtitle Language" field and enter the corresponding language code.

Step 5: Save and Restart

  • Once you have configured your audio and subtitle preferences, click the "Save" button at the bottom of the "Preferences" window.
  • Restart VLC to apply the new settings.

Method 2: Advanced Settings

Step 1: Access Advanced Mode

  • In the "Preferences" window, navigate to the bottom and select "All" instead of "Simple." This provides access to more granular settings.

Step 2: Adjust Audio Track

  • Click the "Inputs/Codecs" tab in the "Advanced Preferences" window.
  • Scroll down to the "Audio Track" section.
  • Try different audio track numbers listed in the drop-down menu. Each track corresponds to a different audio channel or language.

Step 3: Set Audio Language (Advanced)

  • If you know the specific language code, you can enter it directly in the "Audio Language" field in the "Inputs/Codecs" tab. For example, "jpn" for Japanese or "deu" for German.

Step 4: Adjust Subtitle Track (Optional)

  • If necessary, you can also experiment with different subtitle track numbers listed in the "Subtitle Track" section of the "Inputs/Codecs" tab.

Step 5: Save and Restart

  • After configuring your audio and subtitle settings, click the "Save" button at the bottom of the "Preferences" window.
  • Restart VLC to apply the new settings.

Conclusion:

By implementing these methods, you can effortlessly modify your VLC media player's audio and subtitle settings to enhance your viewing experience. Whether you prefer the simplicity of Method 1 or the customization flexibility of Method 2, you'll be able to enjoy your favorite movies and videos with the desired audio and subtitle languages. Remember to save your changes and restart VLC to apply the new settings. Happy streaming!
