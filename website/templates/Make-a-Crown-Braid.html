Unlock the Majestic Charm of Crowned Braids: A Step-by-Step Guide

Crowns have long symbolized royalty, grace, and femininity. Now, you can adorn your own head with a regal touch by mastering the art of crown braids. Whether you prefer a classic full crown, a chic half-crown, or a bohemian twisted rendition, this guide will empower you to achieve these enchanting hairstyles with effortless ease. Prepare to transform your locks into a breathtaking work of art, fit for any occasion.


Method 1: Crown Braid

Step 1: Half and Secure

Commence by dividing your luscious locks into two equal sections. Secure one half temporarily with a hairband or clip, leaving the other half free.

Step 2: Dutch Braid Magic

On the untethered half of your hair, embark on creating a Dutch braid. Unlike a regular braid where you weave the sections over each other, in a Dutch braid, you cross them under. Keep the braid taut, snug against your scalp.

Step 3: Mirror the Magic

Cross over to the other side of your head, and replicate the Dutch braiding process. Once again, keep the braid tight and parallel to your part.

Step 4: The Grand Finale: Crown Formation

Bring the two braids together at the apex of your head, where they will gracefully unite to form a majestic crown. Secure this regal masterpiece with bobby pins, ensuring that the braids remain in place and the crown is symmetrical.

Method 2: Half-Crown Braid

Step 1: Braiding Beginnings

Choose two sections of hair, one on each side of your head. Starting from the top, engage in the art of braiding. Secure each braid with elastic bands once they reach the desired length.

Step 2: Back-to-Back Wrap

Take one braid and gently drape it around the back of your head, settling it snugly above the nape of your neck. Secure it with bobby pins for stability.

Step 3: Interlacing the Other Side

Perform the same procedure with the second braid, wrapping it around the back of your head in the opposite direction. Skillfully tuck it beneath the first braid, creating an elegant interlocking effect.

Step 4: Half-Crown Perfection

Secure both braids firmly with bobby pins, molding them into a captivating half-crown. The contrasting directions of the braids add a touch of whimsy to this enchanting hairstyle.

Method 3: Twisted Crown Braid

Step 1: Twirling Tresses

Begin by selecting two strands of hair at the hairline. Interlace them gracefully by twisting them together, forming a secure hold.

Step 2: Incorporating New Strands

As you progress along the length of the hair, continue to incorporate strands from the bottom to the top, thereby thickening the braid. Maintain an even tension throughout the process.

Step 3: Shaping the Crown

Once the braid reaches the desired length, gently wrap it around your head, forming the outline of a crown. Allow the end of the braid to rest gently on top of the existing braid.

Step 4: A Touch of Precision

Tuck the end of the braid neatly underneath the existing portion, concealing it from view. Secure the entire crown braid firmly with bobby pins, ensuring that it remains in place throughout your regal adventure.


Conclusion:

With these three versatile methods, you can effortlessly elevate your style with a charming crown braid. Whether you prefer the intricate elegance of the Dutch crown, the romantic allure of the half-crown, or the bohemian flair of the twisted crown, these braids offer endless possibilities to enhance your appearance.

Embrace the beauty of these hairstyles and experiment with different hair textures, lengths, and colors to create a crown braid that complements your unique style. Let these braids be a crowning glory, adding a touch of magic to your days and leaving a lasting impression wherever you go.
