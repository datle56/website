How to Share Your PayPal Link

PayPal is a convenient and secure way to send and receive payments online. If you're looking to request payments or donations, you can easily share your PayPal link to make it simple for others to send you money.

For Desktops:

  1. Access the PayPal Website and Log In: Visit the PayPal website (www.paypal.com) and sign in to your account using your email address and password.

  2. Navigate to the Request Menu: Click on the "Send & Request" tab located at the top of the page. From the dropdown menu, select "Request."

  3. Select 'Share Your PayPal.Me': On the Request page, you'll find an option to "Share your PayPal.Me." Click on this link to generate a personalized PayPal link.

  4. Copy the Link: Once you click on "Share your PayPal.Me," a unique link will appear on the screen. This is your PayPal link that you can share with others. Highlight and copy the link by pressing "Ctrl+C" (Windows) or "Cmd+C" (Mac).

  5. Share the Link: Paste the copied PayPal link wherever you want to share it. You can include it in emails, social media posts, or on your website. When others click on the link, they'll be directed to a secure page where they can easily send you a payment.

For Mobile Phones:

  1. Open the PayPal App: Download and install the PayPal app on your smartphone. Launch the app and log in using your registered PayPal account.

  2. Tap 'Request': Once you've logged in, tap on the "Request" button at the bottom of the screen.

  3. Choose 'Share Your Link to Get Paid': You'll be presented with several options for requesting payments. Select "Share your link to get paid."

  4. Share the Link: The app will generate a unique PayPal link. You can choose to share it directly through the app or copy and paste it elsewhere.

  5. Select a Sharing Method: If you choose to share the link through the app, you'll be prompted to select an app to share it with. Options may include Messages, Facebook, or WhatsApp.

  6. Enter Contact Information: If necessary, enter the contact information of the person you're sharing the link with. This is especially important if you're using an app that doesn't automatically pre-populate the recipient's details.

  7. Send or Post the Link: Once you've entered the contact information, tap "Send" or "Post" to share your PayPal link.

Additional Tips:

  • Ensure that your PayPal link is unique and not shared with anyone other than the intended recipient.
  • Keep your PayPal password secure and avoid sharing it with others.
  • When receiving payments, verify the sender and the amount before confirming the transaction.
  • If you have any issues sharing your PayPal link, contact PayPal support for assistance.
