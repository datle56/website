Fades: A Comprehensive Guide

Fades have become a ubiquitous hairstyle, offering a sharp and versatile look that complements various hair textures and face shapes. This guide will delve into the intricacies of fades, providing detailed instructions and pro tips for achieving the perfect cut.

Types of Fades

Low Fade:

  • Ideal for textured hair, as it leaves more hair at the nape, creating a gradual transition from the top to the sides and back.
  • The fade begins low on the head, just above the neckline.

High Fade:

  • Creates a bolder and cleaner look, trimming most of the hair on the back and sides.
  • The fade starts higher up on the head, often at the temples or even the crown.

Preparation:

  • Invest in clippers with multiple guard lengths (attachments) to achieve different fade levels.
  • Wash and towel-dry the hair before cutting to remove any styling products or dirt.

Fading Technique

Step 1: Trim the Top

  • Lift sections of hair and cut them with styling scissors to create the desired length on top.

Step 2: Trim Sides and Back with a Long Guard

  • Hold the clippers vertically and buzz the hair on the sides and back, starting with a long guard.
  • Lift the clippers near the rounded part of the head to create a smooth transition.

Step 3: Trim the Back with a Shorter Guard

  • Switch to a shorter guard and continue cutting from the base of the head towards the crown.
  • This step establishes the shape and gradient of the fade.

Step 4: Buzz the Nape

  • Use the shortest guard and angle the clippers upward towards the center of the nape.
  • This creates a defined and clean line at the nape.

Clean Up and Styling

Step 1: Clean Edges

  • Use clippers with the shortest guard to carefully clean up the edges around the ears and neckline.

Step 2: Inspect and Brush

  • Have the person look at the cut from different angles to ensure it meets their preferences.
  • Use a brush or comb to remove any stray hairs.

Step 3: Style the Top

  • Apply a hair styling product like clay or pomade to shape and define the top hair.

Step 4: Maintenance

  • To maintain the desired length and style, schedule regular trims every 4-6 weeks.
  • Especially in humid environments or for those with coarse hair, the fade may need more frequent touch-ups.

Pro Tips

  • If there's a cowlick that makes fading difficult, start the fade above or below it.
  • Throughout the process, refer to the person to ensure the fade is customized to their liking.
  • Plan on regular trims to keep the fade looking neat and fresh, preventing it from becoming too elongated and losing its shape.
