How to Play Tambola: A Comprehensive Guide

Introduction

Tambola, also known as Housie or Bingo, is a classic game of chance that has entertained families and friends for generations. It's a simple yet engaging game that can provide hours of fun for people of all ages.

Setup

1. Determine the Winning Point:

Before starting the game, decide on the winning point that will trigger the end of the game. This could be:

  • Full House: All numbers marked off on a player's ticket.
  • Single Line: One complete row of numbers marked off.
  • Double Line: Two complete rows of numbers marked off.

2. Choose a Caller:

Select one person to be the caller. This person will be responsible for randomly drawing and announcing the numbers throughout the game.

3. Distribute Tickets:

Provide each player with a tambola ticket. These tickets typically have 3 rows and 9 columns, with each box containing a number between 1 and 90.

4. Prepare the Number Container:

Place all numbers from 1-90 into a container. This could be a bag, box, or even a simple hat.

Gameplay

1. Drawing and Announcing Numbers:

The caller randomly draws a number from the container and announces it out loud.

2. Marking Off Numbers:

Players check their tickets for the announced number. If they find the number on their ticket, they mark it off with a marker or pen.

3. Claiming Tambola:

The first player to mark off all the numbers required for the winning point (e.g., full house, single line, or double line) shouts out "Tambola!"

4. Verification of Winning Ticket:

The caller carefully checks the winning ticket to confirm that all the required numbers have been marked off.

5. Prize Distribution:

The winner receives a prize, which was predetermined before the start of the game. Prizes can range from cash to gift certificates or even special favors.

Variations

Classic Tambola:

As described above, this is the traditional version of the game, with players aiming to mark off all the numbers on their ticket to claim full house.

Joker:

In this variation, a few extra "Joker" squares are added to the ticket. These squares can be filled with any number, giving players more flexibility in completing their patterns.

Patterned Tambola:

Instead of simply marking off numbers, players have to match predetermined patterns with the announced numbers. For example, they may need to create a shape (e.g., circle, triangle) or spell out a word.

Tips for Winning Tambola

  • Stay Focused: Pay attention to the announced numbers and mark off matches immediately.
  • Use Multiple Tickets: Increase your chances of winning by using more than one ticket.
  • Cover All Numbers: Aim to mark off a wide range of numbers, rather than concentrating on a specific row or column.
  • Call Out Clearly: If you claim tambola, make sure to announce it clearly and loudly.
  • Enjoy the Game: Tambola is meant to be fun, so don't get too stressed about winning.
