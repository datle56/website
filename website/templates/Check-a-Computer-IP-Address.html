Uncover Your Computer's IP Address: 3 Effortless Methods for Windows or macOS


Unlocking Your Computer's IP Enigma: A Guide Simplified

Finding Your Computer's IP Address (Simplified)

Public IP Address (for websites)

Your public IP address is the address that websites see when you access them. To find your public IP address, follow these steps:

  • Visit Google and search for "What's my IP address?".
  • Google will display your public IP address at the top of the search results page.

Private IP Address (for your local network)

Your private IP address is the address assigned to your computer by your router or modem. This address is used to communicate with other devices on your local network. To find your private IP address, follow the instructions for your specific operating system:

Windows:

  • Press the Windows key + R to open the Run dialog box.
  • Type "cmd" and press Enter to open the Command Prompt.
  • In the Command Prompt window, type "ipconfig" and press Enter.
  • Your private IP address will be listed next to "IPv4 Address" for your active network connection.

Mac:

  • Click on the Apple menu and select "System Preferences".
  • Click on "Network".
  • Select the network you are connected to (Wi-Fi or Ethernet).
  • Click on the "Advanced" button.
  • Your private IP address will be listed next to "IPv4 Address" or "IPv6 Address".

Understanding IP Addresses

  • Public IP address: A public IP address is an address that is assigned to your router or modem by your internet service provider (ISP). It is used to identify your network on the public internet.
  • Private IP address: A private IP address is an address that is assigned to each device on your local network. It is used to identify devices within your network and is not visible to the outside world.

IPv4 vs. IPv6

  • IPv4: The most common type of IP address, consisting of four sets of numbers separated by periods, such as 192.168.1.1.
  • IPv6: A newer type of IP address that uses a colon-separated hexadecimal notation, such as fe80:0000:0000:0000:0202:b3ff:fe1e:8329.

Why Do I Need to Know My IP Address?

Knowing your IP address can be useful for:

  • Troubleshooting network issues
  • Identifying devices on your network
  • Setting up port forwarding
  • Accessing specific services or resources online
