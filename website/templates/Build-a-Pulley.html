Method 1: Constructing a Fixed Pulley

Materials:

  • A sturdy wire hanger
  • A cylindrical thread spool or a comparable object
  • Scissors or wire cutters
  • A pair of pliers (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Halve the Hanger: Use scissors or wire cutters to cut the wire hanger into two equal halves.

  2. Spoolfest: Slide the thread spool onto one of the hanger halves.

  3. Hook it Up: Bend the ends of the hanger half that holds the spool inward to create two small hooks. Secure the spool in place by bending the hooks over the ends of the spool.

  4. Suspension: Find a suitable rod or dowel and hang the pulley from it by the hooks created in the previous step.

  5. String it On: Loop a length of string over the thread spool, creating a continuous loop that wraps around the spool.

  6. Weighty Matters: Attach weights of your desired weight to one end of the string.

  7. Lift Off: Pull the other end of the string downward to witness the magic of the fixed pulley in action, lifting the weights with ease.

Method 2: Crafting a Simple Compound Pulley

Materials:

  • Two empty cardboard boxes of similar size
  • A thread spool
  • Two pencils
  • String
  • A paperclip
  • Weights

Instructions:

  1. Box-to-Box: Position the two cardboard boxes opposite each other, ensuring they are facing each other.

  2. Pencil Path: Make corresponding holes in the boxes that are aligned with each other. These holes should be large enough to accommodate pencils.

  3. Pencil Power: Insert pencils through the holes so that they extend out from both sides of the boxes.

  4. String Symphony: Tie one end of the string to a pencil that does not have a spool attached to it.

  5. Paperclip Perch: Slide a paperclip onto the string and suspend it between the pencils. This paperclip will serve as the weight-bearing point.

  6. Weighting Game: Attach weights to the suspended paperclip.

  7. Spool-ing into Action: Pull the string over the thread spool. As you do so, the spool will rotate, effectively lifting the weights in the process.

Understanding Pulley Mechanics:

Pulleys are incredibly useful simple machines that enable us to effortlessly lift heavy objects. They operate on the principle of reducing the force required to move an object by distributing the weight over multiple support points. This functionality stems from the fact that when a force is applied to one end of a string that has been looped around a pulley, the tension in the string remains constant throughout the entire length of the string. Consequently, the force required to lift an object using a pulley is merely a fraction of the object's weight, thereby making lifting significantly easier.
