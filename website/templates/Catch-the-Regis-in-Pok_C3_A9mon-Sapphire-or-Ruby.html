Unlocking the Regis: A Comprehensive Guide

The Regis are a trio of legendary Pokémon in the Pokémon universe, renowned for their enigmatic origins and immense power. To encounter and capture these enigmatic beings, trainers embark on an arduous quest involving meticulous preparation, skillful navigation, and fierce battles.

Step 1: Catch or Train Specific Pokémon

Two key Pokémon play a pivotal role in unlocking the Regis:

  • Relicanth: A rare water-type Pokémon that serves as the keystone for the expedition.
  • Wailord: The evolved form of Wailmer, a colossal Pokémon necessary for accessing the underwater realm.

Step 2: Learning Essential Moves

Trainers must acquire three specific moves to progress through the quest:

  • Dig: Obtained from the Fossil Maniac's brother in Mauville City.
  • Surf: Granted after defeating Norman, the Petalburg Gym leader.
  • Dive: Bestowed by Steven in Mossdeep City.

Step 3: Navigating Underwater Currents

With the necessary moves in hand, journey to Pacifidlog Town on Route 134. Surf westward, hugging the shoreline. Upon reaching the southwestern shallow area, Dive into the dark spot.

Step 4: Finding the Braille Tablets and Using Dig

Within the watery depths, locate submerged caves containing Braille tablets. These tablets hold inscriptions that provide clues to the Regis' whereabouts. Approach the tablet marked "DIG HERE" and use Dig to uncover a hidden path.

Step 5: Assembling the Keystone and Unlocking the Doors

Place Relicanth as the first Pokémon in your party and Wailord as the last. Examine the tablet, and an earthquake will ensue, revealing the entrances to three separate caves.

Finding the Regis

Step 6: Preparing for Battle

Before confronting the Regis, ensure your Pokémon are at least level 50. Utilize status moves such as False Swipe or Sleep/Paralysis to capture them without fainting. Stock up on Ultra Balls for optimal capture success.

Step 7: Regice

Located within Island Cave on Route 105, Regice can be encountered by standing in front of the Braille inscription for two minutes.

Step 8: Regirock

Venture into the Desert Ruins on Route 111. Within the cave, locate the marked spot and use Strength to reveal Regirock.

Step 9: Registeel

Ancient Tomb on Route 120 houses Registeel. Fly to the center of the room, and the door will automatically open.

Obtaining Regigigas (Alpha Sapphire and Omega Ruby)

Step 10: Capturing the Regis

Regigigas, the behemoth of the Regi trio, can only be obtained after capturing Regice, Regirock, and Registeel.

Step 11: Nicknaming Regice

Visit the Name Rater in Slateport City and give Regice a nickname.

Step 12: Equipping a "Cold" Item

Equip Regice with a "cold" item such as a Snowball, Icy Rock, Never-Melt Ice, or Casteliacone.

Step 13: Visiting Island Cave Revisited

Return to Island Cave on Route 105 during the daytime.

Step 14: Preparing for a Final Battle

Regigigas is a formidable opponent at level 50. Employ Fighting-type Pokémon and False Swipes to subdue the titan.
