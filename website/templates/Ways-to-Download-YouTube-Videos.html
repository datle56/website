How to Save YouTube Videos on Any Device: A Comprehensive Guide

Preserving your favorite YouTube videos for offline viewing has become a necessity in today's on-the-go world. Whether you're looking to watch them on your daily commute, during a flight, or in areas with limited internet connectivity, there are numerous methods available to save YouTube videos on any device.

Quick and Easy Methods:

1. YouTube Premium:

Subscribing to YouTube Premium grants you exclusive offline viewing privileges. You can download any video to your device and watch it anytime, anywhere, even without an internet connection. This paid membership also includes ad-free viewing and background playback.

2. Open Video Downloader (Computer):

Open Video Downloader is a free and user-friendly software specifically designed for downloading YouTube videos. Simply paste the video link into the software, select the desired format and resolution, and click "Download." It offers various options to suit your needs, including 4K quality.

3. 4K Video Downloader (Computer):

4K Video Downloader is another popular software that provides more advanced features. It allows you to download videos in multiple formats, including MP4, MKV, FLV, and 3GP. You can also download entire playlists, subtitles, and annotations with ease.

4. VLC Player (Computer):

VLC Player is an open-source media player that offers a versatile solution for saving YouTube videos. Navigate to "Media" > "Open Network Stream" and paste the YouTube video link. Then, click "Play" and select "Convert" from the "Tools" menu. Choose the desired format and save the video to your computer.

Online Converters:

If you prefer not to download additional software, you can use online converters to save YouTube videos.

1. SaveTube:

SaveTube is a quick and easy online converter. Simply paste the YouTube video link into the search bar, select the resolution, and click "Download." It supports various resolutions, including 1080p and 4K.

2. Dirpy:

Dirpy is another free online video converter that offers a range of formats, including MP4, MP3, and AVI. It is known for its fast conversion speed and intuitive interface.

3. en.loader.to:

en.loader.to is a reliable online converter that supports high-quality downloads. You can choose from multiple formats and resolutions, including 4K and 60fps.

Offline Solutions for Specific Devices:

1. MediaHuman (Computer):

MediaHuman is a software that specializes in downloading YouTube videos in high quality. It supports downloading entire playlists and converting videos to various formats. The software also includes a built-in player for previewing videos before downloading.

2. Documents by Readdle (iOS):

Documents by Readdle is a powerful file manager app for iOS devices. It includes a built-in web browser that allows you to browse YouTube videos directly within the app. You can then easily download videos to your device for offline viewing.

Tips:

  • The download process can take several minutes, depending on your internet speed and the size of the video.
