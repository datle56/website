Your AirPods Got Soggy? Here's Your Damage Control Emergency Guide

Oh no! Your beloved AirPods took an unfortunate dunk in the water. Don't panic just yet. With swift action and a little patience, you might still save your precious ear companions. In this blog, we'll guide you through the essential steps of what to do (and what not to do) after your AirPods get wet.


Post-AirPods Wash Care: A Comprehensive Guide to Drying and Storage

After giving your AirPods a thorough cleaning, it's crucial to follow the proper drying and storage procedures to ensure their optimal performance and longevity. Here's a detailed guide outlining the dos and don'ts of post-wash care for your AirPods:

Drying Procedures

1. Dry with a Soft Cloth Immediately:

Use a soft, lint-free cloth to gently pat dry the AirPods and their charging case. Avoid rubbing or applying excessive pressure, as this could damage the delicate internal components.

2. Place in a Dry Location for 12 Hours:

Find a dry, airy spot with low humidity to place your AirPods and their case for at least 12 hours. Ensure there is adequate airflow to promote evaporation and prevent moisture accumulation.

3. Use Silica Gel for Enhanced Drying:

For accelerated drying, place the AirPods and their case in a sealed container with silica gel desiccant packets. Silica gel absorbs moisture effectively, creating a dry environment that further aids in the drying process. Leave them in the container for approximately 12 hours.

Storage Precautions

1. Avoid Using Immediately:

Never turn on or use your AirPods while they are still wet. Doing so could result in electrical damage and malfunction.

2. Keep the Case Unplugged:

Refrain from plugging in the charging case while it is wet. Moisture inside the case could damage the charging points and internal circuitry.

3. Avoid Direct Heat Sources:

Do not use a blow dryer, heater, or air conditioner to dry your AirPods. The heat generated by these appliances can warp or damage the delicate components.

4. Avoid Excessive Humidity:

Store your AirPods in a dry place with low humidity levels. High humidity can create a moist environment that encourages moisture accumulation and potential corrosion.

Additional Tips

  • If water or moisture gets stuck in the speaker grills, use a clean, soft-bristled brush to gently remove any debris.
  • Regularly clean the charging ports to prevent corrosion and ensure proper charging.
  • When not in use, store your AirPods in the charging case to protect them from dust and moisture.
  • Avoid submerging your AirPods in water or any other liquid, as they are not waterproof.
  • If you encounter any charging or sound issues after washing, contact Apple Support for assistance.

Following these post-wash care instructions will help maintain the performance and lifespan of your AirPods. By adhering to these guidelines, you can ensure your wireless earbuds continue to deliver high-quality audio and a seamless listening experience for many years to come.


Conclusion:

By following these simple steps, you can ensure that your AirPods are completely dry and ready to use again. Remember, the key is to be patient and allow them to dry naturally. Avoid using any methods that could damage the delicate electronics inside. Keep in mind that AirPods are not waterproof, so it's essential to take extra care whenever they come into contact with liquid. By following these guidelines, you can extend the life of your AirPods and enjoy their excellent sound quality for years to come.
