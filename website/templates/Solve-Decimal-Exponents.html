Part 1: Calculating a Decimal Exponent

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Convert the Decimal to a Fraction:

    • Identify the place value of the last non-zero digit in the decimal.
    • Count the number of digits after the decimal point.
    • Write the decimal as a fraction with the non-zero digits as the numerator and 1 followed by as many zeros as there are digits after the decimal point as the denominator.

  2. Simplify the Fraction if Possible:

    • Reduce the fraction to its lowest terms by dividing both the numerator and denominator by their greatest common factor (GCF).

  3. Rewrite the Exponent as a Multiplication Expression:

    • Introduce a unit fraction (a fraction with 1 as the numerator and the denominator equal to the original denominator).
    • Multiply the fraction by the unit fraction to create an equivalent expression with a numerator of 1.

  4. Rewrite the Exponent as a Power of a Power:

    • Apply the exponential law that (a^b)^c = a^(bc).
    • Multiply the original exponent by the exponent of the unit fraction.

  5. Rewrite the Base as a Radical Expression:

    • Extract the root of the base using the fractional exponent of the unit fraction.
    • Rewrite the base as a radical expression in the form b^(1/n), where n is the denominator of the unit fraction.

  6. Calculate the Radical Expression:

    • Find the specified root (square root, cube root, etc.) of the number using the exponent in the radical expression.

  7. Calculate the Remaining Exponent:

    • Multiply the simplified expression from Step 4 by the result from Step 6 to obtain the final value.

Part 2: Solving a Sample Problem

Problem: Calculate 2.25^(-3/4)

Solution:

  1. Convert 2.25 to a Mixed Improper Fraction:

    • 2.25 = 2 + 0.25 = 2 + 25/100 = 2 + 1/4 = 9/4

  2. Rewrite the Exponent as a Multiplication Expression:

    • 1/4 times 9

  3. Rewrite as a Power of a Power:

    • 1/4 to the 9th power

  4. Rewrite the Base as a Radical Expression:

    • 4 to the 1/4 power

  5. Calculate the Radical Expression:

    • 4th root of 4 = 2

  6. Calculate the Remaining Exponent:

    • 1/4 to the 9th power = 1/(2^9) = 1/512 = 0.00195

Final Answer: 2.25^(-3/4) = 0.00195

Part 3: Understanding Exponents

Exponential Expression: - A combination of a base (a large number) and an exponent (a smaller number). - Represents the base multiplied by itself the number of times specified by the exponent.

Square Root and Rational Exponent: - The square root of a number is the number multiplied by itself to get the original number. - A rational exponent represents a power that is not an integer, such as 1/2 (square root) or 1/3 (cube root).

Exponential Law of Powers of Powers: - When raising a power to another power, you multiply the exponents of the same base. - (a^b)^c = a^(bc)
