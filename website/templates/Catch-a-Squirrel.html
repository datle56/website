Unveiling the Secrets: A Comprehensive Guide to Humane Squirrel Capture in 11 Simple Steps


The Art of Squirrel Wrangling: A Comprehensive Guide to Outwitting Nature's Nimble Acrobats

Have you ever found yourself engaged in an epic cat-and-mouse game with a mischievous squirrel? These furry daredevils can be both charming and frustrating, but when they overstay their welcome indoors or pose a threat to your garden, it's time to take matters into your own hands. With a few clever techniques and a dash of patience, you can outsmart these clever creatures and restore peace to your realm. Let's dive into the thrilling world of squirrel wrangling and uncover the secrets to capturing these elusive masters of agility.


Catching a Squirrel Outdoors

Method 1: Live Trap

  • Choose a live trap: Opt for a trap specifically designed for squirrels, typically made of sturdy wire mesh and featuring one or more entry points.
  • Bait the trap: Entice squirrels with irresistible treats they commonly seek outdoors, such as nuts (e.g., peanuts, acorns), seeds (e.g., birdseed, sunflower seeds), or fruit (e.g., apple slices, banana peels).
  • Place the trap strategically: Identify areas where squirrels frequent, such as near bird feeders, trees, or the perimeter of your property. Position the trap in these locations to maximize chances of catching one.
  • Check the trap regularly: Monitor the trap daily, especially during morning and evening hours when squirrels are most active. If a squirrel is caught, proceed to release it promptly.

Method 2: Humane Squirrel Removal

  • Bait the squirrel: Attract the squirrel by scattering its favorite treats in a designated corner of your outdoor space.
  • Approach cautiously: Move slowly and quietly towards the squirrel while holding a large blanket extended in front of you. This will block the squirrel's view and prevent it from escaping.
  • Cover the squirrel: Once close enough, swiftly throw the blanket over the squirrel, ensuring it is fully enclosed.
  • Roll up the blanket: Gently roll up the blanket, trapping the squirrel securely.
  • Release the squirrel: Take the blanket outside and carefully open it on the ground. Allow the squirrel to escape by releasing it as close as possible to the point of entry.

Catching a Squirrel Indoors

  • Secure food and other attractants: Remove all potential food sources and other items that may have lured the squirrel indoors. This includes closing off pet food bowls, sealing away snacks, and storing garbage securely.
  • Use a live trap: If the squirrel remains in the home, follow the same steps outlined for outdoor live trapping.
  • Humanely capture the squirrel: Alternatively, you can employ the humane squirrel removal technique described above.

Tips for Success

  • Avoid glue traps: These traps are inhumane and can cause serious harm to animals.
  • Release the squirrel nearby: Release the captured squirrel in a location close to where it was caught. This increases its chances of finding its way back to its territory.
  • Prevent future entry: Once you have successfully removed the squirrel, inspect your home thoroughly for any entry points it may have used. Repair or seal off these openings to prevent further intrusions.

Conclusion: Humane Squirrel Removal

By following these steps, you can effectively and humanely catch a squirrel that has ventured into your home or yard. Remember to prioritize the animal's well-being by using non-harmful methods and releasing it promptly. By understanding their behavior and respecting their habitat, you can peacefully coexist with these fascinating creatures while keeping your property safe.
