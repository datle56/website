Creating Ballistics Gel: A Comprehensive Guide

1. Preparing the Materials:

  • Knox Gelatin: Opt for Knox brand gelatin as its composition closely resembles specialized ballistics gel.
  • Water: Measure 1 gallon of water for every 13 ounces of gelatin powder. This 1:10 ratio ensures optimal gel properties.

2. Preparing the Mold:

  • Plastic Storage Container: Choose a large plastic storage container with dimensions of approximately 12" x 12" x 20".
  • Water Fill Line: Measure and mark a line on the inside wall of the container 6 inches up from the bottom to indicate the maximum water fill level.
  • Non-Stick Spray: Spray the interior of the container with non-stick cooking spray to prevent the gel from adhering to the surface.

3. Making the Gel:

  • Warm Water: Fill the container with warm water at a temperature of 105°F. This temperature promotes efficient gelatin hydration.
  • Gelatin Addition: Slowly pour the Knox gelatin powder into the warm water while constantly stirring to prevent lump formation.
  • Stirring and Dissolving: Stir for approximately 20 minutes or until the gelatin is completely dissolved.
  • Foam and Bubble Removal: Remove any foam or bubbles that may have formed during stirring. The presence of bubbles can affect the gel's integrity.
  • Cooling: Allow the gel to cool to 36°F and refrigerate for a minimum of 8 hours. This cooling process induces gel formation.

4. Extracting and Cutting the Gel:

  • Gel Release: Gently invert the container upside down on a flat surface to release the gel.
  • Gel Division: Use a sharp knife to carefully cut the gel into two equal halves, each measuring approximately 6" x 6" x 20".
  • Wrapping and Transportation: Wrap each gel block in plastic wrap and then cardboard for safe transportation.

5. Shooting the Gel:

  • Stable Surface: Place the gel blocks on a stable surface, such as thick plywood, to minimize movement.
  • Shooting Distance: Shoot the gel blocks at a distance of 10 feet from various positions and distances.
  • Covering Variations: Test the gel's performance by shooting it through different covering materials, including naked, lightly clothed, and heavily clothed targets.
  • Firearm Safety: Always adhere to proper firearm safety protocols during testing.

Creating a Small Gel Block for Pellet Rifles, BB Guns, and Airsoft:

  • Smaller Containers: Use two 16-ounce plastic containers, two packets of Knox gelatin, and a measuring cup.
  • Gelatin and Water: Add the gelatin to one container and 3/4 cup of water.
  • Stirring and Refrigeration: Stir slowly and refrigerate for 3-4 hours to allow partial gel formation.
  • Melting and Pouring: Melt the partially formed gel by placing the container in hot water.
  • Non-Stick Spray and Gel Transfer: Spray the second container and pour the melted gel into it.
  • Cooling: Refrigerate the gel for 12 hours or overnight.
  • Gel Removal: Knock the gel out of the container and shoot it at a distance of 10 feet.
