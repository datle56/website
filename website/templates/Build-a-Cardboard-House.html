Building a Cardboard House

Method 1: Cardboard Model House

Materials:

  • Shoebox-sized cardboard box
  • Scissors
  • Glue
  • Ruler or measuring tape
  • Pencil or pen

Instructions:

  1. Prepare the Box: Cut out the bottom of the shoebox to create the base of the house.
  2. Create the Roof: Cut a triangular shape into another piece of cardboard. Angle the triangle to fit the base of the house. Glue the roof onto the base.
  3. Draw and Cut Out Openings: Sketch the shape of the doors and windows on the cardboard. Carefully cut out the openings using scissors.
  4. Assemble and Decorate: Glue the cutouts for the doors and windows into place. Use markers or crayons to draw additional details, such as shutters or windowsills.

Method 2: Criss-Cross Cardboard Dollhouse

Materials:

  • Large cardboard box
  • Ruler or measuring tape
  • Pencil or pen
  • Scissors
  • Glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut the Box into Sections: Cut the large box into three rectangular sections: a large rectangle (center wall) and two smaller squares (side walls).
  2. Create Room Dividers: Cut narrow strips into the side walls. These strips will be inserted into the center wall to create room dividers.
  3. Assemble the House: Glue the side walls to the center wall, inserting the strips for the room dividers.
  4. Add Openings: Cut out doors and windows in the cardboard. Glue the cutouts into place.
  5. Customize and Decorate: Draw furniture, curtains, and other details on the inside of the house. Use paint, markers, or fabric scraps to embellish the exterior.

Method 3: Cardboard Playhouse

Materials:

  • Large cardboard box
  • Scissors
  • Ruler or measuring tape
  • Pencil or pen
  • Glue
  • Additional cardboard or paper for shingles and eaves

Instructions:

  1. Prepare the Box: Cut off the bottom flaps of the large box.
  2. Cut Out Openings: Cut out a door and windows in the cardboard, leaving one edge uncut on the door to allow it to open.
  3. Form the Roof: Cut two triangular shapes from cardboard. Attach these triangles to the top of the box in an A-frame shape to form the roof.
  4. Add Roof Details: Cut out additional cardboard strips to create shingles. Glue the shingles onto the roof, overlapping them to prevent leaks. Add eaves by gluing narrow strips of cardboard along the edges of the roof.
  5. Embellish and Decorate: Use paint, markers, or other materials to decorate the exterior of the playhouse. Add features such as a mailbox, doorbell, or even a small garden.

Decorating Techniques

Painting:

  • Apply a thin layer of gesso to the cardboard to prime it.
  • Sketch out the desired details in pencil.
  • Paint the exterior walls, doors, and windows with acrylic paint or tempera paint.
  • Seal the paint with a clear varnish to protect it.

Paper Decoration:

  • Use wrapping paper, scrapbook paper, or even magazine pages to decorate the inside or outside of the house.
  • Smooth the paper into place and glue it down securely.
  • Add paper flowers to create a yard or window box, cut out fabric scraps to create curtains, or make paper furniture to populate the rooms.
