Part 1: Obtaining Device Information

Accessing the Device Manager

  • Windows: Right-click on the Start menu and select "Device Manager."
  • macOS: Go to "System Preferences" > "Hardware" > "USB."

Inspecting Device Properties

  • Identify the malfunctioning or unknown device.
  • Right-click on the device and select "Properties."
  • Navigate to the "Details" tab.

Retrieving Hardware IDs

  • Select "Hardware Ids" from the drop-down menu.
  • These IDs are unique identifiers assigned to each device by the manufacturer. They typically consist of a combination of letters and numbers.

Part 2: Identifying and Installing Drivers Using Hardware IDs

Searching for Device Information

  • Copy the top-most Hardware ID.
  • Paste it into a search engine, such as Google.
  • Add the term "driver" to your search to narrow down the results to driver-related information.

Identifying the Device

  • The search results should provide information about the device associated with the Hardware ID. This may include the device model, manufacturer, and specifications.

Locating Drivers

  • Based on the identified device, search for the appropriate drivers.
  • Use keywords such as "driver download" or "update driver" to find the necessary software.
  • You can also visit the manufacturer's website to check for the latest drivers.

Alternative Method: Device Hunt

  • Navigate to the Device Hunt website (https://www.devicehunt.com/).
  • Enter the Vendor ID (VENXXXX) and Device ID (DEVXXXX) from the Hardware IDs into the search bar.
  • Device Hunt will provide a list of potential drivers compatible with your device.

Installing the Drivers

  • Once you have identified the correct drivers, download and install them.
  • Follow the on-screen instructions carefully to complete the installation process.

Restart and Verify

  • Restart your computer to apply the changes.
  • Open the Device Manager and check if the device is now recognized and functioning correctly.
