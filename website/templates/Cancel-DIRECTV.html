Canceling DIRECTV Services: A Comprehensive Guide

Satellite Subscription Cancellation

  1. Contact Customer Support: Initiate the cancellation process by dialing (866) 389-1321.

  2. Navigate Automated Prompts: Select "Other" from the automated prompts to reach a live agent.

  3. Request Retention Department: Politely request to speak to a representative in the retention department, who typically offers incentives to retain customers.

  4. State Cancellation Intention: Clearly express your desire to cancel the service and decline any counteroffers or incentives.

  5. Return Equipment: You will receive a pre-paid return shipping box for the leased equipment, including receivers, remotes, and cables. However, the satellite dish remains your property.

  6. Return Eligible Equipment: Pack and return all equipment except the satellite dish within 30 days of cancellation to avoid a non-return fee.

  7. Cancellation Fee: In some cases, you may be subject to an early termination fee (ETF), depending on your contract terms.

Streaming Subscription Cancellation

  1. Access DIRECTV STREAM Website: Visit the DIRECTV STREAM website at www.directv.com.

  2. Open Account Settings: Click on the gear icon located in the top-right corner to access your account settings.

  3. Navigate to Subscription Management: Under "My Account," select "Manage subscription."

  4. Initiate Cancellation: Click on "I want to cancel" and follow the on-screen prompts to complete the cancellation process.

Tips for Avoiding Cancellation Fees

  • Time Cancellation Strategically: Cancel near the end of your billing cycle to minimize the prorated cancellation fee.

  • Request Fee Waiver: Politely inquire if the cancellation fee can be waived due to factors such as moving to an area without DIRECTV coverage.

  • Escalate to Supervisor: If your initial request for a fee waiver is denied, consider asking to speak to a supervisor who may have more authority to grant this request.

  • Avoid Third-Party Scams: Be wary of third-party companies posing as DIRECTV. Only contact the official DIRECTV customer support line or website to avoid falling prey to scams.
