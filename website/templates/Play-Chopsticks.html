Part 1: Basic Rules

  • Objective: To be the last player with at least one "live" hand.

  • Setup:

    • Two players face each other.
    • Each player extends one finger from each hand, totaling four fingers visible.

  • Gameplay:

    • Players take turns tapping each other's hands.
    • When tapping, players use one finger from either hand.
    • If a player taps a hand with two fingers, the other player adds the fingers on their tapped hand (two) to the tapping finger (one) and extends three fingers.
    • If a hand reaches five fingers, it becomes "dead" and is removed from play. However, leftover fingers (beyond five) are not counted.
    • Players hide dead hands behind their backs.

  • Winning:

    • The game continues until all hands but one are dead.
    • The player with the remaining live hand wins.

Part 2: Advanced Rules

Splits:

  • If a player's hand contains four fingers, they can split it into two hands with two fingers each.
  • This prevents the hand from reaching five fingers and becoming dead.

Game of Five:

  • A variation where a hand can only be tapped to equal exactly five fingers.
  • If a player taps a hand and exceeds five fingers, their tapping finger is "dead" and is removed from play.

Nubs:

  • Players can curl fingers into "nubs" that count as half a finger.
  • Nubs can be used for tapping or extending fingers.

Leftovers:

  • When a hand exceeds five fingers after being tapped, it does not become dead.
  • Leftover fingers can be used later to tap other hands or extend fingers.

Part 3: Variations

Multiple Players:

  • The game can be played with three or more players in a circle.
  • Each player taps the hands to their left and right.

Increase Difficulty:

  • Splitting into nubs or playing with leftovers adds complexity and makes the game more challenging.
  • Players can modify the rules to suit their preferences and skill levels.
