Step-by-Step Guide to Creating Stunning Side Braided Hairstyles


Master the Art of Side Braiding: A Guide to Stunning Styles

From casual chic to elegant allure, side braids have captured the hearts of hair enthusiasts worldwide. With endless possibilities and techniques, this versatile hairstyle offers a flattering and effortless way to elevate your look.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll dive into the secrets of creating three captivating side braid styles: the traditional Standard Side Braid, the intricate French-Style Side Braid, and the striking Dutch Side Braid. We'll also unravel the complexities of the intricate 4-Strand Side Braid, promising you an arsenal of breathtaking hairstyles to conquer every occasion.

So, gather your brushes, styling products, and a dash of creativity. Let's embark on a journey of hair transformation and discover the art of side braiding like a pro!


Creating a Standard Side Braid

Materials:

  • Brush
  • Elastic band
  • Hairspray (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Brush and Part: Thoroughly brush your hair to remove any tangles or knots. Part your hair deeply on one side.

  2. Gather and Divide: Gather all your hair on the side farthest from the part. Divide this section into three approximately equal sections using two fingers.

  3. Begin Braiding: Hold the three sections in your hands. Start braiding by crossing the outer left section over the middle section. Then, cross the outer right section over the new middle section.

  4. Continue Braiding: Alternating sides, continue crossing the outer sections over the middle section, tightening the braid as you go.

  5. Secure the End: When the strands become too short to continue braiding, gather them together and secure the end with an elastic band.

  6. Finishing Touches: For a polished look, spray the braid with hairspray to hold it in place.

Creating a French-Style Side Braid

Materials:

  • Brush
  • Elastic band

Instructions:

  1. Prepare the Hair: Brush your hair and gather it under one ear, leaving the other side loose.

  2. Section the Top: Separate a small triangle-shaped section of hair from the top of the gathered hair. Divide this section into three smaller sections.

  3. Begin French Braiding: Start braiding the three sections as usual, crossing the outer strands over the middle one.

  4. Incorporate Hair: As you cross the strands over, add small sections of hair from the loose side to each outer strand.

  5. Continue Braiding: Continuously add hair from the loose side as you braid. Once all the loose hair is incorporated, continue braiding as a regular three-strand braid.

  6. Secure the End: Secure the end of the braid with an elastic band.

Creating a Dutch Side Braid

Materials:

  • Brush
  • Elastic band

Instructions:

  1. Gather the Hair: Sweep your hair to one side and gather it over one eye.

  2. Divide into Three: Divide the gathered hair into three equal sections.

  3. Begin Braiding: Instead of crossing the outer strands over the middle one, cross them underneath it.

  4. Add Hair and Braiding Under: Continue adding small sections of hair from the loose side to each outer strand as you cross them under the middle strand.

  5. Continue Braiding: Keep adding hair and crossing the strands underneath the braid.

  6. Finish as Regular Braid: Once all the loose hair is incorporated, finish braiding as a regular three-strand braid. Secure the end with an elastic band.

Creating a 4-Strand Side Braid

Materials:

  • Brush
  • Elastic band

Instructions:

  1. Divide the Hair: Brush your hair and sweep it to one side. Divide it into four approximately equal sections.

  2. Begin Braiding: Cross strand 2 over strand 1. Then, cross strand 4 over strand 3.

  3. Continue Alternating: Continue crossing the strands alternately, left over right, and right over left.

  4. Secure the End: When all the hair is braided, gather the strands together and secure the end with an elastic band.


In conclusion, side braids offer a gorgeous and versatile hairstyle option that complements any face shape or occasion. Whether you opt for a standard, French, Dutch, or 4-strand variation, there's a style to suit every taste. Remember to practice regularly to master the technique and add a touch of elegance and flair to your everyday look. Experiment with different braiding patterns and accessories to create unique and eye-catching hairstyles that will turn heads.
