Getting the Lightning Electric Bow: A Comprehensive Guide

Embark on an electrifying quest to acquire the coveted Lightning Electric Bow, a formidable weapon that will amplify your firepower in the zombie-infested realm of Dead Ops Arcade. Follow these meticulous steps to unlock its devastating potential:

Phase 1: Summoning the Wrath

  1. Dragon Feast: Locate the three colossal dragons marked on the map. These ancient beasts must be sated with offerings of zombie souls. Approach each dragon and interact with the nearby soul urn to summon zombies. Unload your arsenal of bullets until the dragons are fully satisfied, triggering their transformation into Wrath of the Ancients bows.

  2. Dragon's Breath Arrow: Ascend to the rooftop of the dilapidated church. Observe the intricate dragon weathervane adorning the spire. Aim your Wrath of the Ancients bow at the weathervane and fire a single arrow. Witness as a radiant electric arrow materializes in the air, imbued with the essence of the ancient dragons. Collect this arrow.

Phase 2: Elemental Ascension

  1. Bonfire Bonanza: Enter the Low-Gravity Room and seek out the three bonfires scattered throughout the area. Approach each bonfire and ignite it using any type of arrow. As the flames dance, a series of fiery wind symbols will appear on the wall behind the bonfires.

  2. Windswept Run: With newfound agility, navigate the Low-Gravity Room. Sprint across each of the five wind symbols on the wall. The symbols will illuminate in sequence, marking your progress. Complete this circuit seamlessly to unlock the next step.

Phase 3: Zombie Soul Urns

  1. Soul Harvesting: Return to the rooftops and locate the three urns surrounded by zombies. Engage in fierce combat, unleashing your Wrath of the Ancients bow to eliminate the undead hordes. As you vanquish zombies near the urns, their souls will be absorbed, filling the vessels with ethereal blue energy.

  2. Electric Empowerment: Once all three urns are brimming with zombie souls, equip the electric arrow you obtained from the dragon weathervane. Aim at each bonfire and fire a single electric arrow. The bonfires will erupt with a surge of electricity, illuminating the area with vibrant blue light.

Phase 4: The Lightning Arrow

  1. Celestial Retrieval: Reascend to the church rooftop where you collected the electric arrow from the dragon weathervane. In the same spot where you found the arrow, you will now find a small, ornate chest marked with a lightning bolt symbol. Approach the chest and open it to retrieve another electric arrow.

Phase 5: Lightning's Embrace

  1. Charging the Bow: Return to the Low-Gravity Room and locate the large lightning bolt chest in the center of the area. Approach the chest and insert the electric arrow you retrieved from the rooftop. The arrow will begin to power up, its tip glowing with intense energy.

  2. Zombie Power: Gather a cluster of zombies around the charging arrow. As the zombies approach, their souls will be absorbed into the arrow, providing it with additional power.

Phase 6: Ascended Weaponry

  1. Bow's Final Form: Once the electric arrow has absorbed enough zombie souls, remove it from the chest and insert it into the designated slot on your bow. The bow will transform into the Lightning Electric Bow, a devastating weapon that unleashes bolts of electricity upon your enemies.

Tips for Success:

  • Prioritize feeding the dragons before engaging in excessive zombie combat.
  • Refrain from collecting other quest items while upgrading the electric arrow, as it could interfere with the process.
  • Note that you can only fire one electric tornado at a time, so plan your shots wisely.

Conclusion:

Congratulations, warriors! By following these steps meticulously, you have unlocked the formidable Lightning Electric Bow, a weapon of unparalleled power. With its crackling electricity and devastating tornadoes, this bow will turn the tide of battle in your favor. As you wield this mighty weapon, remember the challenges you faced and the dragons you fed. May it serve you well in your epic battles against the undead hordes that threaten our realm.
