Cross Out Text in Discord: A Comprehensive Guide

Discord, the popular voice and text chat platform, offers a range of text formatting options to enhance communication. Among these is the ability to cross out text, creating a visual effect that indicates something as stricken or invalid. Whether you're on a computer, iOS, or Android device, here's a detailed guide to cross out text in Discord:

On a Computer

  1. Select the Text: Highlight the text you wish to cross out by dragging your cursor over it.

  2. Open the Right-Click Menu: Right-click on the selected text to bring up a context menu.

  3. Choose "Strikethrough": Locate the "Format" section of the context menu and click on the "Strikethrough" icon. It resembles an "S" with a line running through it.

On Mobile (iOS or Android)

  1. Use Tildes: Enclose the text you want to cross out within two tildes (~). For instance, to cross out the sentence "This is a test," you would type: ~~This is a test.~~

Other Text Styles in Discord

Besides cross-out, Discord supports several other text-formatting options, including:

  1. Underline: To underline text, use two underscores (_) at the beginning and end of the text. For example: This is underlined.

  2. Bold: To bold text, use two asterisks (*) at the beginning and end of the text. For example: This is bold.

  3. Italicize: To italicize text, use one asterisk () at the beginning and end of the text. For example: *This is italicized.

Applications of Cross-Out Text

Crossing out text in Discord can be useful in various situations:

  • Denoting Cancellation: Indicate that a message or event has been canceled or invalidated.

  • Highlighting Errors: Visually emphasize incorrect information or typos for easy recognition.

  • Creating Humorous Effects: Add a playful or satirical touch to your messages by crossing out certain words.

  • Emphasis and Differentiation: Use cross-out to draw attention to specific words or phrases, making them stand out from the rest of the text.
