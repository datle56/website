How to Put On False Eyelashes like a Pro

Step 1: Preparation

  • Trim the Eyelashes: Determine the appropriate length for your eyes by holding the false lashes against your natural lash line. Trim any excess from the outer corner of the lashes. This will ensure a natural and flattering fit.

  • Apply Lash Glue: Choose a high-quality lash glue specifically designed for false eyelashes. Apply a thin line along the base of the lash strip, avoiding any excess glue that could irritate your eyes.

Step 2: Application

  • Position the Lashes: Hold the lash strip above your eye, parallel to your natural lash line. Use your tweezers or fingers to gently lift and align the lashes with the corner of your eye.

  • Press and Secure: Once the lashes are aligned, gently press down along the lash strip to secure them to your natural lashes. Hold for a few seconds to allow the glue to set.

  • Wait for Adhesion: Give the glue a few minutes to dry and adhere to your lashes. Avoid blinking too frequently or touching the lashes, as this could disturb their placement.

Step 3: Finishing Touches

  • Apply Mascara: Use a light coat of mascara to blend your natural lashes with the false lashes, creating a seamless transition. This will enhance the overall volume and definition of your lashes.

  • Conceal Gaps: If there are any visible gaps between the false lashes and your natural lash line, use a thin eyeliner to fill them in. This will create a flawless and natural-looking finish.

Step 4: Removal

  • Saturate with Eye Makeup Remover: Dip a cotton swab or a makeup remover pad into a gentle eye makeup remover. Avoid using harsh or oil-based removers, as they can irritate your eyes.

  • Dissolve the Glue: Gently rub the makeup remover along your lash line, allowing it to dissolve the lash glue. Avoid tugging or pulling on the lashes.

  • Gently Remove: Once the glue is softened, carefully lift the lash strip away from your natural lashes. Use your tweezers or fingers to gently pull the strip off, taking care not to damage your natural eyelashes.
