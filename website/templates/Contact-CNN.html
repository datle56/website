Contacting CNN: Comprehensive Guide

Reaching out to CNN for feedback, news tips, or inquiries is crucial for engaging with the network and contributing your perspectives or concerns. Here's an exhaustive guide to all available contact methods:

Phone:

  • Customer Service: 1-404-827-1500
    • Use this number for general inquiries, technical assistance, or subscription-related matters.
  • Corporate Offices: 1-404-827-1700
    • Contact this number for business or legal inquiries, press relations, or partnership proposals.

Mail:

  • Turner Broadcasting System, Inc. One CNN Center Atlanta, GA 30303
    • Send physical mail for formal communications, letters of complaint or appreciation, or any correspondence requiring a tangible record.

Online:

  • Feedback Form: https://www.cnn.com/feedback
    • The official CNN feedback form allows you to submit your comments, questions, or suggestions directly to the network.
  • Email:
    • Comments: comments@cnn.com
      • Use this email address for general feedback, story ideas, or specific content-related inquiries.
    • Help: help@cnn.com
      • Contact this email address for technical support, subscription issues, or any other assistance matters.
  • I-Report: https://ireport.cnn.com/
    • I-Report is CNN's citizen journalism platform where you can submit photos, videos, audio, or written stories for potential broadcast or publication.

Social Media:

  • Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/CNN
  • Twitter: https://twitter.com/CNN
    • Follow CNN on social media and reach out to specific programs or anchors directly through their individual accounts.
  • General Feedback: @TeamCNN
    • Tweet to @TeamCNN to provide general feedback or raise any concerns.

News Tips:

  • Phone: 1-404-827-1500
    • Contact Customer Service at this number to provide breaking news tips or story suggestions.
  • Twitter: @TeamCNN
    • Tweet news tips or story suggestions to @TeamCNN.
  • Email: tips@cnn.com
    • Send emails to this address to submit potential news stories or investigative leads.
  • I-Report: https://ireport.cnn.com/newstips
    • Submit news tips through the I-Report submission page, providing details and any supporting evidence.
  • WhatsApp or Signal: (646) 397-6705
    • Send encrypted messages or make calls to this number to provide confidential or sensitive information.

Note:

  • Preferred Contact Method: CNN strongly recommends using the online feedback form for the fastest response time and efficient processing of your query.
  • Volume of Submissions: Due to the high volume of submissions, CNN cannot guarantee that every tip, story, or feedback will be used or responded to individually.
