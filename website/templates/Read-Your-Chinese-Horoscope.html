  • Snake: Wise, intuitive, mysterious, but can be secretive and resentful.

  • Rooster: Punctual, organized, observant, but may be critical and demanding.
  • Dog: Loyal, faithful, protective, but can be suspicious and judgmental.
  • Pig: Generous, compassionate, optimistic, but may be lazy and materialistic.

The Four Pillars of Destiny: A Comprehensive Analysis

Chinese astrology goes beyond your animal sign to include the Four Pillars of Destiny. These pillars represent your year, month, day, and hour of birth, and each contains an animal, an element, and a yin/yang alignment.

  • Animal: As discussed earlier, your animal sign determines your personality traits and general fortune.
  • Day Pillar: This pillar consists of a stem and branch, revealing your hidden animal, called the secret animal. Your secret animal signifies your inner qualities and potential.
  • Secret Animal: This animal complements your Zodiac animal and provides additional insights into your personality and destiny.
  • Yin/Yang Alignment: Each pillar has a yin or yang alignment, which influences your overall energy and life path.
  • Element: The animal, day pillar, and secret animal each represent one of the five elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water). The interplay of these elements determines the balance and flow of energy in your life.

By considering all four pillars, you gain a comprehensive understanding of your astrological blueprint, revealing your strengths, challenges, and opportunities.
