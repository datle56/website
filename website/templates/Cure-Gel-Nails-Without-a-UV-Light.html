Easy Home Methods for Gel Nail Polish

Non-UV Gel Polish

  • Use a non-UV gel polish that hardens on its own. These polishes contain a unique formula that allows them to dry without exposure to UV light. Apply thin layers and allow them to air dry completely.

Quick-Drying Techniques

  • Spray quick-drying nail polish on freshly painted nails. This helps evaporate the solvents in the polish, accelerating the drying process.
  • Apply canola oil cooking spray to wet polish to help it dry faster. The oil creates a barrier that prevents the solvents from evaporating, speeding up the drying time.

Cold Water Immersion

  • Submerge nails in ice water for 3 minutes to help harden the polish. The cold temperature constricts the polish molecules, making them adhere to the nails more firmly.

Using an LED Lamp for Gel Nail Polish

Safety Precautions

  • Protect your skin with fingerless gloves or sunscreen. UV light from LED lamps can cause skin damage. Use gloves to shield your hands or apply sunscreen to exposed areas.

Steps for Applying Gel Polish with an LED Lamp:

  1. Paint a thin layer of gel base coat on your nails. Apply it evenly to create a smooth foundation.
  2. Cure the base coat under the LED lamp for 45 seconds. This step hardens the base coat and prepares it for the color polish.
  3. Apply a color gel polish and cure it for 45 seconds. Use thin, even coats to prevent smudging or chipping.
  4. Add additional color coats if needed, curing each coat for 45 seconds. Build up the desired opacity by applying multiple coats.
  5. Seal with a gel top coat and cure it for 45 seconds. The top coat protects the color polish and gives it a glossy finish.
  6. Remove the sticky finish on the nails with rubbing alcohol. Dampen a cotton ball with rubbing alcohol and gently wipe the surface of your nails to remove the tacky residue.
  7. Repeat the process for your other hand. Follow the same steps to apply gel polish to the nails on your other hand.
