Getting the Cut HM in Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen

A Detailed Guide to Unlocking the Path-Clearing Power of HM Cut

Step 1: Acquire the Boarding Pass

  • Embark on a trek to Nugget Bridge, located in the vibrant city of Cerulean.
  • Engage in fierce battles against the five formidable trainers who guard the bridge, testing your Pokémon's prowess.
  • Once victorious, proceed northward to a sprawling mansion.
  • Enter the mansion and navigate to the computer within its confines.
  • Interact with the computer, and it will grant you the coveted S.S. Anne boarding pass, a key to unlocking new adventures.

Step 2: Unveil the Secrets of Vermilion City

  • Return to Cerulean City, the bustling hub of activity.
  • Locate the Underground Tunnel, a subterranean passageway concealed beneath the city's streets.
  • Traverse the tunnel, leading you to the vibrant shores of Vermilion City.

Step 3: Embark on the S.S. Anne

  • Approach the boat walkway in Vermilion City, where a stern-faced guard awaits.
  • Present your S.S. Anne boarding pass, proving your authorization to board the luxurious vessel.
  • Step onto the S.S. Anne's grand deck, embarking on a nautical voyage of discovery.
  • Engage in thrilling battles against the formidable trainers who occupy the ship's hallowed halls.
  • Don't forget to confront your spirited rival, eager to test the limits of your Pokémon's capabilities.

Step 4: Claim the Cut HM

  • Ascend to the ship's bridge, where the esteemed Captain awaits your arrival.
  • Approach the Captain and offer a soothing back rub, a gesture of respect and admiration.
  • In recognition of your kindness, the Captain will bestow upon you the coveted HM Cut, a tool of immense utility in your Pokémon journey.

Step 5: Unveil the Power of Cut

  • Once the Cut HM is in your possession, approach a Pokémon within your party and interact with it.
  • Select the "Teach HM" option, followed by the Cut HM, and behold as your Pokémon masters the ability to sever tangled trees.
  • With the Cut HM at your disposal, you can clear paths, explore previously inaccessible areas, and unlock new possibilities within the vast world of Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen. Navigate treacherous forests, overcome obstacles, and embark on adventures that were once beyond your reach.

Conclusion

Obtaining the HM Cut is a crucial step in your Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen journey. It grants you the ability to progress through the game by clearing trees that impede your path. By following the steps outlined in this guide, you can seamlessly acquire the HM Cut and unlock new adventures in the Kanto region. Remember to use Cut wisely to explore hidden areas and advance your Pokémon training experience. As you conquer challenges and make progress, the HM Cut will serve as an invaluable tool in your arsenal, opening up countless possibilities for exploration and growth in the enchanting world of Pokémon.
