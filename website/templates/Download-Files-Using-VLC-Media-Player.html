How to Effortlessly Download Videos Using VLC Media Player: A Comprehensive Guide

Introduction

VLC Media Player, renowned for its versatility and affordability, offers a convenient way to download videos from the internet. By utilizing its advanced functionality, you can capture videos from websites like YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion, and many more.

Step-by-Step Guide to Downloading Videos Using VLC

Step 1: Find the Target Video

Browse the website hosting the video you wish to download. Ensure that the video is compatible with VLC's supported formats.

Step 2: Copy Video URL

Locate the video's web address in the browser's address bar. Select it and copy it using the keyboard shortcut "Ctrl+C" (Windows) or "Command+C" (Mac).

Step 3: Launch VLC Media Player

Open VLC Media Player from your applications list. It is essential to have the latest version installed for optimal performance.

Step 4: Create Network Stream

Navigate to "Media" > "Open Network Stream" in the menu bar (Windows) or "File" > "Open Network" (Mac).

Step 5: Paste Video URL

In the "Open Media" window that appears, paste the copied video URL into the "Network URL" field. Ensure accuracy by double-checking the URL.

Step 6: Initiate Streaming

Click the "Play" button (Windows) or "Open" button (Mac) to begin streaming the video. VLC will start playing the video in its interface.

Step 7: Obtain Codec Information

To extract the location of the video's embedded file, click on "Tools" > "Codec Information" in the menu bar (Windows) or "Window" > "Media Information" (Mac).

Step 8: Copy Location URL

In the "Codec Information" window, scroll down to the "Location" field. Highlight and copy the long URL displayed there.

Step 9: Open Browser Tab

Open a new tab in your preferred web browser. Paste the copied location URL into the address bar and press "Enter." The video should start playing in the browser.

Step 10: Save Video

Right-click on the video in the browser and select "Save video as." A "Save As" dialog box will appear.

Step 11: Customize Save Options

In the "Save As" dialog box, specify the following:

  • File name: Enter a suitable name for the downloaded video.
  • Save location: Choose the desired folder to save the video.
  • Video format (optional): You can adjust the video format by selecting from the available options in the "Format" dropdown menu.

Step 12: Confirm Download

Click the "Save" button to initiate the download process. VLC will download the video from its original source and save it in the specified location.

Tips for Seamless Video Downloads

  • Use the preferred video format: Before saving the video, consider its intended use and compatibility with different devices.
  • Check network connection: Ensure a stable internet connection to avoid download interruptions.
  • Use VLC's latest version: Updates include improvements and bug fixes that enhance the download experience.
  • Convert to other formats: If the downloaded video is in a format that is not compatible with your devices, consider using VLC's conversion features.
  • Consider copyright laws: Be mindful of copyright restrictions before downloading videos from the internet. Only download videos that are freely available or have appropriate permissions.
