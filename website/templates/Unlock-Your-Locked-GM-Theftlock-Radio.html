Unlocking a Locked GM Theftlock Radio: A Step-by-Step Guide for Vehicle Owners


Unlock Your Car Radio with Ease: A Simple Guide to Retrieving Forgotten Codes

If you've ever found yourself locked out of your car radio, fret no more! This comprehensive guide will lead you through the effortless process of unlocking it in just three simple steps. Whether your car is a GM or otherwise, we've got you covered.

Step 1: Uncover Your Unique Radio Code Prepare to embark on a code-finding quest by turning on your car and radio. Observe the display for the telltale sign of a locked radio: "LOC." If it stares back at you, it's time to get your hands on some buttons. Simultaneously press and hold the preset buttons 1 and 4 (or 2 and 3 if button 1 is missing). Three-digit numbers will start to appear, and once you have them, press the AM/FM button to reveal the final three. Jot down these six digits as your radio's unique code.


Step 1: Determining Your Radio's Unique Code

1. Power Up: Turn on your car's engine and radio. If the display reads "LOC," it indicates that your radio is locked.

2. Locate Preset Buttons: Identify the preset buttons on your radio. Typically, these are numbered from 1 to 6.

3. Hold Preset Buttons: a. For radios with presets 1-6: Simultaneously hold down preset buttons 1 and 4 for approximately three seconds. b. For radios without preset 1: Hold down preset buttons 2 and 3 for three seconds.

4. Observe Three-Digit Numbers: The display will showcase three-digit numbers.

5. Obtain Additional Three Digits: Press the AM/FM button to display the remaining three-digit numbers.

6. Record the Code: Write down all six digits that you obtained in steps 4 and 5. This is your radio's unique code.

Step 2: Retrieving the Code from GM

1. Call the GM Radio Hotline: Dial 1-800-537-5140 and follow the automated prompts.

2. Enter the Dealer Access Code: Key in the dealer access code: 106010.

3. Provide Radio Code: Enter the six-digit radio code that you recorded in Step 1.

4. Receive Retrieval Code: GM's automated system will provide you with a four-digit retrieval code.

Step 3: Unlocking the Radio

1. Press Hour Button: On the radio, press the hour button corresponding to the first two digits of the retrieval code.

2. Press Minute Button: Next, press the minute button corresponding to the last two digits of the retrieval code.

3. Press AM/FM Button: Finally, press the AM/FM button. This should unlock your radio.


Conclusion:

Congratulations! You have successfully unlocked your GM radio. By following these simple steps, you have bypassed the security lock and regained access to your favorite tunes. Remember, if you ever need to unlock your radio again, simply repeat these steps. Enjoy listening to your music without any interruptions!
