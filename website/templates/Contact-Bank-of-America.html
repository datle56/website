Contact Bank of America for Inquiries and Support: A Comprehensive Guide

Bank of America, one of the leading financial institutions globally, offers a wide range of banking products and services. If you have any questions, need assistance, or wish to provide feedback, you can reach Bank of America through various channels:

Phone Support

  • Checking and Savings Accounts: 1-800-432-1000
  • Credit Card Customer Service: 1-800-732-9194
  • Online Banking Support: 1-800-432-1000

When calling for phone support, be prepared to provide your account number or card number for identification purposes.

Online Contact Options

  • Secure Message System:
    • Log in to your Bank of America online account.
    • Select "Contact Us" under the "Help & Support" menu.
    • Choose the relevant topic and compose your message.
  • Twitter:
    • Send a tweet to @BofA_Help.
    • The bank's customer service team will respond directly.
  • Facebook:
    • Visit the Bank of America Facebook page.
    • Send a private message with your inquiry.

Mail Correspondence

For general inquiries or documentation submissions, you can write to Bank of America at the following address:

  • PO Box 25118
  • Tampa, FL 33622-5118

In-Person Assistance

  • Branch Visit:
    • Use the branch locator at https://locators.bankofamerica.com/ to find your nearest branch.
    • Visit the branch during business hours and speak to a customer service representative.
  • Appointment Scheduling:
    • Sign in to your online account.
    • Schedule an appointment for personalized assistance with a financial advisor or banker.

Other Support Resources

  • Bank of America Website:
    • Visit https://www.bankofamerica.com for additional contact information, support articles, and frequently asked questions.
  • Mobile Banking App:
    • Download the Bank of America mobile app for convenient access to account information, bill payments, and customer support.

Additional Tips for Contacting Bank of America

  • When contacting the bank, have your account number or card number ready for identification.
  • Be clear and concise in your inquiry or request.
  • Provide specific details and documentation if applicable.
  • Be respectful and courteous to customer service representatives.
  • If you are unable to resolve your issue through the available channels, you may consider contacting the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) at 1-800-613-6743.
