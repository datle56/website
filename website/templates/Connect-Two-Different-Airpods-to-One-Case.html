How to Pair Mismatched AirPods with a Case

Losing one of your beloved AirPods can be a frustrating experience. However, you don't have to despair. Apple provides a convenient solution to pair a new AirPod with your existing case and charging case, ensuring you can continue enjoying your music and other audio content.

Steps to Pair Mismatched AirPods with a Case:

1. Unpair Your AirPods:

  • Navigate to "Settings" on your iPhone or other iOS/iPadOS device.
  • Select "Bluetooth" from the menu.
  • Find your AirPods in the list of paired Bluetooth devices.
  • Tap the "i" icon next to the AirPods' name.
  • Choose "Forget This Device" to unpair the AirPods from your device.

2. Reset the Case:

  • Place both of your AirPods inside the charging case.
  • Ensure that the lid of the case remains open.
  • Locate the setup button on the back of the case.
  • Press and hold the setup button for approximately 15 seconds.
  • Release the button once the status light on the front of the case begins flashing white.

3. Pair the New AirPod:

  • With the AirPods case open, bring it close to your iOS/iPadOS device.
  • A setup animation should appear on the screen of your device.
  • Tap "Connect" on the animation.
  • Follow the on-screen prompts to complete the pairing process.

Troubleshooting:

  • Ensure Compatibility: Make certain that the new AirPod is the same generation as the existing AirPod and that it is compatible with the charging case.
  • Charge the Devices: Vérify that both AirPods and the charging case are fully charged before attempting to pair them.
  • Clean the AirPods: If you are experiencing sound issues, clean your AirPods and the speaker ports using a soft, dry cloth.
  • Reset the AirPods: If you encounter persistent problems, try resetting the AirPods by repeating the steps for unpairing and resetting the case.

Conclusion:

Pairing mismatched AirPods with a case requires a few simple steps, allowing you to restore the functionality of your wireless earbuds. Whether you've lost or damaged an AirPod, you can easily replace it and enjoy the seamless listening experience that AirPods provide. Remember to carefully follow the troubleshooting tips if any issues arise, and you can relink your mismatched AirPods with ease.
