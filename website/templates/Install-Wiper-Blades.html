Wiper Blade Replacement: A Comprehensive Guide

Step 1: Preparation

  • Diagnose the Issue: Start by determining whether you need to replace the wiper blades or the wiper arms. Look for signs of wear, such as streaking, tearing, or excessive noise.
  • Purchase the Correct Blades: Head to an auto parts store and bring your old blades for reference. You'll need to match the make, model, and size of your existing blades.

Step 2: Removing the Old Blade

  • Locate the Release Tab: Find the small tab or lever on the wiper arm that releases the blade.
  • Detach the Blade: Pull the blade straight off the arm, applying gentle pressure.
  • Protect the Windshield: Carefully set the metal arms on the windshield to prevent any damage.

Step 3: Installing the New Blade

  • Position the New Blade: Align the new blade with the wiper arm, ensuring it fits snugly.
  • Secure the Hooks: Push the metal hooks on the blade through the holes on the arm.
  • Lock the Blade: Click the blade into place, ensuring it is securely attached.

Rubber Insert Replacement

Step 1: Removing the Old Insert

  • Release the Tabs: Push in the small tabs located at the ends of the wiper blade.
  • Slide Off the Insert: Carefully slide the rubber insert off the wiper arm.

Step 2: Installing the New Insert

  • Align the Insert: Position the new insert on the wiper arm, making sure it fits properly.
  • Secure the Clamps: Use the provided clamps to attach the insert securely.

Troubleshooting

  • Streaking Wipers: Clean your wiper blades with rubbing alcohol or mineral spirits.
  • Improper Wiper Angle: Check if the wiper blades are installed correctly and that the wiper angle is adjusted appropriately.
  • Persistent Issues: If you continue to experience problems, seek assistance from a professional mechanic.
