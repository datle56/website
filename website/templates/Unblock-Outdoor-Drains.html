Clearing Exterior Drains Yourself: A Comprehensive Guide

Exterior drains are essential for diverting rainwater and preventing flooding around your property. However, they can become clogged over time, leading to unpleasant odors, water pooling, and potential damage to your basement or foundation. Here's a detailed guide on how to effectively clear exterior drains yourself:

Materials You'll Need:

  • Screwdriver
  • Drain auger
  • Baking soda
  • Vinegar
  • Boiling water
  • Drain screen or dome (for prevention)
  • Biological enzyme cleaner (optional)

Step-by-Step Instructions:

1. Identifying the Clog:

Before attempting to clear the clog, it's important to determine its location. Look for signs of water pooling around a particular drain or a strange smell emanating from it.

2. Removing the Drain Cover:

Use a screwdriver to carefully lift the drain cover. Be cautious not to damage the cover or the surrounding area.

3. Manual Removal:

If the blockage is visible and accessible, attempt to remove it manually using your hands. However, avoid inserting objects like sticks or wires into the drain, as they could damage the pipes.

4. Using a Drain Auger:

For deeper clogs, a drain auger is a useful tool. Insert the end of the auger into the drain and rotate it clockwise. The auger will break apart the clog and allow water to flow freely again.

5. Flushing with Bleach:

Once the clog has been removed, flush the drain with water to rinse away any debris. Follow this by pouring a generous amount of undiluted bleach into the drain. Let it sit for a few minutes to disinfect and remove any remaining buildup.

6. Using Baking Soda and Vinegar:

This is a natural and effective way to clear minor clogs. Pour 1 cup of baking soda into the drain, followed by 1 cup of vinegar. The reaction between the two ingredients will create effervescence that helps to dissolve the clog. Allow the mixture to sit for about 30 minutes, then flush the drain with boiling water.

Preventing Clogs:

To maintain clear exterior drains, it's important to take preventive measures:

  • Install a Drain Screen or Dome: This will prevent leaves, twigs, and other debris from entering the drain.
  • Monthly Cleaning: Regularly use a drain auger or baking soda and vinegar to clean the drains and remove any buildup.
  • Biological Enzyme Cleaner: If your drain tends to run slowly, consider using a biological enzyme cleaner. These products contain enzymes that break down organic matter, keeping drains clear and odor-free.

Additional Tips:

  • Always wear gloves when working with bleach or other cleaning chemicals.
  • Avoid using harsh chemicals like drain cleaner, as they can damage the pipes.
  • If the clog is severe or you are unable to clear it yourself, contact a professional plumber.
  • Check your exterior drains regularly, especially during heavy rains, to prevent clogs and ensure proper drainage.

Conclusion:

By following these simple steps, you can effectively clear exterior drains yourself, saving time and money. Remember to address blockages promptly to prevent further issues and keep your outdoor spaces clean and well-maintained. Regular maintenance, such as monthly cleaning and installing drain covers, can significantly reduce the occurrence of clogs, ensuring proper drainage and a worry-free environment for your home.
