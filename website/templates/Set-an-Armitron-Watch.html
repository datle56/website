Setting the Time and Date on Armitron Watches

Digital Sports Watches:

Step 1: Enter Time Setting Mode

  • Press and hold the "Reset" button until it emits a beep. This will activate the time setting mode.

Step 2: Select Value to Adjust

  • Use the "Mode" button to navigate through the different values you want to set, such as hours, minutes, day, or date.

Step 3: Adjust Value

  • Use the "St/Stp" button to adjust the selected value upwards or downwards.

Step 4: Save Changes

  • Once you have adjusted the desired value, press the "Reset" button again to save the changes.

Analog Watches:

Step 1: Pull Out Crown

  • Locate the crown on the side of the watch. Slightly pull it out to its first position. This will allow you to set the date.

Step 2: Set Date

  • Turn the crown clockwise or counterclockwise to adjust the date to the correct day. Ensure that you are in the correct month and year.

Step 3: Further Pull Out Crown

  • Pull the crown out to its second position. This will enable you to adjust the day of the week and the time.

Step 4: Set Day of Week and Time

  • Turn the crown clockwise or counterclockwise to adjust the day of the week to the correct day.
  • Continue turning the crown to adjust the time. Ensure that you are in the correct time zone and daylight saving time (if applicable).

Step 5: Push In Crown

  • Once you have set the time and day of the week, push the crown back into its original position. This will lock the settings in place.

Tips:

  • Consult your Armitron watch's user manual for specific instructions on model variations.
  • If you encounter any difficulties or the watch malfunctions after setting the time and date, contact Armitron customer service.
  • If your Armitron watch has waterproof capabilities, avoid setting the time or date while it is submerged in water.
  • Have the watch serviced by a qualified jeweler or watchmaker if it stops functioning properly, especially if it has water resistance features.
