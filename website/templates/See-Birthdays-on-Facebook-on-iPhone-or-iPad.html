Stay Connected and Celebrate: A Guide to Viewing Birthdays on Facebook

Birthdays are a time to express our love and appreciation for the special people in our lives. With Facebook's user-friendly features, staying on top of upcoming and recent birthdays is a breeze. Whether you prefer to manually check or receive timely notifications, this comprehensive guide will empower you to connect with those you care about during this joyous occasion.


How to View Birthdays on Facebook: A Comprehensive Guide

Method 1: Using the Search Bar

  1. Launch the Facebook app: Navigate to the Facebook app on your mobile device and launch it.

  2. Tap the magnifying glass: On the app's main screen, tap the magnifying glass icon, which serves as the search bar.

  3. Type "birthdays": In the search bar, type "birthdays." As you type, you will see suggested options.

  4. Select the cake icon: Among the suggestions, tap the option that displays a cake icon. This will direct you to the dedicated Birthdays section.

  5. View birthdays: The Birthdays section will display a comprehensive list of upcoming, recent, and belated birthdays of your Facebook friends.

Method 2: Turning On Birthday Notifications

  1. Access the Facebook settings: Tap the three lines at the bottom right corner of the Facebook app to open the menu.

  2. Navigate to Notifications: Scroll down in the menu and select "Settings & Privacy," followed by "Notification Settings." Under the "On Facebook" section, you will find the "Birthdays" option.

  3. Enable on-Facebook notifications: Toggle the "Allow Notifications on Facebook" setting to enable birthday notifications within the Facebook platform.

  4. Choose notification channels: Select your preferred notification delivery methods, such as push, email, or text messages.

  5. Enable upcoming birthday reminders: Additionally, enable the "Upcoming Birthdays" setting to receive reminders prior to birthdays occurring.

Additional Features and Customization

  • Filter birthdays: Within the Birthdays section, you can filter birthdays based on criteria such as the time period (e.g., today, this week, this month) or relationship status (e.g., family, friends, coworkers).

  • Send birthday wishes: From the Birthdays section, you can easily send birthday wishes to your friends by clicking the "Wish" button.

  • Set a custom birthday notification ringtone: Personalize your birthday notifications by setting a custom ringtone through the Notification Settings.

  • Disable birthday notifications: If you no longer wish to receive birthday notifications, you can disable them by toggling off the "Allow Notifications on Facebook" setting within the Notification Settings.


Conclusion: Staying Connected with Loved Ones

With these two convenient methods, you can easily keep track of your friends' and family's birthdays on Facebook. By turning on birthday notifications, you'll never miss an opportunity to wish a heartfelt "Happy Birthday" to your loved ones. Whether you prefer the convenience of the magnifying glass search or the automated reminders of notifications, Facebook makes it effortless to stay connected and celebrate the special occasions of those who matter most. Embrace these methods to ensure that no birthday goes unnoticed and that your well-wishes are delivered with warmth and cheer.
