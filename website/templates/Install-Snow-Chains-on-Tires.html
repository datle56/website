Installing Snow Chains: A Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Acquire Suitable Chains: Determine your tire size and select snow chains designed specifically for your vehicle.
  2. Prepare Chains: Carefully untangle the chains and spread them out close to the tires.
  3. Position Chains: Drape the chains over the top of the tire, tuck them underneath, and drive the car forward slightly to bring the remainder of the chains into view.
  4. Connect Sides: Securely connect the two ends of the chain and tighten them according to the manufacturer's instructions.
  5. Check Alignment and Tension: Ensure the chains are evenly distributed around the tire, neither overly loose nor excessively tight.
  6. Secure and Re-Tighten: Drive a short distance and re-tighten the chains as necessary to ensure a snug fit.

Removing Snow Chains: A Simple Procedure

  1. Remove Chains When Safe: Once you reach clear road conditions, it's time to remove the snow chains.
  2. Disconnect Connections: Safely position yourself beneath the vehicle and release the chain connections on the inside of the wheel.
  3. Unfold and Place Chains: Unfold the chains and lay them flat on the ground below your wheels.
  4. Drive Over Chains: Drive forward slowly to fully expose and retrieve the chains.
  5. Dry and Store: Allow the chains to dry thoroughly before storing them to prevent corrosion.
