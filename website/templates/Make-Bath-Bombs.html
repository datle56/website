Easy Homemade Bath Bomb Recipes for Pampering at Home

Creating Basic Bath Bombs

Escape into a luxurious bathing experience with these easy-to-make bath bombs. The secret lies in the perfect balance of common household ingredients that transform into fizzy bliss once submerged in water.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup baking soda
  • 1/2 cup citric acid
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • Optional: 1/4 cup Epsom salts for muscle relaxation
  • 8-10 drops essential oil of your choice for fragrance

Instructions:

  1. In a large bowl, combine the baking soda, citric acid, and cornstarch.
  2. Create a well in the center and add the Epsom salts, if desired.
  3. Slowly add the essential oil, ensuring it's evenly distributed.
  4. Mist the mixture with water or olive oil from a spray bottle, one spritz at a time, until it starts to clump.
  5. Press the mixture firmly into molds or shape it by hand.
  6. Allow the bath bombs to dry in a well-ventilated area for at least 24 hours before using.

Ultra-Softening Bath Bombs

Indulge in the ultimate home spa treatment with these indulgent bath bombs. Their velvety texture and captivating scents will soothe both body and mind.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup baking soda
  • 1/2 cup citric acid
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 1/4 cup shea butter or cocoa butter
  • 1/4 cup almond oil
  • 1/4 cup coconut oil
  • Essential oils for aromatherapy and scent (optional)

Instructions:

  1. In a heat-resistant bowl, melt the shea butter, cocoa butter, almond oil, and coconut oil over a double boiler or in the microwave.
  2. Add the melted oils to the dry ingredients in a large bowl.
  3. Incorporate the essential oils of your choice.
  4. Mist the mixture with water or olive oil until it becomes pliable.
  5. Press the mixture into molds and allow to dry for 24 hours.

Milky Bath Bombs

Immerse yourself in a milky paradise with these nourishing bath bombs. Their gentle touch and soothing scent will leave you feeling refreshed and rejuvenated.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup baking soda
  • 1/2 cup citric acid
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 1/4 cup powdered milk
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1/4 cup cocoa butter
  • Witch hazel and water for spraying

Instructions:

  1. Combine the baking soda, citric acid, cornstarch, and powdered milk in a large bowl.
  2. Melt the olive oil and cocoa butter together over a double boiler or in the microwave.
  3. Add the melted oils to the dry ingredients.
  4. Spray the mixture with witch hazel and water until it forms a dough.
  5. Fill molds with the mixture and press firmly.
  6. Allow to dry for at least 24 hours before using.

Herb or Flower Bath Bombs

Immerse yourself in the aromatic embrace of nature with these botanical bath bombs. Their vibrant colors and calming scents will transform your bath into a fragrant oasis.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup baking soda
  • 1/2 cup citric acid
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 1/4 cup dried herbs or petals of your choice
  • Essential oils or perfume for additional fragrance (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Combine the baking soda, citric acid, and cornstarch in a large bowl.
  2. Add the dried herbs or petals and mix well.
  3. Incorporate any desired essential oils or perfume.
  4. Mist the mixture with water until it starts to clump.
  5. Press the mixture into molds and allow to dry for 24 hours.
