Section 1: Trigonometric Ratios

Trigonometric ratios, such as sine, cosine, and tangent, are mathematical functions that relate the lengths of sides in a right triangle to the measure of an angle. They provide a precise way to describe and analyze the relationship between angles and side lengths.

For specific angles of 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, and 90°, the values of the trigonometric ratios are as follows:

  • Sine (sin):

    • 0°: 0 (opposite side = 0)
    • 30°: 1/2 (opposite side = half of hypotenuse)
    • 45°: √2/2 (opposite side = hypotenuse/√2)
    • 60°: √3/2 (opposite side = half of hypotenuse x √3)
    • 90°: 1 (opposite side = hypotenuse)

  • Cosine (cos):

    • 0°: 1 (adjacent side = hypotenuse)
    • 30°: √3/2 (adjacent side = hypotenuse/√3)
    • 45°: √2/2 (adjacent side = hypotenuse/√2)
    • 60°: 1/2 (adjacent side = half of hypotenuse)
    • 90°: 0 (adjacent side = 0)

Section 3: Creating a Trigonometry Table

A trigonometry table is a convenient way to organize and visualize the values of trigonometric ratios for different angles. Here are the steps to create one:

  1. Draw a table with 6 rows and 6 columns.
  2. Label the rows with the trigonometric ratios (sine, cosine, tangent, cosecant, secant, cotangent).
  3. Label the columns with the angles (0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°).
  4. Calculate the sine values using the formula √x/2, where x is the numerator of the fraction.
  5. Copy the sine values to the cosine row in reverse order.
  6. Divide the sine values by the cosine values to obtain the tangent values.
  7. Reverse the sine row order to fill in the cosecant row.
  8. Reverse the cosine row order to fill in the secant row.
  9. Reverse the tangent row order to fill in the cotangent row.

Section 4: Mnemonic for Trigonometric Ratios

The mnemonic SOHCAHTOA is a helpful tool for remembering the relationships between trigonometric ratios:

  • Sine = Opposite / Hypotenuse
  • Cosine = Adjacent / Hypotenuse
  • Tangent = Opposite / Adjacent
  • Hy Opposite Adjacent = Sine
  • Adjacent Hypotenuse Opposite = Cosine
  • Adjacent Opposite Hypotenuse = Tangent
