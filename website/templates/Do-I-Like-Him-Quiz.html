Conclusion:

After carefully considering your answers, it's time to unravel the truth. If you've answered 'yes' to most of these questions, chances are you're harboring genuine feelings for this person. You find yourself drawn to their presence, invested in their well-being, and eager to connect with them both socially and emotionally.

However, if your responses primarily consisted of 'no's or 'unsure,' it may indicate that your feelings are more rooted in an idealized image than reality. You may be attracted to the idea of a romantic connection with this person but haven't yet experienced a substantial enough bond to justify it.

Remember, a genuine crush involves a multifaceted mix of admiration, respect, and a desire to explore a genuine connection. If you've discovered a genuine crush, embrace it and allow yourself to see where it leads. But if it turns out to be more of an infatuation with the idea of someone, it's important to acknowledge and respect your own boundaries.
