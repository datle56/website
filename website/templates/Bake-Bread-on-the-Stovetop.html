Part 1: Preparing the Dough

Gather ingredients for a basic loaf:

  • 3 cups (360g) all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
  • 1 packet (2 1/4 teaspoons, 7g) active dry yeast
  • 1 teaspoon (6g) fine sea salt
  • 1 1/2 cups (360ml) warm water (105-115°F)

Mix ingredients well, forming slightly sticky dough:

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, yeast, and salt.
  2. Gradually add the warm water while mixing with a wooden spoon or your hands.
  3. Continue mixing until a slightly sticky dough forms. Do not overmix.

Let dough rest for 18-24 hours to allow yeast to rise:

  1. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and place it in a warm spot (75-85°F).
  2. Let the dough rise for 18-24 hours, or until it has doubled in size.

Shape dough by folding and tucking, then let rise again for 2 hours:

  1. Once the dough has risen, punch it down to release any air bubbles.
  2. Shape the dough into a loaf by folding and tucking the sides over itself.
  3. Place the loaf on a lightly greased baking sheet.
  4. Cover the loaf with plastic wrap and let it rise for an additional 2 hours, or until it has doubled in size again.

Part 2: Baking on the Stovetop

Preheat a dutch oven with a heavy lid:

  1. Choose a dutch oven that is at least 4-quarts in size and has a tight-fitting lid.
  2. Preheat the dutch oven over medium heat.

Place dough in a floured pan inside the oven:

  1. Dust a small pan with flour.
  2. Place the dough in the pan and use your fingers to gently press it into the shape of the pan.

Cover and bake for 30 minutes, checking for a crust:

  1. Cover the dutch oven with the lid.
  2. Bake for 30 minutes, or until the bread has developed a golden crust on the top.

Let bread cool on a rack:

  1. Remove the loaf from the dutch oven and place it on a wire rack to cool completely.

Part 3: Making Flatbread in a Skillet

Make dough with yeast, water, flour, salt, and olive oil:

  • 2 cups (240g) all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
  • 1 teaspoon (2g) active dry yeast
  • 1/2 teaspoon (3g) fine sea salt
  • 3/4 cup (180ml) warm water
  • 1 tablespoon (15ml) olive oil

Knead dough and let rest:

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, yeast, and salt.
  2. Add the warm water and olive oil and mix until a dough forms.
  3. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and knead for 5-7 minutes until it becomes smooth and elastic.
  4. Form the dough into a ball and place it in a lightly greased bowl.
  5. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and let the dough rest for at least 1 hour, or until it has doubled in size.

Shape dough into circles and roll them out:

  1. Once the dough has risen, divide it into 6 equal pieces.
  2. Roll each piece of dough into a circle about 12 inches in diameter.

Heat a skillet on medium-high:

  1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat.

Cook each side of the flatbread for a few minutes until browned:

  1. Place a flatbread in the skillet and cook for 2-3 minutes, or until the surface has puffed up and browned.
  2. Flip the flatbread and cook for an additional 2-3 minutes, or until the other side is browned.
  3. Remove the flatbread from the skillet and repeat with the remaining dough circles.

Part 4: Creating a Dutch Oven

Use a heavy pot with a tight lid:

  1. Choose a heavy pot that is at least 6-quarts in size and has a tight-fitting lid.
  2. A cast iron pot is ideal for this purpose.

Place broken tiles or a tuna can in the bottom as a thermal ballast:

  1. Break 4-6 ceramic tiles into pieces and place them in the bottom of the pot.
  2. Alternatively, you can use a large tuna can filled with sand or rocks.
  3. The ballast will help to retain heat and create an even baking environment.

Find a pan that fits inside the pot on top of the ballast:

  1. Find a pan that is slightly smaller than the diameter of the pot and can easily fit inside.
  2. A round cake pan or a small roasting pan works well.

Secure the lid with a second lid or pan to keep heat in:

  1. Once the dough has been placed in the pan, cover it with a tight-fitting lid.
  2. To ensure that heat is retained, place a second lid or heavy pan on top of the first lid.
  3. This will create an airtight seal that will keep the heat inside the dutch oven.

Part 5: Using a Haybox

Heat bread in the dutch oven on the stovetop for 15 minutes:

  1. Preheat a dutch oven filled with ballast and a pan inside over medium heat for 15 minutes.

Remove and place in a haybox made of blankets or sweatshirts:

  1. Carefully remove the dutch oven from the stovetop and place it in a haybox made of blankets or sweatshirts.
  2. Cover the haybox with additional blankets to insulate it.

Let bread bake in the haybox for at least an hour:

  1. The bread will continue to bake in the haybox as it cools.
  2. Let it bake for at least 1 hour, or until it is cooked through.

Check for doneness and adjust stovetop time as needed:

  1. Check the bread for doneness by inserting a toothpick into the center.
  2. If the toothpick comes out clean, the bread is done.
  3. If the toothpick comes out with doughy crumbs, return the bread to the dutch oven and bake for an additional 5-10 minutes.
  4. Repeat the process of baking and checking until the bread is cooked through.
