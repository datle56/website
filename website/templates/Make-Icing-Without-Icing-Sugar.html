Icing Without Icing Sugar: Creative Substitutes and Detailed Methods

Methods:

1. Grinding Granulated Sugar:

  • Use a high-powered blender or food processor to grind granulated white sugar until it resembles powdered sugar.
  • For long-term storage, add cornstarch (1 teaspoon per cup of sugar) to prevent clumping.
  • Sift the ground sugar through a fine mesh sieve to remove lumps and achieve a light and airy texture.

2. Making Flour Icing:

  • In a saucepan, combine equal parts all-purpose flour and milk (e.g., 1/4 cup each).
  • Bring the mixture to a simmer over medium heat while stirring constantly.
  • Cook until the mixture thickens and coats the back of a spoon, about 2-3 minutes.
  • Remove from heat and let cool slightly.
  • Beat in softened butter (1/4 cup per 1/4 cup flour mixture) and cream cheese (optional, for a creamier flavor) until smooth.
  • Gradually add granulated sugar (1/4 cup at a time) while beating until desired consistency is reached.

3. Creating a Brown Sugar Icing:

  • In a medium saucepan, combine 1 cup brown sugar, 1/4 cup white sugar, 1/4 cup heavy cream, 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, 1/4 teaspoon baking powder, and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract.
  • Bring the mixture to a boil over medium heat, stirring frequently.
  • Reduce heat to low and simmer for 2.5 minutes, stirring constantly. This will caramelize the sugars and create a rich flavor.
  • Remove from heat and let cool slightly before using.

4. Whipping Up a Meringue-Style Icing:

  • In a heatproof bowl, whisk together 1 cup granulated sugar, 3 large egg whites, and 1/4 teaspoon salt.
  • Set the bowl over a simmering pot of water (double boiler) and whisk vigorously until the sugar has dissolved and the mixture becomes glossy and fluffy, about 5-7 minutes.
  • Remove from heat and continue whisking until the mixture has cooled completely and holds stiff peaks.

Tips:

  • For the best results, use granulated white sugar for the closest texture to icing sugar.
  • Always sift the ground sugar to remove lumps and ensure a smooth consistency.
  • When making flour icing, you can use unsalted butter for a buttercream flavor or cream cheese for a cream cheese icing flavor.
  • To prevent the brown sugar icing from hardening, add a pinch of baking soda before boiling.
  • For a meringue-style icing, whisk the mixture constantly over the double boiler to prevent curdling.

Alternative Icing Recipes Without Sugar:

1. Coconut Cream Icing:

  • In a blender, combine 1 can (13.5 ounces) of unsweetened coconut milk, 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder (optional), and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract.
  • Blend until smooth and creamy.
  • Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before using.

2. Avocado Icing:

  • In a food processor, combine 1 ripe avocado, 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder, 1/4 cup honey, and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract.
  • Process until smooth and creamy.

3. Cream Cheese Icing:

  • In a medium bowl, beat 8 ounces of softened cream cheese with 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder, 1/4 cup honey, and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract.
  • Beat until smooth and creamy.
