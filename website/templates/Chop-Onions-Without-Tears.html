Easy Onion-Cutting Hacks to Avoid Tears: A Comprehensive Guide

The Science Behind Onion-Induced Tears

Onions contain an enzyme called lachrymatory factor synthase (LFS). When onions are cut, LFS reacts with other compounds in the onion to release a gas called syn-propanethial-S-oxide (SPSO). SPSO is a potent irritant that triggers the trigeminal nerve, causing a burning sensation in the eyes and a reflex tearing response.

Hacks to Minimize Tearing

1. Use a Super Sharp Knife

A sharp knife slices through onion cells cleanly without crushing them, releasing fewer LFS enzymes. This reduces the amount of SPSO gas produced.

2. Chill Onions Before Cutting

Refrigerating onions slows down the enzymatic reactions that produce SPSO gas. Chilling for 10-15 minutes can make a noticeable difference.

3. Cut Onions Underwater

Submerging onions in water while cutting traps the SPSO gas, preventing it from reaching your eyes. This method requires some dexterity to maneuver the onion.

4. Cut Near Steam or Running Water

The steam or water vapor helps dissipate SPSO gas. Place a pot of boiling water nearby or run water directly over the onion while cutting.

5. Breathe Through Your Mouth and Stick Out Your Tongue

This unorthodox method redirects the onion gases to your mouth instead of your eyes. Breathe through your mouth and protrude your tongue to catch the irritant.

6. Soak Onions in Water

Soaking onions in water for 20-30 minutes weakens their enzymatic activity. However, this can also reduce the flavor intensity of the onions.

7. Cut Onions Away from Yourself

Position yourself at an angle to the cutting board so that the onion spray is directed away from your face.

8. Whistle While Cutting

The airflow created by whistling helps keep the onion gases away from your face.

9. Put Bread in Your Mouth

Chewing bread stimulates saliva production, which helps absorb and neutralize onion gases.

Creative Solutions

1. Wear Goggles or a Mask

Protective eyewear or a face mask effectively blocks onion vapors from reaching your eyes.

2. Cut Near Ventilation

A strong breeze or a fan placed nearby can draw the gas away from you.

3. Use Acidic Solutions

Acidic solutions such as vinegar or salt water can denature the LFS enzymes, reducing SPSO gas production.

4. Light a Candle

The flame of a candle draws the onion gas into it. While this method may not eliminate tearing completely, it can help reduce the intensity.

Additional Tips

  • Choose milder varieties of onions, such as sweet onions or scallions.
  • Cut onions in a well-ventilated area.
  • Use a cutting board that is higher than eye level to minimize gas exposure.
  • If you do experience tearing, rinse your eyes with cold water or apply a cold compress.
