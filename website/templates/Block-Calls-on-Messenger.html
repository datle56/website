Master the Art of Call Blocking: A Comprehensive Guide for Messenger App Users on iPhone, iPad, Android, and Web


Calling All Messenger Users: Block or Mute Unwanted Calls with Ease

Tired of pesky or unwanted calls interrupting your Facebook Messenger conversations? Let's empower you with the ultimate guide to blocking and muting calls to regain control over your virtual interactions. In this comprehensive tutorial, we'll walk you through the simple steps to silence those annoying calls effortlessly, both on your phone and computer. Keep reading to discover the secret to a more peaceful Messenger experience!


How to Block or Mute Calls on Facebook Messenger

Blocking Calls

On Phone or Tablet:

  • Launch Messenger: Open the Messenger application on your mobile device.
  • Select the Conversation: Locate and tap the chat thread with the person you wish to block.
  • Access their Profile: At the top of the conversation, tap the recipient's name or profile picture.
  • Scroll Down: Navigate to the bottom of the profile page and tap on the "Block" option.
  • Confirm Blocking: A confirmation dialog box will appear. Select "Block Messages and Calls" to proceed.

On Computer:

  • Open Messenger Website: Visit messenger.com in your preferred web browser and log in to your account.
  • Start the Chat: Locate the chat thread with the person you want to block and click on it.
  • Privacy Settings: On the right-hand panel, click on "Privacy & Support."
  • Block the User: Choose the "Block" option and select "Block Messages and Calls" to confirm your action.

Muting Calls

On Phone or Tablet:

  • Open the Conversation: Locate the chat thread with the person you want to mute.
  • Access their Profile: Tap their name or profile picture at the top of the conversation.
  • Mute Calls: Tap on the "Mute" icon, which resembles a bell with a line through it.
  • Select Muting Duration: Choose the desired muting period from the available options (e.g., 1 hour, 24 hours, indefinitely).

On Computer:

  • Open the Conversation: In the Messenger web interface, locate and open the chat thread with the person you want to mute.
  • Privacy Settings: On the right-hand panel, click on "Privacy & Support."
  • Mute the Conversation: Select the "Mute Conversation" option.
  • Set Muting Period: Choose the preferred muting duration (e.g., 1 hour, 24 hours, indefinitely).

Turning Off All Calls on Computer:

  • Open Messenger Website: Access messenger.com and ensure you're logged in to your account.
  • Access Settings: Click on the chat bubble icon located at the top-right corner of the screen.
  • Disable Call Sounds: Toggle off the switch labeled "Incoming call sounds."
  • Set Time Period: Choose a specific time frame for call blocking (e.g., 1 hour, 24 hours) or select "Until I turn it back on" to disable all incoming calls indefinitely.

Conclusion:

Blocking or muting calls on Facebook Messenger is a convenient way to manage unwanted communication and distractions. Whether you're dealing with spam callers, persistent solicitors, or simply need some peace and quiet, Messenger provides flexible options to control the flow of incoming phone calls. By following the simple steps outlined above, you can easily block specific contacts from calling you or mute incoming calls for a desired period or indefinitely. With these tools at your disposal, you can maintain control over your Messenger experience, ensuring that only the calls you want to receive come through.
