How to Date in Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas - A Comprehensive Guide

Introduction

Dating in Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas is an immersive feature that allows you to pursue romantic relationships with six unique women. Each woman has her own personality, preferences, and requirements. By understanding the dating mechanics and meeting the specific criteria for each woman, you can build meaningful relationships and earn exclusive rewards.

Dating Prerequisites

Before you can start dating, you must meet certain prerequisites:

  • Physical Requirements: Some women have specific physical requirements, such as high sex appeal, low fat, or high muscle. You can improve your physical attributes by exercising, visiting the gym, or using cheat codes.
  • Mission Completion: For some women, you must complete a specific mission before they become available for dating.

The Dating Process

To start a date:

  1. Visit the Woman: Identify the woman you want to date and visit her during her available hours, typically indicated by a yellow marker on the map.
  2. Initiate a Date: Approach the woman and initiate a conversation. She will suggest a date activity, such as dining, driving, or dancing.
  3. Choose an Activity: Select a date activity that aligns with the woman's preferences (see below).
  4. Fulfill Preferences: During the date, pay attention to the woman's preferences. Meet her expectations by driving at the right speed, choosing the right restaurant, and dancing at her favorite club.
  5. Repeat Dates: To build progress towards a relationship, you need to go on multiple successful dates.

Dating Progress

As you go on successful dates, a progress bar will increase. When the progress reaches:

  • 50%: You can drive the woman's vehicle.
  • 100%: You receive a special reward, such as a unique outfit or a vehicle.

Dating Women

Katie:

Millie:

Barbara:

  • Requirements: 50% fat and high sex appeal.
  • Preferences: Prefers driving at regular traffic speed. Likes Jay's Diner restaurant for food. Enjoys dancing at the Las Venturas club.
