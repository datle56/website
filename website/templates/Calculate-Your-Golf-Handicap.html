Part 1: Calculating Your Handicap Index

Adjusted Gross Score (AGS):

  • Calculate your total golf score for the round.
  • Adjust your score on each hole to the maximum allowed strokes (usually double bogey). This compensates for challenging holes or bad shots.

Handicap Differential:

  • Subtract the Course Rating from your Adjusted Gross Score (AGS).
  • Multiply the result by 113.
  • Divide the product by the Slope Rating of the course.

Handicap Index:

  • Calculate your Handicap Differential for at least five different rounds.
  • Drop the highest and lowest differentials.
  • Average the remaining differentials.
  • Multiply the average by 0.96 to obtain your Handicap Index.

Part 2: Calculating Your Course Handicap

Course Handicap:

  • Multiply your Handicap Index by the Slope Rating of the course you're playing.
  • Divide the result by 113.
  • Round the quotient to the nearest whole number.

Part 3: Improving Your Handicap

Additional Tips:
