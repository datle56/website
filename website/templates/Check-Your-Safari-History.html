Viewing Your Safari History

Safari, the default web browser on Apple devices, keeps a record of your browsing history, including visited websites, search terms, and links clicked. This information can be accessed and managed to streamline your browsing experience.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Launch the Safari app.
  2. Tap the Book Icon located at the bottom of the screen. This icon represents the bookmark manager.
  3. On the next screen, tap the Clock Icon. This button displays your Safari history.

macOS

  1. Open the Safari browser on your Mac.
  2. Click on the History tab located in the top menu bar.

Searching and Clearing Your History

Safari offers convenient ways to search through your history for specific websites and clear it to protect your privacy or improve performance.

iPhone and iPad

Searching Your History:

  1. Type your search query in the Search Bar located at the top of the history list.
  2. Safari will display a list of websites matching your search criteria.

Clearing Your History:

  1. Tap the Clear button located in the bottom-right corner of the history screen.
  2. Choose the desired time range for which you want to delete your history.
  3. Tap Clear to confirm the deletion.

macOS

Searching Your History:

  1. Type your search term into the Search Bar located within the History tab.
  2. Safari will display a list of websites containing the search term in their titles or URLs.

Clearing Your History:

  1. Click on the History menu item in the top menu bar.
  2. Choose Clear History...
  3. Select the desired time range for which you want to delete your history.
  4. Click Clear History to confirm the deletion.
