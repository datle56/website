Connecting Nintendo Switch to Video Capture Card

To connect your Nintendo Switch to a video capture card, follow these steps:

  1. Plug the Switch into the Dock: Insert the Nintendo Switch console into its designated dock.

  2. Connect the Dock to the Capture Card with an HDMI Cable: Use a high-quality HDMI cable to connect the HDMI output port on the Switch dock to the HDMI input port on the video capture card.

  3. Open the Capture Card Software on Your Computer: Launch the software that came with your video capture card on your computer. This software is typically responsible for capturing and processing the video signal from the capture card.

  4. Turn on the Switch: Power on your Nintendo Switch console by pressing the power button on the top of the device.

  5. Connect the Capture Card to Your Computer with a USB Cable: Use a USB cable to connect the USB port on the video capture card to a USB port on your computer. This will allow your computer to communicate with the capture card and transfer the captured video data.

Setting Up a Video Window for Discord

To set up a video window for Discord, follow these steps:

  1. Create a VLC Shortcut on Your Desktop: Create a shortcut for the VLC media player on your desktop.

  2. Edit the Code in the Shortcut to Include the Names of Your Video and Audio Capture Devices: Right-click on the shortcut and select "Properties." In the "Target" field, add the following code to the end of the existing text:

--vout-ar-alsa-device="Your Audio Capture Device Name" --vout-video-output-window-title="Discord"

Replace "Your Audio Capture Device Name" with the actual name of your audio capture device, which can be found in the "Sound" section of your computer's settings.

  1. Open VLC Using the Modified Shortcut: Click "OK" to save the changes to the shortcut. Double-click on the modified VLC shortcut to launch the media player.

Streaming with Discord

To stream your Switch gameplay on Discord, follow these steps:

  1. Join a Voice Channel in Discord: Start by joining a voice channel in the Discord server that you want to stream in.

  2. Click "Screen" and Select the VLC Display Window: In the Discord app, click on the "Screen" icon in the bottom-left corner of the window. A list of available screens will appear. Select the VLC display window that you have created using the modified shortcut.

  3. Click "Go Live" to Start Streaming Your Switch Gameplay: Finally, click on the "Go Live" button to start broadcasting your Nintendo Switch gameplay to your Discord viewers.
