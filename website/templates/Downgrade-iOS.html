Easy Guide to Downgrading Your iOS Device

What You Need to Know Before Downgrading:

  • Apple's policy allows downgrades only for a limited period after a new iOS release is issued. This window of opportunity typically lasts for a week or two, after which Apple closes the downgrade path.
  • Downgrading your iOS device will completely erase all data and settings. It's crucial to note that restoring your device from a backup created with a newer iOS version will reverse the downgrade, replacing your older iOS version with the newer one.

Step-by-Step Downgrading Process:

  1. Visit the IPSW Website:

    • Navigate to https://ipsw.me, an archive of Apple's official IPSW (iOS software) files.

  2. Select Your Device:

    • Choose the device you want to downgrade, such as iPhone, iPad, or iPod.

  3. Choose Your Device Model:

    • Specify the exact model of your device, for example, iPhone 12.

  4. Find the Older iOS Version:

    • Typically, the latest iOS version is listed at the top of the page. Scroll down to locate the older iOS version you want to downgrade to.

  5. Download the IPSW File:

    • Click on the download link for the desired iOS version to initiate the download of the IPSW file. This file contains the software for the older iOS version.

  6. Connect Your Device to Your Computer:

    • Use a compatible USB cable to connect your iOS device to your computer.

  7. Open iTunes or Finder:

    • On your computer, launch iTunes (on older macOS versions) or Finder (on macOS Catalina and later).

  8. Select Your Device in iTunes or Finder:

    • Once connected, your device should appear in the left-hand sidebar of iTunes or Finder. Click on it to select it.

  9. Enter Recovery Mode:

    • While holding down the Shift (PC) or Option (Mac) key, click on the "Restore [Device Name]" button in iTunes or Finder.

  10. Select the IPSW File:

    • A file explorer window will open. Navigate to the location where you saved the downloaded IPSW file and select it. Click "Open."

  11. Confirm and Downgrade:

    • iTunes or Finder will ask you to confirm that you want to restore your device to the selected older iOS version. Click "Restore." The device will now enter Recovery Mode and begin the downgrade process.

Tips for a Successful Downgrade:

  • Ensure that the "Find My iPhone" feature is disabled before restoring.
  • It's highly recommended to create an iCloud backup of your device before downgrading in case you change your mind later.

Conclusion:

Downgrading your iOS device requires careful consideration and adherence to specific steps. While it may seem daunting, this guide simplifies the process, empowering you to revert your device to an earlier iOS version. Remember to weigh the potential consequences, such as data loss, before proceeding. By following these instructions meticulously, you can successfully downgrade your iOS device and regain compatibility with your preferred apps or maintain a specific feature set.
