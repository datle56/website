Method 1: Using Google Maps Street View

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Launch Google Maps Web: Navigate to www.google.com/maps in your computer's web browser.

  2. Locate Your Desired Location: Utilize the search bar or zoom and pan to find the exact location you wish to explore.

  3. Activate Street View: Drag the orange "Pegman" icon from the bottom-right corner of the map onto the desired location. The icon will drop and indicate the activation of Street View.

  4. Navigate Street View: Use your mouse to explore the street-level view. Click and drag to move around, scroll up and down to adjust the camera angle, and double-click to zoom in and out.

  5. Orient Yourself: Refer to the compass icon in the top-right corner to determine your current direction. The compass indicates north, south, east, and west, providing a sense of orientation as you navigate.

Method 2: Understanding Regular Map View

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Open Google Maps Web: Visit www.google.com/maps in your computer's web browser.

  2. Locate Your Target Location: Similar to Street View, find your desired location using the search bar or by zooming and panning the map.

  3. Compass Orientation: The compass orientation is consistent throughout Google Maps. North is always positioned at the top of the map, south at the bottom, west on the left, and east on the right.

Additional Information:

  • Zoom Control: Use the zoom buttons (+/-) in the bottom-right corner to adjust the scale of the map, allowing you to view a broader area or focus on a specific location.
  • Navigation Control: The navigation control in the top-left corner provides options for panning, zooming, and rotating the map, giving you complete control over your exploration.
  • Satellite View: Switch to satellite view by clicking the "Satellite" button in the top-left corner to view aerial imagery of the location.
  • 3D Buildings: In supported areas, Google Maps offers 3D models of buildings, which can be enabled by clicking the "3D buildings" button in the top-left corner. This provides a more realistic and immersive exploration experience.
