Method 1: Throw or Bed Pillow

Materials:

  • 2 pieces of fabric, 18" x 18" each
  • Sewing machine and thread
  • Pillow stuffing
  • Scissors

Instructions:

  1. Cut two pieces of fabric into 18" x 18" squares.
  2. Place the fabric pieces on top of each other, right sides together.
  3. Pin the edges together, leaving a 2-inch opening on one side.
  4. Sew around the edges, using a 1/2-inch seam allowance.
  5. Leave the 2-inch opening unsewn.
  6. Turn the pillowcase right side out.
  7. Stuff the pillowcase with pillow stuffing until it is firm but not overstuffed.
  8. Hand-sew the remaining opening closed using a slip stitch.

Method 2: Envelope Pillowcase

Materials:

  • 1 piece of fabric, size according to pillow insert
  • 2 pieces of fabric for the back, each the width of the pillow and half the height plus 5" for overlap
  • Sewing machine and thread
  • Pillow insert
  • Scissors

Instructions:

  1. Cut a square of fabric equal to the size of your pillow insert.
  2. Cut two smaller pieces of fabric for the back, each the width of the pillow and half the height plus 5" for overlap.
  3. Hem one long edge of each back piece.
  4. Place the pillowcase fabric face up.
  5. Align the back pieces on top of the pillowcase fabric, overlapping the hemmed edges in the center.
  6. Pin the back pieces in place.
  7. Sew all four edges of the pillowcase, using a 1/2-inch seam allowance.
  8. Turn the pillowcase right side out.
  9. Insert the pillow form.

Method 3: Hand-Sewn Pillow

Materials:

  • 2 pieces of fabric, size plus 1" for seam allowance
  • Needle and thread
  • Scissors
  • Pillow stuffing

Instructions:

  1. Cut two pieces of fabric to your desired pillow size plus 1" for seam allowance.
  2. Pin the seam allowance together around all four sides.
  3. Using a large needle and strong thread, single-stitch all three sides of the pillow.
  4. Leave the fourth side open for stuffing.
  5. Turn the pillow right side out.
  6. Fill the pillow with stuffing.
  7. Slip-stitch the opening closed.

Method 4: No-Sew Pillow

Materials:

  • 2 pieces of fabric, 6" wider and longer than desired pillow size
  • Scissors

Instructions:

  1. Cut two pieces of fabric into squares or rectangles 6" wider and longer than the desired pillow size.
  2. Cut 3" squares from each corner of both fabric pieces.
  3. Cut a fringe into each edge of the fabric pieces, leaving about 2 inches of uncut fabric in the center.
  4. Tie the top tassels to the matching bottom tassels.
  5. Add stuffing to the pillowcase.
  6. Tie the remaining tassels together at the top to close the pillow.
