High-Level Methods

Barrows:

  • Engage in a thrilling battle against the infamous Barrows brothers, each boasting unique strengths and weaknesses.
  • Loot their remains for valuable armor sets, including the coveted Ahrim's, Karil's, and Dharok's armor, which are highly sought after by players.
  • Each piece of armor is worth millions of gold, providing a substantial financial reward for your courageous efforts.

Running Double Nature Runes:

  • Delve into the Abyss, a perilous realm filled with ethereal energy, to mine the essence needed to craft powerful nature runes.
  • Utilize the Spirit Graahk method to significantly increase your runecrafting efficiency, allowing you to mass-produce nature runes for an impressive profit.
  • Sell your hard-earned runes to eager buyers, reaping the rewards of your alchemical expertise.

Mid-Level Methods

Catching Red Chincompas:

  • Set ingenious traps in strategic diamond formations to ensnare these elusive hunting creatures.
  • Locate the ideal hunting grounds where red chincompas congregate, ensuring optimal catches.
  • Train your Hunter skill to enhance your trapping prowess, maximizing your yield and financial returns.

Killing Frost Dragons:

  • Embark on a dangerous expedition to the icy lairs of frost dragons, formidable opponents that require high-level combat skills to defeat.
  • Collect their valuable bones, highly prized for prayer training, and sell them for a hefty profit to dedicated prayer enthusiasts.

Cleaning Herbs:

  • Purchase grimy herbs at a reasonable price from various suppliers.
  • Employ your alchemy skills to meticulously clean these herbs, transforming them into pure and valuable ingredients.
  • Sell your purified herbs on the bustling Grand Exchange, where players eagerly seek the finest supplies for their potions and other endeavors.

Low-Level Methods

Making (unf) Potions:

  • Start your entrepreneurial venture by acquiring herbs and vials, the essential ingredients for potion brewing.
  • Combine these elements with your budding alchemy skills to concoct unfinished potions, leaving out the final step of adding water.
  • Sell your unfinished potions on the Grand Exchange or directly to other players, earning a tidy profit from your potioneering prowess.

Creating House Teleport Tabs:

  • Construct an elegant mahogany demon or eagle lectern within the confines of your own player-owned house, a testament to your architectural skills.
  • Use these ornate structures to craft teleport tabs, convenient tools that allow for instant transportation to your house, facilitating quick and easy banking.
  • Sell your surplus teleport tabs on the Grand Exchange, providing a valuable service to players who prioritize convenience and efficiency.

Merchant-Buying from NPCs:

  • Embark on a mercantile expedition, scouring the realm for non-player characters (NPCs) offering items at bargain prices.
  • Purchase these undervalued items and resell them on the Grand Exchange, capitalizing on the disparity between purchase and sale prices.
  • Develop a keen eye for spotting profitable opportunities, amassing wealth through your astute trading acumen.
