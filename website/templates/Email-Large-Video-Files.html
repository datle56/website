Sending Large Videos via Email

In today's digital age, sharing large videos through email has become increasingly common. However, due to file size limitations, sending such videos directly through email can often be challenging. To address this, we explore various effective methods for sending large videos via email using popular platforms.

Method 1: Using Google Drive (Gmail)

  1. Upload the Video: Navigate to Google Drive and click the "New" button. Select "File Upload" and choose the video you wish to send. The maximum file size for Google Drive is 5 TB.
  2. Obtain Shareable Link: After the video has been uploaded, right-click on the file and select "Share." In the "Sharing Settings" dialog box, toggle the "Link can be shared with anyone who has the link" option to "On."
  3. Insert Link in Gmail: Open a new email in Gmail and compose your message. In the email body, click the "Insert Link" icon (paperclip with a link). Paste the shareable link obtained from Google Drive into the "Link" field.

Method 2: Using OneDrive (Outlook)

  1. Upload the Video: Sign in to OneDrive and click the "Upload" button. Select the video you want to send. The maximum file size for OneDrive is 250 GB.
  2. Generate Share Link: Once uploaded, right-click on the video and choose "Share." Under the "Link settings" section, click the "Copy link" button.
  3. Insert Link in Outlook: Create a new email in Outlook. In the "Compose" window, click the "Attach" button and select "Upload and share." Choose "Link" and paste the shareable link obtained from OneDrive.

Method 3: Using Mail Drop (iCloud Mail)

  1. Enable Mail Drop: Open iCloud Mail's Preferences and navigate to the "Mail Drop" tab. Ensure that the "Enable Mail Drop" checkbox is ticked.
  2. Attach Video: Compose a new email and click the "Attach" button. Select the video you want to send. The maximum file size for Mail Drop is 5 GB.
  3. Send as Link: Mail Drop will automatically convert the video to a shared link. The recipient will receive an email with a download link to access the video.

Additional Tips:

  • Compress the Video: Consider using video compression software to reduce the file size without compromising quality. This can make it easier to send via email.
  • Break the Video into Parts: If the video is exceptionally large, consider splitting it into smaller parts and sending them separately.
  • Use a File-Sharing Service: If the available methods are not suitable, consider using external file-sharing services that allow large file transfers. However, these may require the recipient to create an account or have limited storage space.
  • Check Email Limitations: Be mindful of the file size limitations imposed by your email provider. If the video exceeds the maximum size, consider using alternative methods such as cloud storage or file-sharing services.
