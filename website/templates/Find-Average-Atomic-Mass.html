Calculating Average Atomic Mass

1. Gathering Information:

  • Identify the Isotopes of the Element: Determine the different isotopes of the element, which are atoms with the same number of protons and electrons but different numbers of neutrons.
  • Look up the Atomic Mass and Abundance of Each Isotope: Obtain these values from a reliable source, such as the periodic table or scientific databases. Atomic mass is expressed in atomic mass units (amu), and abundance is given as a percentage.

2. Converting Abundances:

  • Divide Each Abundance Percentage by 100: This converts the percentage abundances into decimal abundances between 0 and 1.

3. Calculating Weighted Average:

  • Multiply the Atomic Mass of Each Isotope by its Decimal Abundance: Multiply the atomic mass of each isotope by its corresponding decimal abundance.
  • Add These Values Together: Sum the products obtained in the previous step to calculate the average atomic mass.

Example:

Element: Carbon Isotopes: Carbon-12, Carbon-13, Carbon-14

| Isotope | Atomic Mass (amu) | Abundance (%) | Decimal Abundance | |---|---|---|---| | Carbon-12 | 12.011 | 98.89 | 0.9889 | | Carbon-13 | 13.003 | 1.11 | 0.0111 | | Carbon-14 | 14.0067 | <0.1 | 0.0000 |

Average Atomic Mass = (12.011 x 0.9889) + (13.003 x 0.0111) + (14.0067 x 0.0000) = 12.011 amu

Using the Average Atomic Mass:

1. Converting Mass to Atoms:

  • Divide Mass by Average Atomic Mass: To determine the number of atoms in a given mass, divide the mass by the average atomic mass.

2. Determining Molar Mass:

  • Multiply Average Atomic Mass by 1 Gram per Mole (g/mol): Multiply the average atomic mass by 1 g/mol to obtain the molar mass. This represents the mass of one mole of atoms of the element.

3. Finding Average Molecular Mass:

  • Add the Average Atomic Masses of the Atoms in a Molecule: For covalent compounds, add the average atomic masses of the constituent atoms to calculate the average molecular mass.

Example:

Carbon dioxide (CO2)

Average Molecular Mass = (12.011 x 2) + (16.000 x 2) = 44.011 amu
