Headline: Withdraw Cash from Your FNB Account Without a Debit Card – It's Easier Than You Think!

Introduction:

Are you tired of carrying around your debit card and worrying about losing it or forgetting it at home? What if you could withdraw cash from your FNB account without even having your card? Well, you're in luck! With FNB's innovative mobile banking services, you can now access your cash anytime, anywhere, using just your phone and a few simple steps.

In this step-by-step guide, we'll show you two convenient methods to withdraw cash from your FNB account without needing a debit card: using FNB ATMs and through retail stores. Whether you're in a hurry or don't have access to an ATM, we've got you covered. Get ready to revolutionize the way you manage your money and enjoy the convenience of cardless cash withdrawals with FNB!


Method 1: Using FNB ATMs

Step 1: Initiate the Withdrawal

  • Dial 120277# on your mobile phone using the number linked to your FNB account.
  • Select "Withdraw Cash" from the menu options (option 1).

Step 2: Obtain an ATM PIN

  • Choose "Get ATM PIN" (option 1).
  • You will receive a 4-digit PIN via SMS to the phone number linked to your account.

Step 3: Locate an FNB ATM

  • Within 16 hours of receiving the PIN, locate an FNB ATM.

Step 4: Enter Credentials at the ATM

  • At the ATM, enter your phone number and the PIN received via SMS.

Step 5: Withdraw Funds

  • Enter the amount you wish to withdraw.
  • Ensure that the amount is less than your available account balance.
  • Collect your cash from the ATM.

Method 2: Using Retail Stores

Step 1: Initiate the Withdrawal

  • Dial 120277*4# on your mobile phone using the number linked to your FNB account.
  • Select "Withdraw Cash" from the menu options (option 1).

Step 2: Obtain a Retail PIN

  • Choose "Get Retail PIN" (option 1).
  • You will receive a one-time PIN (OTP) via SMS, which will expire within 30 minutes.

Step 3: Identify a Supported Store

  • Visit one of the supported retail stores listed on the FNB website or in the FNB app.

Step 4: Make a Purchase with Your Card

  • Present your FNB ATM card to the cashier at the supported store.
  • Make a purchase using the card.

Step 5: Request a Withdrawal

  • Inform the cashier that you wish to withdraw funds from your eWallet.
  • Specify the amount you want to withdraw.

Step 6: Enter the OTP

  • Enter the OTP you received via SMS to complete the withdrawal.
  • Collect your cash from the cashier.

These methods provide a flexible and convenient way to manage your finances and have peace of mind knowing you can get cash when you need it. So, whether you're running an errand, making an unplanned purchase, or simply want to have some extra cash on hand, FNB's cash withdrawal options have you covered.
