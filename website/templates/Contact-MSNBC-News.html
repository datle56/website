Your Voice, Heard: How to Reach MSNBC News

MSNBC News, a leading source of news and commentary, welcomes feedback and input from its audience. Whether you have a groundbreaking idea, want to share your perspective, or have a question, there are several ways to connect with the MSNBC team. Here's your comprehensive guide to engaging directly with the newsroom and making your voice heard:


How to Contact MSNBC News

Online Methods:

  • Email:

    • For news inquiries: Send an email to msnbc.digital.editors@nbcuni.com.
    • For general comments or feedback: Send an email to MSNBCTVinfo@nbcuni.com.

    Subject Line: Please clearly indicate the purpose of your email, such as "Comment on [show name]" or "Feedback regarding [topic]."

    Email Body:

    • Introduce yourself and provide your contact information (name, email address, and phone number).
    • State the purpose of your email and provide specific details. For example, if you are commenting on a particular show, include the date, time, and topic of the show.
    • Clearly articulate your comment, question, or feedback. Be concise and respectful.
    • If possible, provide supporting evidence or documentation to substantiate your inquiry.

  • Social Media:

    • Facebook: Send a message to MSNBC News on Facebook at https://www.facebook.com/NBCNews/.
    • Twitter: Send a message to MSNBC News on Twitter at https://twitter.com/NBCNews.

    Message Format:

    • Use a professional and respectful tone.
    • Clearly state your purpose for contacting them.
    • Keep your message brief and to the point.
    • Include relevant hashtags if appropriate.

  • Website Form:

    • Visit the NBC News website at http://www.nbcnews.com/id/10285339/t/contact-nbc-news/.
    • Fill out the contact form provided.
    • Select the appropriate topic from the dropdown menu.
    • Provide your contact information and a detailed description of your inquiry.

Legal Notice or Letter:

  • Write your letter:

    • State your purpose clearly and include all necessary information, such as the specific legal issue, the requested action, and any supporting documentation.
    • Use professional and respectful language.
    • Be specific and provide evidence to support your claims.

  • Address legal notices:

    • For legal notices: Send to the Editor-in-Chief at NBCNews.com, One Microsoft Way, Redmond, WA 98052.

  • Mail a letter:

    • For general comments or questions: Use this address: NBC News, 30 Rockefeller Plaza, New York, N.Y. 10112.

Additional Tips:

  • Be patient in waiting for a response, especially if contacting via email or the website form.
  • Keep a record of all communications with MSNBC News.
  • If you do not receive a response within a reasonable time frame, consider following up or using an alternative method of contact.
  • Be respectful of MSNBC News staff and their time.

Conclusion:

With these diverse contact options at your fingertips, getting in touch with MSNBC News has never been easier. Whether you have a pressing question, want to share your perspective, or require legal assistance, there's a method that suits your needs. By using the email addresses, social media platforms, or website form, you can reach out to the newsroom directly. For legal matters or inquiries involving the editor-in-chief, you can send a written notice to the provided addresses. Remember to provide clear and concise information to ensure a prompt response from MSNBC News.
