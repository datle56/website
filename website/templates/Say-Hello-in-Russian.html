Ending a Conversation

As you conclude a conversation, it is customary to bid farewell in a manner that reflects the level of formality or familiarity. For formal or professional settings, "do svidaniya" (pronounced duh svee-dah-nee-ye) serves as an appropriate choice. It denotes a polite and respectful goodbye.

In more informal contexts, especially among close friends or family, "poka" (pronounced pa-kah) is a warm and friendly way to say farewell. It conveys a sense of casualness and affection.

Once again, the time of day influences the specific goodbye you choose. As the day draws to a close, opt for "Dobroy nochi" (good night), which conveys a restful and peaceful goodbye. For those retiring to bed, the appropriate phrase is "Spakojnoj nochi" (good night, for going to bed), expressing a wish for a tranquil and restorative night's sleep.
