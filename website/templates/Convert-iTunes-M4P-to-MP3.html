Easily Convert M4P Files to MP3

Protected music files purchased from the iTunes store are typically in the M4P format, which uses Apple's FairPlay digital rights management (DRM) technology. This DRM prevents users from freely playing or copying the files on other devices or software. However, there are several ways to easily convert M4P files to the universal MP3 format, which is compatible with most devices and software.

Method 1: Using FileZigZag

FileZigZag is a free online file conversion tool that supports a wide range of file formats, including M4P and MP3. To convert M4P files to MP3 using FileZigZag:

  1. Visit the FileZigZag website (https://www.filezigzag.com/).
  2. Click on the "Select" button and browse to the M4P files you want to convert.
  3. In the "Target Format" drop-down menu, select "MP3."
  4. Enter your email address so that FileZigZag can notify you when the conversion is complete.
  5. Click on the "Convert" button.

Once the conversion is complete, FileZigZag will send you an email containing a link to download the converted MP3 files.

Method 2: Burning a CD and Ripping

This method involves burning the M4P files onto a CD in MP3 format and then ripping the tracks back to your computer in MP3 format.

  1. Insert a blank CD-RW (rewritable CD) into your computer's optical drive.
  2. Open iTunes and create a playlist of the M4P files you want to convert.
  3. Click on the "Burn" button in the top-left corner of iTunes.
  4. In the "Audio CD" drop-down menu, select "MP3 CD."
  5. Click on the "Burn" button to start the burning process.

Once the burning process is complete, insert the CD back into your computer and open it in iTunes.

  1. Select the tracks on the CD and click on "File" > "Convert" > "Create MP3 Version."

iTunes will create MP3 versions of the selected tracks.

Method 3: Using iTunes Match

iTunes Match is a subscription service offered by Apple that allows users to store their music library in the cloud and access it from multiple devices. iTunes Match also removes DRM from protected music files.

  1. Subscribe to iTunes Match (requires a paid subscription).
  2. Delete the protected M4P files from your iTunes library.
  3. Go to the iTunes Store and click on "Purchased" > "Not in My Library."
  4. Find the deleted M4P files and click on the cloud icon next to each one to download the unprotected versions.
  5. Once the unprotected files have been downloaded, convert them to MP3 by clicking on "File" > "Convert" > "Create MP3 Version."

Additional Notes:

  • It is important to note that M4P files protected by Apple's FairPlay DRM cannot be played or converted without authorization from Apple.
  • The FileZigZag method may take a significant amount of time, especially if you have a large number of files to convert.
  • The burning a CD and ripping method is more time-consuming but does not require an internet connection.
  • The iTunes Match method is the most convenient but requires a paid subscription.
