Evolving Feebas into Milotic: A Comprehensive Guide

Feebas, the enigmatic and often overlooked Pokémon, holds a secret that transforms it into one of the most beautiful and powerful creatures in the Pokémon universe: Milotic. Evolving Feebas into Milotic requires patience, dedication, and a deep understanding of the Pokémon's unique characteristics. Follow this comprehensive guide to unlock the hidden potential of your Feebas and witness its metamorphosis into the majestic Milotic.

Step 1: Acquiring Pamtre Berries

  • Defeating the Elite Four: Begin your journey by defeating the formidable Elite Four in the Pokémon League. This triumph will grant you access to the Berry Master's wife, located in Verdanturf Town.
  • Receiving Pamtre Berries: Pay a visit to the Berry Master's wife, and she will generously gift you with Pamtre Berries. These berries are essential for increasing Feebas's beauty stat, a crucial factor in its evolution.

Step 2: Cultivating and Feeding Pamtre Berries

  • Planting Pamtre Berries: Plant the Pamtre Berries you've acquired in soft soil in your Secret Base or in pots in your home. Remember that soft soil provides the optimal conditions for their growth.
  • Berry Growth Period: Allow 3 days for the Pamtre Berries to fully grow and ripen. Once they've reached maturity, they can be harvested and used to create Pokéblocks.
  • Creating Pokéblocks: Visit the Berry Blender in Rustboro City to transform your freshly harvested Pamtre Berries into Pokéblocks. Choose the Beauty Blending Mode to enhance Feebas's beauty stat.
  • Feeding Feebas Pokéblocks: Regularly feed the Beauty Pokéblocks to your Feebas. Each block consumed will incrementally increase its beauty stat, bringing it closer to the threshold required for evolution.

Step 3: Enhancing Feebas's Beauty and Level

  • Beauty Maximization: Continue feeding Feebas Beauty Pokéblocks until its beauty stat reaches level 170. This is the critical beauty threshold that triggers Feebas's evolution into Milotic.
  • Leveling Up Feebas: Once Feebas's beauty stat is maxed out, it must level up once more to initiate the evolution. Use Rare Candies or engage in battles to achieve this final leveling up step.

Step 4: Witness the Transformation

  • Evolution Trigger: After Feebas levels up with its beauty stat at level 170, it will undergo a remarkable transformation. It will shed its unassuming appearance and emerge as the elegant and graceful Milotic.

Tips for Evolving Feebas into Milotic

  • Patience is Key: The evolution of Feebas into Milotic is a gradual process that requires patience and dedication. Don't rush the steps, and allow Feebas ample time to increase its beauty and level up.
  • Berry Cultivation: Consider cultivating multiple Pamtre Berry plants to ensure a steady supply of berries for Pokéblock creation.
  • Max Beauty Pokéblocks: Always choose the Beauty Blending Mode when creating Pokéblocks to maximize Feebas's beauty stat gain.
  • Battle for Experience: Engaging in battles is an effective way to level up Feebas while maintaining its beauty stat.
  • Use Rare Candies Wisely: If you have Rare Candies available, use them strategically to level up Feebas after its beauty stat has reached 170. This will minimize the time spent battling while ensuring the successful evolution.
