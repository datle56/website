Sending Videos on Discord

Introduction:

Discord is a popular communication platform that allows users to connect with friends, family, and fellow gamers through text, voice, and video. Sharing videos is an integral part of Discord's functionality, enabling users to share funny clips, gameplay highlights, or essential information with ease.

Using a Computer:

  1. Open Discord and Select a Chat or Channel: Launch the Discord desktop application and navigate to the server, chat, or channel where you want to share the video.

  2. Click the "+" Icon: In the chat window, locate the "+" icon next to the text input field. This icon represents the "Add Attachment" option.

  3. Navigate to the Video File: Click on the "+" icon and select "Upload File" from the drop-down menu. A file browser window will appear, allowing you to navigate to the location of the video file on your computer.

  4. Double-Click the Video: Once you have located the desired video file, double-click it to initiate the upload process.

  5. Add a Comment or Mark as Spoiler (Optional): Before sending the video, you can optionally add a comment to provide context or mark the video as a spoiler. This option can be useful for sharing sensitive or potentially revealing content.

  6. Click "Upload": Finally, click on the "Upload" button to complete the video sharing process. The video will be uploaded to Discord's servers and shared within the selected chat or channel.

Using a Phone or Tablet:

  1. Open Discord and Select a Chat or Channel: Start the Discord mobile app on your device and navigate to the server, chat, or channel where you want to send the video.

  2. Tap the Image Icon: In the chat window, tap on the image icon located on the left side of the text input field. This icon represents the "Add Attachment" option.

  3. Select the Video from Your Gallery: A gallery view will appear, displaying all the videos stored on your device. Tap on the video file you want to share.

  4. Tap "Send": Once the video is selected, tap on the "Send" button to complete the video sharing process. The video will be automatically uploaded to Discord's servers and shared within the selected chat or channel.

Note on File Size Limits:

Discord imposes a file size limit on video uploads to prevent excessive server load. Free Discord users are limited to uploading videos with a maximum file size of 8MB. Discord Nitro subscribers enjoy a significantly higher file size limit of 50MB, allowing them to share larger video files.

If the video file you want to share exceeds the allowed file size, you will need to use a third-party file-sharing service (such as Dropbox or Google Drive) to upload and share the video. Once uploaded, you can share the link to the video in the Discord chat or channel.
