DIY Dawn Powerwash Spray: A Step-by-Step Guide to Creating an Effective Household Cleaner

Introduction

Tired of harsh chemical cleaners that leave behind fumes and residues? Embrace the power of natural ingredients with our DIY Dawn Powerwash Spray. This homemade cleaner harnesses the cleaning prowess of Dawn Original Dish Soap and rubbing alcohol to effectively remove stains, grease, and grime from a multitude of surfaces. Follow our simple steps to create your own all-purpose cleaning solution that's both effective and affordable.

Materials You'll Need:

  • Empty spray bottle (at least 16 ounces)
  • Dawn Original Dish Soap
  • Rubbing alcohol (isopropyl alcohol)
  • Water
  • Essential oil (optional, for fragrance)
  • Funnel (optional)

Instructions:

Step 1: Fill the Container with Water

Grab your empty spray bottle and fill it with 13 fl oz (380 ml) of room temperature water. Use a funnel if necessary to avoid spills.

Step 2: Add Dawn Dish Soap

Pour 4 tbsp (59 ml) of Dawn Original Dish Soap into the water. This ingredient is the backbone of your cleaning solution, boasting grease-cutting and stain-removing capabilities.

Step 3: Mix in Rubbing Alcohol

Add 2 tbsp (30 ml) of rubbing alcohol to the mixture. Rubbing alcohol acts as a disinfectant and helps to break down stubborn stains.

Step 4: Add Essential Oil (Optional)

If desired, add a few drops of your preferred essential oil to the spray bottle. This is an optional step that will give your cleaner a pleasant scent. Tea tree oil, eucalyptus oil, or lemon oil are popular choices.

Step 5: Shake the Container

Securely close the spray bottle and shake it vigorously for several seconds. This will ensure that all the ingredients are thoroughly combined and emulsified.

Step 6: Use Your Homemade Cleaner

Your DIY Dawn Powerwash Spray is now ready to use! Spray it directly onto surfaces to remove stains, dirt, and grease. Wipe clean with a wet cloth or sponge.

Tips for Use:

  • For stubborn stains, allow the cleaner to sit on the surface for a few minutes before wiping.
  • Test the cleaner on an inconspicuous area first to ensure compatibility with the surface.
  • Store the cleaner in a cool, dry place.
  • Dispose of the solution properly when finished.
