Master the Art of Winged Eyeliner with Step-by-Step Image Guide


Step into the World of Flawless Eyeliner: A Comprehensive Guide to Achieving Picture-Perfect Wings

Whether you're a makeup novice or a seasoned artist, the art of creating flawless eyeliner wings can seem daunting. But don't despair! Our step-by-step guide will empower you to sketch, perfect, and finalize your wings like a pro.

Join us on a journey through three distinct methods, each tailored to suit your skill level and preferences. From the freehand approach for seasoned masters to the guided technique for beginners, we'll provide you with all the tips and tricks you need to elevate your eye makeup game. So, grab your liner and prepare to embark on a transformative journey towards eye-catching eyeliner wings!


Part 1: Sketching Free Hand

1. Prep Your Eyelid with Concealer or Primer:

Concealer or primer helps create a smooth, even base for your eyeliner to adhere to, preventing it from smudging or fading throughout the day. Apply a thin layer of concealer or primer to your eyelid, focusing on the area where you intend to draw your eyeliner.

2. Apply Eyeshadow, if Desired:

For a more dramatic effect, consider applying eyeshadow before sketching your eyeliner. Choose a shade that complements your skin tone and eye color, and apply it to your eyelid up to your brow bone.

3. Draw a Thin Line Along Your Upper Lash Line with Light Pencil Eyeliner:

Start by drawing a thin line along your upper lash line, as close to the base of your lashes as possible. Use a light-colored pencil eyeliner, such as brown or gray, to create a natural-looking outline.

4. Measure the Desired Length of the Wing and Draw a Diagonal Line Out from the End of Your Bottom Lash Line:

Determine the desired length and angle of the wing you want to create. Using your pencil eyeliner, draw a diagonal line outward from the outer corner of your bottom lash line, extending it to the point where you want the wing to end.

5. Draw a Straight Line from the Tip of the Wing to the Middle of Your Eyelid:

Connect the tip of the wing to the center of your eyelid with a straight line. This line should be slightly angled upward to create the illusion of a lifted upper lid.

6. Fill in the Outline with Pencil Eyeliner:

Using the same pencil eyeliner, begin filling in the outline you have created. Start from the inner corner of your eye and work your way outward, using smooth, gentle strokes.

7. Thicken the Line at the Inner Eyelid to Make the Wing Look Thinner as it Goes into the Eye:

To create the illusion of a winged liner that tapers off at the inner corner of your eye, thicken the line along the inner eyelid. This will help balance out the wing and make it look more natural.

Part 2: Sketching with a Guide

1. Place a Piece of Tape at the Corner of Your Eye, Extending Towards Your Eyebrow:

This technique is ideal for beginners or those who want a precise, symmetrical wing. Tear off a small piece of tape and place it at the outer corner of your eye, angling it slightly upward towards your eyebrow.

2. Apply Liner Along the Upper Lash Line:

Start by drawing a thin line along your upper lash line, as described in Step 3 of the Free Hand Sketching method. This will serve as the base for your wing.

3. Trace the Edge of the Tape with Your Liner:

Using your pencil eyeliner, gently trace along the edge of the tape, creating a straight line that extends out from your lash line. The angle and length of the line will determine the shape and size of your wing.

4. Remove the Tape and Go Back Over the Line, Making the Outer Edge Thicker than the Inner Edge:

Once you have traced the edge of the tape, remove it and go back over the line with your pencil eyeliner. Gradually thicken the line as you move towards the outer edge of the wing, creating a tapered effect.

Part 3: Finalizing the Look

1. Step Away and Judge Your Look:

Take a step back from the mirror and observe your eyeliner from a distance. Check for any unevenness or asymmetry, and make adjustments as necessary.

2. Trace the Pencil Liner with Liquid Liner:

For a more defined and long-lasting look, trace over your pencil eyeliner with liquid eyeliner. Use a fine-tipped liquid liner for precision and control.

3. Clean Up Any Uneven Edges or Mistakes:

Using a cotton swab dipped in makeup remover, gently clean up any uneven edges or mistakes. Be careful not to smudge your eyeliner.

4. Let Dry for 10-15 Seconds:

Allow your liquid eyeliner to dry completely for 10-15 seconds before applying mascara. This will help prevent smudging or flaking.

5. Apply Mascara, if Desired:

To complete the look, apply mascara to your lashes. Choose a mascara that volumizes and lengthens your lashes for a dramatic, eye-catching effect.


Conclusion:

Whether you prefer the precision of a guide or the freedom of freehand, these techniques will help you achieve a flawless winged eyeliner look every time. Remember to practice patience, use steady hands, and don't be afraid to experiment with different thicknesses and shapes. With a little practice, you'll be creating stunning, eye-catching winged eyeliner like a pro in no time. So, go ahead, elevate your makeup game and embrace the power of the perfect wing!
