Warm Up Your Vocals

Step 1: Gargle with Warm Salt Water and Baking Soda

  • Mix 1/2 teaspoon of salt and 1/2 teaspoon of baking soda in 8 ounces of warm water.
  • Gargle for 30 seconds, focusing on the back of your throat.
  • This solution helps loosen mucus and reduce inflammation, creating a clear pathway for sound.

Step 2: Practice "Hee-Haw" Sounds

  • Stand up straight with your shoulders relaxed.
  • Inhale deeply and exhale slowly, making a "hee-haw" sound as you exhale.
  • Repeat for 10-15 times, gradually increasing the intensity of the sound.
  • This exercise stretches and strengthens your vocal cords, preparing them for growling and screaming.

Step 3: Warm Up with "Yeah" and "Wow"

  • Inhale deeply and exhale with a forceful "yeah" sound.
  • Repeat with a "wow" sound.
  • Gradually increase the volume and intensity of these sounds.
  • These exercises activate the muscles used for screaming and growling, helping you transition smoothly into those techniques.

Step 4: Protect Your Vocal Chords with Slippery Elm Lozenges

  • Suck on a lozenge containing slippery elm, which forms a protective coating over your vocal cords.
  • This coating helps reduce irritation and dryness, allowing you to practice for longer without vocal strain.

Growl Your Death Metal Vocals

Step 1: Relax Your Throat and Mouth

  • It's crucial to approach growling with a relaxed and open throat.
  • Avoid any tension in your jaw or neck muscles.

Step 2: Inhale Deeply into Your Diaphragm

  • Take a deep breath, consciously filling your diaphragm with air.
  • Your stomach should expand as you inhale.

Step 3: Push the Breath Out Forcefully

  • Use your diaphragm to forcefully push the breath out, creating a high-pressure airflow.

Step 4: Add Growling Sounds

  • As you release the breath, engage the back of your throat to produce a growling sound.
  • Start by making a soft, guttural noise, gradually increasing the intensity and distortion.

Step 5: Sing Lyrics While You're Growling

  • Once you have a strong growl, try incorporating lyrics into your performance.
  • Start with simple phrases and gradually increase the complexity of the lyrics.

Step 6: Practice Regularly

  • Mastering the death metal growl requires consistent practice.
  • Start with short practice sessions and gradually increase the duration and intensity of your practice.
  • Always prioritize vocal health and take breaks when necessary to avoid vocal strain.
