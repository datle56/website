Unveiling the Secrets of Obsidian: A Comprehensive Guide for Minecraft Enthusiasts

Method 1: Obsidian without a Diamond Pickaxe

  • Prepare your materials: You'll need empty buckets and a water source nearby.

  • Locate a lava pool: In the Overworld, search for a lava pool, typically found in caves or near mountains.

  • Collect the lava: Equip your empty buckets and approach the lava pool. Immerse the bucket in the lava and right-click to scoop it up. Repeat this process to gather several buckets of lava.

  • Dig the hole: Determine where you want to place the obsidian and dig a pit approximately the same size as the amount of lava you collected. The pit should be deep enough to fully contain the lava.

  • Pour the lava: Position yourself over the pit and right-click to pour the collected lava into it. Note that the lava will flow and spread out, so be precise with your pouring.

  • Send water over the lava: Retrieve your water source and carefully pour water over the lava in the pit. As the water touches the lava, it will instantly transform it into obsidian blocks.

Important: Without a diamond pickaxe, attempting to mine the obsidian will shatter it into nothingness.

Method 2: Obsidian with a Diamond Pickaxe

  • Prepare your materials: You'll need a diamond pickaxe and a water source nearby.

  • Locate a lava pool: In the Overworld, identify a lava pool. To prevent accidental lava damage, consider fencing off the area around the pool with cobblestone or other fire-resistant blocks.

  • Pour water over the lava: Position yourself near the pool and carefully pour water over the lava. As it touches the lava, it will solidify into obsidian blocks.

  • Test the obsidian's edges: Before mining, check the edges of the obsidian for any underlying lava that may have remained. If there's lava below, you'll need to pour more water to solidify it.

  • Mine the obsidian: Equip your diamond pickaxe and carefully mine the edges of the obsidian blocks. Direct the flow of water as you mine, ensuring that it covers any exposed lava to prevent damage.

Method 3: Making Nether Portals

  • Gather obsidian blocks: In the Overworld, find and mine enough obsidian to create a portal frame (20 blocks).

  • Activate the Nether portal: Arrange the obsidian blocks in a 4x5 rectangle on the ground. Ignite the bottom row of obsidian with a flint and steel, creating a swirling portal.

  • Travel through the portal: Step through the Nether portal to enter the Nether dimension.

  • Build a second portal in the Nether: In the Nether, place a new obsidian portal frame 100 blocks away from the activated portal. Ignite this frame to create a second portal.

  • Create obsidian blocks: Return through the newly created Nether portal back to the Overworld. The obsidian blocks surrounding the portal will have respawned.

  • Mine and use: Mine the obsidian blocks from the portal and exit through it. This will create a new obsidian block in the portal's center. You can repeat this process to obtain additional obsidian blocks.

Method 4: Mining in the End

  • Activate the End portal: In the Overworld, construct an End portal frame with 12 end portal frames. Light the end crystals on the sides of the frame to activate the portal.

  • Spawn an obsidian platform: Step through the End portal to the End dimension. You will spawn on an obsidian platform suspended in the void.

  • Mine the obsidian platform: Equip your diamond pickaxe and begin mining the obsidian platform. The entire platform can be mined.

  • Mine the obsidian pillars: In addition to the platform, there are four obsidian pillars scattered around the End island. Mine these pillars as well.

  • Return through the portal: Once you have mined all the obsidian, return through the End portal back to the Overworld. The obsidian platform will have respawned, allowing you to mine it again.
