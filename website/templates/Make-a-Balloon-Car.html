Method 1: Plastic Water Bottle Balloon Car

Materials:

  • Clean plastic water bottle
  • 2 straws (width of the bottle)
  • 2 skewers
  • Wheels (bottle caps or cardboard circles)
  • Tape
  • Balloon

Instructions:

  1. Prepare the Bottle:
    • Wash and thoroughly dry the plastic water bottle.
  2. Create the Axle:
    • Cut two straws to the width of the bottle using scissors.
    • Tape the straws securely to the side of the bottle, approximately one inch below the top and bottom.
  3. Insert the Skewers:
    • Cut two skewers to a length that extends about half an inch beyond each side of the straws.
    • Insert the skewers into the straws, ensuring they fit snugly.
  4. Make the Wheels:
    • If using bottle caps, poke holes in the center of the caps to accommodate the skewers. If using cardboard, draw circles and cut them out.
  5. Attach the Wheels:
    • Push the skewers through the holes in the wheels, leaving a small amount of skewer on each side for stability.
  6. Install the Propulsion System:
    • Insert a straw into the neck of a balloon and secure it tightly with tape.
    • Feed the straw through a small hole made in the side of the bottle near the top. Secure the straw in place with tape.
  7. Inflate and Launch:
    • Blow up the balloon to a moderate size and pinch it closed to prevent air from escaping.
    • Place the car on a smooth, flat surface and release the balloon.
    • As the air escapes from the balloon, it will propel the car forward through the straws and wheels.

Method 2: Juice Box Balloon Car

Materials:

  • Juice box or milk carton
  • 2 straws (width of the box)
  • 2 skewers
  • Clay
  • Bottle caps or buttons
  • Balloon
  • Tape

Instructions:

  1. Prepare the Box:
    • Use an empty juice box or milk carton as the base.
  2. Create the Axle:
    • Cut two straws to the width of the box using scissors.
    • Tape the straws to the front of the box, approximately half an inch from the top and bottom.
  3. Insert the Skewers:
    • Cut two skewers to a length that extends about half an inch beyond each side of the straws.
    • Insert the skewers into the straws, ensuring they fit snugly.
  4. Make the Wheels:
    • Fill bottle caps or buttons with clay to add weight.
    • Press the weighted caps or buttons onto the skewers to form wheels.
  5. Install the Propulsion System:
    • Insert a straw into the neck of a balloon and secure it tightly with tape.
    • Tape the straw to the top of the juice box, ensuring that part of the balloon hangs over the end.
  6. Inflate and Launch:
    • Blow up the balloon to a moderate size and pinch it closed to prevent air from escaping.
    • Place the car on a flat surface and release the balloon.
    • As the air escapes from the balloon, it will propel the car forward through the straws and wheels.

Method 3: Basic Cardboard Balloon Car

Materials:

  • Cardboard (3x6 inch rectangle)
  • 2 straws (3 inches long)
  • 2 skewers (4 inches long)
  • Circles for wheels (cardboard or bottle caps)
  • Balloon
  • Tape

Instructions:

  1. Prepare the Base:
    • Cut a 3x6 inch rectangle from a piece of cardboard.
  2. Create the Axle:
    • Cut two 3-inch straws using scissors.
    • Tape the straws to the cardboard base, one inch from each end.
  3. Insert the Skewers:
    • Cut two 4-inch skewers using scissors.
    • Slide the skewers into the straws, ensuring they fit snugly.
  4. Make the Wheels:
    • Cut circles out of cardboard or use bottle caps as wheels.
  5. Attach the Wheels:
    • Push the skewers through the centers of the wheels.
  6. Install the Propulsion System:
    • Insert a straw into the neck of a balloon and secure it tightly with tape.
    • Tape the straw to the top of the cardboard base, ensuring that part of the balloon hangs over the end.
  7. Inflate and Launch:
    • Blow up the balloon to a moderate size and pinch it closed to prevent air from escaping.
    • Place the car on a flat surface and release the balloon.
    • As the air escapes from the balloon, it will propel the car forward through the straws and wheels.
