Unveiling the Secrets of an Upright Piano

Step into the fascinating realm of piano restoration with our comprehensive guide to disassembling an upright piano. Whether you're an aspiring piano technician or simply curious about the inner workings of this elegant instrument, this step-by-step tutorial will unravel the mysteries that lie beneath its polished exterior.

As we embark on this enlightening journey, we'll uncover the secrets hidden within the piano's intricate structure. From removing the ornate key cover to disassembling the intricate action mechanism, you'll witness the precision engineering that gives rise to the piano's enchanting melodies. Join us as we delve into the heart of an upright piano, transforming it from a venerated musical instrument to a disassembled masterpiece. Prepare to be amazed by the symphony of parts that create the magic of this timeless instrument.


Step 1: Unscrewing Outer Pieces

  1. Open the Key Cover and Piano Lid: Begin by lifting the key cover, exposing the piano keys, and then raise the piano lid, fully revealing the piano's interior.

  2. Unhinge the Piano Desk: Locate the piano desk, which is typically situated above the keys. Gently unhinge it to expose the harp, the metal frame that holds the piano strings.

  3. Unscrew the Piano Key Cover: Using a screwdriver, unscrew the screws securing the piano key cover to the piano body. Remove the key cover, revealing the keybed beneath.

  4. Pull Out the Lower Board: Locate the lower board, which is the wooden panel beneath the keybed. Pull it towards you to expose the inner mechanism of the piano.

  5. Unscrew the Top Lid: Unscrew the screws holding the top lid of the piano in place. Lift off the top lid to fully expose the piano's interior.

Step 2: Removing Keys and Action

  1. Remove the Muffler Felt: Locate the muffler felt, which is a thin strip of material resting on the strings. Carefully remove the muffler felt, exposing the strings completely.

  2. Unscrew the Bolts from the Action Brackets: Identify the action brackets, which are metal brackets connecting the action mechanism to the piano body. Unscrew the bolts securing these brackets.

  3. Pull the Action Mechanism Forward: Carefully pull the action mechanism forward, away from the piano body. Once it is clear of the body, lift it off and set it aside.

  4. Lift Each Key Off the Keyboard: Gently lift each key off the keyboard, starting from the lowest note and moving upwards. Be careful not to damage the keys or the keybed.

Step 3: Disassembling Piano Structure

  1. Loosen the Tension in All Strings: For safety reasons, it is crucial to loosen the tension in all the strings on the harp before proceeding. Use a tuning key to carefully rotate the tuning pins until the strings are loose.

  2. Unscrew the Key Bed from the Piano Body: Locate the bolts or screws securing the key bed to the piano body. Unscrew these fasteners to detach the key bed from the body.

  3. Lay the Piano on Its Back: Carefully lay the piano on its back, with the pedals facing upwards.

  4. Remove the Piano Side Supports: Unscrew the screws or bolts holding the piano side supports in place. Remove the side supports from the piano body.

  5. Pull Off the Piano Harp: Finally, grip the harp and pull it straight up and away from the piano body. This will complete the disassembly of the upright piano.


Conclusion: Piano Disassembly Success

With careful planning and precision, you have successfully disassembled your upright piano. By following these detailed steps, you have safely removed the outer pieces, keys and action, and disassembled the piano structure. Congratulations on completing this challenging yet rewarding task!

Whether you are a seasoned technician or a curious novice, this disassembly process has provided valuable insights into the intricate workings of an upright piano. The understanding gained will empower you to perform future repairs, maintenance, or restoration projects with confidence.
