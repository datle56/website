Car Maintenance Guide

Elevating the Vehicle

  1. Park on a level surface: Choose a flat and stable area to prevent your car from rolling.
  2. Apply the parking brake: Engage the parking brake to secure the vehicle in place.
  3. Jack up the car or drive it onto ramps: Use a hydraulic jack to lift the car at designated jack points provided by your manufacturer. Alternatively, you can drive the car onto ramps that are positioned to elevate the specific parts you need to access.
  4. Place a wheel block behind a rear tire for safety: Insert a wheel block behind a rear tire that is diagonally opposite the tires you are working on. This provides additional support and prevents the car from moving.

Draining the Oil

  1. Place an oil drain pan under the engine drain plug: Position the oil drain pan directly beneath the oil drain plug to catch the used oil.
  2. Unscrew the oil cap to allow air flow: Open the oil cap on top of the engine to allow air to enter the engine and facilitate the oil flow.
  3. Use a socket or wrench to unscrew the oil plug: Locate the oil drain plug on the bottom of the engine and use a socket or wrench to loosen it.
  4. Wait for the oil to drain out completely: Allow all the oil to drain out into the oil drain pan. This may take several minutes.

Replacing the Oil Filter

  1. Locate the filter assembly under the hood: Open the hood and identify the oil filter assembly, which is usually a cylindrical component connected to the engine.
  2. Unscrew the old oil filter by hand or with a tool: Grasp the old oil filter by hand if possible, or use a wrench designed for oil filters if hand-tightening fails. Turn it counterclockwise to loosen and remove it.
  3. Rub oil on the gasket of the new filter: Apply a thin layer of engine oil to the rubber gasket of the new oil filter. This lubricates the seal and ensures a tight fit.
  4. Screw the new filter into place tightly: Hand-tighten the new oil filter onto the mount, then use the wrench to further tighten it by turning clockwise. Avoid over-tightening.

Adding New Oil

  1. Pour new oil into the fill hole: Use a funnel to pour the recommended amount of new motor oil into the oil fill hole located on top of the engine. Consult your vehicle's owner's manual for the specified oil type and quantity.
  2. Replace the fill cap: Once all the oil is added, screw on the oil fill cap securely.

Disposing of Oil

  1. Transfer the old oil to a sealable container: Pour the used oil from the oil drain pan into a sealable container approved for hazardous waste.
  2. Find an oil-collection site to dispose of the oil properly: Locate a designated oil-collection site or recycling center where you can drop off the used oil for proper disposal.
  3. Consider using recycled oil for your next oil change: Some oil change facilities offer recycled oil as an environmentally friendly alternative for subsequent oil changes.

Conclusion
