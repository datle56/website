Reactivating a Facebook Account

Mobile:

  1. Locate the Facebook app: Launch the Facebook app installed on your mobile device.
  2. Enter credentials: Tap on the login screen and provide your registered email address or phone number and the corresponding password.
  3. Log in: Press the "Log In" button. If the credentials are correct, your account will be reactivated, and you will be taken to your Facebook homepage.

Desktop:

  1. Access Facebook website: Open your web browser and navigate to www.facebook.com.
  2. Enter credentials: On the login page, enter your email address or phone number in the first field and your password in the second field.
  3. Click "Log In": Click on the blue "Log In" button located below the password field. If the provided credentials are valid, your account will be reactivated, and you will be redirected to your Facebook news feed.

Appealing a Deactivated Account:

In case your Facebook account has been deactivated due to policy violations or security concerns, you can initiate an appeal to reinstate your account:

  1. Visit the appeal page: Access Facebook's designated page for appealing deactivated accounts at https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/260749603972907.
  2. Provide login credentials: Enter your email address or phone number and password that you used to access the deactivated account.
  3. Upload ID proof: Submit a clear photo or scan of your government-issued identification document (e.g., driver's license, passport).
  4. Explain deactivation: Briefly describe the reason behind the deactivation of your account and provide any relevant details, such as any suspicious activities or content violations that may have occurred.
  5. Submit the appeal: Click the "Send" button to submit your appeal to Facebook.

Note:

  • Self-deactivated accounts: If you voluntarily deactivated your account, you can reactivate it anytime by simply logging back in using the steps outlined above.
  • Deleted accounts: Once an account is permanently deleted, it cannot be recovered, and all associated data and content will be lost.
  • Content restoration: When you reactivate a deactivated account, please note that any content that was deleted prior to deactivation will not be restored.
