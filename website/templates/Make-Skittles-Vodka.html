Creating the Ultimate Skittles Vodka Infusion: A Step-by-Step Guide

Indulge in the vibrant flavors of your favorite Skittles candies by transforming them into a tantalizing vodka infusion. This easy-to-make cocktail will become an instant hit at your next gathering or simply brighten up your evening. Here's a comprehensive guide to creating this fruity and fun vodka infusion:

Single Serving (Shot)

  1. Color Coordinate: For a visually appealing shot, separate your Skittles into individual colors by hand. Alternatively, you can skip this step for a mixed-flavor infusion.

  2. The Perfect Ratio: In a small glass or shot glass, combine 6 Skittles of your desired color with 25 mL of vodka.

  3. Dissolve the Magic: Gently swirl the mixture until the Skittles completely dissolve. The vibrant colors will begin to swirl, creating a mesmerizing effect.

  4. Strain for Clarity: Using a fine-mesh strainer or cheesecloth, carefully strain out any remaining Skittle bits. This will ensure a smooth and flavorful infusion.

Larger Bottles

  1. Make Room for Color: Select a bottle of vodka and remove enough liquid to create space for the Skittles. The amount of vodka you remove will depend on the size of your bottle and the desired intensity of the infusion.

  2. Skittles Overload: Separate the Skittles into their individual colors or combine them for a delightful blend of flavors. For every 350 mL of vodka, add approximately 20-25 Skittles.

  3. Time for Infusion: Seal the bottle tightly and let it rest for one to two days in a cool, dark place. Occasionally shake the bottle to enhance the infusion process.

  4. Strain and Filter: After the desired infusion time, line a fine-mesh strainer or cheesecloth with multiple layers of paper towels. Carefully strain the vodka through the filter to remove any Skittle residue.

  5. Refrigerate and Savor: For optimal results, refrigerate the infused vodka overnight. This will allow the flavors to fully develop and settle, resulting in a perfectly balanced cocktail.

Dishwasher Trick for Speed:

If you're impatient or prefer a quicker method, the dishwasher can come to your rescue:

  1. Skittles Bonanza: Separate Skittles from approximately 5 bags.

  2. Vodka and Skittles: Pour vodka into several bottles, leaving ample space for the Skittles. Add about 300 mL of vodka to each bottle and add the Skittles.

  3. Dishwasher Intensive Cycle: Place the bottles in your dishwasher and set it to an intensive cycle. This will help extract the flavors and colors from the Skittles more quickly.

  4. Shake it Up: During the dishwasher cycle, shake the bottles vigorously twice to ensure even distribution of flavors.

  5. Freeze and Filter: Once the dishwasher cycle is complete, transfer the bottles to your freezer for at least 2 hours. This will help solidify the remaining Skittle residue. Then, strain the vodka through a filter to remove any remaining bits.

  6. Freezer Storage: Store your Skittles vodka infusion in the freezer until you're ready to enjoy. The freezing process will further enhance the flavors and colors, creating an exceptionally vibrant and flavorful cocktail.
