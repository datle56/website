How to Link a Bank Account to PayPal

Benefits of Linking Your Bank Account to PayPal:

  • Account Verification: Linking your bank account to PayPal verifies your identity and increases your account security.
  • Seamless Payments: Eliminate the need for credit or debit cards by making payments directly from your bank account.
  • Faster Transactions: Send and receive money with PayPal's lightning-fast transfer times.

Step-by-Step Guide to Linking Your Bank Account on a Computer or Mobile Device:

  1. Login to PayPal: Access your PayPal account through the website or mobile app.
  2. Navigate to "Wallet": Find the "Wallet" section in the account menu, which typically contains options related to your funds and payment methods.
  3. Select "Link a Bank Account" or "Banks": Look for the "Link a Bank Account" or "Banks" option within the "Wallet" section. This will initiate the bank linking process.
  4. Search for Your Bank or Enter Details Manually: Search for your bank from the list provided by PayPal. If your bank is not listed, you can manually enter the bank details.
  5. Connect to Bank Account: Enter your bank username and password to connect to your bank account. PayPal will securely verify your credentials.

Troubleshooting: Manual Linking if Automatic Process Fails

If PayPal's automatic linking process is unsuccessful, you can manually link your bank account by following these steps:

  • Confirm Deposit Amounts: PayPal will make two small deposits into your bank account.
  • Log In to Bank Account: Access your bank account online or through the mobile app.
  • Locate Deposit Amounts: Find the two small deposits made by PayPal and record their amounts.
  • Return to PayPal: Go back to the "Link a Bank Account" page on PayPal.
  • Enter Deposit Amounts: Enter the two deposit amounts in the specified fields.
  • Confirm Linking: Click the "Confirm" button to complete the manual linking process.

Advantages of Verifying Your PayPal Account with a Bank Account:

  • Increased Sending Limits: Verified accounts have higher limits for sending money.
  • Reduced Fees: PayPal charges lower fees for transactions made with verified bank accounts.
  • Fast Withdrawals: Verifying your account allows you to withdraw funds directly to your bank account with faster processing times.
