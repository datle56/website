Creating an Ouija Board

Method 1: Paper Board

  • Materials:

    • Large piece of paper (cardstock or poster paper recommended)
    • Black or dark-colored marker or paint
    • Template or ruler for drawing letters
    • Tumbler or glass for use as a pointer

  • Instructions:

  1. Draw the alphabet letters in a circle around the edge of the paper.
  2. Arrange the numbers 0-9 in a smaller circle inside the alphabet circle.
  3. Write the words "Yes," "No," "Hello," and "Goodbye" outside the circles, as indicated on commercially available Ouija boards.
  4. Mark a "Yes" with a sun symbol and a "No" with a moon symbol.
  5. Place the tumbler or glass upside down in the center of the board as the pointer.

Method 2: Wooden Board

  • Materials:

    • Smooth wooden board (plywood, MDF, or hardwood recommended)
    • Soldering iron or wood-burning tool
    • Varnish or polyurethane for finishing
    • Paint or embellishments (optional)

  • Instructions:

  1. Design the board by sketching out the letters, numbers, and words on the wooden surface.
  2. Use a soldering iron or wood-burning tool to etch the design permanently onto the board.
  3. Coat the board with varnish or polyurethane to create a smooth and durable surface.
  4. Add finishing details such as paint, gold leaf, or other embellishments to enhance the board's aesthetics.

Designing the Board

  • Layout:

    • Traditional: Lines of letters arranged in a rectangular shape.
    • Diamond: A diamond-shaped layout with letters arranged in a clockwise direction.
    • Circular: An oval or circular shape with letters arranged in concentric circles.

  • Font:

    • Choose a font that complements the board's design and is easy to read. Consider using an antique or vintage font for a more traditional look.

  • Embellishments:

    • Add symbols such as runes, astrological signs, or sacred geometry to enhance the board's visual appeal and spiritual significance.
    • Use images of spirits, angels, or other beings to evoke a particular atmosphere.

  • Colors:

    • Select contrasting colors for the letters and background to make the board easy on the eyes.
    • Choose colors that reflect the desired ambiance, such as deep navy for mystery or soft pastels for tranquility.

Planchette

  • A planchette is a device used to move around the board and communicate with spirits.
  • It can be a simple piece of wood or glass with a shape that allows it to slide easily.
  • You can create your own planchette or purchase one from a metaphysical store.

  • End the session politely: Always end the session by saying goodbye and moving the pointer to "Goodbye."
  • Ground yourself: After using the board, ground yourself and release any residual energy by touching your feet to the floor or taking a cleansing bath.
