How to Calculate Total Cost with Sales Tax

Method 1: Using a Formula

Calculating the total cost with sales tax using a formula is a straightforward approach. Follow these steps:

  1. Convert the sales tax rate to a decimal. To do this, divide the percentage by 100. For example, if the sales tax rate is 7.5%, the decimal equivalent is 0.075.
  2. Multiply the item cost by the sales tax rate. This step calculates the amount of sales tax due.
  3. Add the result to the original cost. The sum of the item cost and the sales tax amount gives you the total cost.

Formula:

Total Cost = (Item Cost x Sales Tax) + Item Cost

Example:

  • Item Cost: $60
  • Sales Tax: 7.5% (0.075)

Calculation: Total Cost = ($60 x 0.075) + $60 = $64.50

Method 2: Examples

To illustrate the calculation process, here are some examples:

  • Basketball costs $25, sales tax 2.9% (0.029): Total Cost = $25 x 1.029 = $25.73
  • Grocery bill of $300, sales tax 7% (0.07): Total Cost = $300 x 1.07 = $321
  • Car costs $15,000, sales tax 6.25% (0.0625): Total Cost = $15,000 x 1.0625 = $15,937.50

Other Important Information

  • Sales tax exemption: Certain states, such as Delaware and Alaska, do not have a sales tax.
  • Variations in tax rates: Different states may apply different tax rates to different goods. For instance, some states may have a higher tax rate for prepared food.
  • Local variations: Within a state, there may be variations in sales tax rates between cities or counties.
  • Checking for specific rates: It is always advisable to check with your local state and city authorities for the most accurate sales tax rates. You can typically find this information on your local government's website or by contacting your city hall.
