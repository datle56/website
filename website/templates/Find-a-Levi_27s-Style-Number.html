Method 1: Checking the Label

  1. Locate the "Two Horse" Label:

    • Turn your jeans inside out and examine the back of the waistband.
    • Look for a rectangular leather or fabric patch with the iconic "Two Horse" logo embroidered or printed on it.

  2. Identify the Style Number:

    • In the lower left corner of the "Two Horse" label, you will find a series of numbers and letters.
    • The style number is typically a four-digit code starting with the number "5."
    • Example: 501, 505, 511

  3. Check the Care Tag:

    • If the "Two Horse" label is faded or missing, inspect the care tag sewn inside the jeans.
    • Look for a line that includes "STYLE NO.:" followed by the style number.

Method 2: Determining Style Number Without a Label

  1. Compare to Other Levi's Styles:

    • Examine your jeans and compare them to known Levi's styles.
    • Pay attention to the fit, leg opening, and details like rivets and stitching.
    • Refer to online resources or visit a Levi's store to help you narrow down the possibilities.

  2. Check Tab Color:

    • Look at the color of the tab on the back right pocket.
    • Different colors may indicate specific styles.
    • For example, a red tab with a white "Levi's" print typically denotes the iconic 501 model.

  3. Consider Levi's Design Evolution:

    • Take into account the changes in Levi's jeans over time.
    • For instance, rivets were removed from the back pockets in the 1940s, and the lowercase "e" on the red tab was introduced after 1971.

  4. Consult Experts:

    • If you still cannot identify the style number, consider reaching out to Levi's historians or experienced denim enthusiasts for assistance.
    • They may be able to provide information based on specific details or photographs of your jeans.
