How to Create a Classic or Dramatic Smokey Eye

A smokey eye is a timeless makeup look that adds depth, drama, and allure to your eyes. Here's a step-by-step guide to help you create a classic or dramatic smokey eye:

Classic Smokey Eye

Step 1: Prepare Your Lids

Start by applying an eyeshadow primer to your eyelids. This will create a smooth base for your eyeshadow, ensuring that it lasts all day.

Step 2: Create the Base

Sweep the middle shade of eyeshadow over your entire eyelid, stopping at your natural crease. This shade will serve as the base for your smokey eye.

Step 3: Highlight

Apply the lightest shade of eyeshadow in the inner corners of your eyes and under your brow bone. This will help brighten up your eyes and make them appear larger.

Step 4: Define the Crease

Using the darkest shade of eyeshadow, create a "C" shape in the outer corner of your eye. This shape should extend from the outer corner to the crease of your eyelid.

Step 5: Blend

Take a fluffy blending brush and blend the three eyeshadow shades together. Start from the darkest shade at the outer corner, blending towards the lighter shades. This will create a seamless gradient and give your eye a sultry, smokey effect.

Step 6: Line and Smudge

Line your upper lash line with an eyeliner of your choice. For a classic smokey eye, use a black or dark brown liner. Smudge the eyeliner with a blending brush or a cotton swab to create a softer, smokier look.

Step 7: Finish

Apply mascara to your upper and lower lashes. This will help define your eyes and make them stand out. Clean up any fallen eyeshadow or mascara from your cheeks to complete the look.

Dramatic Smokey Eye

Step 1: Prime and Highlight

Follow the same steps as in the classic smokey eye tutorial to apply eyeshadow primer and highlight the inner corners and under your brow bone.

Step 2: Create the Base

Apply the darkest eyeshadow shade along the upper and lower lash lines, extending it towards the crease. This will create the base for your dramatic smokey eye.

Step 3: Blend

Blend the middle eyeshadow shade along the center of your eyelid, blending it into the darker shade at the crease.

Step 4: Gradient

Using a fluffy brush, blend the three eyeshadow shades together to create a seamless gradient. Start from the darkest shade at the outermost corner, blending towards the lighter shades.

Step 5: Line and Smudge

Line your upper lash line with a black or dark brown eyeliner. Smudge the eyeliner using a blending brush or a cotton swab to create a bold, smoky effect.

Step 6: Tightline

Tightline your eyes with a black or dark brown eyeliner to enhance their darkness and intensify the smokey effect.

Step 7: Finish

Apply mascara to your upper and lower lashes. Clean up any excess makeup from your cheeks to complete the dramatic smokey eye look.
