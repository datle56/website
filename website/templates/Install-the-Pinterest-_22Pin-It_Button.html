How to Install and Use the Pinterest Save Button

For Desktop Browsers:

  1. Download the Extension: Visit the Pinterest website and click on the "Download" button for the browser extension compatible with your desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox).
  2. Sign In to Pinterest: Once the extension is installed, sign in to your Pinterest account.
  3. Hover and Save: Navigate to the website where you discover an image or video that captivates your interest. Hover over the content, and you will notice the "Save" button appearing on the bottom right corner.
  4. Click to Save: By clicking the "Save" button, you effortlessly add the content to your Pinterest account.

For iPhone or iPad:

  1. Share and More: When browsing content in the Safari browser on your iOS device, tap on the "Share" icon and select "More."
  2. Edit and Add Pinterest: Scroll down and find the "Edit" option. Tap on it and ensure that "Pinterest" is added to the list of sharing options.
  3. Save with Pinterest: Once you find a piece of content you wish to preserve, simply tap on the "Pinterest" icon to save it directly to your account.
