Step-by-Step Guide to Evolve Eevee in Pokémon Platinum

Eevee, the charming evolutionary Pokémon, holds the unique ability to evolve into multiple forms, each with distinct characteristics and abilities. Pokémon Platinum offers six different evolution paths for Eevee, providing players with a wide range of choices to enhance their team.

Evolving Using Stones

Flareon (Fire Type)

  • Method: Use a Fire Stone on your Eevee.
  • Location: Fire Stones can be purchased at various PokéMarts, found as hidden items in the wild, or obtained from the Underground.

Vaporeon (Water Type)

  • Method: Use a Water Stone on your Eevee.
  • Location: Water Stones can be found in watery areas such as Lake Valor, Route 224, or as hidden items in the Underground.

Jolteon (Electric Type)

  • Method: Use a Thunder Stone on your Eevee.
  • Location: Thunder Stones can be found near electrical towers or as hidden items in the Underground.

Evolving with Friendship Level

Friendship is a crucial factor in unlocking Eevee's psychic and dark evolutions.

Espeon (Psychic Type)

  • Method: Level up your Eevee with high friendship during the daytime.
  • Tips:
    • Use a Soothe Bell to increase friendship faster.
    • Keep your Eevee in your party and interact with it regularly.
    • Avoid letting your Eevee faint.

Umbreon (Dark Type)

  • Method: Level up your Eevee with high friendship during the nighttime.
  • Tips:
    • Similar to Espeon, use a Soothe Bell and keep your Eevee in your party.
    • Level up your Eevee at night to trigger its evolution into Umbreon.

Evolving in Specific Locations

Two of Eevee's evolutions require leveling up near specific landmarks in the game.

Leafeon (Grass Type)

  • Method: Level up your Eevee near the Mossy Rock in Eterna Forest.
  • Location: Eterna Forest is located southeast of Eterna City. The Mossy Rock is a large rock formation in the center of the forest.

Glaceon (Ice Type)

  • Method: Level up your Eevee near the Ice Rock on Route 217.
  • Location: Route 217 is located west of Hearthome City. The Ice Rock is a large boulder covered in ice, found near the end of the route.

Additional Tips

  • Eevees can be found in Bebe's house in Hearthome City.
  • If you have the National Pokédex, you can also catch Eevees in Trophy Garden.
  • If you don't have any evolution stones, you can trade with other players to obtain them.
  • Experiment with different evolution methods to build a diverse team of Eevee evolutions.
