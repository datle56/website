Craft Your Perfect Pair: A Step-by-Step Guide to Making Shorts

Whether it's for a relaxed summer day or an energetic workout, shorts are a wardrobe staple. But why settle for store-bought when you can create your own customized pair? Get ready to unleash your inner designer and embark on an adventure in sewing with our comprehensive guide.

With two easy-to-follow methods, we'll show you how to craft stylish shorts that fit your unique style and comfort. From drafting your own pattern to adding finishing touches like pockets and a waistband, we'll walk you through every step in detail.

So, gather your fabric, thread, and a dash of creativity, and let's dive into the exciting world of DIY shorts-making!


Method 1: Shorts for Women

Step 1: Creating the Pattern

  • Select a pair of shorts that fit you comfortably and provide the desired length and style.
  • Spread out the shorts on a flat surface and smooth out any wrinkles.
  • Place a large piece of paper or fabric over the shorts, aligning the edges.
  • Use a pen or pencil to carefully trace around the outline of the shorts, including all the curves and details.
  • Label the pieces "front" and "back."

Step 2: Cutting the Fabric

  • Fold the fabric in half lengthwise, aligning the selvage edges.
  • Place the pattern pieces onto the folded fabric, aligning the center line of the pattern with the fold line.
  • Pin the pattern securely to the fabric.
  • Cut out two pieces of fabric using the pattern as a guide.
  • Unfold the fabric to reveal the mirrored front and back pieces.

Step 3: Sewing the Seams

  • Place the front and back pieces right sides together, aligning the raw edges.
  • Pin along the curves of the side seams and crotch seam.
  • Using a sewing machine, stitch along the pinned seams with a small stitch length (2.5-3 mm).
  • Trim the excess fabric close to the stitches.

Step 4: Right-Side Out and Inseam Stitching

  • Turn the shorts right side out through one of the leg openings.
  • Press the seams open for a more polished look.
  • Pin and stitch the inseams, again using a small stitch length.
  • Trim the excess fabric close to the stitches.

Step 5: Creating the Waistband

  • Cut a strip of fabric or elastic approximately 2 inches wide and the circumference of your waist.
  • Fold the top edge of the shorts down by 1 inch.
  • Press the fold to create a crisp crease.
  • Place the waistband strip around the waist of the shorts, overlapping the ends by 1 inch.
  • Sew the ends of the waistband together, creating a loop.

Step 6: Inserting the Elastic

  • Thread the elastic band through the waistband loop.
  • Adjust the elastic until the shorts fit snugly.
  • Sew the opening of the waistband closed.

Step 7: Hemming the Legs

  • Fold the bottom edge of each leg up by 1 inch.
  • Press the fold to create a crisp crease.
  • Fold the bottom edge up again by 1 inch and press.
  • Topstitch the hem in place, close to the folded edge.

Method 2: Shorts for Men

Step 1: Acquiring the Pattern

  • Search online for free shorts patterns that match your desired style and size.
  • Download the pattern and print it out on regular printer paper.

Step 2: Cutting Out the Pattern Pieces

  • Cut out all the pattern pieces according to the instructions provided.
  • Make sure to match the grainline (indicated by an arrow on the pattern) of the fabric to the grainline on the pattern pieces.

Step 3: Pinning and Cutting the Fabric

  • Pin the pattern pieces onto the fabric, aligning the grainline and notches.
  • Cut out the fabric sections using sharp scissors or a rotary cutter.

Step 4: Sewing the Back Pockets

  • Place the back pocket pieces on the right side of the back fabric, aligning the notches.
  • Pin and sew around the edges of the pockets.
  • Reinforce the corners by backstitching.

Step 5: Sewing the Front Pockets

  • Fold the top edge of the front pocket pieces down by 1 inch and press.
  • Fold the top edge down again by 1 inch and press.
  • Topstitch the folded edge to secure it.
  • Pin the pockets to the right side of the front fabric, centering them between the side seams.
  • Sew around the edges of the pockets, leaving the top edge open.
  • Turn the pockets right side out and press.
  • Topstitch the top edge of the pockets closed, close to the folded edge.

Step 6: Sewing the Crotch Seam

  • Pin and sew the crotch seam, matching the center notches.
  • Reinforce the seam by backstitching the beginning and end.

Step 7: Sewing the Inseam and Side Seams

  • Pin and sew the inseam and side seams, leaving a 1-inch opening at the top of the waistband.
  • Reinforce the seams by backstitching the beginning and end.

Step 8: Hemming the Shorts

  • Fold the bottom edge of the shorts up by 1 inch and press.
  • Fold the bottom edge up again by 1 inch and press.
  • Topstitch the hem in place, close to the folded edge.

Step 9: Sewing the Waistband Lining

  • Cut a piece of lightweight fabric or interfacing the same length as the waistband and 2.5 inches wide.
  • Fold the lining fabric in half lengthwise and press.
  • Insert the lining inside the waistband, aligning the raw edges.
  • Sew around the top and bottom edges of the waistband, enclosing the lining.

Step 10: Stitching the Elastic Waistband

  • Cut a piece of elastic approximately 2 inches wide and the circumference of your waist minus 1 inch.
  • Fold the elastic in half and sew the ends together, creating a loop.
  • Insert the elastic into the waistband, overlapping the ends by 1 inch.
  • Sew the ends of the elastic together, creating a secure loop.

Step 11: Folding the Elastic into the Lining

  • Fold the lining over the elastic, enclosing it completely.
  • Pin and sew around the top and bottom edges of the waistband, close to the folded edge.

Conclusion:

With these comprehensive methods, you're now equipped to craft tailored and stylish shorts that flatter your body. Whether you prefer the simplicity of women's shorts or the functional design of men's shorts, these tutorials empower you to create custom garments suited to your personal style and fit. Embrace the joy of DIY and enjoy the satisfaction of wearing shorts you've lovingly crafted with your own hands. From weekend adventures to summer beach days, these handmade shorts will become versatile wardrobe staples you'll cherish for years to come.
