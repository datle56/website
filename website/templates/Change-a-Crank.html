Removing Bike Pedals and Crank: A Detailed Guide

Detaching Pedals

Materials Required:

  • Pedal wrench or Allen key

Instructions:

  1. Identify the pedal thread direction:

    • Right pedal: Standard thread (loosens counterclockwise)
    • Left pedal: Reverse thread (loosens clockwise)

  2. Loosen the pedals:

    • Using a pedal wrench or Allen key, turn the right pedal counterclockwise to loosen it.
    • For the left pedal, turn it clockwise due to its reverse threading.

  3. Fully detach the pedals:

    • Once the pedals are loose, rotate the crank clockwise (for the right pedal) or counterclockwise (for the left pedal) to completely detach them from the crank arms.

Removing the Crank

Materials Required:

  • Allen keys
  • Crank extractor (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Remove the cap:

    • Some cranks have a cap covering the crank bolt. Remove this cap using an Allen key.

  2. Loosen the pinch bolts (if applicable):

    • Some crank arms have pinch bolts near the top. If present, loosen them using an Allen key.

  3. Loosen the crank bolt:

    • Insert an Allen key into the crank bolt and turn it counterclockwise to loosen it.

  4. Removing cranks:

    • Self-extracting cranks: If you have self-extracting cranks, simply pull the crank arm away from the bike.
    • Other cranks: If you don't have self-extracting cranks, use a crank extractor to pop off the cranks.

Installing New Cranks and Pedals

Materials Required:

  • New cranks and pedals
  • Allen key
  • Grease

Instructions:

Installing Cranks:

  1. Apply grease to the spindle:

    • Lightly grease the spindle where the cranks will be installed.

  2. Slide on the cranks:

    • Push the right crank onto the spindle, ensuring that any washers are in place.
    • Slide the left crank onto the spindle and tap it into place if necessary.

  3. Tighten the crank bolts:

    • Screw in the new crank bolts using an Allen key.

  4. Replace the cap and pinch bolts (if applicable):

    • Replace the compression cap over the crank bolts and tighten it.
    • If applicable, tighten the pinch bolts.

Installing Pedals:

  1. Apply grease to the pedal threads:

    • Lightly grease the pedal threads where they will screw into the crank arms.

  2. Screw in the pedals:

    • Screw the pedals back into the crank arms by turning them counterclockwise.
    • Ensure that the pedals are securely tightened.
