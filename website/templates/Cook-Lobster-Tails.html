Discover the Culinary Delights of Lobster Tails: A Comprehensive Guide to Steaming, Baking, and Grilling

Embark on a culinary adventure with this comprehensive guide to mastering the art of preparing lobster tails. Whether you're a seasoned chef or an aspiring home cook, we've got you covered. From the succulent delicacy of steamed tails to the mouthwatering flavors of grilled masterpieces, this guide will lead you through every step, ensuring your lobster experience is nothing short of exceptional.


Method 1: Steaming Lobster Tails

Step 1: Preparing the Lobster Tails

  • Remove the lobster tails from the freezer and let them thaw in the refrigerator overnight or at room temperature for several hours.
  • Rinse the lobster tails thoroughly under cold running water.
  • Using kitchen shears, cut through the shell lengthwise along the middle of the tail.
  • Remove the digestive tract, which looks like a thin, dark vein running along the back of the tail.

Step 2: Steaming the Lobster Tails

  • Fill a large steamer or pot with an inch of water. Bring the water to a boil.
  • Place the lobster tails in the steamer basket or on a rack set over the boiling water.

  • Cover the steamer or pot and steam the lobster tails for the following amounts of time, depending on their size:

    • 3-5 minutes for 4-6 ounce tails
    • 6-8 minutes for 8-10 ounce tails
    • 9-12 minutes for 10-12 ounce tails

Step 3: Serving

  • Remove the lobster tails from the steamer and let them rest for a few minutes before serving.
  • Serve the lobster tails with melted butter for dipping.

Method 2: Baking Lobster Tails

Step 1: Preparing the Lobster Tails

  • Preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C).
  • Melt butter in a small bowl.
  • Remove the lobster tails from the freezer and let them thaw in the refrigerator overnight or at room temperature for several hours.
  • Rinse the lobster tails thoroughly under cold running water.
  • Using kitchen shears, cut through the shell lengthwise along the middle of the tail.
  • Remove the digestive tract, which looks like a thin, dark vein running along the back of the tail.

Step 2: Baking the Lobster Tails

  • Dip the lobster tails into the melted butter.
  • Place the lobster tails in a baking dish.
  • Bake for 15-20 minutes, or until the internal temperature reaches 140-145°F (60-63°C) when measured with a meat thermometer.

Step 3: Serving

  • Remove the lobster tails from the oven and let them rest for a few minutes before serving.
  • Serve the lobster tails with lemon slices for squeezing over the meat.

Method 3: Grilling Lobster Tails

Step 1: Preparing the Lobster Tails

  • Preheat the grill to medium heat.
  • Remove the lobster tails from the freezer and let them thaw in the refrigerator overnight or at room temperature for several hours.
  • Rinse the lobster tails thoroughly under cold running water.
  • Using kitchen shears, cut through the shell lengthwise along the middle of the tail.
  • Remove the digestive tract, which looks like a thin, dark vein running along the back of the tail.

Step 2: Grilling the Lobster Tails

  • Brush the lobster tails with olive oil and sprinkle with salt.
  • Place the lobster tails cut-side down on the grill.
  • Grill for 5-7 minutes, or until the shells turn bright red.
  • Turn the lobster tails over and grill for an additional 5-7 minutes, or until the meat is opaque and firm.
  • Brush the lobster tails with melted butter and cook for an additional 4 minutes.

Step 3: Serving

  • Remove the lobster tails from the grill and let them rest for a few minutes before serving.
  • Serve the lobster tails with lemon wedges and melted butter for dipping.
