How to Save Pinterest Videos Offline

Introduction:

Pinterest, a popular visual discovery platform, hosts a vast collection of videos that inspire, inform, and entertain. While many videos are available online, there may be times when you want to save them offline for offline viewing or future use. Here's a step-by-step guide on how to save Pinterest videos offline on both mobile devices and computers.

For Phones (Android and iPhone):

  1. Open the Pinterest App: Navigate to the Pinterest app and sign in to your account if necessary.
  2. Find the Video You Want: Use the search bar or browse through pins to locate the video you want to save offline.
  3. Tap on the Video: Once you find the video, tap on it to open it in full screen mode.
  4. Locate the Three Dots: In the bottom right corner of the video, you will see three vertical dots. Tap on these dots to reveal the video options menu.
  5. Copy Link: From the options menu, select "Copy Link." This action copies the URL of the video to your clipboard.
  6. Use a Video Conversion Website: Open a web browser on your phone and visit a reputable video conversion website, such as expertsphp.com/pinterest-video-downloader.html.
  7. Paste the Link: In the URL field provided on the conversion website, paste the link you copied in step 5.
  8. Click "Download Video": Once the link is pasted, click or tap on the "Download Video" button. Depending on the conversion website, you may have to choose the video quality before downloading.
  9. Save the Video: The video will start downloading. Once it's finished, you can find it in the Downloads folder on your phone.

For Computers (Mac and PC):

  1. Open Pinterest in a Web Browser: Visit Pinterest's website (www.pinterest.com) in your preferred web browser.
  2. Find the Video You Want: Use the search bar or navigate through pins to find the video you want to save offline.
  3. Copy the Video's URL: Click on the video to open it, then copy the URL from the address bar at the top of your browser window.
  4. Use a Video Conversion Website: In a separate tab or window, visit a video conversion website, such as pinterestvideodownloader.com.
  5. Paste the Link: Paste the video's URL into the URL field provided on the conversion website.
  6. Click "Download Video": Click or tap on the "Download Video" button. The website will generate a download link for the video.
  7. Save the Video: Click on the download link to save the video to your computer. The video will typically download to your "Downloads" folder.

Tips and Additional Notes:

  • Some Pinterest videos may have restrictions on downloading and offline viewing.
  • The quality of the downloaded video may vary depending on the original video resolution and the settings you choose during the download process.
  • It is recommended to use reputable video conversion websites to ensure the safety and security of your device.
  • You can also use third-party apps or browser extensions that specialize in downloading Pinterest videos. However, it's important to research and choose these apps carefully to avoid any potential security risks.

Conclusion:

With the methods described above, you can easily save Pinterest videos offline to your phone or computer. Whether you want to watch your favorite pins later without an internet connection, or share them with others, these steps will allow you to do so with ease. Remember to check the copyright and usage rights of the videos before downloading to ensure that you are not violating any restrictions. So, next time you stumble upon an inspiring Pinterest video, don't hesitate to save it offline to enjoy it anytime, anywhere!
