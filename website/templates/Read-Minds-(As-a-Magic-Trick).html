Method 1: Name the Dead

Materials:

  • Three volunteers
  • Three pieces of paper
  • A pen for each volunteer

Steps:

  1. Gather the volunteers: Select three volunteers from the audience.
  2. Distribute paper and pen: Give each volunteer a piece of paper and a pen.
  3. Instruct the volunteers: Explain to the volunteers that they will be writing down the names of individuals.
  4. Assign roles: Instruct the first volunteer to write down the name of a living person. The second volunteer will write down the name of a deceased person (this is the key to the trick). The third volunteer will write down the name of another living person.
  5. Collect the papers: Have the volunteers fold their papers and return them to you.
  6. "Read" the volunteers' minds: Pretend to use your psychic abilities or mind-reading skills to determine which paper contains the deceased person's name.
  7. Reveal the secret: Once you have selected the paper with the deceased person's name, unfold it and reveal the name to the audience, demonstrating your seemingly supernatural powers.

Method 2: Predict the Luckiest

Materials:

  • A chalkboard or whiteboard
  • A marker
  • A hat or container
  • Notecards
  • Audience participation

Steps:

  1. Ask for names: Request audience members to call out their names or write them down on notecards.
  2. Write the same name on notecards: Secretly write the name of your guest of honor or a predetermined individual on every notecard.
  3. Place notecards in hat: Collect the notecards from the audience members and place them in a hat or container.
  4. Write prediction on chalkboard: Write down your prediction (the name of your guest of honor) on the chalkboard or whiteboard.
  5. Select a name: Have a volunteer draw a name from the hat.
  6. Match prediction: The name drawn from the hat will match the prediction you wrote on the chalkboard, creating the illusion of your ability to predict the luckiest person in the audience.

Method 3: Pick a Card

Materials:

  • A deck of cards
  • A card box with a hole cut in the back

Steps:

  1. Prepare the card box: Before the performance, cut a small hole in the back of a card box and tuck it into your pocket.
  2. Have the volunteer shuffle deck: Request a volunteer to shuffle the deck of cards thoroughly.
  3. Select a card: Instruct the volunteer to select a card from the deck and place it secretly at the bottom of the deck.
  4. Place cards in box: Have the volunteer place the deck of cards into the box, with the hole side facing you.
  5. "Read" the volunteer's mind: Pretend to use your psychic powers or observation skills to determine the card the volunteer selected.
  6. Reveal the card: Carefully pull out the bottom card from the box, hiding the hole from the audience. Show the card to the volunteer and reveal your "mind-reading" abilities.

Method 4: The Dictionary Trick

Materials:

  • A dictionary
  • A slip of paper

Steps:

  1. Prepare the hidden word: Before the performance, write down the 9th word on page 108 of a dictionary on a slip of paper and hide it.
  2. Ask for a number: Request a volunteer to call out a three-digit number.
  3. Perform calculations: Guide the volunteer through a series of calculations, such as multiplying the number by 3, adding 8, multiplying by 2, and subtracting 5, so that the result is always 1089.
  4. Find the word: Have another volunteer look up the 9th word on page 108 of the dictionary. This word will match the hidden word you wrote on the slip of paper, creating the illusion that you could predict the dictionary word based on the volunteer's number.

Method 5: Guessing a Volunteer's Thoughts

Materials:

  • A volunteer

Steps:

  1. Ask volunteer to think of a number: Request a volunteer to think of a number between 1 and 5 in their mind.
  2. Psychological prompts: Guide the volunteer through a series of psychological prompts, such as asking them to imagine the number written in their favorite color, surrounded by a specific shape, or associated with a particular object.
  3. Reveal the answer: Most people will end up thinking of "an orange kangaroo in Denmark" as the final answer. Pretend to use your psychic abilities or intuition to reveal this answer, demonstrating your ability to delve into their subconscious thoughts.
