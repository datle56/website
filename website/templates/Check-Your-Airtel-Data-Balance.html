How to Check Your Airtel Data Balance

As an Airtel subscriber, keeping track of your data balance is crucial for managing your usage and avoiding unexpected charges. Here are the various methods you can use to check your data balance:

Mobile App Method

Step 1: Download the Airtel Thanks App

  • Visit the Google Play Store or Apple App Store and search for "Airtel Thanks."
  • Download and install the app on your smartphone.

Step 2: Sign In to Your Account

  • Open the Airtel Thanks app and sign in using your Airtel mobile number.
  • Enter the OTP you receive via SMS to verify your account.

Step 3: View Your Data Balance

  • Once signed in, you will be taken to the app's dashboard.
  • Your data balance will be prominently displayed on the dashboard.

USSD Code Method

Step 1: Dial the USSD Code

  • Dial *121# from your Airtel mobile number and press call.
  • The USSD menu will appear on your screen.

Step 2: Select Option 5

  • Select option 5 from the USSD menu to check balance.

Step 3: Choose Option 1

  • From the next menu, choose option 1 to view your data balance.
  • Your data balance will be displayed on your screen.

Website Method

Step 1: Visit the Airtel Website

  • Open a web browser and navigate to https://www.airtel.in/.

Step 2: Log In to Your Account

  • Click on the "Login" button located in the top-right corner of the webpage.
  • Enter your Airtel mobile number and password to sign in to your account.

Step 3: Select Your Account

  • If you have multiple Airtel accounts, select the account for which you want to check the data balance.

Step 4: View Your Data Balance

  • Under "Account Overview," you will find your data balance displayed.

Broadband Data Usage Check

Step 1: Visit the SmartByte Portal

  • Navigate to https://www.airtel.in/smartbyte-s/page.html in a web browser.

Step 2: Log In (If Necessary)

  • If you are not already logged in, enter your Airtel Broadband ID and password to access the portal.

Step 3: Click on "Broadband"

  • On the SmartByte portal, click on the "Broadband" tab.

Step 4: View Your Data Usage

  • The bar at the top of the page will display your current data usage.
  • You can also view your usage history and track your data consumption over time.

Conclusion:

Checking your Airtel data balance is a simple and convenient process, regardless of the method you choose. Whether you prefer to use the mobile app, the USSD code, or the website, you can easily access your data usage information within minutes. By staying informed about your data balance, you can avoid overages and ensure that you have enough data to stay connected and productive.
