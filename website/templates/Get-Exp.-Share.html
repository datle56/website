Unveiling the Best Strategies: Maximize Your EXP. Share in 9 Simple Steps


Unlocking the Power of EXP. Share: A Comprehensive Guide

In the vast world of Pokémon, meticulous trainers seek every advantage to enhance their team's growth. One such tool is the elusive EXP. Share, an item that grants every Pokémon in your party a share of the experience points earned in battle. But how do you get your hands on this coveted item? Join us on an extraordinary quest as we unveil the secrets to obtaining the EXP. Share in every major Pokémon game.


How to Get the Exp. Share in Pokémon Games:

Red, Blue, Yellow:

  1. Capture at least 50 different Pokémon, excluding those obtained through trading.
  2. Head to the tower near Fuschia City, where the Seafoam Islands are located.
  3. Ascend to the top of the tower and interact with the scientist inside.
  4. The scientist will acknowledge your achievement and present you with the Exp. Share.

Gold, Silver, Crystal:

  1. Make your way to the Lake of Rage, northeast of Goldenrod City.
  2. Engage in battle with the formidable Red Gyarados that resides in the lake. Defeat or capture it.
  3. Obtain the Red Scale from the defeated or captured Red Gyarados.
  4. Travel to Goldenrod City and enter the Pokémon Center.
  5. Approach Mr. Pokémon, who is located near the Poké Mart.
  6. Trade the Red Scale to Mr. Pokémon in exchange for the Exp. Share.

Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald:

  1. In Verdanturf Town, locate the mailbox outside the Pokémon Center.
  2. Obtain a letter from the mailbox addressed to Professor Birch.
  3. Embark on a journey to Slateport City and enter the Devon Corporation building.
  4. Deliver the letter from Professor Birch to Steven Stone on the second floor.
  5. Return to Verdanturf Town and interact with Mr. Stone outside the Devon Corporation building.
  6. Mr. Stone will reward you with the Exp. Share for completing the errand.

FireRed, LeafGreen:

  1. Follow the same steps outlined in Red, Blue, Yellow.
  2. Upon capturing 50 different Pokémon, return to the tower near Fuschia City.
  3. The scientist at the top of the tower will acknowledge your achievement and grant you the Exp. Share.

Diamond, Pearl, Platinum:

  1. Explore the Sinnoh region and encounter at least 30 unique Pokémon species.
  2. Head to Route 209, which connects Oreburgh City to Floaroma Town.
  3. Locate the Pokétch Company building near the bike trail.
  4. Enter the building and approach Lucas's or Dawn's father, who is standing behind the counter.
  5. Lucas's or Dawn's father will present you with the Exp. Share after recognizing your Pokémon knowledge.

HeartGold, SoulSilver:

  1. Repeat the steps outlined in Gold, Silver, Crystal.
  2. Engage in battle with the Red Gyarados at the Lake of Rage and obtain the Red Scale.
  3. Trade the Red Scale to Mr. Pokémon in Goldenrod City for the Exp. Share.

Black, White:

  1. Option 1: Participate in battles at the Battle Company in Castelia City.
  2. Option 2: Join the Pokémon Fan Club in Opelucid City and level up your Pokémon to levels 25-49.

Black 2, White 2:

  1. Option 1: Repeat the steps for Black and White at the Battle Company or Pokémon Fan Club.
  2. Option 2: After defeating the previous boss, you can obtain the Exp. Share from them.

X, Y:

  1. Progress through the game until you reach Lumiose City.
  2. Engage in a battle with the Gym Leader at the Lumiose Gym and emerge victorious.
  3. The Gym Leader's sister, located outside the gym, will bestow upon you the Exp. Share.

Conclusion:

Mastering the art of acquiring the coveted Exp. Share in Pokémon games is crucial for leveling up your team efficiently. Whether you're an avid collector like Professor Oak or an aspiring Pokémon League Champion, these detailed instructions will guide you on your quest. Remember to refer to this comprehensive guide whenever the hunt for the Exp. Share arises, as it holds the key to unlocking your Pokémon's full potential. By following these steps, you'll ensure that your team stays one step ahead in the never-ending battle for supremacy in the Pokémon world.
