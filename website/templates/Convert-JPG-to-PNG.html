How to Convert JPG to PNG: A Comprehensive Guide

Why Convert JPG to PNG?

JPEG (JPG) and PNG are two widely used image formats. While JPG is known for its compression capabilities, resulting in smaller file sizes, PNG offers higher image quality and supports transparency. Converting JPG to PNG is beneficial in situations where image quality and transparency are crucial.

Convert JPG to PNG on Windows

Method 1: Using Photos App

  1. Open the JPG image using the Photos app.
  2. Click on the three dots in the top-right corner and select "Save as."
  3. In the "Save File" dialog box, navigate to the desired save location.
  4. In the "Save as type" dropdown menu, select "PNG (*.png)."
  5. Click the "Save" button.

Convert JPG to PNG on Mac

Method 1: Using Preview

  1. Open the JPG image using the Preview app.
  2. Click on the "File" menu and select "Export."
  3. In the "Export" dialog box, select "PNG" from the "Format" dropdown menu.
  4. Choose a save location and click the "Save" button.

Convert JPG to PNG Online

Method 1: Adobe Express

  1. Visit the Adobe Express website (https://express.adobe.com/).
  2. Click on the "Tools" menu and select "Convert to PNG."
  3. Click on "Upload Image" and select your JPG image.
  4. Once the image is uploaded, click on the "Convert" button.
  5. Click on the "Download" button to save the PNG file.

Method 2: Online Converter

  1. Visit a reputable online image converter website (e.g., https://jpg2png.com/).
  2. Click on the "Upload Files" button and select your JPG image.
  3. Click on the "Convert" or "Start" button.
  4. Once the conversion is complete, click on the "Download" button to save the PNG file.

Tips for Converting JPG to PNG

  • Preserve Image Quality: PNG files support lossless compression, ensuring that the image quality remains intact during conversion.
  • File Size Considerations: PNG files are typically larger in size than JPG files because of their higher quality and transparency capabilities.
  • Bulk Conversion: Many online converters and desktop applications allow you to convert multiple JPG files to PNG files simultaneously.
  • Transparency: PNG supports transparency, allowing you to create images with transparent backgrounds.
  • Compatibility: PNG is a widely supported image format compatible with most image editors, web browsers, and operating systems.
