Using a NutriBullet: A Comprehensive Guide

Step 1: Chop Ingredients for Seamless Blending

Prepare your ingredients by chopping them into small pieces to ensure easier and more efficient blending. This step is particularly important for tough ingredients like fibrous fruits, leafy greens, and nuts. Smaller pieces allow the blades to cut through more easily, resulting in a smoother blend.

Step 2: Fill Cup to the Maximum Fill Line

Insert the chosen ingredients into the NutriBullet cup, filling it to the designated "Max Fill" line. This line indicates the optimal volume for the blender and prevents ingredients from overflowing while blending, ensuring a mess-free experience.

Step 3: Select the Appropriate Blade

The NutriBullet system offers two distinct blade options:

  • Extractor Blade: Designed for blending liquids, such as smoothies, juices, and soups. The extractor blade is ideal for creating smooth and velvety textures.
  • Milling Blade: Used for grinding solid ingredients into powders, including nut butters, oat flour, and spices. The milling blade effortlessly breaks down materials into fine, consistent textures.

Step 4: Securely Attach the Blade

Screw the chosen blade securely onto the bottom of the cup. Ensure that the blade is firmly attached to prevent any leaks or spills during operation.

Step 5: Initiate Blending

Place the cup assembly onto the NutriBullet Power Base. As the cup is pressed down, the blender will automatically start operating. The motion of the blades quickly chops and mixes the ingredients, creating a vortex that incorporates all ingredients into the blend.

Step 6: Dislodge Stuck Ingredients (Optional)

If necessary, gently tap or shake the cup while blending to dislodge any stubborn ingredients that may adhere to the walls. This action helps ensure that all ingredients are evenly blended and prevents clumping.

Step 7: Detach Cup and Blade

Once the desired consistency is achieved, remove the cup from the Power Base. Unscrew the blade from the bottom of the cup, taking care not to spill any remaining mixture.

Step 8: Serve or Pour

Pour the blended mixture into a desired container, such as a glass or bowl, or enjoy it directly from the NutriBullet cup. The versatile design allows for both serving and consumption from the same vessel.

Cleaning a NutriBullet: Maintenance for Optimal Performance

Step 1: Dishwasher-Safe Cup and Lid

The NutriBullet cup and lid are conveniently dishwasher-safe. Place them on the top rack of your dishwasher for easy and thorough cleaning.

Step 2: Hand-Wash Blades

To preserve the sharp edges of the blades, hand-wash them with warm, soapy water immediately after each use. Avoid using abrasive sponges or scouring pads, as these can damage the blades.

Step 3: Wipe Down Power Base

Use a clean, damp rag to wipe down the exterior of the Power Base after each use. This helps remove any food residue or splatters, keeping the base clean and hygienic.

Step 4: Remove Stubborn Residue (Optional)

If stubborn residue remains after hand-washing, blend a mixture of warm soapy water in the cup. This will help loosen and flush out any stuck-on debris, restoring the blades and cup to their original condition.
