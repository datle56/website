Remodeling Your Bathroom: A Step-by-Step Guide to Bathtub Removal and Installation

Tired of your outdated or damaged bathtub? Embark on a transformative journey to upgrade your bathroom's style and functionality with this comprehensive guide to bathtub removal and installation. Whether you're a seasoned DIY enthusiast or prefer professional assistance, this step-by-step tutorial will empower you to achieve a beautiful and rejuvenated bathroom.

From meticulously disconnecting the old bathtub to securely installing its sleek replacement, every step is meticulously explained with crystal-clear instructions and helpful tips. Dive into the world of home improvement and witness the transformative power of replacing your bathtub. Get ready to unveil a bathroom that exudes comfort, style, and timeless elegance.


Removing the Old Bathtub

1. Shut Off Water Supply

  • Locate the water supply valves for both hot and cold water under the sink or in a nearby utility closet.
  • Close the valves completely to prevent water from flowing into the bathtub.

2. Remove Accessories

  • Unscrew the faucet handles with an adjustable wrench or Allen key.
  • Remove the drain stopper with a drain wrench or pliers.
  • Detach the showerhead and hose from the faucet.

3. Cut Out Bathtub Surround and Wall

  • Use a utility knife or reciprocating saw to carefully cut around the perimeter of the bathtub surround.
  • Remove the surround by gently prying it away from the wall.
  • Behind the surround, cut out any drywall that extends beyond the edge of the bathtub.

4. Access Plumbing

  • Determine the location of the plumbing pipes, typically behind the tub wall or under the floor.
  • If necessary, cut a small hole in the wall or floor to access the pipes.

5. Disconnect Drains and Remove Bathtub

  • Disconnect the sink and tub drains from the plumbing pipes.
  • Carefully lift the bathtub out of the space, using a pry bar if needed.

Installing the New Bathtub

1. Choose a Suitable Bathtub

  • Select a bathtub that fits the dimensions of the space and matches the desired style.
  • Check the weight capacity and material composition to ensure suitability.

2. Position and Level Bathtub

  • Place the bathtub in the designated area and adjust its position until it is level.
  • Use a level to check both sides and ensure the tub is stable.

3. Install Ledger Board

  • Attach a ledger board to the wall studs at the top of the bathtub to support its weight.
  • Screw the ledger board securely into the studs.

4. Install Drain

  • Connect the drain pipe to the bathtub drain hole.
  • Install the overflow drain, ensuring it fits snugly.

5. Fit Bathtub Surround

  • Position the new bathtub surround around the edges of the tub.
  • Secure the surround to the studs with screws.

Securing the Tub and Surround

1. Secure Bathtub to Studs

  • Drill pilot holes through the bathtub flange into the wall studs.
  • Insert bolts through the holes and tighten them securely to anchor the tub to the wall.

2. Caulk and Secure Surround

  • Apply a bead of caulk along the seam between the bathtub and surround.
  • Secure the surround to the wall studs with additional screws.

3. Connect Water Lines and Accessories

  • Reconnect the water supply lines to the faucet and shower valve.
  • Reinstall the faucet handles, drain stopper, and showerhead.

4. Hook Up Drain to P-Trap

  • Install a new P-trap, if necessary, and connect the drain pipe to the P-trap.

5. Caulk Tub to Floor

  • Apply a bead of caulk around the perimeter of the bathtub where it meets the floor.
  • Smooth the caulk with a caulk tool.

6. Wait for Sealants to Dry

  • Allow all caulking and adhesive to dry completely for 24 hours before using the bathtub to prevent leaks.

Congratulations! You have successfully removed your old bathtub and installed a brand-new one. Remember to follow the instructions carefully and take precautions to ensure safety throughout the process. Enjoy your new and refreshed bathing space!
