How to View Old Facebook Stories

On Your Phone:

Step 1: Access Your Profile

Open the Facebook app on your mobile device. At the bottom of the screen, tap on your profile picture's icon to access your profile page.

Step 2: Locate the Archive Tab

Scroll down your profile page and tap on the "See All" tab below the "Stories" section. This will display all of your current and past stories.

Step 3: Enter the Story Archive

At the top of the "See All" page, tap on the "Archive & Story Archive" button. This will take you to the Story Archive, where you can view your older stories.

Step 4: Browse and Select a Story

Within the Story Archive, you'll find a list of all your past stories. These stories are categorized by date, making it easy to locate the one you wish to view. Tap on the story you want to watch.

On Your Computer:

Step 1: Log In to Facebook

Using your web browser, navigate to facebook.com and log in to your account.

Step 2: Access Your Profile

Once logged in, hover over your profile picture located in the top right corner of the Facebook window. A drop-down menu will appear.

Step 3: Locate the Story Archive

Within the drop-down menu, select the "Story Archive" option. This will take you to the Story Archive page.

Step 4: Browse and Select a Story

On the Story Archive page, you'll find a grid of your past stories. Each story is labeled with the date it was created. Click on the story you want to watch.

Note:

It's important to keep in mind that you can only view your own stories using these methods. Additionally, you can only view stories that are over 24 hours old. Stories older than 24 hours are automatically archived and can be accessed through the Story Archive feature.
