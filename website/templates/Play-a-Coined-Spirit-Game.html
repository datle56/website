Unlock the Secrets of the Mystical Talking Board

From the depths of ancient lore and the realms of enigmatic spirits, the talking board beckons, whispering promises of communication with the unknown. Prepare for an unforgettable journey as we delve into the art of crafting this mystical tool and unravel the secrets of its enigmatic workings.

Gather your materials like a seasoned sorcerer—a large sheet of parchment, a pen dipped in the ink of curiosity, and a coin imbued with the essence of destiny. Let your pen dance across the paper, etching the alphabet along the edges, inviting each letter to participate in this extraordinary ritual. In the heart of the parchment, inscribe the numbers 0-9, the silent guardians of hidden messages.

But our canvas demands more than mere symbols. Let us summon the words of power—"YES," "NO," "START," and "END"—and bestow them upon the parchment. These ethereal incantations will guide our communication with the spirits that dwell beyond our mortal realm.


Creating the Talking Board

Embarking on a mystical adventure requires a handcrafted talking board that serves as a conduit between the ethereal and the tangible. To create this enigmatic artifact, gather the following materials:

  • A large, unlined sheet of paper (approximately 18 x 24 inches)
  • A black or dark-colored pen
  • A coin (preferably an old, weathered one for added authenticity)

Assembling the Board

  1. Alphabet's Embrace: Along the edges of the paper, meticulously write the entire alphabet (A-Z) in bold, clear lettering. This alphabetical border will guide the movement of the coin as it communicates with the unknown.

  2. Numerical Enigma: In the center of the paper, arrange numbers (0-9) in a logical grid pattern. These numbers will provide a deeper level of insight, allowing for more detailed responses.

  3. Words of Guidance: Enhance the board with additional words that facilitate communication and game play. These include "YES," "NO," "START," and "END." Place them strategically within the board's layout.

Playing the Game

The talking board is best played in a dimly lit room, where flickering candles cast an ethereal glow.

  1. Gather in Circle: Invite like-minded souls to join you in a circle, ensuring there is ample space for everyone to participate comfortably.

  2. Coin's Initiation: Place the chosen coin at the designated "START" position, the starting point for the coin's journey.

  3. Questioning the Unknown: Formulate questions that seek answers of a "yes" or "no" nature. These queries should be concise and clear, allowing the coin to move freely and accurately.

  4. Coin's Guided Movements: With gentle fingers, lightly touch the coin and ask your question. The coin will seemingly glide across the board, traversing letters and numbers to spell out the answers.

  5. End of the Session: To conclude the game, move the coin to the "END" position, bringing the communiqués to a close. This signals the cessation of communication with the unknown realm.


While the simplicity of the talking board makes it an intriguing pastime, it's essential to approach it with a healthy dose of skepticism. The movement of the coin is often attributed to unintentional muscle movements or the psychological phenomenon known as the ideomotor effect. Nevertheless, the allure of the talking board lies in its potential to spark curiosity, foster imagination, and provoke thoughtful conversations about the nature of communication and belief. Whether you choose to believe in its paranormal abilities or not, the talking board remains a captivating tool that can provide hours of entertainment and provoke both amusement and introspection.
