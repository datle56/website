Additional Tips:

Locating a Leak:

  • If you suspect a leak but can't pinpoint its location, spray soapy water on the components. Bubbles will form where there's a leak.

Checking Refrigerant Pressure in Your Air Conditioner:

  • Connect a pressure gauge to the low-pressure line port of your air conditioner. The reading should be within the specified range.

Recharging Your Air Conditioner:

  • Follow the instructions provided with the charging kit. Overcharging the system can damage it.
