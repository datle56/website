How to Reset a Garage Door Opener

Method 1: Reprogramming the Opener

  1. Initiate Programming Mode: Press and hold the "Program Set" button on the opener for 3-5 seconds until the circular LED light glows.
  2. Activate Learn Code (For Openers Manufactured Between 1995 and 2011): Press the "Learn Code" button repeatedly until the "Radio Signal" LED flashes purple.
  3. Program Remote Controls: Quickly press any rectangular button on your remote control. The oblong purple LED on the opener will illuminate. Repeat this step 2 more times to complete the reset.

Method 2: Resetting the Wireless Keypad

  1. Enter Programming Mode: Simultaneously press and hold the "Program" and up/down buttons on the keypad for 5 seconds. The keypad LED will flash twice and then turn off.
  2. Sync with Opener: Press the "Program Set" button on the opener for 3 seconds.
  3. Enter Keypad Code: On the keypad, enter the sequence "357."
  4. Test Operation: Press the up/down button 3 times to open the garage door.
