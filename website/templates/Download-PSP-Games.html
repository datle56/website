Step 1: Preparing Your PSP

Updating Firmware

  • Connect your PSP to a stable Wi-Fi network.
  • Navigate to the PSP's "Settings" menu.
  • Select "System Settings" and then "System Update."
  • Follow the on-screen instructions to download and install the latest firmware (6.61).

Installing Custom Firmware

  • Download the custom firmware specifically designed for PSP 6.61 from a reputable source.
  • Extract the downloaded firmware files to a USB drive.
  • Insert the USB drive into your PSP.
  • Run the "Custom Firmware Installer" from the PSP's Game menu.
  • Follow the on-screen instructions to install the custom firmware.

Running "Fast Recovery"

  • After each restart, select the "Recovery Mode" option from the PSP's Game menu.
  • Press the X button to run "Fast Recovery."
  • This ensures that the custom firmware is loaded correctly.

Step 3: Transferring Games to Your PSP

Using a Memory Stick Duo

  • Connect your PSP or Memory Stick Duo to your computer using the provided USB cable.
  • Create an "ISO" folder on the root directory of your PSP or Memory Stick Duo.
  • Extract the downloaded RAR files and transfer the extracted ISO files to the "ISO" folder.
  • Safely remove your PSP or eject your Memory Stick Duo from your computer.

Using an SD Card Adapter

If your PSP does not have a built-in Memory Stick Duo slot, you can use an SD card adapter to expand the storage space.

  • Insert your SD card into the SD card adapter.
  • Follow the same steps as transferring games to a Memory Stick Duo.

Step 4: Launching Downloaded Games

  • Select the "Game" menu on your PSP.
  • Navigate to the "Memory Stick" option.
  • Locate the game you downloaded from the list.
  • Press the X button to launch the game.

Tips:

  • Use a high-capacity Memory Stick Duo or SD card to store numerous games.
  • Verify that the websites you download ROMs from are trustworthy to avoid malware infection.
  • Always scan downloaded files for viruses before transferring them to your PSP.

Warnings:

  • Downloading copyrighted games illegally may violate the law in your region.
  • Installing custom firmware on newer PSP models (3000 and PSP Go) can potentially brick your system, rendering it unusable.
