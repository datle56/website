Banking Rules

  • The electronic banking unit facilitates transactions using buttons and symbols.
  • "M" denotes million, while "K" represents thousand.
  • The "C" button conveniently cancels actions or resets balances.
  • An arrow button located on the unit dispenses $2 million into a player's account upon passing "GO."
  • Debit and credit cards can be inserted into designated slots to add or subtract funds from a player's balance.

Basic Gameplay

  • Players select their respective tokens and roll dice to establish the starting order.
  • Movement around the board is determined by the outcome of dice rolls.
  • Upon landing on certain spaces, players may be required to pay rent, draw community chest cards, or contribute taxes.
  • Completion of a full lap around the board rewards players with a $2 million bonus.

Acquiring and Trading Properties

  • Players can purchase properties to establish monopolies and collect rent from other players passing through.
  • Utilities and airports present opportunities to collect fees from players landing on those spaces.
  • Properties and houses can be sold for cash to generate income.

Developing Properties

  • Monopolies enable players to construct houses on their properties.
  • Exchanging four houses of the same color group allows players to upgrade to a hotel on the corresponding property.

Winning and Losing

  • Mortgaging properties provides players with temporary liquidity, but they forgo the ability to collect rent on those properties while they remain mortgaged.
  • Bankruptcy occurs when a player owes more money than they possess.
  • The final player remaining in the game is declared the victor.
