How to Find and Catch Feebas in Pokémon Emerald: A Comprehensive Guide

Introduction

Feebas, the enigmatic Fish Pokémon, is a unique and highly sought-after creature in the Pokémon world. Exclusive to Pokémon Emerald, this Water-type Pokémon is not only elusive but also boasts the potential to evolve into the elegant and graceful Milotic. This guide will delve into the intricacies of finding and capturing Feebas, providing you with all the necessary knowledge and techniques to enhance your chances of success.

Prerequisites

Before embarking on your Feebas-catching expedition, ensure that you have the following prerequisites:

  • Copy of Pokémon Emerald: This guide is specific to Pokémon Emerald, as Feebas cannot be found in other generations.
  • Fishing Rod: Any type of fishing rod (Old, Good, or Super) will suffice.
  • Potion or Super Potion: These items can be used to heal your Pokémon if they get into trouble during the encounter.
  • Patience and Determination: Feebas is a rare Pokémon, so be prepared to invest time and effort into the search.

Method

1. Locate the Fishing Spot

Feebas can only be found in the river that runs through Route 119. Head to the eastern end of the route and locate the body of water.

2. Equip Your Fishing Rod

Equip your chosen fishing rod. Any type will do, as Feebas has no specific rod preference.

3. Fish Every Tile

Using the "Surf" ability or standing by the river's edge, systematically fish every water tile in the river. Feebas only spawns in 6 random tiles, so it is crucial to cover every possible spot.

4. Fish Multiple Times Per Tile

Even though Feebas has a relatively high catch rate, it is possible to miss it on the first attempt. To increase your chances, fish multiple times in each tile. This persistence will help you maximize your likelihood of encountering the elusive Pokémon.

5. Move Back and Forth

Do not stay in one spot while fishing. Instead, move back and forth along the river section by section, ensuring that you cover all areas thoroughly. This technique will prevent you from missing any potential Feebas spawns.

6. Catch Feebas

When you finally encounter Feebas, use any type of Poké Ball to capture it. Feebas has a high catch rate, so it is unlikely that you will need to use any special strategies or items.

7. Catch Multiple Feebas

If you desire multiple Feebas or its evolved form Milotic, take advantage of the opportunity while you are in the area. Continue fishing until you have obtained the desired number of encounters.

Feebas evolves into Milotic when its Beauty stat reaches 170. To increase its Beauty, feed it Poffins or Pokéblocks that contain blue Berries. Additionally, the number of contest ribbons Feebas earns will also contribute to its Beauty.
