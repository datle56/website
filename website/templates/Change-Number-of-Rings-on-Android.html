Discover the Simple Guide with Step-by-Step Pictures: How to Customize Ring Count on Your Android Device


Unlock the Secret to Customizing Your Call Duration!

Tired of missing important calls because they go straight to voicemail too quickly? We've got you covered with two foolproof methods to extend the ring time on your Android device. Dive right in and say goodbye to missed opportunities!


Method 1: Using a Code

Step 1: Open the Phone App

  • Locate and tap the Phone icon on your Android device's home screen, in the app drawer, or in the notification bar.

Step 2: Dial the Service Code

  • Once in the Phone app, tap the dial pad icon.
  • Enter the code *#61# and press the green call button.

Step 3: Note the Divert Number

  • After dialing the service code, you will see a message on the screen that displays a phone number.
  • This number is the "call divert" number, which is where all your calls will be forwarded after the specified number of seconds.
  • Write down or memorize this number.

Step 4: Set the Diversion Time

  • Return to the dial pad and enter the following code: 61phonenumber*seconds#
  • Replace "phonenumber" with the divert number you noted earlier.
  • Replace "seconds" with the desired number of seconds before the call goes to voicemail (between 5 and 30).
  • For example, to set a 15-second delay, you would enter 611234567890*15#.

Step 5: Confirm the Setting

  • Once you have entered the code, press the green call button.
  • You will receive a message confirming that the call forwarding settings have been updated.

Method 2: Changing the Ringtone

Step 1: Access the Settings

  • On your Android device, swipe down from the top of the screen to access the notification panel.
  • Tap the gear icon to open the Settings menu.

Step 2: Navigate to Sound Settings

  • Scroll down and tap on the "Sounds" or "Sounds and vibration" option.

Step 3: Select Ringtone

  • Within the Sounds menu, tap on the "Ringtone" or "Phone ringtone" option.

Step 4: Choose a Longer Ringtone

  • Browse the list of available ringtones and select one that has a longer duration.
  • This will provide you with more time to hear the phone ringing before it is sent to voicemail.

Step 5: Save Changes

  • Once you have selected a new ringtone, tap the "Save" button or press the back button to apply your changes.

Tips:

  • Consider Custom Ringtones: If you want a truly unique ringtone, consider downloading free ringtones from online sources such as Zedge or Mobile9.
  • Unrelated to Ring Delay: Changing the ringtone does not directly impact the delay before a call goes to voicemail. It only affects the sound that plays during the ringing period.

Conclusion:

By implementing the methods outlined above, you can effectively adjust the timing of your voicemail settings to suit your preferences. Whether you prefer to use a specific code or opt for a longer ringtone, these techniques empower you to customize your call forwarding intervals and ensure that you never miss an important call again. So, whether you're a business professional managing multiple lines or a personal user seeking to optimize your communication experience, take advantage of these tips and tailor your voicemail settings to your unique needs.
