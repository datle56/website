Easy Tips to Thicken Runny Homemade Slime

Creating your own slime can be a fun and satisfying activity, but what happens when your slime turns out too runny? Don't worry! With a few simple tricks, you can easily thicken your slime and get the perfect texture.

Understanding Slime Consistency

Slime is a non-Newtonian fluid, which means its viscosity changes depending on the force applied to it. When you apply pressure, such as by kneading or poking it, slime thickens and becomes more solid-like. When the pressure is released, slime returns to its liquid state.

Thickening Runny Slime

Using Thickening Agents:

  • Clear glue: Clear glue is an excellent thickener for slime made with liquid starch. Add 1/4 cup of clear glue and knead for 2-3 minutes. The glue will help bind the molecules together, making the slime thicker.
  • Baking soda: Baking soda can thicken slime made with saline solution or clear glue. For slime with saline solution, add 1/4 teaspoon of baking soda. For clear glue slime, add 1/2 teaspoon. Baking soda neutralizes the acidity of the ingredients, allowing the slime to form stronger bonds.
  • Cornstarch: Cornstarch is a natural thickener that works well with cornstarch-based slime. Stir in 1/2 teaspoon of cornstarch for cornstarch-only slime. For cornstarch and glue slime, add 1 teaspoon. Cornstarch absorbs excess moisture, resulting in a firmer slime.
  • Shaving cream: Shaving cream can add volume and thickness to fluffy slime. Add a small handful of shaving cream and knead it into the slime until the desired consistency is achieved. Shaving cream contains surfactants that help create a foam, which contributes to the slime's thickness.
  • Borax: Borax is a powerful thickener that should be used cautiously and only with Borax slime. Mix in 1/4 tablespoon of borax at a time and knead until the slime reaches the desired thickness. Borax forms cross-links between the slime molecules, making it stronger and less runny.

Alternative Solutions:

  • Drain excess liquid: If your slime contains excess liquid, carefully pour it out of the container. This will remove the extra moisture and make the slime thicker.
  • Knead for 5 minutes: For slime that contains glue, kneading can help activate the bonding properties of the glue. Knead the slime for 5 minutes or until it reaches the desired consistency. Kneading helps the glue molecules to cross-link and form a stronger network.
  • Freeze shampoo slime: If you're working with shampoo slime, placing it in a sealed container and freezing it for 5-10 minutes can also thicken it. The cold temperature lowers the kinetic energy of the molecules, allowing them to form stronger bonds and become less fluid.

Remember:

  • Always test a small amount of the thickener first to avoid making the slime too thick.
  • If the slime is still too runny after adding a thickener, you can repeat the process until you achieve the desired consistency.
  • If you over-thicken the slime, you can add a small amount of water or slime activator to restore it to its preferred texture.

In conclusion, these easy and effective tips will help you adjust the consistency of your homemade slime to achieve the desired thickness. Whether you're using clear glue, saline solution, cornstarch, or borax, there's a simple solution that will revitalize your runny slime and bring joy back to your crafting experience.
