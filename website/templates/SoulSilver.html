How to Catch All 7 Eevee Evolutions in Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver

Eevee, the enigmatic Pokémon known for its diverse evolutionary capabilities, possesses an impressive seven unique evolutions, each with a distinct method of attainment. In the realm of Pokémon HeartGold and SoulSilver, acquiring all these evolutionary marvels is an enchanting adventure that requires careful planning and execution.

1. Fire Stone Evolutions: Flareon

  • Requirement: Fire Stone
  • Obtaining the Fire Stone: Visit Bill's grandfather in Goldenrod City. He will gift you the Fire Stone once you defeat the eighth Gym Leader.

2. Water Stone Evolutions: Vaporeon

  • Requirement: Water Stone

  • Obtaining the Water Stone:

    • Receive it from Bill's grandfather in Goldenrod City after defeating the third Gym Leader.
    • Participate in the Pokéathlon Dome's Tri Match and earn enough points to exchange for a Water Stone.

3. Thunder Stone Evolutions: Jolteon

  • Requirement: Thunder Stone

  • Obtaining the Thunder Stone:

    • Receive it from a Pichu in the Sprout Tower if your friendship with Pichu is high enough.
    • Participate in the Pokéathlon Dome's Tri Match and earn enough points to exchange for a Thunder Stone.

4. Friendship-Based Evolutions: Espeon and Umbreon

  • Requirement: High friendship and level up at the right time of day

  • Raising Friendship:

    • Battle with Eevee in your party.
    • Keep Eevee in your party as your walking Pokémon.
    • Feed Eevee berries to increase its happiness.
    • Take Eevee to the National Park for a relaxing stroll.

  • Evolution:

    • Espeon: Level up Eevee with high friendship during the daytime.
    • Umbreon: Level up Eevee with high friendship at night.

5. Trade-Based Evolutions: Leafeon and Glaceon

  • Requirement: Trade to Pokémon Diamond/Pearl/Platinum and level up near the respective rock

  • Trading:

    • Transfer Eevee to a copy of Pokémon Diamond, Pearl, or Platinum.
    • Trade Eevee back to your copy of HeartGold/SoulSilver.

  • Evolution:

    • Leafeon: Level up Eevee near the Moss Rock in Eterna Forest.
    • Glaceon: Level up Eevee near the Ice Rock on Route 217.

6. Post-Trade Evolutions:

After you have traded Eevee back to HeartGold/SoulSilver, it will retain its evolved form. You can now continue to evolve it further if desired.
