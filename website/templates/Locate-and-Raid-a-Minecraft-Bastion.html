Embark on a Minecraft Bastion Expedition: A Step-by-Step Guide with Visual Aids


Embark on a thrilling adventure into the perilous depths of the Nether, where ancient Bastion Remnants hold untold treasures and formidable foes. Step into this treacherous realm armed with your sharpest tools, enchanted weapons, and unwavering courage.

Prepare for a treacherous journey through sprawling Nether landscapes, avoiding the scorching heat of Basalt Deltas. Forge your path towards grand structures known as Bastion Remnants, each boasting unique designs and tantalizing rewards. But beware, for these ancient ruins are not without their protectors – the fearsome Piglins.


Part 1: Finding a Bastion Remnant

  1. Equip Yourself Adequately: Don robust armor, preferably enchanted, to withstand the perils of the Nether. Carry sharp tools for excavation and formidable weapons to defend against hostile mobs. Enchantments can significantly enhance your gear's effectiveness.

  2. Enter the Nether: Craft a Nether Portal or locate an existing one to transport yourself to the crimson realm. Remember to equip gold armor to avoid provoking Piglins, the aggressive natives of this hostile dimension.

  3. Avoid Basalt Deltas: While exploring, steer clear of Basalt Deltas, as these desolate biomes are devoid of bastions. Instead, focus your search on more promising locations.

  4. Types of Bastions: Bastion Remnants come in various forms, each with its distinct features and potential rewards.

    • Hoglin Stable Bastion: Characterized by stable-like structures, these bastions offer limited loot.

    • Housing Bastion: Featuring a central courtyard adorned with Nether Wart, these bastions may harbor valuable items within their humble abodes.

    • Bridge Bastion: Recognizable by its golden bridge, this bastion offers a substantial haul of up to 24 gold blocks.

    • Treasure Room Bastion: These impressive structures consist of two connected compartments joined by a bridge, concealing a treasure trove of precious loot.

Part 2: Exploring a Bastion Remnant

  1. Loot Outer Buildings: Commence your exploration by thoroughly looting the exterior buildings of the bastion remnant. Piglins often conceal their treasures within chests hidden among the structures.

  2. Use Hoppers for Stealthy Looting: Employ hoppers to extract loot from chests discreetly. This tactic prevents alerting nearby Piglins and drawing their wrath upon you.

  3. Subdue Piglins with Lava: If Piglins become overly aggressive, lure them into lava pits or douse them with lava using a bucket. This method effectively eliminates them without attracting further attention.

  4. Mine Gold Blocks with Caution: Exercise caution when mining gold blocks, as this action can provoke Piglins into attacking.

  5. Treasure Room Exploration: Venture into the heart of the bastion remnant – the main treasure room – and plunder the riches it holds. Numerous chests await your eager hands, laden with coveted loot.

  6. Fire Resistance and Magma Cube Spawner: Prepare yourself with fire resistance potions, as you may encounter lava hazards during your exploration. Locate and destroy the Magma Cube spawner at the base of the structure to prevent these hostile entities from spawning.

  7. Harvest Central Gold Blocks and Defend: Gather the substantial gold blocks located centrally within the treasure room. Be prepared to fend off any Piglins that may challenge your claim to this precious treasure.

  8. Escape with Grace: Once your exploration is complete, consider utilizing an elytra and fireworks for a swift and stylish escape. This aerial liberation will allow you to soar above the perils of the Nether and return to the safety of the Overworld.


Conclusion: Unveiling the Nether's Hidden Treasures

Venturing into the depths of the Nether to explore bastion remnants is an exhilarating experience filled with challenges and rewards. Remember to prepare thoroughly, donning sturdy armor and packing enchanted gear. As you navigate the treacherous landscape, keep an eye out for the various types of bastions, each offering unique loot and hazards.

Approaching bastions with caution is crucial to avoid provoking the Piglin inhabitants. Employ clever tactics such as using hoppers to access chests and utilizing lava to discreetly eliminate aggressive Piglins. The key is remaining patient and observant, as the rewards that await within the treasure rooms are well worth the effort.

Once within the main chamber, take advantage of the short window before Piglins swarm to seize the loot. Defeating the Magma Cube spawner and mining the central gold blocks will earn you substantial rewards. As you prepare to escape, don your elytra and light up the fireworks, leaving the perilous confines of the bastion behind with a treasure trove of Nether riches.
