Indulge in a Whipped Wonder: Easy Cool Whip Frosting Recipes

Prepare to elevate your desserts with fluffy, flavorful frostings that are surprisingly easy to make. Cool Whip, a beloved dessert topping, transforms into a versatile base for a variety of delectable frostings. From classic to decadent, these recipes will add an irresistible touch to your cakes, cookies, and more.

Whether you prefer the simplicity of a classic frosting or crave the richness of cream cheese or the tangy sweetness of pineapple, we've got you covered. Dive into this collection of Cool Whip frosting recipes and let your culinary creativity soar!


Classic Cool Whip Frosting

Ingredients:

  • 1 (3.4-ounce) package instant pudding mix, any flavor
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 (8-ounce) container Cool Whip, thawed

Instructions:

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the pudding mix, milk, and vanilla extract until smooth.
  2. Gently fold in the thawed whipped topping until completely combined.
  3. Use immediately or refrigerate for up to 3 days before frosting cakes or cupcakes.

Powdered Sugar Cool Whip Frosting

Ingredients:

  • 1 (3.4-ounce) package instant pudding mix, any flavor
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 (8-ounce) container Cool Whip, thawed

Instructions:

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the pudding mix, milk, and powdered sugar until smooth.
  2. Gently fold in the thawed whipped topping until completely combined.
  3. Use immediately or refrigerate for up to 3 days before frosting cakes or cupcakes.

Cream Cheese Cool Whip Frosting

Ingredients:

  • 1 (8-ounce) package cream cheese, softened
  • 1 (8-ounce) container Cool Whip, thawed
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions:

  1. In a large bowl, beat the cream cheese until smooth.
  2. Add the Cool Whip, powdered sugar, and vanilla extract and beat until well combined.
  3. Use immediately or refrigerate for up to 3 days before frosting cakes or cupcakes.

Extra-Rich Cream Cheese Cool Whip Frosting

Ingredients:

  • 1 (8-ounce) package cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 (8-ounce) container Cool Whip, thawed

Instructions:

  1. In a large bowl, beat the cream cheese and butter until light and fluffy.
  2. Add the sugar and vanilla extract and beat until combined.
  3. Fold in the thawed whipped topping until well combined.
  4. Use immediately or refrigerate for up to 3 days before frosting cakes or cupcakes.

Pineapple Cool Whip Frosting

Ingredients:

  • 1 (8-ounce) container Cool Whip, thawed
  • 1 (3.4-ounce) package instant pineapple pudding mix
  • 1/2 cup drained canned pineapple, chopped

Instructions:

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the Cool Whip and pudding mix until smooth.
  2. Fold in the chopped pineapple until combined.
  3. Use immediately or refrigerate for up to 3 days before frosting cakes or cupcakes.

Strawberry Cool Whip Frosting

Ingredients:

  • 1 (8-ounce) container Cool Whip, thawed
  • 1 (3.4-ounce) package instant strawberry pudding mix
  • 1/2 cup fresh or frozen strawberries, liquified in a food processor

Instructions:

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the Cool Whip and pudding mix until smooth.
  2. Fold in the strawberry liquid until combined.
  3. Use immediately or refrigerate for up to 3 days before frosting cakes or cupcakes.

Peanut Butter Cool Whip Frosting

Ingredients:

  • 1 (8-ounce) container Cool Whip, thawed
  • 1 (3.4-ounce) package instant chocolate pudding mix
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar

Instructions:

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the Cool Whip and pudding mix until smooth.
  2. Add the peanut butter and sugar and beat until combined.
  3. Use immediately or refrigerate for up to 3 days before frosting cakes or cupcakes.
