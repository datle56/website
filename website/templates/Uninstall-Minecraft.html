How to Uninstall Minecraft

Minecraft, the beloved sandbox video game, can sometimes need to be uninstalled for various reasons, such as compatibility issues, storage space constraints, or simply a desire for a fresh start. Here's a comprehensive guide to uninstalling Minecraft from different operating systems and devices:

Windows

1. Back Up Saved Games (Optional)

Before uninstalling Minecraft, it's highly recommended to back up your saved worlds and other crucial files. Here's how:

  • Navigate to the Minecraft game folder in File Explorer: %appdata%.minecraft
  • Copy the "saves" folder and any other custom files or mod folders you want to preserve.
  • Paste the copied files to a safe location on your computer.

2. Uninstall Minecraft

  • Click on the Start menu and navigate to the Control Panel.
  • Under "Programs," select "Programs and Features."
  • Find Minecraft in the list of installed programs and click on it.
  • Click the "Uninstall" button and follow the on-screen instructions.

3. Delete Remaining Minecraft Folder

In some cases, Minecraft may not appear in the list of installed programs. To manually delete any remaining files:

  • Open the Run box by pressing Windows key + R.
  • Type %appdata% and hit Enter.
  • Locate and delete the ".minecraft" folder.

Mac OS X

1. Back Up Saved Games (Optional)

  • Open Finder and navigate to ~/Library/Application Support/.
  • Copy the ".minecraft" folder to a safe location on your Mac.

2. Uninstall Minecraft

  • Open the Launchpad and search for Minecraft.
  • Drag the Minecraft icon to the Trash.
  • Confirm the deletion when prompted.

3. Delete Remaining Minecraft Folder

  • Navigate to ~/Library/Application Support/ again.
  • If the ".minecraft" folder still exists, delete it.

Linux

1. Back Up Saved Games (Optional)

  • Open a terminal window.
  • Type the following command: tar -cvf minecraft_backup.tar ~/Library/Application\ Support/.minecraft
  • Press Enter and type your password when prompted.

2. Uninstall Minecraft

  • Type the following command in the terminal window: apt remove minecraft
  • Press Enter and type your password when prompted.

3. Delete Remaining Minecraft Folder

  • Type the following command: rm -vr ~/.minecraft/*
  • Press Enter to confirm the deletion.

iPhone, iPad, and iPod Touch

1. Back Up Saved Games (Optional)

  • Connect your device to a computer and open iTunes.
  • Click on the "Device" icon in the top left corner.
  • Select "Apps" in the left sidebar and scroll down to Minecraft.
  • Check the box next to "Documents & Data."
  • Click the "Back Up" button.

2. Uninstall Minecraft

  • On your device, press and hold the Minecraft icon until it wiggles.
  • Tap the "X" that appears on the icon.
  • Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Android

1. Back Up Saved Games (Optional)

  • Open the File Manager app on your Android device.
  • Navigate to the "Minecraft" folder in the internal storage.
  • Copy the "games" folder to a safe location.

2. Uninstall Minecraft

  • Go to Settings > Apps & notifications.
  • Find Minecraft in the list of installed apps.
  • Tap on "Uninstall."
  • Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Conclusion:

Uninstalling Minecraft is a straightforward process, whether you're using Windows, Mac OS X, Linux, or mobile devices. Remember to back up your saved games before uninstalling to preserve your progress.

If you're experiencing any issues with the specified steps, consult additional resources or contact Minecraft support for assistance. Whether you're clearing space on your devices or simply making way for a fresh installation, these steps will ensure a smooth and hassle-free Minecraft uninstallation.
