How to Sign in to X (Formerly Twitter): A Comprehensive Guide

X, formerly known as Twitter, is a popular social media platform that allows users to connect with others, share updates, and stay informed. Signing in to X is a straightforward process, whether you're using a computer or a mobile device. Here's a detailed guide on how to do it:

Signing in on Your Computer:

  1. Navigate to the X Website: Begin by visiting the X website at twitter.com.
  2. Locate the Login Button: Once you're on the X homepage, click on the "Log in" button located in the top-right corner of the screen.
  3. Enter Your Credentials: A login form will appear. Enter either your phone number, email address, or username in the designated field. Next, enter your password in the corresponding field.
  4. Click "Log In": After you've entered your credentials, click on the "Log In" button to complete the sign-in process.

Signing in on Your Phone:

  1. Launch the X App: Locate the X app on your mobile device and tap on the icon to open it.
  2. Tap "Sign In": Once the app opens, you'll see a "Sign In" button on the main screen. Tap on it to proceed.
  3. Enter Your Credentials: Similar to the computer method, enter your phone number, email address, or username in the first field. Then, enter your password in the second field.
  4. Tap "Sign In": Tap on the "Sign In" button to complete the login process and access your X account.

Logging in to Other Sites with X:

X also offers a convenient option for logging in to other websites that support X authentication. Here's how to do it:

  1. Locate the "Sign In with X" Button: Visit a website that offers X login. You'll typically find a button labeled "Sign In with X," "Login with X," or something similar.
  2. Click the Button: Click on the "Sign In with X" button to initiate the X login process.
  3. Authorize X: A new window or tab will open, prompting you to enter your X login details. Enter your credentials and click "Authorize" to grant the website permission to access your X account.
  4. Log In: Once you've authorized the website, you'll be automatically logged in without having to create a separate account for that specific site.
