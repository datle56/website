Craft an Extraordinary Submarine-Watching Device: A Step-by-Step Guide with Visual Aids


Unveiling the Secrets of Periscopes: An Adventure into Easy and Fun DIY Creations

Imagine exploring hidden nooks and crannies without stepping an inch from your safe haven. Welcome to the fascinating world of periscopes, a time-honored tool now made accessible to anyone with a knack for DIY projects.

In this step-by-step guide, we will embark on a thrilling adventure to craft your very own periscopes using two ingenious methods. Whether you prefer the versatility of cardboard or the durability of PVC pipes, we've got you covered. Embrace your inner engineer and let's dive right into the art of creating optical wonders!


Method 1: Cardboard Periscope

Materials:

  • Two small rectangular mirrors (approximately 2" x 3")
  • Two empty milk cartons with square bases (e.g., half-gallon size)
  • Strong tape (duct tape or packing tape)
  • Scissors or craft knife

Steps:

  1. Prepare the Milk Cartons: Cut off the top flaps of both milk cartons to create two open-top tubes.

  2. Join the Tubes: Overlap the open ends of the tubes by a few inches and secure them together with tape, forming a single long tube.

  3. Make Holes for the Mirrors: On the sides of the tube, opposite each other and approximately halfway down its length, cut out two rectangular holes that are slightly smaller than the mirrors.

  4. Insert the Mirrors: Insert the mirrors into the holes at a 45° angle, facing each other. The reflective surfaces should point towards the center of the tube.

  5. Adjust the Mirrors: Look through the holes created in Step 3 and adjust the mirrors until you see a clear image. You may need to slightly bend or tilt the mirrors to achieve optimal alignment.

  6. Secure the Mirrors: Once satisfied with the image quality, use tape or glue to secure the mirrors firmly in place within the holes.

Method 2: PVC Pipe Periscope

Materials:

  • PVC pipe (12-20 inches in length, diameter can vary)
  • Elbow joints (2)
  • Small rectangular mirrors (approximately 2" x 3")
  • Putty or tape
  • Adhesive (optional)

Steps:

  1. Assemble the Periscope: Connect the PVC elbow joints to each end of the pipe, creating a U-shape.

  2. Insert the Mirrors: Insert the mirrors into the elbow joints at a 45° angle, with the reflective surfaces facing each other. The mirrors should be parallel and centered within the elbow joints.

  3. Adjust the Mirrors: Ensure that the mirrors are properly aligned to provide a clear image. You may need to slightly adjust the angle or position of the mirrors until you are satisfied with the viewing quality.

  4. Secure the Mirrors: Use putty or tape to firmly hold the mirrors in place within the elbow joints. This will prevent them from moving or becoming misaligned.

  5. Optional: Enhance the Image: To further improve image clarity, you can apply a thin layer of adhesive to the inside of the PVC pipe. This will help to reduce internal reflections and enhance the viewing experience.


Conclusion:

With these simple methods, you can now construct your own periscope and embark on covert observations or explore hidden corners. Whether you're using cardboard or PVC pipe, the principles of reflection and light refraction remain the same. Remember that the angle of the mirrors and the alignment of the tubes are crucial for achieving clear images.

So, pick your preferred method, gather the materials, and get ready to unlock a world of secret surveillance or adventurous exploration through the lens of your homemade periscope. May your discoveries and adventures be as intriguing as the principles behind this timeless optical device!
