Unlocking the Secrets: How to Preserve Ring Camera Recordings Without a Costly Subscription

Are you a security-conscious individual who relies on Ring cameras to safeguard your home? If so, you've likely encountered the frustrating reality that saving captured footage requires a hefty subscription fee. However, fear not, for there are ingenious ways to bypass this financial burden and still preserve your precious recordings. In this comprehensive guide, we'll uncover three foolproof methods to save Ring camera videos without succumbing to a subscription. Prepare to empower yourself with video storage solutions that won't drain your wallet!


How to Save Ring Camera Videos Without a Subscription

With the increasing popularity of home security systems, Ring cameras have become a common choice for homeowners looking to enhance their home's security. However, Ring's plans can incur monthly fees for features such as video storage and sharing. If you're looking to avoid these subscription costs while still saving your Ring camera footage, here are several effective methods:

Method 1: Free Trial Subscription

  • Take advantage of Ring Protect's 30-day free trial period. When you first install a new Ring device, you will be eligible for a free trial of Ring Protect, which includes cloud storage and video saving capabilities.
  • During this trial period, you can access and save videos without any charges. Simply sign up for the trial through the Ring app or website.

Method 2: Screen Recording

If you don't want to sign up for a subscription, you can use screen recording to capture footage from your Ring camera.

  • Android or iPhone: Use the built-in screen recorder found in your device's settings. While in the Ring app, start a Live View session and begin recording the screen.
  • Computer: Log into Ring.com through a web browser. Initiate a Live View session and use a screen recording software, such as QuickTime Player on Macs or OBS Studio on Windows, to capture the footage.

Method 3: Non-Subscription Camera

An alternative to using the methods above is to invest in a Ring-compatible camera that doesn't require cloud storage. Several brands offer cameras with local video storage options, eliminating the need for a subscription. Here are some non-subscription camera recommendations:

  • Kasa Smart Doorbell: Stores videos on a microSD card.
  • Hikvision Smart Doorbell: Features onboard storage for video recording.
  • Eufy Video Doorbell: Supports local storage on a microSD card.

Tips:

  • If you prefer not to screen record, consider opting for non-subscription cameras that offer local video storage without the need for monthly fees.
  • Remember that each new Ring device comes with its own separate 30-day free trial. By installing multiple Ring devices, you can extend your free trial period and access video storage for an extended duration.

Conclusion:

While Ring's cloud storage offers convenience and peace of mind, it's not always a feasible expense for everyone. By following the methods outlined in this guide, you can effectively save Ring camera videos without the need for a subscription. Whether it's through a free trial, screen recording, or opting for a non-subscription camera, there's a solution that aligns with your budget and security preferences. Remember, exploring these alternatives empowers you to maintain the safety and security of your home without breaking the bank.
