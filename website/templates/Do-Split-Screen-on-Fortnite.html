How to Play Fortnite Split-Screen on Consoles: A Comprehensive Guide

Fortnite, the immensely popular battle royale game, offers an exhilarating split-screen mode that allows you to team up with a friend or family member on the same console for an adrenaline-fueled gaming session. Here's a detailed guide on how to enjoy Fortnite's split-screen mode on PlayStation and Xbox consoles:

Equipment Requirements:

  • Compatible console: PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X/S
  • Stable internet connection
  • Two controllers

Note: Split-screen mode is not available on Nintendo Switch, PC, or mobile devices.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

1. Launch Fortnite:

  • Power on your console and launch Fortnite.
  • Ensure both controllers are connected and ready for use.

2. Start a Game:

  • Select the "Battle Royale" or "Arena" game mode.
  • Choose either "Duos" or "Squads" as the matchmaking option.
  • Click on "Play" to start searching for a game.

3. Enable Split-Screen:

  • Once you're in the pre-game lobby, turn on the second controller by pressing the "PlayStation" or "Xbox" button.
  • A new login screen will appear.

4. Sign In to a User Account:

  • Use the second controller to sign in to a user account that has access to Fortnite. This can be a guest account or a user account linked to an Epic Games profile.

5. Join as Player 2:

  • After signing in, hold down the "X" button (PlayStation) or "A" button (Xbox) to log in as "Player 2."
  • Player 2's character will now load and appear on the bottom half of the screen.

6. Configure Split-Screen Settings:

  • Player 1 will control the top half of the screen, while Player 2 will control the bottom half.
  • Adjust the split-screen settings in the "Game Options" menu to customize the screen size and layout.

7. Start Playing:

  • Select "Ready" on both controllers to confirm your participation and start the game.
  • Battle alongside your teammate and strive for victory!

Additional Information:

  • Inventory sharing is not available in split-screen mode. Each player has their own separate inventory.
  • Communication is essential for success in split-screen mode. Use headsets or microphones to coordinate strategies and avoid friendly fire.
  • Make sure both players are situated comfortably and have a clear view of the screen.
  • Enjoy the adrenaline rush and memorable moments that split-screen gaming provides!
