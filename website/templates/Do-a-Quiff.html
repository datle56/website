Introducing the Quiff: A Timeless Style with Modern Appeal

Prepare yourself for a hairstyle revolution! The quiff, an iconic cut that has captivated generations, is back with a bang. Characterized by its distinguished raised front that gracefully curves back, the quiff exudes both charm and sophistication. Whether you're yearning for a fresh look or embracing a classic, this guide will lead you through the secrets of getting and styling a quiff that will turn heads.


Getting a Quiff Haircut

A quiff is a versatile hairstyle that adds volume and sophistication to any hair type. Its defining feature is the elevated front section that curves backward, creating a distinctive and stylish look.

Consulting Your Barber

Before visiting the barber, it's essential to do some research online or in magazines to identify different quiff styles. Browse through images and select the ones that resonate with your personal preferences and face shape. Bring these images to your appointment to provide a clear visual reference for your desired style.

Creating the Quiff

The key to achieving a perfect quiff lies in contrasting lengths between the top, sides, and back of your hair. Your barber will typically cut the sides and back shorter, creating a tapering effect that emphasizes the height of the quiff. The length and thickness of the top section will depend on your desired quiff style.

Hair Considerations

While a quiff is a flattering hairstyle for many, it's important to consider your hair texture. Avoid getting a quiff if you have extremely curly or frizzy hair, as it can be challenging to style and maintain the desired shape.

Styling a Quiff for Short Hair

Step 1: Preparation

Start by washing, conditioning, and towel-drying your hair.

Step 2: Applying Product

Apply a small amount of styling wax or gel to your fingers.

Step 3: Drying and Lifting

Use a hair dryer and brush to lift the top section of your hair sideways. Direct the airflow through the brush to create volume and shape.

Step 4: Styling the Quiff

Use your fingers to work the hair upwards and backward into a quiff. Avoid combing or brushing it straight back, as this can flatten the style.

Step 5: Finishing Touches

Use wax to smooth out any flyaway hairs and refine the edges of your quiff. To hold the shape in place, spray with hairspray.

Styling a Quiff for Long Hair

Step 1: Preparation

Wash your hair and blow it dry straight.

Step 2: Sectioning

Section off the front part of your hair for the quiff. The size of this section will depend on the desired height and width of your quiff.

Step 3: Backcombing

Backcomb the front section of hair to create volume. This will give the quiff its characteristic lift.

Step 4: Smoothing and Twisting

Smooth the surface of the backcombed hair and twist the ends.

Step 5: Securing

Pin the twisted hair to the ponytail or bobby pin it to your remaining hair. Conceal any exposed bobby pins or hair ties.

Step 6: Finishing

Spray with hairspray to finish and hold the shape in place.


Mastering the art of a quiff hairstyle is an endeavor that requires precision, patience, and a dash of charisma. Whether you sport short or long locks, following the techniques outlined above will guide you towards achieving the quintessential quiff that exudes both style and confidence. Remember to tailor your approach based on your hair texture and length, and don't be afraid to experiment until you find the perfect balance that complements your unique features. By embracing the power of the quiff, you can elevate your style and make a statement wherever you go.
