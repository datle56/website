Managing Multiple Clash of Clans Accounts

Introduction

Clash of Clans is a popular mobile strategy game that allows players to create and manage multiple accounts. This can be useful for various reasons, such as experimenting with different strategies, developing specific villages, or maintaining separate accounts for friends and family members. Managing multiple accounts on the same device can be a bit daunting at first, but with a few simple steps, you can easily switch between them without losing any progress.

Disconnecting Your Current Game

To disconnect your current game and start a new one, follow these steps:

  1. Access the Settings Menu: Tap on the gear icon in the bottom left corner of the main screen to open the Settings menu.
  2. Navigate to Connected Account: Select the "Connected" tab in the Settings menu.
  3. Supercell ID Settings: Tap on the Settings button (three horizontal lines) in the Supercell ID screen.
  4. Log Out: Choose "Log Out" and confirm your choice by tapping "Yes" when prompted.

Starting a New Game

Once you have disconnected your current game, you can start a new one by following these steps:

  1. Launch Clash of Clans: Open the Clash of Clans app on your device.
  2. Select Play Mode: Tap on "Play without Supercell ID" to start a new game without connecting it to your Supercell ID.

Creating a Supercell ID

If you want to create a Supercell ID to connect and manage your accounts, follow these steps:

  1. Access Disconnected Menu: Tap on the "Disconnected" tab in the Settings menu.
  2. Register Now: Select the "Register Now" button.
  3. Enter Email Address: Enter a valid email address and tap "Continue."
  4. Create Password: Enter a strong password and tap "Next."
  5. Confirm Email: Check your email for a confirmation message and follow the instructions to activate your Supercell ID.

Selecting an Account

Once you have created a Supercell ID, you can easily switch between your accounts by following these steps:

  1. Access the Settings Menu: Tap on the gear icon in the bottom left corner of the main screen.
  2. Supercell ID Selection: Tap on the blue button next to "Connected."
  3. Account List: A list of your registered Supercell IDs will appear.
  4. Choose Account: Select the account you want to play with and tap "Connect."

Tips for Managing Multiple Accounts

  • Use Separate Email Addresses: Create a unique email address for each Supercell ID to avoid confusion and maintain separate accounts.
  • Nickname Your Accounts: Give each account a unique nickname to help you identify them easily in the game and on the device.
  • Sync Your Accounts: Ensure that your Supercell IDs are properly synced with all your devices to access your accounts from any supported platform.
  • Keep Track of Progress: Monitor the progress of each account regularly to avoid neglecting any of them.
  • Seek Support: If you encounter any issues while managing multiple accounts, contact Supercell support for assistance.

By following these steps and tips, you can easily manage multiple Clash of Clans accounts on the same device, giving you the flexibility to customize your gameplay and achieve your strategic goals in the game.
