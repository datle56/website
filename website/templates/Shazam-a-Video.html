Easy Ways to Shazam Music from Videos

Auto Shazam on Mobile:

Step 1: Install the Shazam App

Download and install the Shazam app from the App Store or Google Play Store on your mobile device.

Step 2: Activate Auto Shazam

  • Open the Shazam app and tap the Gear icon in the top-right corner.
  • Select "Auto Shazam" and toggle it to "On."

Step 3: Play Any Video

Now, whenever you play any video on your device, Shazam will automatically listen for music and identify it in the background.

Control Center on iPhone/iPad:

Step 1: Add Shazam to Control Center

  • Go to "Settings" on your device.
  • Tap "Control Center."
  • Scroll down and tap "+ Music Recognition."
  • Select "Shazam."

Step 2: Play a Video

Play the video that contains music you want to identify.

Step 3: Swipe Down and Tap the Shazam Icon

  • Swipe down from the top-right corner of the screen (iPhone X+ devices) or up from the bottom (earlier iPhone models).
  • Tap the Shazam icon that appears to identify the music.

Pop-Up Shazam on Android:

Step 1: Enable Pop-Up Shazam

  • Open the Shazam app on your Android device.
  • Tap the Profile icon in the bottom-right corner.
  • Select "Settings."
  • Under "Library," toggle "Shazam from Pop-Up" to "On."

Step 2: Play a Video in Any App

Play a video in any app on your device, such as YouTube, Facebook, or Netflix.

Step 3: Tap the Floating Shazam Icon

A floating Shazam icon will appear on the screen. Tap it to identify the music.

Shazam Chrome Extension on PC/Mac:

Step 1: Install the Shazam Chrome Extension

  • Go to the Chrome Web Store and search for "Shazam."
  • Click on the "Add to Chrome" button and follow the prompts to install the extension.

Step 2: Play a Video in Chrome

Play a video on a streaming platform such as Netflix, YouTube, or Hulu in the Chrome browser.

Step 3: Click the Shazam Icon

Click on the Shazam icon in the extensions toolbar. A small window will appear, displaying the identified music track.
