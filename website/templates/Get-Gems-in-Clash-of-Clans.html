Making Gems

Gems are a valuable resource in Clash of Clans, used to accelerate upgrades, purchase items, and boost your progress. There are several methods to acquire Gems within the game:

1. Remove Obstacles

  • Clear away plants (using Elixir) and rocks (using Gold) from your village and Builder Base. Each obstacle removed will reward you with a certain number of Gems.
  • Note that plants regrow every 8 hours, so strategically remove them to maximize Gem income.

2. Complete Achievements

  • Engage in various game activities such as upgrading buildings, winning battles, and collecting resources to earn achievement rewards.
  • Visit the "Achievements" tab to track your progress and prioritize completing the more lucrative achievements.

3. Manage Gem Mine

  • In the Builder Base, repair the Gem Mine to begin producing Gems on a daily basis.
  • Upgrade the Gem Mine to enhance its production rate, increasing your Gem output.

Spending Gems Wisely

To ensure the best use of your Gems, consider the following guidelines:

Avoid Speeding Up Timers:

  • Refrain from using Gems to expedite construction or production times. This is a costly and inefficient use of Gems.

Prioritize Builder's Huts:

  • Utilize Gems to purchase Builder's Huts, which increase your total number of builders. This enables you to construct and upgrade buildings simultaneously, accelerating your overall progress.

Other Strategies

Use Google Play Rewards (Android Only):

  • Install the Google Opinion Rewards app to participate in surveys and earn Google Play credit.
  • Redeem this credit to purchase Gems in Clash of Clans.

Additional Tips

  • Join an active clan to access Clan Games, which offer Gem rewards for participation.
  • Participate in seasonal events and challenges that may award Gems as prizes.
  • Consider purchasing Gem packs during special promotions or discounts.

Remember, patience and strategic planning are key to accumulating Gems and maximizing their impact on your Clash of Clans progress.


In summary, acquiring Gems in Clash of Clans has several routes. By removing obstacles, completing achievements, and managing the Gem Mine, players can steadily accumulate these valuable resources. However, it's essential to spend Gems wisely. Prioritize using them for Builder's Huts to accelerate the game's progress. Avoiding unnecessary spending on speeding up timers will preserve your Gems for more impactful investments. Additionally, Android users can utilize Google Play Rewards to earn credit towards in-game purchases, offering a unique way to acquire Gems without spending real money. Whether you're a seasoned veteran or a budding strategist, these strategies will empower you to maximize your Gem earnings and wisely allocate your resources, ultimately enhancing your overall Clash of Clans experience.
