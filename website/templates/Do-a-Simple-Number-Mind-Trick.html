Method 1: Simple Number Prediction

Detailed Instructions:

  1. Write down the number 3: On a piece of paper or notecard, clearly write the number "3." Ensure it is large enough to be read from a distance.
  2. Fold the paper: Once you have written the number, fold the paper in half, covering the number. Crease the fold firmly to secure the secret.
  3. Ask your friend to choose a number: Instruct your friend or participant to select any number between 1 and 20. Encourage them to select a number randomly without any influence from others.
  4. Perform the calculations: Have your friend perform the following calculations aloud:
    • Add 1 to the chosen number.
    • Double the result.
    • Add 4 to the doubled number.
    • Divide the sum by 2.
    • Subtract the original chosen number from the quotient.
  5. Unfold the paper: While your friend is performing the calculations, keep the folded paper concealed. Once they have completed all the steps, unfold the paper and reveal the number 3.
  6. Reveal the match: The number written on the paper, which is 3, will always match the final answer calculated by your friend. This trick works every time, regardless of the number they initially chose.

Method 2: Guessing Someone's Age

Detailed Instructions:

  1. Ask for the first digit: Politely ask the person whose age you wish to guess to reveal only the first digit of their age. For example, if they are 25, ask them to say "2."
  2. Multiply by 5: Have them multiply the first digit by 5. So, in the example, 2 x 5 = 10.
  3. Add 4 and double: Instruct them to add 4 to the product and then double the result. For instance, 10 + 4 = 14, and 14 x 2 = 28.
  4. Add the second digit: Ask them to add the second digit of their age to the previous result. For example, if their age is 25, the second digit is 5, so they would add 5 to 28, which equals 33.
  5. Subtract 8: Have them subtract 8 from the final answer. In our example, 33 - 8 = 25.
  6. Reveal their age: The result obtained after subtracting 8 will be their actual age, which is 25 in this case.

Method 3: Magical 37

Detailed Instructions:

  1. Request a 3-digit number: Ask someone to write a 3-digit number on a piece of paper or notecard. Ensure that all three digits are the same. For example, they could write 333.
  2. Add up the digits: Have them add up the three digits of their number. So, in the example of 333, 3 + 3 + 3 = 9.
  3. Divide the original number by the sum: Next, instruct them to divide the original 3-digit number by the sum they just found. In this case, 333 divided by 9 equals 37.
  4. Reveal the magic: No matter what 3-digit number is chosen, dividing it by the sum of its digits will always result in 37. This mathematical trick never fails to amaze.
