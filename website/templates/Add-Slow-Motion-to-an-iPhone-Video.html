Slow Motion Video Creation and Editing with iMovie

Slow Motion with Existing Video

  1. Launch iMovie: Open the iMovie application on your Mac or iOS device.

  2. Import Video: Click the "File" menu and select "Import Media" to choose the video you want to slow down.

  3. Select Speedometer Icon: Once the video is imported, click on the video clip in the timeline at the bottom of the screen. A small speedometer icon will appear on the right side of the clip.

  4. Adjust Playback Speed: Click on the speedometer icon and a slider will appear. Drag the slider to the left to slow down the video. The speed can be adjusted from 100% to 25%.

  5. Preview the Changes: While dragging the slider, you can preview the slow-motion effect by holding down the spacebar or clicking the play button.

  6. Apply Changes: Once you are satisfied with the speed, click "Done" to save the changes.

  7. Export Video: Click the "Share" icon in the top right corner and select "Save Video" to export the slowed-down video to your computer or device.

Slow Motion Recording (iPhone 11 and 12)

  1. Open Camera App: Launch the Camera app on your iPhone 11 or 12.

  2. Switch to SLO-MO Mode: Swipe to the left to access the slow motion mode (SLO-MO). The frame rate will be indicated at the top of the screen.

  3. Start Recording: Tap the red record button to begin recording in slow motion.

  4. Stop Recording: Tap the red record button again to stop recording.

  5. View Slow-Motion Video: A thumbnail of the slow-motion video will appear in the bottom left corner. Tap on the thumbnail to preview the video.

Tips:

  • Selective Slow Motion: In iMovie, you can selectively choose which portions of the video should be played in slow motion. Drag the speed controls in the editor to adjust the speed of specific sections.

  • Front and Rear Camera Support: iPhone 11 and 12 models allow you to record slow-motion videos with both the front and rear cameras.

  • Limited Compatibility: For iPhone models prior to iPhone 11, slow motion recording is only available with the rear camera.

By utilizing these methods, you can create and edit slow-motion videos to enhance your storytelling and visual impact.
