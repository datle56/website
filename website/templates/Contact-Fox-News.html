Contacting Fox News

Fox News, a renowned news and opinion channel, provides multiple channels for viewers to reach out, share perspectives, or seek assistance. With both online and offline options available, individuals can connect with Fox News seamlessly.

Online Contact Methods:

  • Contact Form: Visit the Fox News website and fill out the online contact form. Provide your name, email address, and a detailed message outlining your query or concern.

  • Social Media: Engage with hosts or shows directly through Twitter or Facebook. Tweet at the official Fox News handle (@FoxNews) or the specific show you wish to contact using their designated hashtags. Similarly, message or comment on their Facebook pages.

  • News Tips and Corrections: If you have newsworthy information or feedback on news coverage, you can report it through email (newstips@foxnews.com), Twitter (@FoxNewsTips), or Facebook.

  • Local Affiliates: Find contact information for your local Fox News affiliates on the official website.

Phone and Mail Contact Methods:

  • General Inquiries: For general questions or concerns, call Fox News at 1-888-369-4762.

  • Mailing Address: Write to Fox News at their headquarters located at 1211 Avenue of the Americas, New York, NY 10036.

  • Specific Shows: Email individual Fox News shows directly by accessing their respective show pages (e.g., Hannity@foxnews.com).

  • International Inquiries: Send emails to FOXaroundtheworld@foxnews.com for assistance from Fox News's global team.

  • Press Office: Media inquiries can be directed to the press office by emailing irena.briganti@foxnews.com.

Getting Questions Answered:

  • Help Page: For questions related to television programming, job opportunities, or technical issues, visit the Fox News help page.

  • Online Help: Seek assistance with website or mobile app problems through the dedicated online help page.

  • Mobile Device Help: If you encounter issues using the Fox News app on your mobile device, refer to the mobile device help page.

By utilizing these contact methods, you can effectively reach out to Fox News and engage with their platform. Whether you have questions about news coverage, want to provide feedback, or simply have technical inquiries, Fox News offers a range of convenient communication channels to facilitate your needs.


Conclusion:

With these comprehensive contact options, reaching Fox News has never been easier. Whether you wish to share your perspectives, report breaking news, or seek assistance, the team at Fox News is readily available to connect with you. By leveraging the convenient online forms, social media platforms, and designated phone and email channels, you can effectively convey your messages and receive prompt responses. Remember, Fox News values your feedback and strives to maintain open communication with viewers, ensuring that your voice is heard and your inquiries are addressed with the utmost professionalism and attention.
