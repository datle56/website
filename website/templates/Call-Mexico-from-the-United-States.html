Calling Mexico from the United States: A Comprehensive Guide

Dialing Procedures:

Landline to Landline:

  • Dial 011 (exit code for the US)
  • Dial 52 (Mexico's country code)
  • Drop the leading zero from the Mexican area code (e.g., 81 for Monterrey becomes 181)
  • Enter the remaining eight-digit phone number

Example: To call a landline in Mexico City, dial: 011-52-55-5555-5555

Cell Phone to Cell Phone:

  • Use the "+" sign (denotes international call)
  • Dial 52 (Mexico's country code)
  • Omit the leading zero of the area code (e.g., 81 for Monterrey becomes 181)
  • Enter the remaining eight-digit phone number without the area code

Example: To call a cell phone in Mexico City, dial: +52-55-5555-5555

Free Calling Options:

  • FaceTime: Apple's proprietary video calling app allows free calls between Apple devices, even if the recipient doesn't have the app.

  • WhatsApp: A popular messaging app that offers free voice and video calls to both landlines and cell phones with an internet connection.

  • Facebook Messenger: Similar to WhatsApp, Messenger allows free calls to other Facebook users with an internet connection.

  • Skype: Skype offers free audio and video calls between Skype users with an internet connection.

Paid Calling Options:

  • International Calling Plan: Many phone providers offer international calling plans that bundle minutes for calls to specific countries, including Mexico. Check with your provider for availability and pricing.

  • Mexico-Specific Calling Cards: Physical or online calling cards are available specifically for calling Mexico. Look for cards with low rates and connection fees to maximize savings.

  • Online Calling Services: Websites such as Rebtel and Telestial allow you to buy credit to make international calls via your cell phone or landline. Rates may vary depending on the provider and destination country.

  • Skype: Skype offers paid calls to Mexican phone numbers, even if the recipient doesn't have Skype. Rates vary based on the destination and time of day.

Tips:

  • Area Code Lookup: Before dialing, verify the area code of the city or region in Mexico you're calling. Area codes can be found online or through phone directories.

  • Free and Low-Cost Calling Apps: Take advantage of apps like Skype and Google Voice for free or low-cost international calling. These apps use internet data instead of traditional phone lines, reducing the cost of calls.

  • Beware of Shady Calling Cards: Avoid calling cards with unclear fees and terms. Read the fine print carefully before purchasing to avoid hidden charges or unexpected expenses.
