How to Turn Closed Captions On and Off on Netflix: A Comprehensive Guide

Netflix, the streaming giant, offers a wide range of content with the convenience of closed captions (CC) to enhance viewing experiences for those who are deaf or hard of hearing, non-native speakers, or simply prefer to read along with the dialogue. Turning CC on or off is a straightforward process that varies slightly depending on the device you're using.

Streaming Device or Gaming Console:

  • Step 1: Launch Netflix and Select Video

Open the Netflix app on your preferred streaming device or gaming console. Choose the video you want to watch.

  • Step 2: Display Options Menu

Use your remote control to navigate the video playback screen. Press the up or down button to reveal the options menu at the bottom of the screen.

  • Step 3: Access Audio & Subtitles Settings

Locate the "Audio & Subtitles" option within the menu. It might be represented by an icon of a speech bubble or a gear.

  • Step 4: Select Language or Disable Captions

In the Audio & Subtitles settings, a list of available languages for closed captions will appear. Choose your desired language. If you wish to turn off captions, select the "Off" option.

Mobile App:

  • Step 1: Open Video in Netflix App

Launch the Netflix app on your mobile device. Tap on the video you want to watch.

  • Step 2: Show Playback Controls

Tap anywhere on the video screen to display the playback controls at the bottom of the screen.

  • Step 3: Access Audio & Subtitles Settings

Locate the "Audio & Subtitles" icon, often represented by a speech bubble or a gear. Tap on it.

  • Step 4: Select Language or Disable Captions

Similar to the streaming device method, choose your preferred language for closed captions from the available options. To turn off captions, tap on the "Off" option.

Desktop:

  • Step 1: Play Video in Web Browser

Open Netflix in your preferred web browser and start playing the video you want to watch.

  • Step 2: Hover to Show Playback Controls

Move your mouse cursor over the video screen to reveal the playback controls at the bottom of the screen.

  • Step 3: Click Audio & Subtitle Icon

Locate the icon that resembles a speech bubble and a subtitle box, representing the "Audio" and "Subtitle" settings. Click on it.

  • Step 4: Select Language or Disable Captions

In the Audio & Subtitle settings, select your preferred language for closed captions or turn them off by clicking on the "Off" option.

Apple TV:

  • Step 1: Start Video and Swipe Down

Launch the Netflix app on your Apple TV and start playing the desired video. Using your remote, swipe down on the touch surface to display the settings menu at the bottom of the screen.

  • Step 2: Access Subtitles Tab

Navigate to the "Subtitles" tab within the settings menu.

  • Step 3: Select Language or Disable Captions

Choose your preferred language for closed captions or turn them off by selecting the "Off" option.

Note on Availability:

It's important to note that the availability of closed captioning in different languages may vary depending on the content license agreements and the region you're located in.
