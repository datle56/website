Elevate Your Tables: Three Simple Methods to Increase Table Height


Elevate Your Dining Experience: Uncover Ingenious Methods to Make Your Table Taller

Are you tired of hunching over your table, struggling to reach your plate? Fear not! We present three foolproof methods to effortlessly increase your table's height, transforming your dining experience into a comfortable and enjoyable affair. From quick and easy extensions to elegant leg replacements, we've got you covered. Embark on this transformative journey to achieve the perfect dining height and elevate your mealtimes to new heights of convenience and style.


Method 1: Adding Extensions Using Bed Risers or Leg Extensions

  • Bed risers: These are simple and inexpensive devices that can be placed under the legs of your table to instantly increase its height. They are available in various sizes, so you can choose the ones that will elevate your table to your desired height.
  • Leg extensions: These are similar to bed risers, but they are designed to be attached to the bottom of the table legs. They typically consist of a metal or wooden frame that adds several inches to the leg length.

Attaching Bun Feet to a Wooden Table

  • Bun feet: These are decorative feet that can be attached to the bottom of a wooden table leg to add an elegant touch. They are available in various styles and shapes, so you can choose ones that complement your table's design.
  • Steps for attaching bun feet:
    • Measure and mark the center of each table leg where you want to attach the bun feet.
    • Drill a pilot hole for each screw.
    • Apply wood glue to the base of each bun foot.
    • Align the bun feet with the holes and screw them into the table legs.

Extending the Height of a Metal Table with PVC Pipes

  • PVC pipes: These are lightweight and versatile pipes that can be used to extend the height of metal tables.
  • Steps for extending with PVC pipes:
    • Measure the desired height of the table.
    • Cut PVC pipes to the desired length.
    • Attach the PVC pipes to the metal table legs using zip ties or hose clamps.
    • Ensure the pipes are securely fastened and provide stable support.

Method 2: Attaching Wooden Extensions

  • Selecting wooden extensions: Choose sturdy wooden pieces that match the diameter and style of your table legs. The length of the extensions should be equal to the desired increase in table height.
  • Drilling and inserting tee nuts:
    • Drill holes into the bottom of the table legs, making sure they are aligned with the holes in the wooden extensions.
    • Insert tee nuts into the holes.
  • Attaching hanger bolts: Screw hanger bolts into the holes in the wooden extensions.
  • Screwing extensions onto legs: Align the hanger bolts with the tee nuts in the table legs and screw the extensions securely onto the legs.
  • Sanding and finishing: Once the extensions are attached, sand the area to smooth out any rough edges and make the extensions blend in with the table legs. You can also stain or paint the extensions to match the color of the table.

Method 3: Replacing Table Legs

  • Determining new leg length: Measure the current table height and the desired increase in height. Add these two measurements together to determine the length of the new table legs.
  • Removing old legs: Use an electric drill to remove the screws or bolts that are holding the old table legs in place. Carefully remove the legs.
  • Sanding and finishing new legs: Sand the new table legs to smooth out any imperfections. If desired, apply a stain or finish to match the table or create a different look.
  • Marking and drilling holes: Mark the desired locations for the new table legs under the table. Drill holes that are slightly smaller than the diameter of the table leg mounting bolts.
  • Attaching new legs: Apply woodworking glue to the base of the new table legs and insert them into the holes. Align the legs and screw them into place. Allow the glue to dry overnight before using the table.

Conclusion: Choosing the Best Method

Choosing the most suitable method for increasing your table height depends on the table's material, design, and desired height adjustment.

  • Method 1: Extensions: Quick and effortless, bed risers or leg extensions are ideal for temporary height adjustments of wooden or metal tables. Bun feet add an elegant touch to wooden tables.
  • Method 2: Wooden Extensions: This method offers a more permanent solution for wooden tables. It involves attaching wooden extensions to the existing legs, providing greater height and stability.
  • Method 3: Replacing Table Legs: If a significant height increase is needed or the legs are damaged, replacing them with longer legs is the best option. This requires more time and effort but ensures a sturdy and lasting solution.

No matter the method you choose, it's crucial to consider the height of the table relative to the height of the chairs or stools used with it. By carefully planning and executing your table height adjustment, you can create a comfortable and functional dining or work surface that meets your specific needs.
