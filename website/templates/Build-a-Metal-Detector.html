How to Make a Simple Metal Detector

Method 1: Using a Calculator and Radio

Materials:

  • Small AM radio
  • Calculator
  • Glue
  • Stick or pole

Instructions:

  1. Tune the radio: Adjust the radio to a static frequency that does not broadcast any stations. This will create a consistent background noise.
  2. Combine the devices: Use glue to attach the back of the radio to the back of the calculator, creating a combined search head.
  3. Create a handle: Affix the search head to a stick or pole to make it easier to handle.
  4. Detect metal: Move the search head slowly over a suspected metal object. If metal is present, the radio's static noise will change, indicating the detection.

Method 2: Using a Smartphone

Materials:

  • Metal detector app (available for download on iOS or Android)
  • Smartphone

Instructions:

  1. Download and open the app: Install and open a metal detector app on your smartphone.
  2. Calibrate the app: Follow the app's instructions to calibrate it to the magnetic field conditions in your environment.
  3. Scan for metal: Holding the phone parallel to the ground, move it slowly over the suspected metal object.
  4. Observe the readings: The app will display changes in the magnetic field strength, indicating the presence of metal.

Method 3: Assembling a Kit

Materials:

  • Metal detector kit (containing all necessary components and instructions)

Instructions:

  1. Inspect the kit: Unpack the kit and ensure that all components are present.
  2. Assemble the hardware: According to the kit's instructions, assemble the coil, control box, and other hardware components.
  3. Solder the circuit board: Carefully solder the components onto the circuit board located inside the control box.
  4. Connect the coil: Attach the coil to the control box using the provided connectors.
  5. Test the metal detector: Power on the detector and pass it over various metal objects to ensure proper operation.
  6. Adjust sensitivity: If necessary, adjust the sensitivity knob on the control box to optimize the detector's performance.
  7. Use the detector: You can now use your assembled metal detector to search for buried treasures, hidden objects, or any other metallic items.
