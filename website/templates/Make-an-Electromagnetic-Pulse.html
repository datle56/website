Method 1: Simple EMP Emitter

Materials:

  • Disposable camera
  • Copper wire (enameled or insulated)
  • Rubber gloves
  • Solder
  • Iron rod or non-metal substitute (e.g., wooden dowel)

Instructions:

  1. Prepare the Electromagnetic Coil: Wrap the copper wire tightly around the iron rod (or substitute) in a helical manner, creating an electromagnetic coil. Ensure the coil is wound in the same direction throughout.
  2. Solder the Capacitor Leads: Carefully remove the capacitor from the disposable camera, wearing rubber gloves for safety. Solder the ends of the coil to the leads of the capacitor.
  3. Discharge the Capacitor (Optional): Before handling the charged capacitor, it is advisable to discharge it. Connect a resistor across the capacitor terminals to discharge any residual energy.
  4. Connect to the Camera Battery: Connect the capacitor to the battery terminals of the disposable camera. This will charge the capacitor.
  5. Position the Test Object: Place the electronic device or object you wish to disrupt near the electromagnetic coil. The closer the object is to the coil, the stronger the EMP effect will be.
  6. Activate the Camera Flash: Trigger the camera's flash by depressing the shutter button. This will discharge the capacitor through the coil, creating an electromagnetic pulse.
  7. Observe the Effects: Immediately observe the effects on the nearby electronic device. You may witness disruption or malfunctions as the EMP affects its electronic circuitry.

Method 2: Handheld EMP Device

Materials:

  • Disposable camera
  • Copper wire (enameled or insulated)
  • Iron core (e.g., a nail or bolt)
  • Walkie-talkie antenna (or similar conductor)
  • Cardboard or plastic sheet
  • Electrical tape
  • AA battery holder
  • AA battery
  • Electrical switch (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Remove Camera Circuit Board: Carefully remove the circuit board from the disposable camera. This board contains the capacitor mentioned in Method 1.
  2. Wind the Coil: Wrap the copper wire around the iron core, creating a tightly wound coil. Attach one end of the coil to the walkie-talkie antenna (or other suitable conductor).
  3. Mount the Circuitry: Mount the camera circuit board on a piece of cardboard or plastic sheet for stability.
  4. Connect the Battery Holder: Solder the wires from the battery holder to the appropriate terminals on the camera circuit board.
  5. Solder the Capacitor: Solder the coil you made in Step 2 to the capacitor from the camera board.
  6. Attach the Mount: Attach the cardboard mount with the camera circuit board and coil to the antenna using electrical tape.
  7. Add Switch (Optional): If desired, install an electrical switch in series with the battery connection to enable easy activation of the EMP device.
  8. Insert Battery and Test: Insert the AA battery into the battery holder. Test the device by holding it near an electronic object, such as a calculator, and activating the switch or shutter button. Observe the disruption or malfunction of the target device.
