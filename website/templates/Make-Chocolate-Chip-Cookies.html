How to Make Chocolate Chip Cookies: A Comprehensive Guide

Indulge yourself in the warm, gooey goodness of homemade chocolate chip cookies. Whether you prefer classic, chewy, or vegan treats, this comprehensive guide will lead you through the perfect recipe for each.

Basic Chocolatey Chip Cookies: The Classic Delight

  1. Gather your ingredients:

    • 2 1/4 cups (281g) all-purpose flour
    • 1 teaspoon (5g) salt
    • 1 teaspoon (5g) baking soda
    • 1 cup (2 sticks/227g) unsalted butter, softened
    • 3/4 cup (150g) granulated sugar
    • 3/4 cup (180g) packed light brown sugar
    • 2 large eggs
    • 2 teaspoons (10ml) vanilla extract
    • 2 cups (340g) semi-sweet chocolate chips

  2. Combine dry ingredients:

    • In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, salt, and baking soda.

  3. Cream butter and sugar:

    • In a large mixing bowl, use an electric mixer to cream together the softened butter and granulated sugar until light and fluffy.

  4. Add eggs and vanilla:

    • Beat in the eggs one at a time, then stir in the vanilla extract.

  5. Gradually add dry ingredients:

    • Gradually add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture, mixing until just combined. Do not overmix.

  6. Fold in chocolate chips:

    • Stir in the chocolate chips until evenly distributed.

  7. Prepare baking sheet:

    • Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

  8. Drop dough onto baking sheet:

    • Using a spoon or cookie scoop, drop spoonfuls of dough onto the prepared baking sheet, spacing them about 2 inches apart.

  9. Bake:

    • Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Bake for about 10 minutes, or until the edges are just beginning to brown.

  10. Cool and enjoy:

    • Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for a few minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

Vegan Dairy- and Egg-Free Chocolate Chip Cookies: Guilt-Free Delight

For those allergic to dairy or eggs, don't despair! Here's how to make vegan chocolate chip cookies:

  • Preheat oven: Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C) and grease a baking sheet.
  • Combine dry ingredients: Whisk together 2 1/4 cups (281g) all-purpose flour, 1 teaspoon (5g) salt, and 1 teaspoon (5g) baking soda.
  • Cream margarine: In a large bowl, cream together 1 cup (2 sticks/227g) vegan margarine, 1/2 cup (100g) brown sugar, 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar, 2 teaspoons (10ml) vanilla extract, and 2 tablespoons (30ml) egg replacer.
  • Stir in flour mixture: Gradually add the dry ingredients to the margarine mixture, stirring until just combined.
  • Add chocolate chips: Fold in 2 cups (340g) semi-sweet chocolate chips.
  • Bake: Drop dough onto the prepared baking sheet and bake for 8-10 minutes.

Banana Chocolate Chip Cookies: A Fruity Twist

Infuse your chocolate chip cookies with the sweetness of bananas with this variation:

  • Preheat oven: Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  • Combine dry ingredients: In a medium bowl, combine 2 1/4 cups (281g) all-purpose flour, 1 teaspoon (5g) salt, 1 teaspoon (5g) baking powder, and 1 teaspoon (5g) baking soda.
  • Cream sugars: In a large bowl, cream together 3/4 cup (150g) granulated sugar and 3/4 cup (180g) packed light brown sugar until light and fluffy.
  • Add butter and eggs: Add 1 cup (2 sticks/227g) unsalted butter, softened, and beat in 2 large eggs and 2 teaspoons (10ml) vanilla extract.
  • Mash bananas: In a separate bowl, mash 2 large ripe bananas. Add the mashed bananas to the wet ingredients and stir until combined.
  • Gradually add flour mixture: Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing until just combined.
  • Fold in chocolate chips: Stir in 2 cups (340g) semi-sweet chocolate chips.
  • Bake: Drop dough onto a greased baking sheet and bake for 12-15 minutes.
