Unlocking the Sixth Builder in Clash of Clans: A Comprehensive Guide

Requirements:

To embark on the journey of acquiring a sixth builder in Clash of Clans, you must meet the following prerequisites:

  • Builder Base Upgrade: Your Builder Base must be upgraded to level 9.
  • Town Hall Upgrade: Your Town Hall in your Home Village must be upgraded to level 10.
  • Gear Up Upgrades:
    • Gear up a Cannon, Archer Tower, and Mortar in your Home Village.
    • Gear up a Double Cannon, Archer Tower, and Multi Mortar in your Builder Base.
  • O.T.T.O Hut Upgrade: Upgrade the O.T.T.O Hut in your Builder Base to level 5.

Steps:

1. O.T.T.O Hut Prerequisites:

  • Complete the gear up upgrades as mentioned above.

2. O.T.T.O Hut Level 4 Upgrade:

  • Upgrade your O.T.T.O Hut to level 4, which requires a level 9 Mega Tesla in your Builder Base.

3. Battle Machine Level 30 Upgrade:

  • Upgrade your Battle Machine to level 30.

4. Master Builder Hut Construction:

  • Build a Master Builder Hut in your Home Village.

5. O.T.T.O Hut Travel:

  • In your Builder Base, select the O.T.T.O Hut and choose the "Travel" option.
  • Your Master Builder will be dispatched to your Home Village, where they will act as your sixth builder.

Tips:

  • Time Investment: Completing all the steps can take several months of dedicated gameplay.
  • Season Pass: Purchasing the Season Pass can significantly accelerate the process by providing valuable rewards and resources.
  • Resource Farming: Your efficiency in farming resources and raiding other bases will directly impact the time required to reach your goal.
  • Builder Usage: Once you have acquired your sixth builder, utilize them strategically for optimal progress in both your Home Village and Builder Base.
  • Patience: Unlocking the sixth builder is a gradual process that requires patience and persistence.
