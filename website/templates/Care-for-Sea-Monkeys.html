Setting Up the Sea Monkey Tank: A Comprehensive Guide

Materials Required:

  • Clean plastic container with a deep base (at least 2 liters)
  • Unchlorinated water (distilled or bottled)
  • Salt packet (water purifier)

Instructions:

  1. Choose a Suitable Container: Select a plastic container made from food-grade materials to ensure the safety of your Sea Monkeys. It should have a deep base (at least 2 liters) to provide ample vertical space for swimming and egg-laying.

  2. Prepare Unchlorinated Water: Sea Monkeys are sensitive to chlorine, which can be present in tap water. Use distilled or bottled water that is free of any chemical additives.

  3. Add the Salt Packet: Open the salt packet labeled "Water Purifier" and carefully pour its contents into the water. This salt solution serves as the optimal environment for Sea Monkeys to thrive.

  4. Allow Water to Warm: Place the container in a shaded area at room temperature (approximately 72°F). Allow the water to rest for 24-36 hours to warm up and dissolve the salt completely.

Feeding the Sea Monkeys:

Schedule:

  • Sea Monkeys begin feeding approximately five days after hatching.

Method:

  • Gently sprinkle a small pinch of Sea Monkey food into the tank. Avoid overfeeding to prevent fouling the water.

Frequency:

  • Feed every five days until algae develops in the tank, which typically takes several weeks.
  • Once algae appears, gradually reduce feeding frequency to weekly.

Maintaining the Tank:

Aeration:

  • Sea Monkeys require oxygenated water to survive. Aerate the water twice daily using an air pump or a turkey baster to manually circulate the water.

Temperature:

  • Sea Monkeys prefer warm temperatures around 72°F. Keep the tank in a shaded area at room temperature or use a small aquarium heater to regulate the temperature.

Water Changes:

  • Only change the water if it becomes visibly cloudy or develops a foul odor. Use unchlorinated water to refill the tank.

Ensuring Sea Monkey Health:

White Spots:

  • White spots in the tank can indicate bacteria. Remove any visible white spots using a clean pipette or turkey baster.

Light:

  • Provide a gentle light source to entertain the Sea Monkeys and encourage swimming activity.

Mating Behavior:

  • Male Sea Monkeys have visible whiskers, while females may carry eggs in a transparent sac. Observe the Sea Monkeys for mating behavior to ensure population growth.

Lifespan and Longevity:

  • Sea Monkeys can typically live for an average of two years. With proper care and maintenance, the population can sustain itself indefinitely.
