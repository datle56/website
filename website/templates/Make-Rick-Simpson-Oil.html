Making Rick Simpson Oil: A Comprehensive Guide for Medicinal Relief

Introduction

Rick Simpson Oil (RSO) is a potent cannabis oil extracted from the dried buds of the Indica strain of cannabis. This oil has gained significant recognition for its purported medicinal properties, which include pain relief, anxiety reduction, and other therapeutic benefits. In this comprehensive guide, we will explore the step-by-step process of making RSO at home, ensuring safety and efficiency.

Materials Required:

  • 1 pound (16 ounces) dried cannabis buds (preferably Indica)
  • 1 gallon of food-grade alcohol or isopropyl alcohol (if not ingesting)
  • Large bucket or container
  • Mixing bowl
  • Wooden spoon or spatula
  • Cheesecloth or coffee strainer
  • Rice cooker or double boiler
  • Plastic syringe with catheter tip

Step-by-Step Instructions:

1. Cannabis Dissolution in Alcohol

  • In a spacious bucket or container, combine the dried cannabis buds with the food-grade alcohol.
  • Using a wooden spoon or spatula, stir vigorously and crush the cannabis buds thoroughly until most of the material (approximately 80%) has dissolved into the alcohol.

2. Draining and Repeated Dissolution

  • Pour the alcohol solution into a new container using a cheesecloth or coffee strainer to remove any residual plant matter.
  • Add more alcohol to the remaining cannabis buds and stir again to dissolve the remaining material.
  • Drain the solution into the same container as the previous step.

3. Alcohol Evaporation

  • Transfer the strained alcohol solution into a rice cooker or double boiler.
  • Set the temperature to between 210-230°F (100-110°C) and allow the alcohol to evaporate gradually.
  • Replenish the alcohol as needed to maintain a steady evaporation process.
  • Continue evaporating until the liquid in the cooker or boiler turns dark, thick, and viscous.

4. Oil Siphoning

  • Once the alcohol has been thoroughly evaporated, a dark, resinous oil will remain.
  • Use a plastic syringe with a catheter tip to carefully siphon the oil from the cooker or boiler.
  • Transfer the oil into small containers or syringes for convenient storage.

Usage and Methods:

Rick Simpson Oil can be used for various medicinal applications. Common methods of usage include:

  • Topical Application: Apply directly to the skin for pain relief, inflammation, and skin conditions.
  • Oral Ingestion: Take small doses orally under the tongue or mixed with food for overall well-being and therapeutic effects.
  • Ingestion with Food: Add a small amount to food or beverages for a discreet and convenient way to consume RSO.

Cautionary Notes:

  • Ventilation: Always work in a well-ventilated area to avoid inhaling alcohol fumes.
  • Flammability: Alcohol is flammable. Avoid naked flames or sparks while working with it.
  • Medical Consultation: Before using Rick Simpson Oil, consult with a healthcare professional, especially if you have any underlying medical conditions or are taking medications.
  • Dosage: Start with a small dose and gradually increase as needed. Monitor your body's response and adjust accordingly.
