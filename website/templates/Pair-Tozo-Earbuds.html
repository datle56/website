How to Connect Your TOZO Earbuds

Step-by-Step Instructions for All Devices:

  1. Remove the earbuds from the charging case: Gently lift both earbuds out of their designated slots within the charging case.

  2. Activate Bluetooth on your device: Navigate to the Bluetooth settings on your smartphone, computer, or other device. Toggle the Bluetooth switch to the "On" position.

  3. Search for "TOZO Earbuds": Once Bluetooth is activated, your device will begin scanning for nearby devices. Look for the entry labeled "TOZO Earbuds" in the list of detected Bluetooth connections.

  4. Select and Connect: Tap or click on "TOZO Earbuds" from the list. Your device will initiate the pairing process. Once successful, you will hear a voice prompt saying "BT connected."

Additional Instructions for Specific Devices:

Android:

  • Disconnect from previous devices: If your earbuds have previously been paired with another device, turn off Bluetooth on that device to ensure proper connection with your current device.

  • Enter pairing mode: If necessary, press and hold the button located on the back of one of the earbuds for approximately 5 seconds. This will disconnect the earbuds from any existing connections and enter pairing mode, indicated by a flashing LED light.

iPhone and iPad:

  • Disable Bluetooth on previous devices: Similar to Android, turn off Bluetooth on any devices that have previously been connected to the earbuds.

  • Enter pairing mode: Press and hold the button on the back of one of the earbuds for 5 seconds to initiate pairing mode. The LED light will flash to indicate that the earbuds are ready to pair.

Windows and Mac:

  • Ensure disconnection from other devices: Make sure that the earbuds are not currently connected to any other devices via Bluetooth.

  • Add new device: Access the Bluetooth settings on your computer. Click on the option to "Add device" or "Connect."

  • Select "TOZO Earbuds": Choose "Bluetooth" as the connection type and then select "TOZO Earbuds" from the list of available devices.

Troubleshooting Tips:

  • Restart the earbuds: Turn off and then turn on the earbuds to refresh their connection.

  • Check battery levels: Ensure that both the earbuds and the charging case have sufficient battery power. Poor audio quality can be a sign of low battery.

  • Eliminate interference: Turn off Bluetooth on any other devices that may be in close proximity and potentially interfering with the earbuds' connection.

  • Reset the earbuds: If you experience any unusual sound quality or difficulty syncing the left and right earbuds, consider resetting the earbuds to their factory settings. Refer to the earbuds' user manual for specific instructions on performing a factory reset.
