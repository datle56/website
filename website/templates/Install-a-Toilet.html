A Step-by-Step Guide to Toilet Installation: A Comprehensive Tutorial with Detailed Images


Transform Your Bathroom with a Toilet Makeover: A Step-by-Step Guide

Are you tired of your old, grody toilet? Ready to upgrade your bathroom with a fresh, gleaming one? Fear not, bathroom enthusiasts! We're here to guide you through the ins and outs of removing an old toilet and installing a new one – a DIY project that's easier than you think. So, grab your tools, roll up your sleeves, and let's embark on a sanitary adventure!


Removing an Old Toilet

Materials:

  • Adjustable wrench
  • Sponge or towel
  • Gloves
  • Bucket or bowl
  • Toilet flange tool (optional)

Steps:

  1. Measure the Distance to the Wall: Determine the distance from the finished wall surface to the center of the toilet bolts. Standard toilets typically have a distance of 12 inches.
  2. Turn Off Water Supply: Locate the water supply valve behind the toilet and turn it clockwise to shut off the water supply.
  3. Flush the Toilet: Flush the toilet to drain the tank and bowl of water.
  4. Sponge Remaining Water: Put on gloves and use a sponge or towel to absorb any remaining water in the tank or bowl.
  5. Disconnect Water Supply Line: Using an adjustable wrench, disconnect the water supply line from the fill valve on the toilet tank and the shutoff valve on the wall.
  6. Remove Tank Bolts: Use the adjustable wrench to loosen the two tank bolts located at the back of the tank. Gently lift the tank straight up and set it aside securely.
  7. Remove Toilet Base: Grip the toilet base firmly and lift it straight up off the floor. If the toilet is particularly heavy, you may need assistance.
  8. Remove Wax Seal: Using a toilet flange tool or a flathead screwdriver, carefully pry away the old wax seal from the toilet flange on the floor. Clean the flange thoroughly to remove any residue.
  9. Plug Drain Opening: Insert a rubber drain plug into the toilet flange opening to prevent sewer gases from escaping.

Installing a New Toilet

Materials:

  • New toilet
  • Toilet bolts and nuts (if not included with the toilet)
  • Wax ring
  • Adjustable wrench
  • Tank bolt gasket
  • Plumber's putty or caulk
  • Screwdriver

Steps:

  1. Install Toilet Bolts: Thread new toilet bolts into the flange nuts and tighten them until they are flush with the top of the nuts.
  2. Install Wax Ring: Apply a thin layer of plumber's putty or caulk around the base of the wax ring. Center the wax ring on the flange, making sure the tapered side faces down.
  3. Position Toilet Base: Carefully lower the toilet base onto the flange, aligning the bolts with the mounting holes in the toilet base.
  4. Tighten Toilet Bolts: Tighten the toilet bolts evenly using the adjustable wrench. Avoid overtightening.
  5. Cut Bolts and Install Caps: If the bolts extend too far, use a hacksaw to cut off the excess length. Install decorative caps to conceal the bolts.
  6. Set Tank: Place the tank bolt gasket on the tank bolts. Carefully lift the tank and set it onto the bowl, aligning the bolts with the holes in the tank.
  7. Tighten Tank Bolts: Tighten the tank bolts evenly using the adjustable wrench.
  8. Connect Water Supply: Connect a new water supply line to the fill valve on the tank and the shutoff valve on the wall.
  9. Turn on Water Supply and Flush: Turn on the water supply and flush the toilet to fill the tank and bowl with water.
  10. Caulk or Seal: Caulk or seal around the front base of the toilet where it meets the floor to prevent water seepage. Leave the back uncaulked to allow for water to escape in case of a leak.

Conclusion: A Seamless Renovation

By following these steps carefully, you can successfully remove an old toilet and install a new one, transforming your bathroom with minimal effort. Remember to secure the toilet firmly, ensure a proper seal with the wax ring, and connect the water supply and tank seamlessly. With a touch of caulking for a polished finish, your new toilet will not only enhance the aesthetics of your bathroom but also provide years of trouble-free use. Enjoy the satisfaction of a successful DIY plumbing project and the refreshed ambiance of your renovated space.
