Ace Riddle School 3: A Step-by-Step Guide with Visual Aids


Prepare for a Thrilling Escape Mission: Outsmarting the Teachers and Breaking Free from the Mysterious School

Imagine being trapped in a school where the teachers are anything but ordinary. Each one holds a unique secret that must be uncovered to secure your freedom. Are you ready to embark on an adventure filled with cunning, strategy, and a healthy dose of mischief?

In this captivating game, you'll navigate treacherous hallways, outsmart enigmatic teachers, and uncover hidden combinations to unlock the path to your long-awaited escape. But beware, every move you make will determine your fate. Can you outwit Mr. Soggy, evade the janitor, and secure the precious combination that leads to your ultimate triumph?


Getting Out of Mr. Soggy's Room

  1. Find a Ruler and a Piece of Gum: Locate a ruler and a piece of gum. They are commonly found in school supplies or vending machines.

  2. Make a Sticky Ruler: Attach the gum to one end of the ruler, creating a sticky surface.

  3. Retrieve a Rubber Band from the Vent: Position the sticky ruler inside the vent above Mr. Soggy's head. The gum will adhere to the rubber band, allowing you to retrieve it.

  4. Knock Off Mr. Soggy's Glasses: Use the rubber band as a projectile. Aim it at Mr. Soggy's glasses, knocking them off his face.

  5. Exit the Room: With Mr. Soggy temporarily blind, seize the opportunity to quickly exit the room and proceed towards your next destination.

Getting the Janitor Out of His Closet

  1. Acquire a Penny and a Dime: Obtain a penny and a dime. These coins can be found in various locations within the school, such as desks, lockers, or the floor.

  2. Retrieve the Dime Using the Ruler: Position the long ruler inside the gap beneath the supply closet door. Use the penny as bait to attract the dime. The janitor will reach for the penny, allowing you to swiftly retrieve the dime using the ruler.

  3. Clog the Toilet: Flush the toilet in the men's room repeatedly until it becomes clogged.

  4. Vacate the Janitor's Closet: The janitor will leave his closet to attend to the clog, providing you with the opportunity to escape.

Getting Richy's Combination

  1. Locate Mrs. Oddverb's Room: Identify Mrs. Oddverb's classroom based on the information provided in the game.

  2. Obtain a Quarter from the Janitor's Closet: Return to the janitor's closet and retrieve a quarter from the table.

  3. Inquire about Richy's Combination: Approach Mrs. Oddverb in the teacher's lounge and politely ask for Richy's locker combination.

  4. Retrieve the Nickel from the Drawer: While Mrs. Oddverb is distracted, quickly open the middle drawer of her desk and collect the nickel.

Rescuing Richy

  1. Enter the Combination: Proceed to Richy's locker and input the combination "5-3-7-8-8-0-7-8" into the lock.

  2. Obtain the Quarter: As a token of gratitude, Richy will hand you a quarter.

Getting the Keyboard Key and Button

  1. Retrieve the Keyboard Key: Locate Miss Count's classroom and search for the computer keyboard key. It may be found on a desk, the floor, or in a drawer.

  2. Obtain a Penny from Mrs. Flask's Room: Visit Mrs. Flask's classroom and find a penny on the floor or in a desk.

  3. Create a Button: Insert the keyboard key into the machine located in Mrs. Flask's room. Use the penny to press the button, creating a new button.

Getting Pudding for Chubb

  1. Purchase Pudding from Mrs. Chubb: Head to the lunchroom and purchase a bowl of pudding from Mrs. Chubb for $1.

  2. Feed the Pudding to Chubb: Locate Chubb and offer him the pudding. He will readily accept it and proceed to roll down the stairs, creating a pathway for your escape.

Escape the School

  1. Obtain the Key: Go to the office and inform the receptionist that you are there to meet Mr. Potato.

  2. Receive the Key: The receptionist will provide you with a key.

  3. Locate the Exit Doors: Exit the office and proceed to the exit doors on the first floor.

  4. Escape: Use the key to unlock the exit door and make your way out of the school, successfully completing your mission.


And there you have it, folks! The ultimate guide to escaping Mr. Soggy's Room and completing all the tasks in School: The Final Destination. With a little ingenuity, a dash of bravery, and a whole lot of gum, you too can conquer this challenging game and emerge victorious on the other side. So gather your quarters, dimes, and rubber bands, and get ready for an unforgettable adventure! Remember, anything is possible with a little bit of creativity and a whole lot of determination. Good luck, escapees!
