Step-by-Step Guide to Installing an Operating System on a New Computer

Installing and Setting Up Windows 10

Creating a Windows Installation Drive:

  1. Download the Media Creation Tool from Microsoft's official website.
  2. Insert a USB flash drive (8GB or larger) into your computer.
  3. Run the Media Creation Tool and choose "Create installation media (USB flash drive, DVD, or ISO file)".
  4. Select your language, edition, and architecture (32-bit or 64-bit).
  5. Select the USB flash drive as the destination media.
  6. Click "Next" and follow the on-screen instructions to complete the creation process.

Backup Your Files (Optional):

Before proceeding with the installation, it's highly recommended to back up your important files to an external hard drive or cloud storage service to prevent any data loss.

Booting from the Installation Drive:

  1. Insert the installation drive into a USB port on your computer.
  2. Restart your computer.
  3. When prompted, press any key to boot from the USB drive.

Language and Region Settings:

  1. Once the installation wizard starts, select your preferred language, time zone, and keyboard layout.
  2. Click "Next" to proceed.

Installing Windows 10:

  1. Click the "Install Now" button.
  2. Enter your Windows product key, if available. Otherwise, you can choose to activate Windows later.
  3. Select the edition of Windows you wish to install.
  4. Accept the license terms by clicking "I agree".
  5. Choose the type of installation:
    • Upgrade: This option preserves your files and settings from a previous Windows installation.
    • Custom: This option allows you to select a specific drive for installation and create additional partitions if desired.

Drive Selection and Formatting:

  1. Select the drive where you want to install Windows.
  2. If the drive is not formatted or contains previous data, you may need to format it by clicking "Format".
  3. Click "Next" to begin the installation process.

Post-Installation Configuration:

  1. Once the installation is complete, remove the installation drive and restart your computer.
  2. Sign in with your Microsoft account or create a local user account.
  3. Configure Cortana, your digital assistant, by choosing the appropriate settings.
  4. Adjust privacy settings to control what information is shared with Microsoft and other apps.

Installing and Setting Up macOS

Backup Your Files:

  1. Before proceeding with the installation, back up your data to an external hard drive or Time Machine to prevent any data loss.

Entering Recovery Mode:

  1. Restart your Mac and immediately hold down the "Command (⌘)" and "R" keys until you see the Apple logo or the recovery mode utility.

Connecting to Wi-Fi and Reformatting:

  1. Click "Wi-Fi" from the menu bar and connect to a known Wi-Fi network.
  2. Select "Disk Utility" from the menu bar.
  3. Click "Erase" and select "APFS" as the format for your hard drive (recommended for SSDs). Otherwise, choose "Mac OS Extended (Journaled)" for traditional hard drives.

Reinstalling macOS:

  1. Quit Disk Utility and return to the recovery mode menu.
  2. Click "Reinstall macOS" and follow the on-screen instructions.
  3. Select the hard drive you want to install macOS on.

Post-Installation Configuration:

  1. Choose your language and time zone.
  2. Connect to Wi-Fi and enter your network password.
  3. Decide whether to transfer data from another device using Migration Assistant or choose not to transfer.
  4. Sign in with your Apple ID or create a new one.
  5. Accept the terms and conditions and create a user account.
  6. Configure Siri, your digital assistant, by selecting the appropriate settings.
  7. Adjust privacy settings to control what information is shared with Apple and other apps.
