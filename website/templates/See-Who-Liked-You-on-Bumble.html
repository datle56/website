Unveiling the Secrets: How to See Who Likes You on Bumble

Are you curious about the people who find you attractive on Bumble? It's no longer a mystery! With a few clever tricks, you can unlock the hidden roster of admirers who have secretly swiped right on your profile.

Get ready to embark on a thrilling journey as we reveal two ingenious methods to expose the truth. First, we'll show you how to expand your search range and make those hidden admirers appear right before your eyes. Then, we'll take a deep dive into the web browser's source code and unleash the power of JSON to identify those who have fallen for your charms.

So, brace yourself for a surge of excitement as we guide you through these simple but effective techniques to quench your curiosity and unravel the truth about who likes you on Bumble!


Method 1: Expanding Distance

Step 1: Modify Distance Settings

  • Launch the Bumble app on your device.
  • Navigate to your profile by tapping the profile icon in the bottom right corner.
  • Scroll down to the "Distance" section.
  • Adjust the distance range to the maximum value available. This effectively increases the radius of your search, allowing you to see profiles from a wider geographical area.

Step 2: Restart the Bumble App

  • Once you have modified the distance settings, force close the Bumble app completely.
  • To do this on an iPhone, double-press the home button and swipe up on the Bumble app preview to close it.
  • On an Android device, open the Settings app, navigate to "Apps" or "Application Manager," locate Bumble, and force stop it.

Step 3: View Potential Matches

  • Relaunch the Bumble app.
  • The profiles of individuals who have expressed interest in you (i.e., "liked" you) within the expanded distance range will now appear at the top of your match queue.
  • You can swipe right on these profiles to start a connection or swipe left to pass.

Method 2: Web Browser Source Code Trick

Step 1: Open Bumble on a Computer

  • Open Google Chrome or another web browser on your computer.
  • Navigate to bumble.com and log in to your account.

Step 2: Open Developer Console

  • Press the following keyboard shortcuts depending on your operating system:
    • Windows/Linux: Ctrl + Shift + J
    • Mac: Cmd + Option + J

Step 3: Navigate to Network Tab

  • In the opened developer console window, click on the "Network" tab.

Step 4: Refresh the Page

  • Click the refresh button in the browser toolbar or press F5.
  • This will refresh the Bumble matches page and generate a new list of potential profiles.

Step 5: Identify URL for Encounters

  • In the "Network" tab, look for and click on the request that has the following URL: "/mwebapi.phtml?SERVERGETENCOUNTERS"
  • This URL represents the server request for fetching potential matches.

Step 6: Open Response Tab

  • Click on the "Response" tab in the developer console.

Step 7: Copy Response Data

  • Select and copy all the text displayed in the "Response" box.

Step 8: Open JSON Reader

  • Open a JSON reader in a new tab in the same web browser. You can use an online tool like JSONLint or a browser extension like "JSONView."

Step 9: Paste Copied Data

  • Paste the copied text from the Bumble response box into the JSON reader.

Step 10: Expand ClientEncounters Section

  • Locate the "ClientEncounters" object in the JSON data.
  • Expand the "Results" section within "ClientEncounters."

Step 11: Check User Vote Values

  • You will see a list of numbered folders (0-9) within "Results."
  • Expand each numbered folder and look for the "hasuservoted" property.

Step 12: Analyze Vote Values

  • If the value of "hasuservoted" is "True," it indicates that the user with that folder number has likely expressed interest in you by swiping right.
  • If the value of "hasuservoted" is "False," it means that the user has not yet swiped right or has swiped left on your profile.
