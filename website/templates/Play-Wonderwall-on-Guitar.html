Getting Started

Before embarking on your musical journey, let's gather the necessary tools:

  • Chord Progression: Embark on a harmonious adventure with Em7, G, Dsus4, and A7sus4.
  • Strum Pattern: Let your fingers dance to the rhythm of DD D UDUD D U (first bar) and DU D D DU U UDUDU (second bar).

Chords

  1. Em7: Cradle the first and second strings with your third and fourth fingers at the third fret. Simultaneously, embrace the fourth and fifth strings with your first and second fingers on the second fret.
  2. G: Shift the second finger from the fifth string to the third fret of the sixth string while maintaining the Em7 shape.
  3. Dsus4: Employ the Em7 shape and add a touch of dissonance by fretting the third string on the second fret with your first finger.
  4. A7sus4: Reposition the Em7 shape one string over, embracing the fifth, fourth, and third strings.
  5. Cadd9: From the A7sus4, gently move your second finger down to the third fret of the fifth string.
  6. G/F#: Lift the second finger and relocate the first finger to the second fret of the sixth string, transforming the G into a G/F#.

Strumming Pattern

  1. Muted Practice: Silently simulate the strumming motion to internalize the rhythm.
  2. First Bar: Unleash the strum down-down-down-up-down-up-down.
  3. Second Bar: Gracefully strum down-up-down-down-down-up-up-up-down-up-down-up-down.
