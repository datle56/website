How to Make Yahoo! Your Homepage: A Comprehensive Guide for Common Browsers

Customizing your browser homepage to your preferred website can enhance your browsing experience. If you prefer the vast array of news, entertainment, and services offered by Yahoo!, here's a detailed guide on how to set Yahoo! as your homepage in various browsers:

Google Chrome

  1. Enable the Home button: Click on the three dots in the top-right corner of the browser window and select "Settings." Navigate to the "Appearance" section and enable "Show Home button."
  2. Enter Yahoo! URL: Below the "Homepage" section, type "yahoo.com" into the text field. You can also enter a specific Yahoo! section, such as "sports.yahoo.com" or "news.yahoo.com."

Apple Safari

  1. Open Settings: Click on the "Safari" menu in the top-left corner and select "Preferences."
  2. Navigate to General: In the preferences window, select the "General" tab.
  3. Enter Yahoo! URL: In the "Homepage" field, enter "yahoo.com." You can also specify a specific Yahoo! section here if desired.

Mozilla Firefox

  1. Drag a Yahoo! tab: Open a new tab and navigate to "yahoo.com." Drag the Yahoo! tab onto the Home button located in the top-left corner of the browser window.
  2. Drop and confirm: Drop the tab onto the Home button and a confirmation message will appear. Click "Yes" to set Yahoo! as your homepage.

Microsoft Edge

  1. Enable the Home button: Click on the three dots in the top-right corner of the browser window and select "Settings." Navigate to the "Appearance" tab.
  2. Enter Yahoo! URL: Under the "Set button URL" field, type "yahoo.com." You can also add a specific Yahoo! section.

Tips for a Smooth Experience

  • Browser Hijacker Detection: If your homepage keeps changing back to an undesired site, you may have a browser hijacker. Scan your computer using reputable malware removal software to clear any malicious programs.
  • Alternative Startup Options: In addition to setting Yahoo! as your homepage, you can also set it as your "Start" page (the page that opens when launching the browser) or "New Tab" page (the page that opens when creating a new tab).
  • Browser-Specific Settings: Note that different browsers may have slightly different settings for homepage configuration. It's best to follow the specific instructions provided for your browser above.
