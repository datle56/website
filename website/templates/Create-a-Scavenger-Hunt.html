Home Scavenger Hunt

How to Play:

  1. Hide the Items: Conceal small trinkets, toys, or household items around your home. Avoid hiding objects in dangerous or hard-to-reach places.
  2. Create a List: Compile a detailed list of the hidden items, providing clear descriptions or clues to facilitate their discovery.
  3. Divide into Teams: Split the participants into multiple teams of equal size.
  4. Set the Rules: Explain the rules of the game, including the time limit, the number of items to find, and any specific search parameters.
  5. Commence the Hunt: Provide the teams with the list and start the scavenger hunt.
  6. Find the Items: The teams embark on a quest to locate all the hidden items, ticking them off as they find them.
  7. Declare the Winner: The first team to find all the listed items is declared the winner.

Tips:

  • For younger players, hide easily identifiable objects such as brightly colored toys or familiar household items.
  • Consider offering a "consolation prize" to teams that don't win to encourage participation and maintain enthusiasm.

Park Scavenger Hunt

How to Play:

  1. Scout the Park: Familiarize yourself with the park and identify interesting landmarks, historical markers, or natural features.
  2. Create a List: Compile a list of the chosen landmarks, providing their names or detailed descriptions.
  3. Capture the Evidence: Equip the teams with cameras or smartphones and instruct them to take pictures of themselves at each landmark to serve as proof of their discovery.
  4. Set the Boundaries: Define the search area within the park to prevent teams from wandering too far.
  5. Start the Hunt: Initiate the scavenger hunt and provide the teams with the list of landmarks.
  6. Explore and Discover: The teams explore the park, searching for the designated landmarks and taking pictures to document their findings.
  7. Submit the Evidence: Once all the landmarks have been captured, the teams submit their photographs for verification.
  8. Determine the Winner: The team with the most accurate and complete set of photographs wins the scavenger hunt.

Tips:

  • Limit the number of landmarks to ensure the hunt is manageable for younger players.
  • Choose landmarks that are clearly visible and easily identifiable.

Mall Scavenger Hunt

How to Play:

  1. Obtain Permission: Contact the mall management and request permission to conduct the scavenger hunt.
  2. Map Out the Stores: Visit the mall and identify specific stores that carry specific items.
  3. Create a List: Compile a list of the desired items and their corresponding stores.
  4. Provide the List: Distribute the list to the teams, explaining that they must ask store employees for the listed items.
  5. Collect and Verify: The teams visit the stores and request the items. Store employees may ask for proof of purchase or additional information for verification purposes.
  6. Cross Off the List: As teams successfully collect the items, they cross them off the list.
  7. Declare the Winner: The team that completes the list first is declared the winner.

Tips:

  • Check with store managers ahead of time to ensure the items on your list are available.
  • Be polite and respectful when interacting with mall employees.
