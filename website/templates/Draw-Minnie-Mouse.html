How to Draw Minnie Mouse

Method 1: Minnie's Face

  1. Outline the Head: Start by sketching a circle to represent Minnie's head.

  2. Create the Ears: Add two smaller circles on either side of the head for her iconic ears.

  3. Shape the Nose: Draw an oval in the center of the head for her nose. Above the oval, add a short horizontal line to define her nose bridge.

  4. Define the Eyes: Position two circles above the nose line for her eyes. Add black dots within the circles for her pupils and gently curved lines above them for her eyelashes.

  5. Create the Mouth: Draw a crescent-shaped line for her mouth, with an "M" shape at the center to depict her tongue.

  6. Add Cheeks and Bow: Draw two small circles on either side of her mouth for her rosy cheeks. Top it off with a small, triangular bow on the top of her head.

  7. Color: Use pink for her face, black for her eyes and nose bridge, red for her tongue, and your choice of color for her bow.

Method 2: Full Body Minnie

  1. Draw the Body: Start with a large circle for her head and a smaller circle below it for her torso, connected by a straight line for her neck.

  2. Add Ears, Hands, and Feet: Draw two pointed ears on top of the head, two small ovals for her hands, and two symmetrical ovals for her feet.

  3. Create the Gloves and Face: Sketch ovals around her hands for her gloves. Draw Minnie's face on the head with large eyes, a small nose, and a wide smile.

  4. Dress and Shoes: Create a simple dress by drawing a curved line from the bottom of her torso to her feet. Add two black dots beneath her feet for her shoes.

  5. Ribbon: Draw a thin line from Minnie's neck to the top of her head, representing the ribbon around her neck.

  6. Color: Fill in Minnie's dress with a color of your choice, her gloves with white, her shoes with black, and her ribbon with your desired color.

Method 3: Classic Minnie

  1. Divide the Head: Draw a circle and divide it into four equal sections using two perpendicular lines intersecting at the center.

  2. Create the Nose: Position an oval at the intersection of the lines for her nose. Above the oval, draw a short horizontal line to define her nose bridge.

  3. Define the Eyes and Smile: Create two large ovals within the top two sections for her eyes and a curved line in the bottom section for her smile. Draw a curved line below the smile to define her chin.

  4. Add Cheeks, Ribbon, and Ears: Add two small circles on either side of her mouth for her cheeks. Draw a triangular ribbon on her head and two pointed ears on either side of the ribbon.

  5. Eyelashes and Tongue: Draw curved lines above her eyes for her eyelashes and an "M" shape within her mouth for her tongue.

  6. Clean Up and Color: Erase any unnecessary lines and clean up your drawing. Color Minnie's nose, eyes, cheeks, ears, ribbon, and dress in your desired colors.
