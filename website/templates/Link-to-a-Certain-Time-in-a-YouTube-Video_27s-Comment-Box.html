How to Link to a Specific Time in a YouTube Video

Introduction

YouTube has become a ubiquitous platform for sharing and consuming video content. Often, we encounter situations where we want to share not only a video itself, but also a specific moment or highlight within that video. Fortunately, YouTube provides a simple yet powerful feature that allows users to link to a specific time within a video, enabling easy reference and precise discussions. In this comprehensive guide, we will explore the step-by-step process of creating a timestamp link, both on mobile and desktop platforms.

Linking on Mobile Devices

  1. Locate the Desired Video and Pause at the Target Time: Start by finding the video you wish to link to in the YouTube app. Navigate to the specific moment within the video and pause it at the precise time you want to highlight.

  2. Access the Comment Section: Scroll down below the video and tap the "Add a public comment" field located just below the video description.

  3. Enter the Time Stamp: Carefully note the time stamp displayed in the bottom-left corner of the video player. It will appear in a format such as "0:00 / 5:00". Type the exact time stamp into the comment box, ensuring that you include both the minutes and seconds.

  4. Publish the Comment with Time-Linked URL: Tap the "Post" button to publish your comment. Your comment will now include a hyperlink that automatically skips to the specified time when clicked.

Linking on Desktop Computers

  1. Find the Target Video and Note the Time Stamp: Locate the YouTube video you want to link to on your desktop or laptop browser. Pause the video at the desired time and pay attention to the time stamp displayed in the bottom-left corner of the video player. It will also be presented in the format of "0:00 / 5:00".

  2. Navigate to the Comment Section: Scroll down below the video description and click on the "Add a public comment" box.

  3. Type the Time Stamp in the Comment: Enter the time stamp into the comment box, ensuring that you type it exactly as it appears in the video player's display. For instance, if the time stamp is "2:03 / 5:00", type "Check out 2:03".

  4. Post the Comment with Timed Link: Click the "Comment" button to publish your comment. The comment will now include a hyperlink that allows viewers to jump to the specified time when clicked.

Additional Considerations

  • Precise Time Indication: It is crucial to type the time stamp accurately, including both minutes and seconds. Any deviation will result in the link pointing to a different time within the video.

  • Case-Sensitive Time Stamp: The time stamp is case-sensitive, meaning that uppercase and lowercase letters matter. Therefore, ensure that you enter the time stamp exactly as it appears in the video player.

  • Link Availability: The timestamped link will only be available for the specific video it was created for. It will not work if the video is deleted or made private.
