Mastering the Art of Writing Dates in French: A Comprehensive Guide

Embark on a linguistic adventure as we delve into the intricacies of writing dates in the enchanting language of French. From the melody of month names to the precision of day formatting, this guide unlocks the secrets of expressing time with finesse. Whether you're a Francophile navigating a foreign calendar or a language learner seeking to expand your vocabulary, this comprehensive resource will empower you to communicate dates like a native. Let's begin our journey into the temporal tapestry of French!


Writing Dates in French

Months:

The French language has unique names for each month, much like English. Here is a list of the French month names:

  • Janvier (January)
  • Février (February)
  • Mars (March)
  • Avril (April)
  • Mai (May)
  • Juin (June)
  • Juillet (July)
  • Août (August)
  • Septembre (September)
  • Octobre (October)
  • Novembre (November)
  • Décembre (December)

Format:

When writing a date in French, follow the "day month year" format without using commas. For instance, instead of "February 15, 2023," you would write "15 février 2023."

Exception:

When indicating the first day of the month, use "1er" in written form and "premier" when speaking. For example, "1er janvier" (January 1st) or "Premier janvier" (spoken).

Including Days of the Week:

To include the day of the week, place it before the date. For example, "lundi 10 octobre 2023" (Monday, October 10, 2023).

Articles with Days of the Week:

French days of the week use the article "le." However, "samedi" (Saturday) takes a different form:

  • "Le lundi" (Monday)
  • "Le mardi" (Tuesday)
  • "Le mercredi" (Wednesday)
  • "Le jeudi" (Thursday)
  • "Le vendredi" (Friday)
  • "Le samedi" (a specific Saturday)
  • "Samedi" (Saturday in general, e.g., "Je vais au cinéma samedi.")

Phrases with Dates:

Today's Date:

  • "Quelle est la date aujourd'hui?" (What's today's date?)

Day of the Week:

  • "Quel jour sommes-nous aujourd'hui?" (What day is it today?)

Answer:

  • "Aujourd'hui, c'est le..." (Today is...)

Preposition "en":

Use the preposition "en" to indicate "in" a month or year. For example:

  • "En mars" (in March)
  • "En 2024" (in 2024)

In conclusion, writing dates in French follows a specific set of rules and conventions. By adhering to these guidelines, you can effectively express dates in French, both in written and spoken form. Whether you're traveling, communicating with Francophones, or simply exploring the nuances of the language, understanding the proper way to write dates will enhance your proficiency in French and allow you to navigate your interactions with confidence.
