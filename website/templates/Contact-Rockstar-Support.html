Calling All Gamers: Get Direct Access to Rockstar Games!

Calling all gamers! Do you have a burning question or a technical issue that needs immediate attention? Or maybe you just want to share your love for the legendary Rockstar Games? Gear up and prepare to connect with the gaming giants behind your favorite virtual worlds!


Urgent Help:

If you require immediate assistance, you can reach Rockstar Games through the following channels:

  • Phone: Call their support number for your region:
    • US: 866-922-8694
    • Canada: 800-269-5721
    • UK: 08701 200060

For urgent inquiries, it is highly recommended to contact Rockstar Games via phone as it provides the quickest response time.

Non-Urgent Inquiries:

For non-urgent inquiries, you can use the following methods to contact Rockstar Games:

  • Email: Send an email to support@rockstargames.com.
  • Social Media: Post a message or comment on Rockstar Games' official social media pages:
    • Twitter: @RockstarSupport
    • Facebook: @rockstargames
    • Instagram: @rockstargames

While social media can be a convenient way to reach out, it may take longer for Rockstar Games to respond compared to phone or email support.

Other Options:

In addition to the above methods, you can also contact Rockstar Games through the following alternative channels:

  • Support Page: Visit Rockstar Games' official support page at https://support.rockstargames.com/hc/en-us. The website offers a comprehensive knowledge base and troubleshooting guides that may resolve your issue without needing to contact support directly.

  • Mailing Address: To send a written letter, address it to:

Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. 622 Broadway, New York, NY 10012, United States

Written correspondence may take longer to process compared to other methods.

Tips for Contacting Rockstar Games:

  • When contacting support, provide as much clear and detailed information as possible about your issue.
  • Be patient and remember that support representatives are working to assist as many people as possible.
  • If your issue persists or you do not receive a response within a reasonable timeframe, you can try reaching out again through a different channel.
  • For in-game support, you can also access the Rockstar Support Center from within the game.
