Step into the Metaverse as a VTuber: Your Ultimate Guide to Virtual Embodiment

Prepare to dive headfirst into the captivating world of VTubing, where virtual avatars take center stage. As a master of this digital realm, let us guide you through the essential equipment, software, and know-how to craft your own unique VTuber persona. From high-flying webcams to the secrets of creating a captivating 3D model, we'll unlock the secrets to transforming yourself into a mesmerizing virtual star.


Essential Equipment for VTubing

Computer:

  • High-Speed Internet: VTubing requires a stable and fast internet connection for smooth streaming and data transfer.
  • 8 GB RAM or More: Ample memory is crucial to handle the demanding software and background processes during VTubing. Consider upgrading to 16 GB or higher for optimal performance.

Webcam:

  • Motion-Tracking Webcam: Invest in a webcam that supports facial motion tracking technology, such as Apple TrueDepth, Microsoft Azure Kinect, or webcams specifically designed for VTubing. These devices capture subtle facial expressions and movements, allowing for realistic avatar animations.

VR Headset (Optional):

  • Full-Body Tracking: For a more immersive VTubing experience, consider using a VR headset that enables full-body tracking. This allows you to move and interact with your virtual environment using your own body movements.

VTubing Software

Avatar Creation and Animation:

  • Facerig: A comprehensive software that offers a wide range of avatars and advanced animation tools.
  • Luppet: A user-friendly software with intuitive animation controls, making it ideal for beginners.
  • VSeeFace: A powerful software that provides accurate facial tracking and flexible character customization options.
  • VTube Studio: A free and open-source software that supports a variety of avatars and offers extensive customization features.

Other Software Options:

  • Wakaru: A simple and lightweight software that focuses on real-time motion capture.
  • 3tene: An animation software designed specifically for creating VTuber models and animations.
  • Puppemoji: An app that allows you to create custom avatars and animate them using motion capture data.

Cost of VTubing

The cost of VTubing can vary greatly depending on the equipment, avatar upgrades, and software fees you choose.

  • Entry-Level: For a basic setup with a webcam, entry-level software, and a pre-made avatar, you can expect to spend around a few hundred dollars.
  • Mid-Range: To enhance your experience with a high-quality motion-tracking webcam, more advanced software, and a custom avatar, you may invest a few thousand dollars.

Creating a 3D Model

  • Online Studios: Utilize modeling software like Blender, Daz3D, or Pixiv to create your own 3D model from scratch.
  • Commissioned Artists: Hire a professional animator to design and create a unique model that meets your specifications.

VTubing on Mobile

Yes, you can become a VTuber on mobile devices using the VTube Studio app, available for both Android and iOS. This app allows you to create and animate avatars, and stream directly from your phone or tablet.

Joining Hololive

Hololive is a renowned VTuber agency that nurtures and supports talented VTubers. To join Hololive, you can monitor their website for audition announcements and submit an online application and video. If selected, you will proceed through an interview process to determine your suitability for the agency.
