How to Copy a WhatsApp Message:

On Android:

  1. Locate the Message: Navigate to the WhatsApp chat containing the message you wish to copy. Scroll through the conversation until you find the specific message.

  2. Long-Press to Highlight: Once you've found the desired message, press and hold on it for a few seconds. This action will highlight the message, indicating it has been selected.

  3. Tap the "Copy" Icon: On the highlighted message, you'll notice a toolbar appear above it. Locate the "Copy" icon, which typically resembles a square with an arrow inside. Tap on this icon to copy the message into your device's clipboard.

On iPhone:

  1. Locate the Message: Similar to Android, open the WhatsApp chat and scroll through the conversation to find the message you want to copy.

  2. Long-Press to Highlight: Press and hold on the message for a few seconds until it becomes highlighted, indicating its selection.

  3. Tap "Copy": After highlighting the message, a pop-up menu will appear. One of the options in this menu is "Copy." Tap on this option to copy the message into your device's clipboard.

To Paste the Copied Message:

  1. Locate a Text Field: Navigate to any text field or area where you wish to paste the copied message. This could be a new WhatsApp chat, a note-taking app, or any other application that allows text input.

  2. Long-Press to Paste: Long-press the text field until a pop-up menu appears. One of the options in this menu is "Paste." Selecting this option will insert the copied message into the text field.

Additional Notes:

  • Multiple Messages: The copy feature allows you to select and copy multiple messages at once. Simply long-press and select each message you want to copy. The "Copy" icon will appear once all the messages are highlighted.

  • Share Option: Instead of copying the message, you can also share it directly with other contacts or via other applications. Long-press the message and tap on the "Share" icon in the toolbar or pop-up menu (depending on your device).

  • Keyboard Shortcut: On Android devices, you can also use the keyboard shortcut Ctrl + C (hold down the Ctrl key and press C) to copy the highlighted message.
