Choosing a Fascinator

1. Size:

  • Smaller Fascinators: Ideal for slicked-back or short hairstyles, where the focus should be on the face.
  • Larger Fascinators: Suitable for voluminous hair, updos, or elaborate coiffures. They create a more dramatic statement and balance the proportion of the head and hair.

2. Material:

  • Straw: A lightweight and breathable material perfect for summer events. It adds a touch of natural elegance and is available in various colors and weaves.
  • Wool: A warm and cozy material ideal for winter festivities. Wool fascinators offer a sophisticated and classic look, adding depth and texture to the ensemble.
  • Other Materials: Velvet, silk, lace, and feathers are also popular choices for fascinators, each providing a unique aesthetic. Consider the overall theme and style of the event when selecting the material.

3. Color:

  • Subtle and Classy: Match the fascinator's color to your outfit for a cohesive and sophisticated look. This creates a harmonious effect without overwhelming the ensemble.
  • Complementary Hair Color: Choose a fascinator shade that flatters your hair color. Earthy tones like terracotta and emerald complement redheads, warm colors like amber and honey suit blondes, while deep shades like navy and burgundy enhance brunettes.

4. Face Shape:

  • Round Faces: Tall fascinators with a vertical orientation help elongate the face. Styles such as feather plumes or long, narrow headbands can create a slimmer effect.
  • Long Faces: Sphere-shaped fascinators balance the length of the face. Opt for round or oval-shaped designs with a shorter height to avoid exaggerating facial elongation.

5. Headband Considerations:

  • With Glasses: Avoid wearing a headband with glasses, as it can be uncomfortable and restrict visibility. Instead, secure the fascinator directly onto your hair using clips or combs.
  • Without Glasses: Headbands can provide extra stability and prevent the fascinator from slipping. Choose a thin, flexible headband that blends with your hair color for a seamless look.

Securing a Fascinator

1. Hair Preparation:

  • Wash your hair a day prior to wearing the fascinator to reduce any natural oils that could affect grip.
  • Backcomb the hair where the fascinator will be placed, creating volume and friction for the comb to hold onto.
  • Spray the backcombed hair generously with hairspray to maintain its shape and provide a secure base.

2. Placement:

  • Push the comb on the fascinator firmly against your scalp, ensuring it is securely in place.
  • Position the fascinator on the side of your head, slightly above your eyebrow for an elegant and flattering angle.
  • Adjust the elastic band or headband to sit comfortably behind your ears, ensuring the fascinator is not too tight or loose.

Wearing Fascinators Appropriately

1. Placement and Angle:

  • Wear the fascinator on the side of your head, slightly angled towards the center of your face. Avoid placing it too far forward or backward, as it can disrupt the balance of your features.
  • Tip the fascinator slightly upwards to create a more flattering silhouette.

2. Correct Headband Use:

  • If using a headband, ensure the elastic band rests behind your ears, not under your chin. This ensures that the headband remains comfortable and the fascinator is securely in place.

3. Appropriate Styles:

  • Stick with classic and elegant shapes for a sophisticated look. Brimmed hats, mini pillboxes, and teacups are timeless options that never go out of style.
  • Avoid overly elaborate or flashy fascinators with excessive rhinestones or feathers, as they can detract from the overall elegance.

4. Event Suitability:

  • Fascinators are traditionally worn to formal events such as garden parties, weddings, and afternoon teas.
  • Avoid wearing fascinators to events with stadium-style seating, as they can obstruct the view of those behind you.

Conclusion:

Choosing and securing a fascinator can enhance your ensemble with a touch of elegance and sophistication. Wear your fascinator with confidence and embrace the charm it adds to your overall look. Whether you're attending a formal event or simply adding a touch of glamour to your everyday style, a fascinator will elevate your presence and leave a lasting impression.
