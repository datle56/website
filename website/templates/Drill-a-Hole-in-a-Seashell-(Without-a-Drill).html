Easy Seashell Drilling Techniques

For Small Shells (Using a Thumbtack)

  1. Clean and Mark: Wipe the shell clean with a damp cloth to remove any dirt or debris. Use a pencil or marker to mark the desired location for the hole.

  2. Twist and Widen: Gently press the tip of a thumbtack into the marked spot. Slowly twist the tack back and forth, applying slight pressure to create a small indentation. Repeat this process multiple times, gradually widening the hole until it reaches the desired size.

  3. Clear Away Dust: After each insertion of the thumbtack, blow away any accumulated dust to ensure the hole remains clear.

For Thick Shells (Using Scissors)

  1. Sterilize and Mark: If the shell was found outdoors, sterilize it by boiling it in water for a few minutes. Place the shell on a stable surface and mark the location for the hole.

  2. Twist the Scissors: Use a pair of sharp scissors to twist the pointed end into the marked spot. Apply gentle pressure and twist steadily until the point starts to penetrate the shell.

  3. Pierce and Remove: Continue twisting until the point of the scissors emerges from the other side of the shell. Slowly remove the scissors and immediately blow away any dust particles from the hole.

Tips:

  • Hole Widening: If the hole is too small, reinsert the thumbtack or scissors and twist again to widen it. Do this gradually to prevent the shell from cracking.
  • Jump Rings: To string the shell for jewelry, use a jump ring. This provides a professional finish and accommodates thicker chains.
  • Safety: Avoid breathing shell dust as it can be harmful. Use a mask or work in a well-ventilated area.
