Discover a Step-by-Step Guide with Illustrated Instructions for Sharing Your PC's Internet Connection with Your Mobile Device Using Wi-Fi


Unlock the Power of Your Computer: Turn it into a Wi-Fi Hotspot

Imagine having the convenience of turning your computer into a wireless gateway, allowing you to share your internet connection with nearby devices. Whether you're in a coffee shop, a library, or on the go, you can now easily create a Wi-Fi hotspot using your trusty laptop or desktop.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll explore two hassle-free methods to transform your computer into a Wi-Fi beacon. We'll dive into the straightforward steps using Windows Settings and introduce the user-friendly Connectify app. Whether you're a tech enthusiast or simply looking for a portable internet solution, this guide will empower you with the knowledge and tools you need. Let's embark on this connectivity adventure and unlock the versatility of your computer!


Method 1: Using Windows Settings

1. Access System Settings:

Navigate to your computer's "Settings" menu, which can be accessed through the Start menu or the Windows key (⊞) + I keyboard shortcut.

2. Configure Network & Internet Options:

Locate the "Network & Internet" category within the Settings menu. This will display options related to your internet connectivity.

3. Activate Mobile Hotspot Functionality:

Select "Mobile hotspot" from the left-hand navigation pane. In the main content area, you will find the "Mobile hotspot" switch.

4. Enable Hotspot Broadcast:

Toggle the "Mobile hotspot" switch to the "On" position. This will activate your computer's built-in mobile hotspot functionality, allowing it to broadcast your internet connection wirelessly.

5. Note Network Credentials:

Once the hotspot is enabled, the "Network name" and "Network password" will be displayed in the "Mobile hotspot" section. Make sure to note these credentials as they will be used to connect your smartphone to the newly created Wi-Fi network.

6. Connect Smartphone:

Using your smartphone's Wi-Fi settings, search for and select the network name you noted earlier. Enter the network password and establish a connection to the hotspot.

Method 2: Using the Connectify App

1. Download and Install Connectify:

Visit the Connectify website and download the latest version of the Connectify app. Install it on your computer, following the on-screen instructions.

2. Launch and Configure Connectify:

Open the Connectify app from your Start menu or desktop shortcut. By default, you will be presented with a "Try it Out" screen.

3. Select Wi-Fi Hotspot Tab:

Navigate to the "Wi-Fi Hotspot" tab in the left-hand menu within the Connectify app. This tab will contain options for configuring the Wi-Fi hotspot you intend to create.

4. Modify Network Password (Optional):

If desired, you can change the default network password by clicking on the "Password" field and entering a new one. This password will be required to connect your smartphone to the hotspot.

5. Activate Hotspot Broadcast:

Click the "Start Hotspot" button to initialize the hotspot broadcasting. Connectify will create a virtual Wi-Fi adapter on your computer, enabling it to share its internet connection.

6. Connect Smartphone:

On your smartphone, navigate to the Wi-Fi settings and connect to the new Wi-Fi network with the name and password you configured in Connectify. Your smartphone will now have access to the shared internet connection.


In conclusion, sharing your internet connection from your computer to your smartphone is a simple and convenient way to stay connected on the go. By following the steps above, you can easily set up a mobile hotspot using either Windows Settings or the Connectify app. Whether you're traveling, working remotely, or just need to connect multiple devices, these methods provide a hassle-free way to share your internet connection. Remember to check with your internet service provider about any data usage limits or fees associated with mobile hotspots before using this feature extensively.
