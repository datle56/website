Crafting Exquisite Blooms from Tissue Paper

Materials for Dahlia:

  • 12 sheets of tissue paper (chosen color)
  • Sharp scissors
  • Floral wire (gauge 22-24)
  • Glue (optional)

Instructions for Dahlia:

  • Accordion-fold the tissue papers together, ensuring the edges are aligned.
  • With the scissors, gently curve the ends of the folded papers, creating a realistic petal shape.
  • Take a piece of floral wire, approximately 6-8 inches in length, and insert it into the center of the folded papers.
  • Spread apart the petals layer by layer, carefully pulling them outwards to create a voluminous and fluffy effect. The number of petals can be adjusted for desired fullness.
  • Optionally, apply a small dot of glue to the base of each petal to secure them to the stem. This step is recommended for a more durable flower.

Materials for Rose:

  • Tissue paper (one or two colors)
  • Floral wire (gauge 22-24)
  • Glue

Instructions for Rose:

  • Cut strips of tissue paper, approximately 2-3 inches wide and 12-15 inches long.
  • Fold the strips together accordion-style, ensuring they are parallel to each other.
  • Curve one end of the folded strips, using scissors to create a rounded petal shape.
  • Glue the petals around the base of a floral wire stem, overlapping them slightly. Start from the outer edge of the stem and gradually work towards the center.
  • Loosen the folded petals as you glue them to mimic a blooming rose. Gradually separate and spread out the petals, adding fullness to the flower.

Materials for Hydrangeas:

  • 120 teardrop-shaped petals (cut from colored tissue paper)
  • 30 pieces of floral wire (gauge 22-24)
  • Glue
  • Small leaves cut from green tissue paper (optional)

Instructions for Hydrangeas:

  • Form a bundle of 30 floral wires to create the flower's base.
  • Glue the petals to the wires, starting from the center and working outwards.
  • Double up a few petals for added fullness and to conceal the wires as much as possible.
  • Glue additional layers of petals until the desired size and shape is achieved.
  • Attach leaves to the base of the stem for added realism.
  • After the glue has dried, gently fluff up the petals to create a delicate and airy bloom.
