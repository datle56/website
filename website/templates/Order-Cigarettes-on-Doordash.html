Ordering Cigarettes on DoorDash: A Comprehensive Guide

Introduction

DoorDash, the popular delivery service, has expanded its offerings to include tobacco products, including cigarettes. This convenience allows smokers to have their favorite brands delivered right to their doorstep. However, there are specific requirements and restrictions that customers must be aware of before placing tobacco orders.

Step-by-Step Ordering Process

To order cigarettes on DoorDash, follow these steps:

  1. Choose a Participating Store: Open the DoorDash app and browse for convenience stores or gas stations that offer tobacco products. Not all stores will allow cigarette orders, so it's essential to verify this before proceeding.

  2. Place a Regular Food Order: To qualify for tobacco delivery, customers must place a regular food order. This can be any item from the store's menu, regardless of value.

  3. Specify Cigarette Details in Special Instructions: Once you have added food items to your cart, scroll down to the "Special Instructions" section. Here, clearly indicate the specific cigarette brand you wish to order, as well as the quantity. Example: "Marlboro Red Box - 1 pack"

  4. Complete Your Order: Once you have entered the cigarette details, complete your order like any other DoorDash transaction. Provide your delivery address, payment information, and any additional instructions.

Restrictions on Tobacco Orders

DoorDash strictly adheres to regulations regarding the delivery of tobacco products. Orders are prohibited to the following locations:

  • Schools
  • Hospitals
  • Government Buildings
  • Prisons
  • Tobacco-selling Businesses

ID Verification

Due to legal requirements, DoorDash drivers are required to request valid identification from all individuals ordering tobacco products. Customers must be 21 years of age or older to place such orders. Without proper identification, drivers have the right to refuse delivery.

Additional Considerations

  • Availability: Tobacco product availability may vary depending on the participating store's inventory.
  • Delivery Fees: Delivery fees apply for tobacco orders, just like any other DoorDash delivery.
  • Driver Discretion: DoorDash drivers have the authority to refuse tobacco orders if they suspect foul play or non-compliance with guidelines.
  • Responsible Use: DoorDash encourages responsible smoking and advises customers to consume tobacco products in accordance with local laws and regulations.
