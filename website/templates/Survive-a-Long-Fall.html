Surviving a Fall from Many Stories

In the harrowing event of a fall from a significant height, the chances of survival can be increased by adopting specific strategies:

Reducing Impact Forces:

  • Grab something: If an object is within reach, grasp it firmly to cushion the impact. A larger object, such as a chair or mattress, offers increased protection.
  • Break the fall: Aim to strike an intermediate surface, such as a ledge or tree branch, to dissipate speed gradually.
  • Relax your body: Tense muscles amplify the impact on organs. Maintain a relaxed posture to minimize internal injuries.

Landing Technique:

  • Bend your knees: Flexing your knees acts as a shock absorber, reducing the impact on your spine and joints.
  • Land feet-first: Spread your feet wide to distribute the force over a larger surface area. Land on the balls of your feet, rather than flat, to minimize damage to the ankles.
  • Fall to the side: If possible, angle your body to land on your side. This offers a greater chance of protecting your vital organs than falling on your back or front.
  • Protect your head: Cover your head with your arms or any object available to prevent bouncing injuries and skull fractures.

Immediate Aftermath:

  • Get medical help immediately: Even if you do not experience any immediate symptoms, seek medical attention to rule out hidden injuries, such as internal bleeding or concussions.

Surviving a Fall from a Plane

Falling from an aircraft poses a unique set of challenges, requiring additional measures:

Preparing for Impact:

  • Spread out your body: Adopt the "arch position" by arching your back, spreading your arms and legs wide. This creates a larger surface area, reducing the impact force.
  • Find a good landing spot: Identify a location with a gentle slope or soft ground, such as a field or forest.
  • Steer yourself: Make subtle movements with your arms and legs to guide your direction towards a more favorable landing spot.

Landing Technique:

  • Follow the landing technique: Relax your body, bend your knees, land feet-first, and protect your head.
  • Transition to vertical: As you approach the ground, gradually bring your body into an upright position to avoid awkward impacts and minimize injuries.

Remember, these strategies are guidelines and cannot guarantee survival. However, they offer a framework to increase your chances of surviving a fall from a significant height by reducing impact forces, optimizing landing technique, and seeking prompt medical attention.


While it's impossible to guarantee survival in all cases, following these guidelines can significantly increase your chances of emerging from a high-altitude fall with minimal injury. Remember, remaining calm and executing the techniques correctly is crucial for maximizing your odds. However, even if you feel unscathed after impact, it's imperative to seek medical attention promptly, as you may have sustained hidden internal injuries that require immediate treatment.
