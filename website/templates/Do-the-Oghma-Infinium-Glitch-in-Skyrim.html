Unlock the Ancient Secrets of the Oghma Infinium

Embark on an epic quest in the realm of Skyrim, where the Oghma Infinium awaits, a legendary book rumored to hold the power to enhance your skills beyond measure. Our comprehensive guide will unveil the secrets of obtaining this tome, from the challenging quests to a hidden glitch that allows you to exploit its knowledge for unparalleled character growth. Prepare to delve into the depths of ancient mysteries and emerge with a character that defies the boundaries of ordinary mortals.


Part 1: Acquiring the Oghma Infinium

Prerequisites:

  • Character level 15 or higher
  • Completion of the "Throat of the World" main quest

Step 1: Locate Septimus Signus' Outpost

  • Travel to Septimus Signus' Outpost, situated in the far northeast corner of the Skyrim map. It's located near the border with Winterhold and overlooks the Sea of Ghosts.

Step 2: Initiate the Quest

  • Approach Septimus Signus and engage in conversation. He will entrust you with one of two quests: "Discerning the Transmundane" or "Elder Knowledge." Both quests lead to the acquisition of the Oghma Infinium.

Step 3: Obtain the Runed Lexicon

  • Retrieve the runed lexicon from the table next to Septimus' workstation. This lexicon serves as a repository for various types of knowledge.

Step 4: Fill the Lexicon with Knowledge

  • Explore Skyrim and gather diverse forms of knowledge. This includes reading books, deciphering inscriptions, and engaging in conversations with knowledgeable individuals. Each piece of knowledge you acquire fills a specific section of the runed lexicon.

Step 5: Return to Septimus

  • Once the runed lexicon is fully filled, return to Septimus Signus. If your character is level 15 or higher, he will initiate a dialogue option allowing you to hand over the lexicon.

Step 6: Retrieve the Blood Samples

  • After giving Septimus the lexicon, he will instruct you to collect blood samples from five distinct races: orcs, dark elves, wood elves, high elves, and Falmer. The locations of these races are scattered throughout Skyrim.

Step 7: Return to Septimus and Receive the Oghma Infinium

  • Collect all five blood samples and return them to Septimus Signus. He will reward you with the Oghma Infinium, an ancient tome with the power to boost specific skills by five levels.

Part 2: Utilizing the Oghma Infinium Glitch

Note: This glitch is exclusive to the Xbox 360 version of Skyrim.

Step 1: Find a Bookshelf

  • Locate an empty bookshelf in any interior location. Bookshelves can be found in various homes, shops, or libraries throughout the game.

Step 2: Clear the Bookshelf

  • Remove any books currently on the bookshelf by picking them up and placing them in your inventory.

Step 3: Save the Game

  • Save your game quickly before proceeding with the glitch.

Step 4: Open the Bookshelf and Inventory

  • Open the bookshelf and select the "Books" tab in your inventory.

Step 5: Open the Oghma Infinium and Upgrade Skills

  • Open the Oghma Infinium and choose the skill path you wish to upgrade.

Step 6: Place the Oghma Infinium on the Bookshelf

  • Without closing the bookshelf, place the Oghma Infinium on the empty shelf.

Step 7: Reopen the Oghma Infinium

  • Open the Oghma Infinium again and take it back into your inventory. Your selected skills should now be upgraded by five levels.

Step 8: Repeat for Multiple Skills

  • Repeat steps 7-10 to level up any additional skills you desire. This glitch allows you to level up multiple skills with the Oghma Infinium without consuming its charges.

Conclusion:

With the Oghma Infinium and the bookshelf glitch, you can ascend your Skyrim character to unprecedented heights. Whether you seek to excel in combat, magic, or stealth, the Oghma Infinium provides a path to rapid skill progression. The bookshelf glitch further amplifies this power by allowing you to access unlimited skill upgrades, enabling you to create an unparalleled warrior, mage, or rogue. Remember, this glitch is exclusive to the Xbox 360 version, so PC and PlayStation players will need to explore alternative methods of leveling up. Regardless of your platform, embracing the Oghma Infinium's wisdom and exploiting its glitch can transform your Skyrim journey into an unforgettable adventure where anything is possible.
