Mastering the Art of Billiards: A Comprehensive Guide to Refurbishing Your Table

Prepare to elevate your billiards experience with our expert guide to refurbishing your table. Whether you're a seasoned pro or a novice enthusiast, this step-by-step tutorial will empower you to restore your table to its former glory, ensuring optimal accuracy and impeccable style. Join us as we delve into the intricacies of table preparation, felt installation, and railing renewal. Get ready to rediscover the captivating allure of billiards, one shot at a time!


Part 1: Preparing the Table

  • Dismantle the Table: Before you begin, disassemble the table by removing the railings and cleaning the slate. This will provide you with a clear working surface.
  • Level the Table: Once dismantled, inspect the table for levelness. Use a level or spirit level to determine if the table is flat in all directions. If not, make the necessary adjustments to ensure a level playing surface.
  • Seal Gaps: Inspect the slate for any gaps or holes. If any are found, fill them with beeswax using a putty knife or similar tool. This will prevent the felt from sinking into the gaps and compromising the playing surface.
  • Measure for Felt: Measure the table to determine the amount of felt required. This includes measuring the length of the table from head to foot, and the width from side to side. Add an extra 6 inches to each measurement for trimming and tucking the felt.

Part 2: Stapling the Felt

  • Attach the Felt: Use a hammer tacker or staple gun to attach the felt to the wooden or particle board lining underneath the slate. Start at the head end of the table and pull the felt tightly over the surface. Staple the felt along the edge, ensuring the felt is taut.
  • Continue Stapling: Proceed down the left side, foot end, and right side of the table, stapling the felt as you go. Keep the felt stretched tightly to create a smooth playing surface.
  • Trim and Staple Pockets: Once the felt is secured around the edges, trim the excess felt around the pockets. Fold the excess felt inside the pockets and staple it securely.

Part 3: Gluing the Felt

  • Protect Table Edges: In cases where there is no wooden backing for the felt, use spray adhesive to attach the felt directly to the slate. However, before applying the adhesive, protect the table edges from spills by covering them with newspaper or masking tape.
  • Cut Felt: Measure and cut the felt to the desired size, ensuring that it covers the playing surface of the table.
  • Apply Glue: Apply spray adhesive to the head end of the felt and the corresponding area on the slate.
  • Attach Felt: Carefully align and attach the felt to the table, pressing down as you move along the surface. Repeat the process for the other sides of the table.
  • Trim and Line Pockets: Trim the excess felt around the edges and use the remaining felt to line the pockets. Glue the felt lining inside the pockets for a finished look.

Part 4: Replacing Railing Felt

  • Remove Old Felt and Strip: Carefully remove the old felt from the railings, and remove the featherstrip from the inside of the railings.
  • Place New Felt: Place a new felt strip on the rail, with the "face down" towards the table.
  • Insert Featherstrip: Use a tapping block and hammer to wedge the featherstrip into the felt. Start from the center of the rail and work towards the ends, tapping gently but firmly to secure the strip.
  • Trim and Fold: Once the featherstrip is in place, trim the excess felt around the edges and fold it over the ends of the railing.
  • Reassemble Outer Rails: Reassemble the outer rails on the table, ensuring they are securely attached.

Conclusion: Enhancing Your Playing Experience

With these simple yet effective steps, you have successfully revitalized your pool table, restoring its pristine condition and ensuring optimal playing performance. By replacing the felt, you have created a smooth, responsive playing surface that will enhance your aim and accuracy. The new railing felt provides a comfortable grip and eliminates distraction during intense games.

This revitalized table will not only elevate your gaming experience but also add aesthetic value to your gaming room. Its polished appearance and the satisfaction of playing on a well-maintained table will undoubtedly make every shot a memorable one. So, reap the benefits of your hard work and enjoy countless hours of fun and competitive play on your rejuvenated pool table.
