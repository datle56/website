Method 1: Manual Creation Using Microsoft Excel

Step 1: Create the Spreadsheet

  • Open Microsoft Excel and create a new spreadsheet.
  • Label the columns as follows:
    • A: Period
    • B: Beginning Balance
    • C: Payment
    • D: Principal
    • E: Interest
    • F: Cumulative Principal
    • G: Cumulative Interest
    • H: Ending Balance

Step 2: Enter Loan Details

  • In the "Loan Amount" column (Cell B1), enter the original loan amount.
  • In the "Interest Rate" column (Cell B2), enter the annual interest rate as a percentage (e.g., 5% = 0.05).
  • In the "Months" column (Cell B3), enter the total number of months over which the loan will be repaid.

Step 3: Calculate Monthly Payment

  • In cell B4, enter the PMT formula to calculate the monthly payment:

excel PMT(B2/12, B3, B1)

  • This formula calculates the loan payment by multiplying the loan amount by the monthly interest rate and the loan term.

Step 4: Create the Amortization Table

  • Enter the first payment date in cell A8.
  • Use relative references to populate the remaining rows in column A.
  • In cell B8, enter the loan amount (same as Cell B1) as the beginning balance.

Step 5: Calculate Amortization Components

  • To calculate the monthly interest, enter the following formula in cell E8:

excel B8 * B2 / 12

  • To calculate the monthly principal payment, enter this formula in cell D8:

excel C8 - E8

  • To calculate the cumulative principal, enter this formula in cell F8:

excel D8 + F7

  • To calculate the cumulative interest, enter this formula in cell G8:

excel E8 + G7

  • To calculate the ending balance, enter this formula in cell H8:

excel B8 - D8

Step 6: Complete the Table

  • Use the fill handle to duplicate the formulas in rows 8 through the desired number of payments, allowing Excel to calculate the amortization schedule for the entire loan term.

Method 2: Using an Excel Template

Step 1: Download the Template

  • Visit Office.com and search for "loan amortization schedule template."
  • Select the desired template and download it to your computer.

Step 2: Enter Loan Details

  • Open the downloaded template in Excel.
  • Find the section titled "Loan Details" and enter the following information:
    • Loan Amount
    • Interest Rate
    • Loan Duration
    • Number of Payments per Year

Step 3: Calculate Schedule

  • The template will automatically calculate the monthly payment, total interest, and a detailed schedule of payments.
  • The schedule will include the period, beginning balance, payment, principal, interest, and ending balance for each payment.

Step 4: Save the Template

  • Once you have entered the necessary information and the schedule is calculated, go to File > Save As.
  • Choose a custom name and location for the file, and click Save.
