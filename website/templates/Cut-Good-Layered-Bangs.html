Cutting Side Bangs: A Step-by-Step Guide

Side bangs are a versatile and stylish way to add a touch of personality to your look. Whether you want a diagonal or straight-across style, cutting side bangs yourself is easier than you might think with the right techniques and a little practice.

Cutting Diagonal Side Bangs

Materials:

  • Sharp scissors
  • Comb
  • Clips (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Create a Diagonal Part: Use the comb to create a diagonal part from your natural part to your hairline. This line will serve as the angle for your bangs.
  2. Cut Along the Angle: Hold the hair along your part firmly between your fingers and cut straight along the angle to your desired length. Make sure to keep the scissors parallel to your part to ensure even cutting.
  3. Create Layers: To add volume and texture to your bangs, cut vertically upward from the bottom of your bangs towards the front. This will create layers and help blend the bangs with your other hair.

Cutting Straight Across Bangs

Materials:

  • Sharp scissors
  • Comb
  • Clips (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Create an Inverted V Part: Create an inverted V shape starting from your hairline. The point of the V should align with the edge of your desired bangs.
  2. Cut Straight Across: Comb the bangs forward and hold them flat against your face. Cut horizontally from one side of the V to the other, keeping the scissors parallel to your face. This will create the straight across shape.
  3. Create Layers: Just like with diagonal bangs, cut vertically upward to create layers and blend the bangs with your other hair.

Fixing Mistakes

If you make a mistake while cutting your side bangs, don't panic! Here are a few tricks to help you fix it:

  • Avoid Cutting More Hair: It's tempting to cut more hair to fix a mistake, but it's better to wait and let your bangs grow out slightly before reattempting a cut.
  • Part on the Opposite Side: If the bangs are too short on one side, try parting your hair on the opposite side to cover them up.
  • Use Accessories: Hair accessories like scarves or headbands can help conceal shorter bangs or create a different style while they grow out.
  • Consult a Stylist: If the bangs are too short, crooked, or in the wrong spot, it's best to consult a professional stylist for help. They can assess the damage and correct the mistake safely.

Tips for Success

  • Use sharp scissors to prevent split ends and jagged edges.
  • Cut your bangs slightly damp to prevent them from sliding around.
  • Hold the scissors parallel to the angle or shape you want.
  • Cut gradually and take your time to avoid making big mistakes.
  • Remember, practice makes perfect! Don't get discouraged if your first attempt doesn't turn out exactly how you want it.

Conclusion: Mastering the Art of Side Bangs
