How to Become a Delta Force Operator

Step 1: Enlist in the Army

To embark on the path towards becoming a Delta Force Operator, the first step is to enlist in the Army. Candidates must meet the following eligibility criteria:

  • Be at least 22 years old at the time of enlistment
  • Possess a high school diploma or its equivalent (e.g., GED)
  • Pass the Physical Fitness Assessment (PFA) with a score that meets or exceeds the minimum standards
  • Obtain a security clearance

Step 2: Basic Training

After enlisting, recruits undergo Infantry One Station Unit Training (OSUT) at Fort Benning, Georgia. This comprehensive 14-week training program transforms candidates into proficient soldiers. It encompasses:

  • Basic combat skills
  • Weapons handling and proficiency
  • Physical conditioning
  • Infantry tactics and operations

Step 3: Airborne Training

Once candidates complete Basic Training, they proceed to Airborne School at Fort Benning. This four-week course provides specialized training in paratrooping. Candidates learn the fundamentals of:

  • Aerial operations
  • Parachute packing and rigging
  • Canopy control and landing techniques
  • Airfield operations and safety procedures

Step 4: Special Forces Preparation Course

After Airborne Training, candidates with aspirations of joining Special Forces must complete the Special Forces Preparation Course (SFPC) at Fort Bragg, North Carolina. This intensive four-week program evaluates candidates' physical and mental capabilities and ensures they possess the foundational skills necessary for Special Forces training.

Step 5: Special Forces Qualification Course (SFQC)

The Special Forces Qualification Course (SFQC) is the cornerstone of Special Forces training. It consists of three phases:

Individual Skills Phase: Candidates undergo rigorous training in essential skills, including:

  • Land navigation
  • Map reading and interpretation
  • Small unit tactics
  • Combat marksmanship
  • Survival skills

Military Operational Specialty (MOS) Training Phase: Candidates develop specialized skills based on their experience and training. MOS options include:

  • Special Operations Medic
  • Engineer Sergeant
  • Weapons Sergeant
  • Communications Sergeant

Collective Training Phase: Candidates train in advanced special operations tactics and techniques, such as:

  • Unconventional warfare
  • Counterterrorism
  • Direct action missions
  • Foreign internal defense

In addition to tactical training, candidates also receive language training in a foreign language relevant to special operations.

Step 6: Delta Force Selection

After completing SFQC, soldiers who aspire to join Delta Force must make their interest known to their superiors. They must demonstrate exceptional physical fitness, trainability, and motivation. Candidates are expected to excel in land navigation and possess other required skills.

Delta Force selection is notoriously rigorous and has a high rejection rate. Candidates undergo a series of grueling physical and mental challenges designed to test their limits and identify those with the necessary qualities to serve in this elite unit.

Step 7: Standing Out

To increase their chances of being selected for Delta Force, candidates should:

  • Communicate Goals Clearly: Express their aspiration to join Delta Force to their superiors and demonstrate their determination and commitment.
  • Build Connections: Establish relationships with ranking officers and other individuals who can provide guidance and support.
  • Practice Land Navigation: Develop exceptional land navigation skills through independent practice and seek opportunities to enhance their abilities.
  • Reapply if Not Accepted: If initially denied entry into Delta Force, candidates should not give up. They can reapply after a specified period and demonstrate their continued progress and improvement.

Conclusion:

The path to becoming a Delta Force Operator is arduous, demanding both physical and mental fortitude. It requires unwavering determination, exceptional skills, and a deep-seated belief in the mission. While the selection process is notoriously competitive, those who possess the necessary attributes and unwavering perseverance can aspire to join this elite force. The journey to become a Delta Force Operator is not for the faint of heart, but for those who seek adventure, challenge, and the ultimate test of their limits, it is an opportunity to serve their country at the highest level of special operations.
