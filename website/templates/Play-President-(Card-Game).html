Step into the World of Presidential Chaos: Dive into the Hilariously Addictive Game of Presidents

Prepare yourself for a wild and unpredictable ride as we delve into the hilarious world of Presidents, a card game that will put your strategic thinking and social maneuvering skills to the ultimate test. With its simple yet chaotic gameplay, Presidents promises hours of laughter, heated debates, and a touch of friendly rivalry.

Gather your crew of up to seven players, grab a standard deck of cards, and get ready to embark on a presidential race like no other. In this game of wits and audacity, you'll compete to be the first to shed your entire hand while causing havoc for your opponents. So, let the cards be your scepter, and the presidency be your trophy as we navigate the treacherous waters of Presidents. Buckle up and prepare for an unforgettable gaming experience!


Part 1: Starting the Game

  1. Gather Players and Select Deck: Bring together 4-7 players and use a standard 52-card deck.

  2. Deal the Cards: Distributing starting hands, deal all the cards evenly until some players have one more card than others. This ensures the game will end without any remaining cards.

  3. Start with the 3 of Clubs: Begin the game by having the player who possesses the 3 of clubs place it face up in the center as the starting pile.

  4. Arrange Cards in Order: Players take turns adding cards to the pile in numerical sequence, ascending or descending.

  5. Special Role of the 2 Card: When a player places a 2 card on the pile, it serves a unique function. It instantly clears the entire pile, allowing the player who placed it to designate a new card from their hand to continue the game sequence.

Part 2: Continuing the Game

  1. President Elect: In subsequent rounds, the player who first ran out of cards becomes the president. The president holds the advantage of starting the next round with any card of their choice.

  2. Card Outranking: Players engage in a race to play cards that outrank the highest card on the discard pile. If a player succeeds, their card becomes the new top card.

  3. Running Out of Cards: The first player to deplete their hand of all cards becomes the new president and earns 2 points. The second player to run out of cards becomes the vice president, scoring 1 point.

  4. Last Player with Cards: The player who is left with cards at the end of the round becomes the "a-hole" and receives no points.

House Rules (Optional)

  1. Additional Positions: To accommodate more players, create additional positions such as treasurer and vice-a-hole, each with their own unique privileges and responsibilities.

  2. President's Rule-Making Authority: Empower the president with the ability to establish new rules if they win multiple consecutive rounds, adding an element of strategy and unpredictability to the game.

  3. Matching Pairs: Introduce a rule that allows players to match pairs of cards to triumph over any single card on the pile, adding a layer of defense to the gameplay.

  4. Skipping Turns: Permit players to use matching rank cards as a way to skip the next player's turn, creating a tactical advantage.

  5. Presidents as a Drinking Game: Transform the game into a drinking game by assigning specific penalties or actions for certain rule violations or specific card plays, adding an extra dimension of fun and camaraderie.


Conclusion: A Perfect Game for Any Occasion

With its customizable rules and endless opportunities for creativity, Presidents allows players to tailor the game to their preferences. Whether you prefer a traditional or more unconventional approach, Presidents provides a platform for endless fun and laughter.
