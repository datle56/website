Homemade Doughnut Delights: A Step-by-Step Guide to Sweet Sensations

Step into the enchanting world of homemade doughnuts, where the aroma of freshly fried delights fills the air and every bite promises pure bliss. With our easy-to-follow recipes, you can transform your kitchen into a doughnut haven and indulge in the sweet symphony of these delectable treats.

1. Fried Glazed Doughnuts: A Classic Indulgence

A. Ingredients:

  • 2 ¼ teaspoons active dry yeast
  • 1 cup warm water (110-115°F)
  • ½ cup milk, warmed
  • ⅓ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • ¼ cup shortening, softened
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour

B. Instructions:

a. In a small bowl, combine the yeast and warm water. Let stand for 5 minutes, or until foamy.

b. In a large bowl, whisk together the milk, sugar, salt, eggs, shortening, and foamy yeast mixture.

c. Gradually add the flour, 1 cup at a time, and knead for 5-7 minutes until the dough is smooth and elastic.

d. Place the dough in a lightly greased bowl, cover with plastic wrap, and let rise in a warm place for about 1 hour, or until doubled in size.

e. Punch down the dough and turn it out onto a lightly floured surface. Roll out the dough to a thickness of ½ inch.

f. Using a 3-inch doughnut cutter, cut out doughnuts from the dough. Place the doughnuts on a greased baking sheet.

g. Cover the doughnuts with a damp towel and let rise again for 30-60 minutes, or until doubled in size.

h. Heat vegetable oil in a large Dutch oven or deep fryer to 375°F (190°C).

i. Carefully drop the doughnuts into the hot oil and fry for 1-2 minutes per side, or until golden brown.

j. Remove the doughnuts from the oil and drain on paper towels.

k. For the glaze: Whisk together 1 cup confectioners' sugar, ¼ cup melted butter, 1 teaspoon vanilla extract, and 2-3 tablespoons hot water. Dip the warm doughnuts into the glaze and let the excess drip off.

2. Baked Glazed Doughnuts: A Healthier Alternative

A. Ingredients:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ⅔ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ¼ cup shortening, melted

B. Instructions:

a. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C).

b. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, nutmeg, cinnamon, and salt.

c. In a separate bowl, whisk together the milk, eggs, vanilla extract, and shortening.

d. Add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients and stir just until combined. Do not overmix.

e. Grease a doughnut pan and fill each cup ¾ full with batter.

f. Bake for 8-10 minutes, or until doughnuts are springy to the touch.

g. For the glaze: Whisk together 1 cup confectioners' sugar, 1-2 tablespoons hot water, and 1 teaspoon almond extract. Dip the warm doughnuts into the glaze and let the excess drip off.
