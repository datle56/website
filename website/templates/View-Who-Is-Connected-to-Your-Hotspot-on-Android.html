Unlock the Secrets: Unveiling Connected Devices to Your Android Hotspot


Unveil the Secret: Who's Lurking on Your Android Hotspot?

In the realm of digital connectivity, Android hotspots reign supreme, enabling us to share our internet bliss with friends, family, and even strangers. But amidst the convenience, a nagging question remains: who's hitching a ride on our precious Wi-Fi wagon?

Don't fret, fellow Android aficionados! This comprehensive guide will shed light on the mystery of "who's connected to my hotspot." With three foolproof methods, you'll become the reigning sovereign of your Wi-Fi domain, banishing any unwelcome digital squatters.

Get ready to dive into the depths of Android's hotspot settings, unleash the power of mobile apps, and even explore the realm of rooted devices. Knowledge is power, and with this knowledge, you'll reclaim control of your Wi-Fi destiny!


Method 1: Settings

Detailed Steps:

  1. Navigate to your device's Settings app.
  2. Select Network & internet.
  3. Search for the Hotspot & Tethering option and tap on it.
  4. Under Wi-Fi Hotspot, you will see the number of connected devices indicated next to the heading "Connected devices."

Method 2: Fing App

Detailed Steps:

  1. Install the Fing app from the Google Play Store on another device.
  2. Ensure that this device is connected to your Android hotspot.
  3. Launch the Fing app and tap on the SCAN button.
  4. Wait for the app to complete the scanning process.
  5. Once the scan is complete, you will be presented with a list of all devices connected to your hotspot. Each device will be listed along with its IP address, MAC address, manufacturer, and other relevant details.

Method 3: Rooted Android Device

Prerequisites:

  • Your Android device must be rooted.
  • You will need to install a command terminal app (e.g., Terminal Emulator).

Detailed Steps:

  1. Warning: Rooting your Android device can void its warranty and introduce security risks. Proceed with caution.
  2. Open the Terminal Emulator or the command terminal app.
  3. Type the following command: ip neigh
  4. Press the Enter key.
  5. The output will display a list of IP/MAC addresses representing the devices connected to your Android hotspot. Each line in the output corresponds to a connected device.

Additional Tips:

  • If you want to know more details about a connected device (e.g., its hostname), type the following command in the command terminal: arp -a
  • You can also use third-party apps specifically designed to monitor and manage connected devices on your Android hotspot. These apps typically provide additional features such as bandwidth monitoring, device filtering, and security alerts.

Conclusion:

By following these three methods, you can easily monitor the devices connected to your Android hotspot. Whether you want to check for unauthorized access, limit data usage, or troubleshoot connection issues, having this information at your fingertips is invaluable. Remember, it's important to be mindful of your hotspot's security and set a strong password to prevent unwanted intrusion. By taking these steps, you can ensure that your internet connection remains secure and under your control.
