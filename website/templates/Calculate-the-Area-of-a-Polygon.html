Calculating the Area of Regular Polygons

Regular polygons are shapes with an equal number of sides and angles. Their area can be calculated using specific formulas or by using the more general formula involving the perimeter and apothem.

Using Apothems:

  • Formula: Area = (1/2) * (Perimeter) * (Apothem)
  • Steps:
    • Find the perimeter, or the sum of the lengths of all sides.
    • Find the apothem, or the distance from the center of the polygon to the midpoint of a side.
    • Multiply the perimeter and apothem together, then divide by 2 to get the area.

Using Specific Formulas:

For specific types of regular polygons, there are dedicated formulas that simplify the calculation:

  • Triangle: Area = (1/2) * (Base) * (Height)
  • Square: Area = (Side Length)^2
  • Rectangle: Area = (Length) * (Width)
  • Trapezoid: Area = [(Base 1 + Base 2) * (Height)] / 2

Calculating the Area of Irregular Polygons:

Irregular polygons, which do not have equal sides or angles, require a more complex approach to calculate their area. This method involves using coordinates and geometric concepts:

  • Steps:
    • List the coordinates of all the vertices of the polygon in order.
    • Multiply the x-coordinate of each vertex by the y-coordinate of the next vertex, and then add the results.
    • Multiply the y-coordinate of each vertex by the x-coordinate of the next vertex, and then add the results.
    • Subtract the second sum from the first sum.
    • Divide the difference by 2 to get the area of the polygon.
