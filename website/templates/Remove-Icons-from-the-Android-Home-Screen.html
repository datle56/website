Declutter Your Android Home Screen: A Comprehensive Guide to Removing Unwanted Icons


Your Android Home Screen: Declutter It with Effortless Icon Removal

Does your Android Home screen resemble a chaotic sea of app icons, drowning the true essence of your device? Fear not! We've got you covered with a comprehensive guide that will transform your screen into an oasis of organization and style. Join us on this journey to banish unruly icons, leaving you with a streamlined and personalized Android experience.


How to Remove App Icons from Your Android Home Screen

Method 1: On Stock Android

  1. Long-press the app icon: Locate the app icon you want to remove from your Home screen. Press and hold the icon until a menu appears.
  2. Select "Remove" or "Delete": If the menu offers the "Remove" or "Delete" option, simply tap on it. This will remove the app icon from your Home screen.
  3. Drag and drop to "X" or trash can symbol: If the "Remove" or "Delete" option is not present, you can try dragging the app icon to the top of the screen. In some versions of Android, an "X" or trash can symbol may appear at the top of the screen, indicating that you can drop the icon there to remove it.

Method 2: On Samsung Galaxy

  1. Long-press the app icon: Similar to stock Android, long-press the app icon you wish to remove until a menu appears.
  2. Select "Remove shortcut": In the menu that appears, select the option that reads "Remove shortcut." This will remove the app icon from your Home screen while leaving the app installed on your device.

Method 3: Using Nova Launcher

  1. Long-press the app icon: If you are using Nova Launcher, press and hold the app icon you want to remove.
  2. Select "App info": In the menu that appears, tap on the "App info" option.
  3. Long-press the "App info" icon: This will bring up a shortcut menu. Long-press the "App info" icon again.
  4. Select "Remove": From the shortcut menu, select the "Remove" option. This will remove the app icon from your Home screen and uninstall the app from your device.

Method 4: Disabling Automatic Shortcuts on Oreo

  1. Go to Settings: Open the Settings app on your Android device running Oreo (Android 8.0 or higher).
  2. Select Home screen settings: Locate and tap on the "Home screen settings" option.
  3. Toggle off "Add icon": Scroll down and find the "Add icon" option. Toggle the switch next to it to the "off" position. This will prevent app icons from being automatically added to your Home screen when new apps are installed.

Method 5: Disabling Automatic Shortcuts on Nougat

  1. Open the Google Play Store: Launch the Google Play Store app on your Nougat device (Android 7.0 or lower).
  2. Go to Settings: Tap on the three-line menu icon in the top-left corner and select "Settings."
  3. Uncheck "Add icon to Home screen": Scroll down and find the option that says "Add icon to Home screen." Uncheck the box next to this option. This will disable the automatic addition of app icons to your Home screen when apps are installed.

Conclusion:

Customizing your Android home screen is a breeze with these simple methods. Whether you prefer the stock Android experience, Samsung's One UI, or a third-party launcher like Nova, there's an effortless way to declutter and personalize your home screen by removing unwanted app icons. By following these steps, you can create a streamlined and organized interface that reflects your preferences, enhancing your smartphone experience.
