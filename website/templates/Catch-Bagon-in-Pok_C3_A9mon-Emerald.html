How to Find and Catch Bagon in Pokémon Emerald: A Comprehensive Guide

Introduction

Bagon, the Dragon-type Pokémon, is a formidable addition to any Pokémon Trainer's team. Known for its powerful physical attacks and formidable defenses, it evolves into the majestic Salamence, a legendary presence in Pokémon battles. In Pokémon Emerald, finding and capturing Bagon can be a challenging but rewarding endeavor. This guide will provide a detailed step-by-step process to help you successfully acquire this elusive Pokémon.

Step 1: Acquire HM07 Waterfall

Before embarking on your Bagon hunt, you must obtain the HM07 Waterfall, a crucial move that allows your Pokémon to scale waterfalls. The path to obtaining Waterfall involves defeating Team Aqua and acquiring the Mind Badge:

  • Defeat Team Aqua in the Seafloor Cavern: Head to the Seafloor Cavern, located south of Sootopolis City. Confront and defeat Team Aqua to earn the Mind Badge.
  • Find the Sky Pillar and Rayquaza: After obtaining the Mind Badge, travel to the Sky Pillar, a towering structure accessible via Waterfall. Ascend the Sky Pillar and encounter Rayquaza, the Sky High Pokémon.
  • Return to the Sootopolis Gym and Defeat Juan: Once you have encountered Rayquaza, return to the Sootopolis Gym and challenge the Gym Leader, Juan. Defeat him to earn the Rain Badge.
  • Surf Past the Meteor Falls Cave Entrance and Use Waterfall: Exit the Sootopolis Gym and surf east along the coast. You will encounter the entrance to Meteor Falls Cave. Use Waterfall to ascend the large waterfall beside the cave entrance.

Step 2: Locate Bagon

After obtaining Waterfall, you can now access the cave where Bagon can be found:

  • Enter the Cave at the Top of the Waterfall: Once you have scaled the waterfall, there will be a cave entrance on the top. Enter the cave.
  • Climb Down and Back Up the Ladder: Inside the cave, descend the ladder. Once you reach the bottom, climb back up to the top level.
  • Skip Down the Ledges to the Left: From the top level, skip down the ledges to the left. You will come to another ladder.
  • Climb Down the Second Ladder: Descend the second ladder.
  • Surf Through the Cave Entrance and Climb Up: Surf through the cave entrance and ascend to the top of the room.

Step 3: Find and Capture Bagon

Once you have reached the top of the room, you are in the area where Bagon can be found:

  • Walk Around on the Small Patch of Land: Walk around on the small patch of land until you encounter Bagon. Bagon has a low encounter rate, so be patient.
  • Lower Bagon's Health with False Swipe: Once you encounter Bagon, use False Swipe, a move that lowers Bagon's HP to 1 without causing it to faint.
  • Trap Bagon with Sleep or Paralyze: Use moves like Sleep or Paralyze to prevent Bagon from attacking or escaping.
  • Throw an Ultra Ball to Increase Capture Chances: Once Bagon's health is low and it is trapped, throw an Ultra Ball to have the best chance of successfully catching it.

Congratulations!

By following these steps, you have now captured Bagon. With patience and persistence, you can now begin training it to evolve into the formidable Salamence. Best of luck on your Pokémon journey!


Conclusion:

Congratulations! You have successfully found and captured a Bagon in Pokémon Emerald. This elusive Dragon-type Pokémon is a valuable asset to any team and can evolve into the mighty Salamence. Remember to train Bagon carefully, and it will prove to be a formidable ally in your Pokémon adventures. Happy hunting!
