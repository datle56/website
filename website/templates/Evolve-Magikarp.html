How to Train Magikarp Into a Powerful Gyarados: The Ultimate Guide

Magikarp, the infamous Water-type Pokémon known for its comical inability to attack, holds a hidden secret: it has the potential to transform into one of the most formidable Pokémon in the game, Gyarados. This guide will delve into the various methods to efficiently train your Magikarp and unlock its hidden power.

Main Pokémon Games: The Path to Evolution

1. Leveling Up to 20: The Traditional Approach

The most straightforward method to evolve Magikarp is through leveling it up to level 20. This can be achieved through traditional methods such as battling wild Pokémon, participating in trainer battles, or using Exp. Share. However, due to Magikarp's weak stats, this process can be tedious and time-consuming.

2. Utilizing the Exp. Share: A Convenient Way to Boost Experience

Equipping Magikarp with an Exp. Share allows it to passively gain experience even when it is not directly participating in battle. This item can significantly speed up the leveling process, as Magikarp will receive experience from any Pokémon in your party that defeats opponents.

3. Day Care Training: A Hands-Off Approach

Leaving Magikarp in the Day Care Center is another convenient option for accumulating experience. The Pokémon will automatically earn experience over time, allowing you to focus on training other members of your party. The downside to this method is the inability to monitor Magikarp's progress.

4. Exp. Candy Boost: A Swift Leveling Solution

Exp. Candies and Rare Candies offer an accelerated method of leveling up Magikarp. These items can instantly increase Magikarp's experience and level, making the evolution process significantly faster. However, obtaining these candies requires effort and patience, as they are typically found sparingly throughout the game.

Pokémon Go: Evolve Through Collection and Candying

1. Candy Collection: The Foundation of Evolution

In Pokémon Go, Magikarp evolves by accumulating 400 Magikarp Candies. These candies can be obtained by catching multiple Magikarp and transferring duplicates to Professor Willow. Additionally, walking with Magikarp as your buddy will earn you candies over distance.

2. Evolution at 400 Candies: The Moment of Transformation

Once you have collected 400 Magikarp Candies, you can initiate the evolution process by selecting Magikarp in your Pokémon inventory and tapping the "Evolve" button. Your useless Magikarp will then transform into the formidable Gyarados.

Mega-Evolving Gyarados: Unlocking the Ultimate Power (Gen VI-VII)

1. Obtaining the Key Stone and Mega Ring: Essential Items

To Mega-Evolve Gyarados in Generation VI and VII, you must first obtain the Key Stone and Mega Ring. The Key Stone is typically acquired through in-game challenges or found as hidden items. The Mega Ring is awarded after completing the Mega Evolution storyline.

2. Acquiring Gyaradosite: The Specific Mega Stone

Each Mega-Evolving Pokémon has its own unique Mega Stone. In the case of Gyarados, you will need to find the Gyaradosite. This item can be found in specific locations throughout the games or obtained as rewards for completing certain objectives.

3. Equipping Gyarados with Gyaradosite: Power Up

Once you have obtained the Gyaradosite, equip it to Gyarados as a held item. This will allow Gyarados to Mega-Evolve in battle.

4. Mega Evolution in Battle: Unleash the Fury

During battle, select the Mega Evolution option when Gyarados is in combat to transform it into its powerful Mega form. Mega Gyarados boasts enhanced stats and exclusive moves, making it a formidable opponent.

Additional Tips for Training Magikarp

  • Catch Magikarp with High IVs: Magikarp with high Individual Values (IVs) will have better stats, resulting in a more powerful Gyarados.
  • Power Up Before Evolution: Before evolving Magikarp, consider using Stardust and Candy to increase its stats. This will give Gyarados a significant advantage at level 20.
  • Learn Powerful Moves: Magikarp can learn moves such as Waterfall and Crunch, which will be crucial in battles after evolution.
  • Train in Water-Type Battles: Exposing Magikarp to Water-type battles will boost its experience yield, facilitating the leveling process.
  • Be Patient and Persistent: Training Magikarp can be a lengthy endeavor. Stay dedicated to the process and don't give up on your dream of having a formidable Gyarados.
