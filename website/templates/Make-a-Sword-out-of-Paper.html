Small Origami Sword

Step-by-Step Instructions:

1. Initial Creases:

  • Take a square piece of paper and fold it diagonally in half from corner to corner, creating a triangle.
  • Unfold the paper and fold it along the other diagonal, creating an X.

2. Diamond Orientation:

  • Rotate the paper so that the X looks like a diamond shape.

3. Side Folds:

  • Fold the left and right corners of the diamond to the center point.
  • Flip the paper over.

4. Hexagonal Formation:

  • Fold the long sides of the now-hexagonal shape inward to the center, forming triangles on each side.

5. Triangle Inwards:

  • Fold the triangles inward so that they touch the center point.

6. Halving and Diamond Up:

  • Fold the model in half along the vertical axis.
  • Fold the top diamond shape upward.

7. Centerline Fold:

  • Fold the top paper back along the center line of the model.

8. Hilt Formation:

  • Fold the edges of the short part inward to form the hilt of the sword.

9. Upper Blade Fold:

  • Fold down and up the top part of the blade to cover the hilt.

10. Blade Definition:

  • Fold in the sides of the blade to give it its shape.

11. Handle Detail:

  • Fold the bottom tip of the handle upward to complete the full sword form.

Full-Size Paper Sword

Materials:

  • 7-8 sheets of newspaper
  • Tape

Step-by-Step Instructions:

1. Blade Creation:

  • Roll up the newspaper sheets diagonally into a tight cylinder.
  • Secure the ends with tape to form the blade.

2. Handle Construction:

  • Roll up another pile of newspaper into a smaller cylinder to make the handle.

3. Handle Attachment:

  • Bend the handle in half and tape it securely to the blade.

4. Sword Completion:

  • The sword is now fully assembled and ready for play or display.
