Embrace the Curtain Hair Revolution: A Guide to DIY Styling

Step into a world of effortless glamour with the ethereal beauty of curtain hair. How to Cut Curtain Hair Yourself

Step 1: Prepare Your Hair

  • Dampen slightly: If you have straight or wavy hair, lightly dampen it to prevent breakage and make it easier to work with.
  • Keep dry if curly: Curly hair should be cut dry to avoid shrinkage and ensure accurate shaping.

Step 2: Part and Divide

  • Straight center part: Create a clean part down the middle of your head, starting from the top and extending to the nape of your neck.
  • Forward combing: Comb the sides of your hair forward, keeping it away from the back section.

Step 3: Cut the Back Section

  • Shoulder length: Measure and cut the back section to approximately 3 inches long.
  • Curved shape: As you cut, create a slight curve on the top edge of the back section, tapering it shorter towards the neck.

Step 4: Cut the Side Sections

  • Length: Trim the side sections to 3-4 inches long, maintaining the curved shape at the top.
  • Modified bob: The side sections should frame your face like a modified bob, with slightly longer layers towards the front.

Step 5: Cut the Top Section

  • Face-framing layers: Part the top section off from the sides. Hold sections of hair at an angle and trim the ends to create face-framing layers.
  • Length: Cut the front portion of the top section to 7-8 inches long, gradually shortening it towards the back.

Step 6: Add Layers (Optional)

  • Volume and movement: For added volume and movement, continue adding layers to the top section by holding sections at an angle and trimming the ends.
  • Gradation: Create a gradual transition from shorter layers in the back to longer layers in the front.

How to Style Curtain Hair

Step 1: Part and Dry

  • Middle or side part: Part your hair in the middle or slightly to one side, depending on your preference.
  • Downward drying: Dry your hair downward with a blow dryer to prevent frizz and enhance the natural movement.

Step 2: Create Volume (Straight Hair)

  • Round brush: Use a round brush while blow-drying straight hair to create volume and shape.
  • Lift and shape: Lift the hair at the roots with the brush and roll it down towards the ends to create bouncy curls.

Step 3: Finish with Anti-Frizz Cream or Gel

  • Smoothness and control: Once dry, apply a small amount of anti-frizz cream or gel to the ends of your hair to smooth out any flyaways and control frizz.
  • Enhance shine: Anti-frizz products can also add shine and definition to your hair.

Tips for Cutting Curtain Hair

  • Use sharp scissors for clean and precise cuts.
  • Work in small sections to avoid unevenness.
  • Take your time and don't be afraid to make adjustments as needed.
  • Don't over-cut the back section, as it can be difficult to blend with the sides later.
  • For longer layers in the front, cut the top section at an angle and blend it into the side sections.
  • Use a mirror for both the back and sides to ensure even cutting.
