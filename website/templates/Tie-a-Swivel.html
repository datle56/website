Quick and Easy Knots for Fishing Swivels

Swivels are an essential component of any fishing setup, as they prevent line twist and tangles, which can significantly hinder your fishing experience. Choosing the right knot for attaching a swivel is crucial for ensuring its secure and effective function. Here are three simple and reliable knots that you can use for this purpose:

1. Simple Knot

The Simple Knot is a basic and time-tested method for attaching a swivel to your fishing line. It is quick to tie and suitable for most fishing applications.

Instructions:

  • Create a loop in the fishing line that is approximately 4 inches long.
  • Pass the loop through the eye of the swivel hook.
  • Bring the loop back around the main line and thread it through its own opening.
  • Guide the swivel through the loop and tighten the knot by pulling on the line and the short end of the loop.

2. Offshore Swivel Knot

The Offshore Swivel Knot is a stronger and more secure knot, making it particularly suitable for heavier lines and larger swivels. It is often used in offshore fishing or when targeting large fish.

Instructions:

  • Thread the fishing line through the swivel twice, forming a loop.
  • Twirl the loop once above the swivel to create a twisting effect.
  • Fold the loop back and insert the swivel through the opening created by the twist.
  • Repeat this process 3-6 times, depending on the desired strength of the knot.
  • Pull on the main line and the short end to tighten the knot firmly against the swivel.

3. Improved Clinch Knot

The Improved Clinch Knot is a versatile knot that can be used to attach both swivels and lures to your fishing line. It is known for its strength and reliability.

Instructions:

  • Thread the fishing line through the swivel and double it back on itself.
  • Twist the doubled line 5-7 times to create a series of coils.
  • Thread the end of the line through the loop created by the coils.
  • Pull on both the short end and the main line simultaneously to tighten the knot against the swivel.

Additional Tips:

  • Always wet the knot before tightening it to reduce friction and prevent the line from breaking.
  • Trim any excess line close to the knot to minimize bulk and potential snags.
  • Regularly inspect your knots for any signs of wear or damage, especially after catching fish or encountering obstacles.
