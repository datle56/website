How to Draw Realistic People

Method 1 (Semi-Realistic)

Step 1: Sketch the Basic Head Shape

  • With light strokes, sketch a slightly oval circle for the head. This circle will serve as the foundation for the face.

Step 2: Divide the Circle

  • Draw a vertical line that bisects the circle from top to bottom. This line will help you position the facial features.

Step 3: Outline the Jaws and Chin

  • From the bottom of the vertical line, draw two curved lines that extend outwards to form the jaws.
  • At the base of the jaws, sketch a small horizontal line for the chin.

Step 4: Draw Guidelines for the Facial Features

  • Draw a horizontal line through the circle slightly below the vertical line to indicate the eye level.
  • Draw two short horizontal lines beneath the eye line for the nose.
  • Draw a slightly curved line below the nose for the mouth.

Step 5: Shape the Eyes and Nose

  • Sketch almond-shaped eyes on the eye line, taking care to angle them slightly towards the center.
  • Add a triangular shape for the nose, with the tip aligned with the vertical line.

Step 6: Draw Lips and Eyebrows

  • Sketch two small curves for the lips, extending outwards from the nose.
  • Add curved lines above the eyes for the eyebrows.

Step 7: Outline the Ears

  • Sketch two rounded shapes on either side of the head, above the eye level. These will be the ears.

Step 8: Draw Guides for the Neck and Shoulders

  • Draw a vertical line from the chin downwards for the neck.
  • Add two short horizontal lines at the base of the neck for the shoulders.

Step 9: Add Hair

  • Based on the guidelines, sketch in the desired hairstyle. Consider the hair's length, texture, and style.

Step 10: Erase and Outline

  • Erase the initial sketch lines as they are no longer needed.
  • With a darker pencil or pen, outline the final shape of the head and facial features.

Step 11: Color the Face and Hair

  • Use colored pencils or markers to add color to the face and hair. Flesh tones, browns, and blacks are commonly used.

Step 12: Enhance with Shadows and Highlights

  • To add depth, draw shadows under the nose, chin, and around the eyes.
  • Add highlights on the brow bone, cheekbones, and nose bridge.

Method 2 (Realistic)

Step 1: Sketch the Perspective Circle

  • Draw a smaller circle than in Method 1, tilted slightly to create a perspective effect.

Step 2: Divide and Outline

  • Follow the same steps outlined in Step 2 and Step 3 of Method 1 to divide the circle and outline the jaws and chin.

Step 3: Draw Realistic Facial Features

  • Sketch the eyes, nose, and mouth with a more anatomically accurate approach. Consider the shape of the iris, the structure of the nose, and the curvature of the lips.

Step 4: Draw Hair and Details

  • Render the hair with more detail, paying attention to individual strands and volume.
  • Add details to the face, such as eyebrows, eyelashes, freckles, and skin texture.

Step 5: Create Guidelines for the Body

  • Sketch guidelines for the shoulders, arms, and torso, taking into account the perspective and proportions.

Step 6: Erase and Outline

  • As in Method 1, erase the sketch lines and outline the final shape with a darker medium.

Step 7: Color with Shades and Highlights

  • Use a variety of colored pencils or markers to add color to the face and body. Pay attention to the different skin tones and subtle variations in shading.
  • Add highlights to create a three-dimensional effect, particularly on the cheekbones, nose bridge, and forehead.
