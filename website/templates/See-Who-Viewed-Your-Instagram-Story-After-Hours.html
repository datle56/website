How to See Who Viewed Your Instagram Stories After 24 Hours

Instagram Stories, the ephemeral content feature that allows users to share snippets of their lives for just 24 hours, has become an integral part of the platform. While stories disappear after a day, the curiosity to know who viewed them often lingers. Here's a detailed guide to help you uncover who has viewed your Instagram Stories even after they've expired:

Steps:

  1. Access Your Story Archive:

    • Begin by navigating to your Instagram profile page.
    • Tap the "three lines" icon in the top-right corner to open the menu.
    • Select "Archive" from the list of options.
    • Once in the Archive, switch to the "Stories Archive" tab to view all your past stories.

  2. Choose the Desired Story:

    • Here, you will find a chronological list of all the stories you've posted.
    • Scroll through the archive and select the story you're interested in seeing the viewers for.

  3. Swipe Up to Reveal Viewers:

    • Tap on the chosen story to open it.
    • Once the story is playing, swipe up on the screen.
    • This action will reveal a list of Instagram users who have viewed your story.

Additional Notes:

  • Time Limitation: It's important to note that you can only view the viewers of stories that were posted within the last 48 hours. After that, the viewer data is automatically removed.
  • Private Account: If you have a private Instagram account, only your approved followers will be able to view your stories. Therefore, the list of viewers you see will be limited to your followers.
  • Multiple Views: Some users may view your story multiple times. In such cases, each individual view will be counted separately, and their username will appear multiple times on the viewers' list.
  • Expiration Warning: Before a story expires, Instagram will send you a notification reminding you that the viewers list will be removed in 24 hours. If you wish to permanently save the names of those who viewed your story, consider taking a screenshot of the viewers' list before it disappears.

Conclusion:

Now that you know how to access your Instagram Story viewers after 24 hours, you can gain insights into who's engaging with your content. Whether you're a business owner, influencer, or simply want to track your popularity, this knowledge can be invaluable.

Remember that Instagram only stores viewer data for up to 48 hours, so it's important to archive your stories if you want to keep a permanent record. By utilizing the Story Archive feature, you can analyze your audience, track your reach, and engage with your followers in a meaningful way.
