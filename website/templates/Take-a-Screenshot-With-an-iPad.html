How to Take a Screenshot on an iPad

Taking screenshots on an iPad is a quick and easy way to capture and share important information, images, or moments on your screen. Here's a detailed guide on how to take a screenshot on an iPad, depending on the model and its features:

Without a Home Button:

This method applies to newer iPad models, including the iPad Air (4th and 5th generation), iPad mini (6th generation), and iPad Pro (11-inch and 12.9-inch 3rd, 4th, and 5th generation).

  1. Locate the Buttons: Identify the power button and the volume up or volume down button on the side of your iPad.

  2. Press and Hold Buttons Simultaneously: Press and hold down both the power button and either volume up or volume down button at the same time.

  3. Release Buttons: When you hear a shutter sound, release both buttons.

  4. Screenshot Icon Appears: A screenshot icon will appear in the lower-left corner of the screen.

  5. Edit or Save: Tap the screenshot icon to open the screenshot in the Markup tool, where you can make edits or save it directly to your Photos app.

With a Home Button:

This method is applicable to older iPad models that have a physical home button, such as the iPad Air (1st, 2nd, and 3rd generation), iPad mini (1st through 5th generation), and iPad Pro (9.7-inch and 10.5-inch).

  1. Locate the Buttons: Identify the power button on the top of your iPad and the home button at the bottom of the screen.

  2. Press and Hold Buttons Simultaneously: Press and hold down both the power button and the home button simultaneously.

  3. Release Buttons: When you hear a shutter sound, release both buttons.

  4. Screenshot Icon Appears: A screenshot icon will appear in the lower-left corner of the screen.

  5. Edit or Save: Tap the screenshot icon to open the screenshot in the Markup tool, where you can make edits or save it directly to your Photos app.

Using AssistiveTouch (if Buttons Don't Work):

If the physical buttons on your iPad are not functioning, you can use AssistiveTouch to take a screenshot.

  1. Enable AssistiveTouch: Go to Settings > Accessibility > AssistiveTouch and toggle it on.

  2. Add Screenshot to AssistiveTouch: Tap on Customize Top Level Menu and select the "Screenshot" option to add it to the AssistiveTouch menu.

  3. Open AssistiveTouch Menu: Tap the AssistiveTouch icon that appears on the screen.

  4. Select Screenshot: Tap on the "Screenshot" button in the AssistiveTouch menu.

  5. Screenshot Taken: A screenshot will be captured, and the screenshot icon will appear in the lower-left corner of the screen for editing or saving.

Additional Tips:

  • Screenshot Location: Screenshots are automatically saved to the Photos app under the "Albums" section in the "Screenshots" album.

  • Editing Screenshots: When you tap on the screenshot icon, it opens in the Markup tool. Here, you can annotate, crop, sign, or add text and shapes to the screenshot before saving it.

  • Sharing Screenshots: From the Markup tool or the Photos app, you can easily share screenshots via email, AirDrop, Messages, or other shareable channels.
