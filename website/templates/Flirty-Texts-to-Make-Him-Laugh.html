Tips for Sending the Perfect Flirty Text:

  • Be Authentic: Let your unique personality shine through in your texts.
  • Use Emojis Sparingly: A few well-chosen emojis can enhance your message, but don't overdo it.
  • Don't Be Overbearing: Send texts at a reasonable frequency and avoid bombarding your crush with messages.
  • Embrace the Banter: Flirting should be a fun and lighthearted exchange. Don't take yourself too seriously and be open to playful banter.
  • Read Between the Lines: Pay attention to your crush's responses and adjust your tone and content accordingly.
