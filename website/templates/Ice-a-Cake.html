Basic Icing

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup confectioner's sugar
  • 1-2 tablespoons boiling water (adjust for desired consistency)
  • Food coloring (optional)
  • Flavorings (optional, e.g., vanilla extract, almond extract)

Instructions:

  1. Let the cake cool: Allow the cake to cool completely before icing to prevent the icing from melting.

  2. Prepare the icing: In a bowl, whisk together the confectioner's sugar and boiling water until a smooth and lump-free paste forms. Adjust the amount of water as needed for a desired consistency. The icing should be thick enough to spread easily without being runny.

  3. Add flavorings and color: If desired, add food coloring and flavorings to the icing and whisk until thoroughly combined. Adjust the amount of color and flavoring to suit your preferences.

  4. Spread the icing: Use a knife or offset spatula to spread the icing evenly over the cooled cake. Start from the center and work your way towards the edges, smoothing the icing as you go. For a seamless finish, dip the knife or spatula in warm water before spreading to prevent sticking.

  5. Decorate and set: You can decorate the iced cake with sprinkles, edible glitter, chopped nuts, or any other desired embellishments. Place the iced cake in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes to allow the icing to set and firm up.

Layer Cake Icing

Ingredients:

  • 1 batch Basic Icing
  • 1/2 cup cake filling (e.g., whipped cream, buttercream frosting, chocolate ganache)

Instructions:

  1. Prepare the bottom layer: Use a serrated knife to trim the rounded top off the bottom cake layer, creating a flat surface for the filling and icing.

  2. Spread the filling: Spread a layer of your desired filling evenly over the trimmed bottom cake layer.

  3. Place the top layer: Carefully place the second cake layer on top of the filling. Gently press down to secure the layers together.

  4. Apply the crumb coat: Use about 1/4 cup of the Basic Icing to apply a thin layer (crumb coat) to the entire cake, both sides and top. The crumb coat will help catch any loose crumbs and ensure a smooth final layer of icing.

  5. Ice the cake: Apply the remaining Basic Icing to the cake, smoothing it evenly over the crumb coat. Use a knife or offset spatula to create a smooth and decorative finish.

  6. Decorate and set: Decorate the iced cake as desired and place it in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before serving. This will allow the icing to set and firm up, resulting in a stable and beautiful layer cake.
