Making Samosas: A Beginner's Step-by-Step Guide

Samosas, the beloved Indian street food, are renowned for their crispy shells and flavorful fillings. With this easy step-by-step guide, you can recreate this delectable treat in your own kitchen.

Ingredients

For the Dough:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil or ghee

For the Filling:

  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon minced ginger
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 green chili, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 large potato, boiled and mashed
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas
  • Salt to taste

For Frying:

  • Vegetable oil for deep frying

Instructions

Making the Dough:

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour and salt.
  2. Add the oil or ghee and work it into the flour using your fingertips until it resembles coarse breadcrumbs.
  3. Gradually add water, 1 tablespoon at a time, and knead until a soft and pliable dough forms.
  4. Cover the dough with a damp cloth and let it rest for at least 30 minutes.

Preparing the Filling:

  1. Heat a little oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the onion and sauté until translucent.
  3. Add the ginger and garlic and cook for another minute.
  4. Add the green chili, cumin, coriander, and turmeric and cook for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the mashed potato and peas and cook until the mixture is dry and flavorful.
  6. Season with salt to taste.
  7. Let the filling cool completely before using.

Assembling the Samosas:

  1. Divide the dough into 8 equal pieces.
  2. Roll out each piece into a thin, flat circle.
  3. Cut each circle in half to form two semicircles.
  4. Place a small amount of filling in the center of one semicircle.
  5. Fold the semicircle over the filling to form a triangle.
  6. Press the edges firmly to seal the samosa.

Frying the Samosas:

  1. Heat the vegetable oil in a deep fryer or large pot to 375 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Carefully drop the samosas into the hot oil and fry for 3-4 minutes, or until golden brown and crispy.
  3. Drain the samosas on paper towels.

Serving and Enjoying:

  • Serve the samosas hot with your favorite dipping sauce, such as green chutney or tamarind chutney.
  • Enjoy the crispy shell and flavorful filling of these delicious Indian treats.

Tips for Perfect Samosas:

  • Experiment with different fillings, such as paneer, minced meat, or other vegetables.
  • Make sure to preheat the oil to the correct temperature before frying to prevent soggy samosas.
  • Don't overcrowd the fryer when frying the samosas.
  • Let the samosas rest for a few minutes before serving to ensure they are cooked through and crispy.
