Easy Ways to Send Secret Messages with Invisible Ink

Materials:

  • Lemon juice
  • Lemon juice and water
  • Baking soda and water
  • Grape juice
  • Milk
  • Laundry detergent
  • White crayon
  • Watercolor paints
  • Pencil
  • Paper

Methods:

Lemon Juice and Heat:

  • Materials: Lemon juice, cotton swab or brush, lightbulb or candle, paper

  • Steps:

    • Squeeze lemon juice and dilute it with a small amount of water.
    • Dip a cotton swab or brush into the solution and write your message on paper.
    • Allow the paper to dry completely.
    • Reveal the message by holding it close to a lightbulb or candle. The heat will cause the lemon juice to turn brown, revealing the hidden text.

Baking Soda and Grape Juice:

  • Materials: Baking soda, water, cotton swab, grape juice, paper

  • Steps:

    • Create a baking soda solution by mixing baking soda with a small amount of water.
    • Write your message on paper using a cotton swab dipped in the baking soda solution.
    • Allow the paper to dry completely.
    • Dip a cotton swab into grape juice and paint over the dried message. As the grape juice reacts with the baking soda, the secret message will slowly appear.

Milk and Heat:

  • Materials: Milk, pencil or cotton swab, lightbulb or candle, paper

  • Steps:

    • Dip a pencil or cotton swab into milk and write your message on paper.
    • Allow the paper to dry completely.
    • Reveal the message by holding it close to a lightbulb or candle. The heat will caramelize the milk solids, making the message visible.

Laundry Detergent and Black Light:

  • Materials: Laundry detergent, cotton swab, black light, paper

  • Steps:

    • Dip a cotton swab into laundry detergent and write your message on paper.
    • Allow the paper to dry completely.
    • Shine a black light on the paper. The laundry detergent will absorb the ultraviolet light from the black light and emit a faint glow, revealing the secret message.

White Crayon and Watercolor Paints:

  • Materials: White crayon, watercolor paints, paintbrush, paper

  • Steps:

    • Write your message on paper using a white crayon. The crayon should be applied with enough pressure to create a smooth surface.
    • Apply watercolor paints over the entire paper. The paint will adhere to the areas where there is no crayon coating, leaving the message visible.

Pencil Indents:

  • Materials: Pencil, paper

  • Steps:

    • Write your message on paper using a pencil and press down firmly.
    • Remove the top sheet of paper to create a carbon copy.
    • Shade the carbon copy with a pencil, applying light pressure. The indentations from the original message will become visible on the shaded copy.
