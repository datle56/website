Protecting Yourself and Contacting Spirits

Method 1: Preparing for a Session

Before embarking on a spirit communication session, it is crucial to take precautions to protect yourself and ensure a positive experience. Here's a comprehensive guide:

  • Prayer and Visualization: Invoke divine protection by saying a prayer or mantra. Simultaneously, visualize yourself enveloped in a radiant light, shielding you from any negative spirits.
  • Meaningful Objects: Bring an item of sentimental value, such as a piece of jewelry that belonged to a loved one. This object serves as a conduit to attract and connect with a specific spirit.
  • Abstinence from Substances: Avoid consuming alcohol or drugs prior to the session. These substances can impair your judgment and make you more susceptible to negative energies.
  • Smudging: Burn sage incense to purify the area where the session will take place. The aromatic smoke of sage disperses negative vibrations and creates a spiritually conducive atmosphere.

Method 2: Using a Dowsing Pendulum

A dowsing pendulum is a tool used to communicate with spirits by harnessing the energy field around your body. Here's how to use it:

  • Crystal Pendant: Suspend a crystal or stone from a string, chain, or cord. The type of crystal you choose is not crucial.
  • Yes/No Patterns: Establish a clear communication protocol by asking the spirits to indicate "yes" or "no" by causing the pendulum to swing in specific patterns.
  • Ask Questions: Pose questions to the spirits and interpret the pendulum's movements based on the agreed-upon patterns. The pendulum's response can provide insights into the spirit's presence, identity, and intentions.

Method 3: Using a Ouija Board

A Ouija board is a board with letters and numbers that is used as a medium for communicating with spirits. Here are the protocols to follow:

  • Respectful Atmosphere: Gather a small group of friends or family members who approach the session with respect and a shared intention of connecting with the spirit world.
  • Polite Invitation: Begin the session by summoning the desired spirit politely and with patience. Allow ample time for the planchette (the small triangular pointer that moves across the board) to respond.
  • Questions and Interpretation: Ask questions and interpret the planchette's movements based on the arrangement of the letters and numbers. The planchette's responses can reveal the spirit's identity, information, or guidance.
  • Beware of Negative Spirits: Be discerning of the spirit's energy. If you experience random or figure-eight patterns in the planchette's movement, end the session immediately. These patterns may indicate the presence of a negative or malevolent spirit.
  • Closing the Connection: Conclude the session by guiding the planchette to the "goodbye" symbol on the board. This action effectively disconnects the communication channel and releases the spirit from the board.
