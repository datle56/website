Indulge in the Sweet Delicacy: A Comprehensive Guide to Crafting Pastillas

Introduction

Pastillas, a delectable Filipino candy, tantalize taste buds with their creamy, milky flavor and irresistible sugar coating. Traditionally made with condensed and powdered milk, this treat can be prepared in both cooked and no-cook variations, offering a delightful experience for all.

Crafting No-Cook Pastillas

For those seeking a fuss-free approach, no-cook pastillas provide a delightful and effortless treat.

  1. Gather your Ingredients:

    • 1 cup condensed milk
    • 1 cup powdered milk
    • 1/4 cup margarine (unsalted), softened

  2. Combine the Base:

    • In a large bowl, whisk together the condensed and powdered milk until smooth.

  3. Incorporate the Margarine:

    • Gradually add the softened margarine and stir vigorously until the mixture forms a cohesive dough.

  4. Shape and Coat:

    • Roll the dough into small circles or cylindrical shapes, approximately 1-inch in diameter.
    • Generously roll each candy in granulated sugar to coat.

  5. Store and Enjoy:

    • Individually wrap each pastilla in cellophane and store in an airtight container. They will keep for 2-3 days at room temperature.

Creating Cooked Pastillas

While no-cook pastillas offer convenience, cooked pastillas boast a distinct creaminess and whiteness.

  1. Assemble your Ingredients:

    • 1 cup condensed milk
    • 1/2 cup powdered milk
    • 1/2 cup sugar
    • 1/4 cup butter (unsalted), softened
    • 1/2 cup evaporated milk (optional)

  2. Bring to a Boil:

    • In a medium saucepan, combine the condensed milk, powdered milk, sugar, and evaporated milk (if using). Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring constantly.

  3. Add the Butter:

    • Once boiling, reduce heat to low and add the butter. Continue stirring until the butter melts and the mixture thickens.

  4. Cool and Shape:

    • Remove the saucepan from the heat and let the mixture cool slightly.
    • Using a spoon or piping bag, shape the cooled mixture into small candies.

  5. Sugar Coating and Storage:

    • Roll each candy in granulated sugar.
    • Individually wrap the pastillas in cellophane and store in an airtight container. They will keep for 2-3 days at room temperature.

Tips for Enhancing Pastillas

  • Enhance creaminess: Boiling evaporated milk before adding it to the cooked pastillas mixture yields a creamier and whiter candy.
  • Experiment with flavors: Add a touch of vanilla extract or fruit puree to the milk mixture for a unique twist.
  • Choose quality ingredients: High-quality condensed and powdered milk produce richer, more flavorful pastillas.
  • Proper storage: Store pastillas in an airtight container at room temperature to maintain their freshness.
  • Serve chilled: Refrigerating pastillas for a few hours before serving enhances their texture and flavor.
