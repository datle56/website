Multiplying by 9:

  1. Hold your hands up with palms facing you. Imagine your hands as a clock, with your thumbs representing 12 o'clock and your remaining fingers representing the other hours.

  2. Each finger represents a number, starting with 1 on your left thumb. Assign the following numbers to your fingers, starting from your left thumb and moving towards your pinky: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.

  3. Point down the finger you want to multiply by 9. For example, if you want to multiply by 3, point down your left middle finger, which represents the number 3.

  4. Count the fingers to the left and right of the pointed finger. Starting from the finger to the left of the pointed finger, which represents 2, count the fingers until you reach the pointed finger. In this case, you would count 2. Then, starting from the finger to the right of the pointed finger, which represents 4, count the fingers until you reach the pointed finger again. In this case, you would count 7.

  5. Combine the two numbers you counted. The first number you counted, 2, represents the tens digit of the answer. The second number you counted, 7, represents the units digit of the answer. Therefore, the answer to 9x3 is 27.

To multiply 9x6, follow these steps:

  1. Hold your hands up with your palms facing you and your fingers spread out.
  2. Point down your left thumb, which represents the number 1.
  3. Count the fingers to the left of your left thumb: none.
  4. Count the fingers to the right of your left thumb: 5.
  5. The answer is 05, or 5.

Multiplying by 6, 7, 8, or 10:

  1. Hold your hands with palms facing each other, fingers touching. Visualize your hands as two sides of a book, with your pinkies representing the front cover and your thumbs representing the back cover.

  2. Each finger represents a number:

    • Pinkies: 6
    • Ring fingers: 7
    • Middle fingers: 8
    • Index fingers: 9
    • Thumbs: 10

  3. Touch the fingers representing the multiplication problem. For example, to multiply 7x6, touch your left ring finger with your right pinky.

  4. Count the touching fingers and the ones beneath them to get the 10s. In this case, there are 3 fingers beneath the touching fingers: your left pinky, your right thumb, and your right pinky. Therefore, the 10s digit is 3.

  5. Count the remaining fingers above the touching ones. In this case, there are 1 finger above the left ring finger (your left middle finger) and 2 fingers above the right pinky (your right ring finger and your right index finger). Therefore, the units digit is 12.

  6. Multiply the remaining fingers on each hand. In this case, there are no remaining fingers on your left hand and 1 remaining finger (your left index finger) on your right hand. Therefore, the product of the remaining fingers is 0.

  7. Add the 10s and the product of the remaining fingers to find the answer. In this case, 30 + 0 = 30. Therefore, the answer to 7x6 is 30.

Example:

To multiply 8x7, follow these steps:

  1. Hold your hands with your palms facing each other and your fingers touching.
  2. Touch your left middle finger (which represents 8) with your right ring finger (which represents 7).
  3. Count the touching fingers and the ones beneath them: 3 (left pinky, right thumb, right pinky)
  4. Count the remaining fingers above the touching ones: 2 (left index finger, right ring finger)
  5. Multiply the remaining fingers on each hand: 0 (no remaining fingers on the left hand, 1 remaining finger on the right hand)
  6. Add the 10s and the product of the remaining fingers: 30 + 0 = 30
  7. The answer is 30.
