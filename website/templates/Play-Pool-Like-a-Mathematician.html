Predicting Ball Bounce Angle

Rail Bounce Angle

  • When a ball strikes a rail at an angle X, it will bounce off at the same angle X. This is known as the law of reflection.
  • If the cue ball and object ball are equidistant from a rail, aim at the midpoint of the rail section. This will ensure that the cue ball bounces directly into the object ball.
  • If the cue ball is X times farther from the rail than the object ball, imagine perpendicular lines from the rail to the balls. Aim at a point on the rail (X / (X + 1)) of the distance to the object ball's line. This will account for the different distances of the balls from the rail.

Calculating Angle for Object Ball

Ghost Ball Technique

  • Aim for the ghost ball: Draw a line from the pocket through the object ball. Imagine a ghost ball touching the object ball and sitting on this line. Aim for the center of the ghost ball. This technique ensures that the object ball travels towards the intended pocket.

Using English (Side Spin)

English is a technique used to impart side spin on the cue ball, which affects the direction of the object ball. To ensure the object ball does not pick up undesirable spin, use the following percentages of outside English:

  • Cut angle 15°: Slightly more than 20%
  • Cut angle 30°: 40%
  • Cut angle 45°: 55%
  • Cut angle 60°: 70%
  • Cut angle 90°: 80%

Higher percentages of outside English will result in the object ball rolling around the edge of the table, while lower percentages will limit the amount of spin transferred to the object ball. The exact percentage of English required depends on the specific shot and table conditions.
