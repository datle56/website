How to Unlock and Utilize Rock Smash in Pokémon Emerald: A Comprehensive Guide

1. Embark on a Journey to Mauville City:

Commence your quest to acquire Rock Smash by making your way to Mauville City, the bustling hub of technology and innovation. Secure your second gym badge and proceed towards this vibrant metropolis.

2. Seek Wisdom from the Rock Smash Guy:

Venture into the heart of Mauville City and locate the humble abode nestled adjacent to the bustling Pokémon Mart. Within its modest confines resides the enigmatic Rock Smash Guy, who holds the coveted knowledge you seek.

3. Receive the Rock Smash HM:

Engage in a conversation with the Rock Smash Guy, expressing your desire to harness the power of Rock Smash. However, before he bestows this potent ability upon you, you must prove your worth by acquiring both the Stone Badge and the Knuckle Badge from your valiant gym conquests.

4. Assign Rock Smash to a Worthy Ally:

Once you have demonstrated your prowess, the Rock Smash Guy will entrust you with the coveted HM. However, choosing the right Pokémon to wield this ability is crucial. Select a loyal companion who will not require the move slot in the future.

5. Triumph Over the Electric Enigma:

To unlock the full potential of Rock Smash, embark on a quest to claim the Dynamo Badge from the Electric-type gym leader reigning over Mauville City Gym. Engage in a fierce battle to overcome this formidable opponent.

6. Unleash the Power of Rock Smash:

Victory over the Electric-type gym leader grants you the privilege of using Rock Smash anywhere. Its might allows you to shatter boulders and impediments that once blocked your path, opening up new horizons and advancing your journey.

7. Test Your Might in Verdanturf Town:

Initiate your newfound skill by venturing into Verdanturf Town's cave. Summon forth Rock Smash and shatter the obstructive boulders that hinder your progress, paving the way for further exploration.

8. Advance Your Saga on Route 112:

Continue your odyssey on Route 112, where a formidable boulder stands in your path. Fear not, for your mastery of Rock Smash grants you the power to demolish it, granting you access to the fiery depths of the Fiery Path and propelling you forward in your adventure.
