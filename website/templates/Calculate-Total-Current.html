Understanding Electrical Concepts

Current

Current is the flow of charged particles, typically electrons, through a conductor. It is measured in amperes (A). Electrons flow from areas of high negative charge to areas of low negative charge, creating a current.

Voltage

Voltage is the force that drives current to flow. It is measured in volts (V). Voltage creates an electrical potential difference between two points, causing electrons to move.

Resistance

Resistance is the opposition to the flow of current. It is measured in ohms (Ω). Resistance arises from the material's structure and impedes electron movement. The higher the resistance, the harder it is for current to flow.

Calculating Current in Series Circuits

Series circuits are electrical circuits with components connected end-to-end. In a series circuit:

  • Total Resistance: The total resistance is simply the sum of the individual resistances of each component in the circuit: R_total = R1 + R2 + R3 + ...

  • Total Voltage: The total voltage across the circuit is equal to the sum of the individual voltages across each component: V_total = V1 + V2 + V3 + ...

  • Total Current: The total current flowing through the circuit is the same throughout the circuit and is calculated by dividing the total voltage by the total resistance: Itotal = Vtotal / R_total

Calculating Current in Parallel Circuits

Parallel circuits are electrical circuits with components connected side-by-side. In a parallel circuit:

  • Total Voltage: The total voltage across the circuit is the same across each parallel path: V_total = V1 = V2 = V3 = ...

  • Total Resistance: The total resistance of the circuit is calculated using the reciprocal formula: 1 / R_total = 1 / R1 + 1 / R2 + 1 / R3 + ...

  • Total Current: The total current flowing through the circuit is the sum of the currents flowing through each parallel path: I_total = I1 + I2 + I3 + ...

Example Calculations

Series Circuit:

  • Components: Resistor 1 (10 Ω), Resistor 2 (20 Ω), Voltage Source (30 V)

  • Total Resistance: R_total = 10 Ω + 20 Ω = 30 Ω

  • Total Voltage: V_total = 30 V

  • Total Current: Itotal = Vtotal / R_total = 30 V / 30 Ω = 1 A

Parallel Circuit:

  • Components: Resistor 1 (10 Ω), Resistor 2 (20 Ω), Voltage Source (30 V)

  • Total Voltage: V_total = 30 V

  • Total Resistance: 1 / Rtotal = 1 / 10 Ω + 1 / 20 Ω Rtotal = 1 / (0.1 Ω + 0.05 Ω) R_total = 6.67 Ω

  • Total Current: Itotal = Vtotal / R_total = 30 V / 6.67 Ω = 4.5 A
