Share Xbox Game Pass with Family: A Comprehensive Guide

Xbox Game Pass, Microsoft's premier gaming subscription service, offers an extensive library of games, exclusive benefits, and the latest releases. For those with the Xbox Game Pass Ultimate subscription, an additional perk awaits: the ability to share it with your entire household.

Step-by-Step Guide to Sharing Xbox Game Pass Ultimate:

Setting Your Console as the "Home Xbox":

  1. Ensure the account holding the subscription is signed in on your primary console.
  2. Navigate to "System" in the dashboard menu.
  3. Select "Settings" and then "Personalization."
  4. Under "My Home Xbox," choose "Make this my home Xbox."

Logging In Additional Accounts:

  1. Turn on the secondary console in your household.
  2. Sign in to the account you wish to share the subscription with.

Benefits of Home Xbox Sharing:

By setting your primary console as the "home Xbox," all accounts that log into it will automatically gain access to the following benefits of your Game Pass Ultimate subscription:

Games Library:

  • Unlimited access to the vast library of Xbox Game Pass games, including first-party Microsoft exclusives like Halo and Forza.

Downloadable Content (DLCs):

  • All downloadable expansions and add-ons for Game Pass games, allowing for a richer and more comprehensive gaming experience.

Xbox Live Gold:

  • Online multiplayer, party chat, and access to exclusive member discounts, normally available with Xbox Live Gold membership.

Xbox Cloud Gaming:

  • The ability to stream Game Pass games directly to compatible devices, such as laptops, tablets, and smartphones.

Additional Perks:

  • Early access to select upcoming games through Game Pass Early Access.
  • Member-exclusive discounts and promotions on digital purchases in the Microsoft Store.

Sharing Restrictions:

It's important to note that Game Pass Ultimate can only be shared with accounts within the same household and on the same console. Accounts outside the home Xbox can access the subscription's benefits only as long as they're signed in to the shared console.

Tips:

  • Ensure all accounts are signed in with Microsoft accounts.
  • Only one "home Xbox" can be designated per subscription.
  • Changing the home Xbox is limited to a specific number of times per year.
  • If you encounter any issues, restart both consoles and try again.

By following these steps and adhering to the sharing restrictions, you can extend the benefits of Xbox Game Pass Ultimate to your entire household, unlocking a world of gaming possibilities for everyone.
