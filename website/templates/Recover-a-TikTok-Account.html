Recovering a TikTok Account

Forgotten Password

Step 1: Visit the TikTok website or open the TikTok mobile app. Step 2: Click or tap on "Log in." Step 3: Select "Use phone/email/username." Step 4: Click or tap on "Forgot password?" Step 5: Enter your username or email address associated with your TikTok account. Step 6: Click or tap on "Send code" to receive a password reset code via email or SMS. Step 7: Enter the code you received and create a new password.

Deleted Account

Step 1: Log in to TikTok within 30 days of deleting your account. Step 2: You will be notified that your account is deactivated. Step 3: Click or tap on "Reactivate" to restore your account.

Note: If you have deleted your account and it has been more than 30 days, you will not be able to recover it.

Banned Account

Step 1: Submit an appeal within the TikTok app. Navigate to "Account" > "Settings" > "Appeal." Step 2: Alternatively, you can submit an email appeal to TikTok's business support team at business-servicesupport@tiktok.com. Step 3: Clearly explain why you believe your account should not be banned, providing evidence and context if possible. Step 4: Create a backup account to stay connected with your followers while your account is under review.

Troubleshooting Login Issues

If you are experiencing login issues, try the following troubleshooting steps:

  • Restart your phone or device.
  • Check your internet connection. Make sure you have a stable Wi-Fi or cellular connection.
  • Clear your TikTok app cache. For iOS: Navigate to "Settings" > "General" > "iPhone Storage" > "TikTok" > "Offload App." For Android: Navigate to "Settings" > "Apps" > "TikTok" > "Storage" > "Clear Cache."
  • Wait a few hours and try again. Sometimes, login issues can be temporary due to server issues.
  • Contact TikTok directly. If you have tried the above steps and are still unable to log in, submit feedback through the following link: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/report/feedback.
