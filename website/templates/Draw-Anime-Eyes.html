Step-by-Step Guide to Mastering the Art of Anime Eye Drawing


Discover the Art of Crafting Enchanting Anime Eyes

Immerse yourself in the vibrant world of anime and learn to create captivating eyes that express a myriad of emotions. This comprehensive guide will unveil the secrets of drawing both male and female anime eyes, empowering you to bring your characters to life with unparalleled depth and expression.

Step into the studio and delve into the intricacies of male eyes. Master the gentle curves of the eyelids, the glistening sheen of the iris, and the piercing gaze of the pupil. Uncover the nuances of conveying sadness, surprise, and anger through the subtle adjustments of your lines.

Prepare to unravel the mysteries of female anime eyes, renowned for their captivating allure. Discover the graceful downward sweep of the upper eyelids, the alluring curve of the lower eyelids, and the mesmerizing glow of the iris. Learn to paint expressive eyes that convey happiness, sadness, and a spectrum of other emotions.


Easy Anime Eyes

Anime eyes are a distinctive feature of the iconic anime art style. They are characterized by their exaggerated features, such as large, expressive irises and shimmering glares. Drawing anime eyes can seem daunting at first, but with a few simple steps, you can easily create beautiful and captivating eyes for your anime characters.

Male Eyes:

  1. Upper Eyelid: Begin by drawing a slightly curved horizontal line for the upper eyelid. The curve should be subtle, just enough to suggest the rounded shape of the eye.

  2. Lower Eyelid: Draw a shorter horizontal line underneath the upper eyelid, curving it slightly upward at the outer edges. This line will form the lower eyelid.

  3. Iris: Sketch a semicircle for the iris, ensuring it touches the lower eyelid. The semicircle should be slightly flattened on the top, giving the iris a curved shape.

  4. Glares: Add two small circles within the iris for the glare, representing the reflection of light. The circles should be placed slightly off-center, towards the upper part of the iris.

  5. Pupil: Draw a small black circle in the center of the iris for the pupil. The pupil should be perfectly round and should contrast sharply with the white of the iris.

  6. Shading: Shade in the iris around the glares and pupil, leaving the glares white. Use a soft pencil to create a subtle gradient from light to dark, adding depth and realism to the iris.

Female Eyes:

  1. Upper Eyelid: Draw a downward curving line for the upper eyelid. The curve should be more pronounced than for the male eyes, giving a more feminine and expressive look.

  2. Lower Eyelid: Draw a horizontal line underneath the upper eyelid, curving it upward at the ends. The lower eyelid should be slightly shorter than the upper eyelid, creating a wider gap between the lids.

  3. Iris: Draw a partial oval for the iris, similar to the male eyes. However, leave the top of the oval open, creating a crescent-like shape.

  4. Glares: Add two circles within the iris for the glare, placing them towards the upper part of the iris. The circles should be slightly larger than in the male eyes, giving the female eyes a brighter and more sparkling appearance.

  5. Pupil: Draw a small black circle in the center of the iris for the pupil. The pupil should be perfectly round and should contrast sharply with the white of the iris.

  6. Shading and Lashes: Shade in the iris around the glares and pupil, leaving the glares white. Add eyelashes along the upper eyelid, extending from the inner to outer corners. The eyelashes should be drawn in curved lines, varying in length and thickness for a natural effect.

Different Eye Expressions:

  1. Sadness: Narrow the eyes by drawing the upper and lower eyelids closer together. Curve the lower eyelids down at the outer corners to convey a sense of sadness and sorrow.

  2. Surprise: Widen the eyes by increasing the gap between the upper and lower eyelids. Make the pupils larger and add pronounced glares to emphasize the shock or surprise.

  3. Anger: Slope the upper eyelids down steeply from the inner corners. Curve the lower eyelids slightly upward to create a glaring expression. Add dark shading under the eyes to enhance the intensity of the anger.

  4. Happiness: Draw the eyes closed with downward curving upper eyelids. Add a slight smile line underneath the eyes to convey a sense of joy and contentment.
