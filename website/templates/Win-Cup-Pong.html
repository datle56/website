How to Play Cup Pong: A Comprehensive Guide

Getting Started

  1. Open the Messages App: Launch the Messages app on your iPhone or iPad.

  2. Start a Chat with Your Opponent: Select the contact you wish to play against and open a chat window with them.

  3. Swipe Left and Tap Game Pigeon: Slide your finger left across the message composition area and tap the Game Pigeon icon. This will reveal a list of available games.

  4. Select Cup Pong and Send Invitation: Choose "Cup Pong" from the game selection and press the send button to invite your opponent. Your opponent must have the Game Pigeon app installed for this to work.

Playing the Game

  1. View Your Opponent's Turn: When it's your opponent's turn, you'll see a video still of their game screen. Tap the still to view their turn.

  2. Drag the Ball for Your Turn: To take your turn, tap and hold the ball on your game screen and drag it towards the center cups.

  3. Aim for the Center Cups: Position the ball directly above the center cups for the highest chance of success.

  4. Adjust Angle for Subsequent Turns: As the game progresses, the cups will start to move. Adjust your aim slightly left or right to hit more cups in subsequent turns.

  5. Continue Playing Until All Cups Are Cleared: Keep taking turns until all cups on one side of the screen have been knocked down. The first player to clear all their cups wins the game.

Installing Cup Pong

  1. Open the Messages App: Ensure that you have the Messages app installed on your device.

  2. Select an iMessage Contact: Start a new message thread with someone who uses iMessage (indicated by blue text bubbles).

  3. Tap the App Store Icon: Locate the App Store icon below the text field where you type messages.

  4. Search for Game Pigeon: Type "Game Pigeon" into the search bar and press enter.

  5. Install the Game: Tap the "Get" button to download and install the Game Pigeon app.

  6. Launch Cup Pong: Once installed, return to the chat thread and tap the Game Pigeon icon again. Select "Cup Pong" from the games list to launch the game.

Tips for Winning

  • Aim Accurately: Practice to improve your aim and ensure you're hitting the center cups consistently.

  • Use a Straight-Edged Object: Place a ruler, chopstick, or similar straight-edged object across your screen to help visualize the ball's trajectory.

  • Start with Center Cups: Focus on clearing the center cups first as they provide the best chance of success.

  • Practice Regularly: The more you play, the better your aim and control will become.

  • Keep Calm and Steady: Don't get discouraged if you don't win immediately. Remain calm and focus on making precise shots.
