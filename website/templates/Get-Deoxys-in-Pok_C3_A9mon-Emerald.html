How to Catch Deoxys in Pokémon Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald: A Comprehensive Guide

Prerequisites:

  • Obtain the Key Item "Eon Ticket" from the Old Man in the house south of Slateport City.
  • Reach Birth Island by boarding the S.S. Tidal in Lilycove City.
  • Activate the Birth Island event by solving the triangle puzzle.

Before the Battle:

1. Team Preparation:

  • Deoxys is a Psychic-type Pokémon, making it immune to Ground-type attacks, resistant to Fighting and Psychic-type attacks, and weak to Dark, Ghost, and Steel-type attacks. Choose your team accordingly, avoiding Pokémon with Fighting and Poison-type weaknesses.
  • Consider bringing a Pokémon with the "False Swipes" move, which reduces an opponent's HP to 1 without fainting it. This will make capturing Deoxys easier.

2. Items:

  • Stock up on Ultra Balls, which have a higher catch rate than other Poké Balls.
  • Carry plenty of healing items such as Potions, Super Potions, and Full Restores to keep your Pokémon healthy.
  • If you have a Master Ball, consider using it for an instant capture.

Capturing Deoxys:

1. Weakening Deoxys:

  • Once you have activated the event and Deoxys appears, use "False Swipes" to lower its health significantly. This will make it easier to catch, as Deoxys will not faint prematurely.

2. Throwing Poké Balls:

  • Once Deoxys's health is sufficiently low, start throwing Ultra Balls at it.
  • Be patient and persistent, as it may take several throws before Deoxys is captured.
  • Avoid using moves that could faint Deoxys, such as strong physical or special attacks.

3. Master Ball Option:

  • If you have a Master Ball, you can use it to instantly capture Deoxys, regardless of its health or status. However, Master Balls are extremely rare, so use them wisely.

Going Home:

1. Leaving Birth Island:

  • After capturing Deoxys, board the S.S. Tidal and return to the mainland.

2. Returning Home (Low-Level Trainers):

  • If you are a low-level trainer and do not have access to Fly, you can use the "Home" cheat code to return to your character's house.
  • To do this, enter the code "00000C29" into the pause menu cheat screen. This code will immediately transport you to your home.

Additional Tips:

  • Deoxys has four Forms (Normal, Attack, Defense, and Speed). Each Form has different stats and weaknesses. Choose your Pokémon and strategy accordingly.
  • Be prepared for a challenging battle, as Deoxys is a powerful Pokémon with high stats.
  • Save your game before entering the battle, in case things go wrong.
  • With patience and preparation, you can successfully capture the elusive Deoxys and add it to your Pokémon team.
