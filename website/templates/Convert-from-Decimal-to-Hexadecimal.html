Converting Denary (Base 10) to Hexadecimal (Base 16)

Hexadecimal is a base-16 number system, meaning it uses 16 symbols to represent numbers: 0-9 and A-F. Converting from denary (base 10) to hexadecimal involves a series of divisions and remainders.

Intuitive Method:

  1. Find the Largest Power of 16 that Divides the Denary Number: Start with the largest power of 16 (256) and determine if it fits into the denary number. If not, move down to the next power of 16 (16, 4, 1).

  2. Divide the Denary Number by the Largest Power: Perform the division to find the first hexadecimal digit. The quotient represents the digit's value.

  3. Find the Remainder: After dividing, determine the remainder. This remainder will be used in subsequent steps.

  4. Repeat Steps 1-3 until the Remainder is Less than 16: Continue dividing the number by the appropriate powers of 16 and finding the remainders until the final remainder is less than 16.

  5. Convert Remainders 10-15 to Hexadecimal Equivalents: When encountering remainders between 10 and 15, convert them to their hexadecimal equivalents: 10 becomes A, 11 becomes B, and so on up to 15, which becomes F.

  6. Assemble the Hexadecimal Number: Write the hexadecimal digits in the reverse order they were obtained, starting with the first division.

Fast Method:

  1. Divide by 16 Repeatedly: Continuously divide the denary number by 16, recording the remainders.

  2. Reverse the Remainders: Take the remainders from the last to the first division and write them down in reverse order.

  3. Convert Remainders 10-15 to Hexadecimal Equivalents: As before, convert any remainders of 10-15 to their hexadecimal equivalents.

Example (Using the Fast Method):

Convert 495 to hexadecimal:

  1. Divide by 16:

    • 495 ÷ 16 = 30 (remainder 15 = F)
    • 30 ÷ 16 = 1 (remainder 14)
    • 1 ÷ 16 = 0 (remainder 1)

  2. Reverse the Remainders: 1, 14 (E), F

  3. Convert Remainders: E, F

  4. Hexadecimal Number: 1EF


Conclusion:

Converting from denary to hexadecimal involves employing powers of 16 and following either the intuitive method or the fast method. The intuitive approach offers a step-by-step guide to understanding the process, while the fast method provides a more efficient way to determine the hexadecimal representation. By mastering these techniques, you gain the ability to seamlessly convert between these two number systems, which is a valuable skill in various fields, including computer science, programming, and mathematics.
