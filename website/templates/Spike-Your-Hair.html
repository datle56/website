Preparing Your Hair for Spiking

1. Wash and Condition:

  • Begin by thoroughly washing your hair with a clarifying shampoo to remove any dirt, oil, or product buildup.
  • Follow with a nourishing conditioner to hydrate and strengthen your strands.
  • Rinse both products completely and towel dry your hair gently.

2. Towel Dry and Detangle:

  • Use a soft, absorbent towel to pat your hair until it's damp but not soaking wet. Excess water can dilute hair products and make it more difficult to style.
  • Gently brush or comb your hair with a wide-tooth comb or brush to remove any tangles or knots. This will help prevent breakage and ensure even distribution of products.

Spiking Your Hair

1. Choose Your Product:

  • Hair Gel: For a wet, shiny, and long-lasting hold.
  • Hair Wax: For a matte, more natural, and flexible hold.
  • Hair Pomade: For a slick, polished, and structured hold.
  • Hair Mousse: For volume, definition, and hold.
  • Consider products that offer heat protection to prevent damage from heat styling tools.

2. Apply the Product:

  • Start with a dime-sized amount of product in your hands.
  • Rub the product between your palms to warm it up and distribute it evenly.
  • Apply the product to the front section of your hair, beginning at the roots.
  • Smooth the product upward from roots to tips, using your fingers or a comb to evenly distribute it.

3. Create Spikes:

  • With your fingers slightly dampened, run them through your hair in an upward motion.
  • Start at the base of the front section and gently pull upwards, shaping the hair into spikes.
  • Adjust the size and direction of your spikes as desired.

4. Check and Adjust:

  • Check your spikes in a mirror regularly to ensure they are even and symmetrical.
  • If necessary, use the tips of your fingers to gently adjust the direction or angle of the spikes.
  • Use more product as needed to improve hold and coverage.

Selecting Your Hair Products

  • Choose hair products that are appropriate for your hair type and desired look.
  • Consider the strength of hold you want, as well as the finish (wet, matte, or slick).
  • Products with natural ingredients or protective properties can be beneficial for your hair's health.
  • Pay attention to the amount of product you use, as too much can weigh your hair down or make it appear greasy.
  • Experiment with different products to find the ones that work best for you.

Conclusion:

With these simple steps and the right hair products, you can achieve stylish and edgy spiked hair that will turn heads wherever you go. Experiment with different products and techniques to create unique and personalized styles that express your personality. Remember to take care of your hair by using gentle products and avoiding excessive heat styling to maintain its health and vitality while rocking the perfect spikes.
