Converting Hexadecimal to Binary

Hexadecimal, a counting system based on 16 symbols (0-9, A-F), can be readily converted into binary, a system based solely on 0s and 1s. This process involves converting each hexadecimal digit into its respective 4-digit binary representation.

Example: Convert the hexadecimal digit "A" to binary. - A in hexadecimal = 1010 in binary

Converting Hexadecimal to Decimal

Decimal, the familiar counting system we use daily, can be obtained from hexadecimal by following these steps:

Step 1: Write the Hexadecimal Number as an Addition Problem - Represent the hexadecimal number as a sum of its individual digits multiplied by their respective powers of 16.

Step 2: Convert Alphabetic Characters to Decimal Equivalents - Replace any hexadecimal digits A-F with their corresponding decimal values (10-15).

Step 3: Multiply Hexadecimal Digits by Powers of 16 - Multiply each hexadecimal digit by its corresponding power of 16 (16^0, 16^1, 16^2, ..., 16^n).

Step 4: Add Up the Results - Sum up the products obtained in Step 3 to arrive at the decimal equivalent.

Example: Convert the hexadecimal number "1AF" to decimal: - 1AF = 1 x 16^2 + A x 16^1 + F x 16^0 - 1 x 256 + 10 x 16 + 15 x 1 - 256 + 160 + 15 = 431

Understanding Hexadecimal Basics

  • Hexadecimal is a base-16 number system that employs 16 symbols: 0-9 (digits) and A-F (alphabetic characters representing values 10-15).
  • In hexadecimal notation, a subscript 16 is appended to the number to signify its base-16 representation (e.g., 1AF16).
  • When working with large hexadecimal numbers, online conversion tools can simplify the decimal conversion process.
