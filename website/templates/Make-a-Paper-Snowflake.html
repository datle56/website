Craft beautiful paper snowflakes with these step-by-step instructions from wikiHow.


Calling all snowflake enthusiasts! Ready to unleash your inner artist and create stunning wintery masterpieces? Join us as we dive into the world of folded paper snowflakes, where the intricate beauty of nature meets the precision of origami. We'll explore two captivating techniques - the Round Snowflake and the Angular Snowflake - that will transform your blank paper into mesmerizing creations.


Round Snowflake

Create a delicate and symmetrical round snowflake with these steps:

  1. Origami Mastery: Begin by expertly folding your square sheet of paper into a crisp cone shape. This is the foundation for your snowflake's intricate design.

  2. The Intriguing Tip: Once the cone is formed, introduce a subtle curve at the pointy tip. This minor incision will become the focal point of your snowflake's symmetrical beauty.

  3. Precision Cuts: Along the edges of the cone, unleash your creativity by making precise and harmonious cuts. These incisions will transform the paper into a captivating snowflake pattern. Guided by your steady hand, create a symphony of delicate cuts.

  4. Unveiling Symmetry: As you gently unfold the cone, a mesmerizing sight emerges. Behold a flawlessly symmetrical six-sided snowflake, boasting intricate designs that radiate from the central curve you skillfully created. Each snowflake is a unique masterpiece, a testament to your patience and precision.

Angular Snowflake

Embrace the angular charm of this distinctive snowflake:

  1. Geometric Precision: Commence by folding your square paper into a perfect square, ensuring sharp angles and crisp edges. This forms the base for your snowflake's geometric exploration.

  2. Triangular Alchemy: Transform the square into a triangle with deft folds. Repeat this process, creating a smaller base if desired. This sequence of folds establishes the foundation for the snowflake's angular design.

  3. Intricate Incisions: Armed with your sharp scissors, embark on a journey of intricate cuts. Curve, angle, and swirl your way through the paper, allowing your creativity to dance freely. Each incision becomes a stroke of artistry, shaping the snowflake's unique character.

  4. Unveiling Angularity: With meticulous care, unfold your creation to unveil a snowflake that embodies geometry's allure. Admire the interplay of acute angles, graceful curves, and captivating swirls. Each snowflake is a testament to your artistic vision and the boundless possibilities of origami.


Conclusively, crafting intricate and captivating snowflakes is a rewarding and versatile art form. Whether you prefer the ethereal elegance of the round snowflake or the geometric precision of the angular snowflake, the possibilities are limitless. Grab your scissors and paper, let your imagination soar, and create a mesmerizing winter wonderland filled with your very own unique and stunning snowflake designs.
