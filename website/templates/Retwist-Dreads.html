Retwisting Dreadlocks: A Comprehensive Guide

Dreadlocks are a unique and iconic hairstyle that requires regular maintenance to keep them healthy and looking their best. Retwisting is an essential part of this maintenance routine, as it helps to tighten the dreads, prevent unraveling, and promote regrowth. Here is a detailed guide on how to retwist dreadlocks using two common methods: finger and palm rolling, and preparation and washing.

Method 1: Finger and Palm Rolling

Step 1: Divide Hair into Small Sections

Gently use a rattail comb or your fingers to separate your dreadlocks into small, manageable sections. This will make the retwisting process easier and more effective.

Step 2: Gather Loose Hair at the Base of a Dreadlock

Identify the section of dreadlock that needs to be retwisted. Gather the loose hair at the base of the dreadlock, ensuring that you include all of the loose strands.

Step 3: Twist Between Your Fingers

Firmly grip the gathered loose hair between your thumb and forefinger. Twist the hair clockwise or counterclockwise, depending on your preference. Continue twisting until the hair is tightly intertwined.

Step 4: Roll Between Your Palms

Once the hair is twisted, place it between your palms. Roll your palms back and forth to tighten and smooth the dreadlock. Apply gentle pressure to ensure that the dread is secure.

Step 5: Repeat for All Dreadlocks

Repeat steps 2-4 for all the dreadlocks that need to be retwisted. Be patient and thorough, taking your time to create tight and even dreads.

Method 2: Preparation and Washing

Step 1: Check for Dreadlocks That Need Retwisting

Examine your dreadlocks to identify those that are loose or have started to unravel. Focus on the new growth areas, as these typically require more frequent retwisting.

Step 2: Gather Supplies

Prepare the following supplies:

  • Rattail comb or fine-toothed comb
  • Hair clips (optional)
  • Towel
  • Dreadlock wax or gel (optional)

Step 3: Position the Head

Position the person's head at a comfortable height for working, either by having them sit in a chair or lie down on a flat surface.

Step 4: Washing

Properly washing your dreadlocks before retwisting is crucial for removing dirt, oil, and product buildup. Use a shampoo specifically formulated for dreadlocks, as it will gently cleanse without stripping your hair of its natural oils.

  • Shampoo: Apply a small amount of shampoo to the dreadlocks and gently massage it in with your fingers. Avoid using harsh circular motions that could cause the dreads to unravel.
  • Conditioner (Optional): If desired, apply a conditioner designed for dreadlocks to soften the hair and make it more manageable.
  • Rinsing: Thoroughly rinse the hair to remove all shampoo and conditioner residue.
  • Drying: Blot excess water with a towel and allow the dreadlocks to dry completely. You can use a hair dryer on a low heat setting to speed up the drying process, but avoid direct heat on the dreads.

Step 5: Retwisting (Using Finger and Palm Rolling Method)

Once the dreadlocks are completely dry, you can begin the retwisting process using the finger and palm rolling method described in the previous section.


Conclusion

Retwisting dreadlocks is an essential part of maintaining their health and aesthetics. By following the techniques outlined above, you can effectively retwist your dreads at home, saving time and money on salon visits. Remember to be patient, gentle, and consistent with your retwisting routine to achieve long-lasting, well-defined dreads that showcase your unique style. If you encounter any difficulties or have specific concerns about your dreadlocks, don't hesitate to consult with a professional loctician for guidance and support.
