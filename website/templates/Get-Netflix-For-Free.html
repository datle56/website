How to Save Money on Netflix

Can You Get Netflix for Free?

Unfortunately, Netflix has discontinued its free trial program. This means that you can no longer sign up for a free trial period to test out the service before committing to a subscription.

Get a Deal Through Your Phone Provider

Certain phone providers, such as T-Mobile, offer exclusive deals that include a complimentary Netflix subscription as a bonus perk with their mobile plans. If you're a customer of one of these providers, be sure to inquire about their Netflix bundles to potentially save money on your streaming expenses.

Share an Account

One of the most popular ways to save money on Netflix is to share an account with a friend or family member. Netflix allows you to create multiple profiles within a single account, so you can each have your own personalized viewing experience. By splitting the cost of the subscription, you can significantly reduce your monthly expenses.

Additional Ways to Save:

Use Netflix Gift Cards:

Instead of paying for your subscription directly through your Netflix account, consider purchasing Netflix gift cards from reputable retailers. These gift cards can be used to fund your subscription, and you may find them on sale or discounted at certain times of the year.

Consider the Basic Subscription with Ads:

Netflix offers a cheaper tier of subscription called Basic with Ads, which costs $6.99 per month. This plan includes a limited number of advertisements during your viewing experience, but it provides access to the same library of movies and TV shows as the more expensive plans.

Netflix does Not Offer Student Discounts:

Unlike some other streaming services, Netflix does not currently offer dedicated student discounts. However, you may be able to save money by sharing an account with a student who qualifies for a discounted Spotify Premium subscription, which includes access to Netflix.


Conclusion:

By exploring these smart strategies, you can enjoy your favorite Netflix shows and movies without breaking the bank. Remember that sharing accounts is not endorsed by Netflix, but it's a common practice to save money. Additionally, if you're looking for a convenient way to pay, consider using Netflix gift cards. With these cost-saving techniques, you can continue your streaming adventures without sacrificing your financial well-being.
