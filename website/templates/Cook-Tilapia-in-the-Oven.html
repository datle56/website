Easy Oven-Baked Tilapia Recipes

Lemon Garlic Tilapia Filets

Ingredients:

  • 4 Tilapia filets (4-6 ounces each)
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon lemon zest

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together the melted butter, garlic, lemon juice, and lemon zest.
  3. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  4. Place the tilapia filets on the prepared baking sheet.
  5. Brush the tilapia filets with the butter mixture.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven for 10-12 minutes, or until the fish is cooked through and flakes easily when tested with a fork.
  7. Serve immediately with your favorite sides.

Foil Pack Tilapia Filets with Vegetables

Ingredients:

  • 4 Tilapia filets (4-6 ounces each)
  • 1 cup fresh asparagus, trimmed and cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano or thyme

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C).
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together the butter, garlic, lemon juice, and oregano.
  3. Cut 4 pieces of aluminum foil large enough to wrap the tilapia and vegetables.
  4. Place a tilapia filet and some asparagus in the center of each piece of foil.
  5. Pour a quarter of the butter mixture over each filet and vegetables.
  6. Wrap the foil tightly around the fish and vegetables, creating a sealed packet.
  7. Place the foil packets on a baking sheet.
  8. Bake for 15-20 minutes, or until the fish is cooked through and the vegetables are tender.
  9. Serve immediately with your favorite sides.

Broiled Tilapia with Lemon Mayonnaise

Ingredients:

  • 4 Tilapia filets (4-6 ounces each)
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 tablespoon lemon zest
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to broil.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, parsley, lemon zest, salt, and pepper.
  3. Line a baking sheet with foil.
  4. Season the tilapia filets with salt and pepper.
  5. Spread the mayonnaise mixture over the tilapia filets.
  6. Broil in the preheated oven for about 8 minutes, or until the fish is cooked through and the mayonnaise mixture is golden brown.
  7. Serve immediately with your favorite sides.

Whole Baked Tilapia

Ingredients:

  • 1 whole tilapia (1-1.5 pounds)
  • Olive oil
  • 2 lemons, sliced
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 1/2 cup fresh herbs (e.g., cilantro, garlic)

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Rinse and pat dry the tilapia.
  3. Brush the fish with olive oil.
  4. Place the sliced lemons and red onions in a baking tray.
  5. Stuff the tilapia cavity with the fresh herbs.
  6. Bake uncovered for 15-20 minutes, or until the fish is cooked through and flakes easily when tested with a fork.
  7. Serve immediately with your favorite sides.
