Multiplayer Snap (Option 1)

Setup:

  • Gather 2-6 players around a table or playing surface.
  • Provide each player with a standard deck of 52 playing cards.

Gameplay:

  • Initial Deal: Shuffle the decks and deal an equal number of cards facedown to each player.
  • Starting the Game: Each player simultaneously flips the top card of their deck and places it facedown in the center of the table to form a new pile.
  • Identifying Matching Cards: Players continue flipping the top cards of their decks and placing them facedown on the central pile. When any two cards of the same rank appear on the pile (e.g., two Aces), the first player to shout "Snap!" grabs both the central pile and the matching pair that initiated the shout.
  • Accumulating Piles: The player who successfully calls "Snap!" keeps the cards they have acquired and places them in a separate pile known as their "snap pile."
  • Winning the Game: The game continues until one player has accumulated all the cards from the central pile and their opponents' snap piles. This player is declared the winner.

Variations:

  • Snap with Jokers: Include jokers in the decks to add an element of unpredictability. Jokers can be used as wild cards, matching any other card for a successful "Snap!" call.
  • Timed Snap: Set a timer and encourage players to call "Snap!" as quickly as possible. The player who grabs the pile with the shortest reaction time wins the round.
  • Snap with Categories: Assign different suits or ranks of cards to specific categories (e.g., hearts = animals, spades = food). When a card from a particular category appears, players must call out the category instead of "Snap!" to win the pile.

Single Pile Snap (Option 2)

Setup:

  • Gather any number of players around a table or playing surface.
  • Provide each player with a standard deck of 52 playing cards.

Gameplay:

  • Initial Deal: Shuffle the decks and deal an equal number of cards facedown to each player.
  • Starting the Game: Each player simultaneously flips the top card of their deck and places it faceup in the center of the table to form a central pile.
  • Matching Cards in Sequence: Players continue flipping cards from their decks and placing them faceup on the central pile. When two consecutive cards match (e.g., a 5 followed by a 6), the first player to shout "Snap!" grabs the central pile.
  • Accumulating Piles: The player who successfully calls "Snap!" keeps the cards they have acquired and places them in a separate pile.
  • Winning the Game: The game continues until one player has accumulated all the cards from the central pile and their opponents' snap piles. This player is declared the winner.

Variations:

  • Double Snap: If two consecutive cards match with a gap between them (e.g., a 5 followed by a space and then a 6), the player who calls "Snap!" wins only half of the central pile.
  • Reversed Snap: Instead of matching cards in ascending order, players try to match cards in descending order (e.g., a 6 followed by a 5).
  • Number Snap: Assign a specific number to each player. When a card with that number appears in the central pile, the player calls "Snap!" and grabs it. The first player to collect all the cards with their assigned number wins the game.
