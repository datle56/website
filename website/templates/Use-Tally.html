Setting Up Tally

  1. Download and Installation:

    • Visit the Tally Solutions website and download the latest version of Tally software compatible with your platform.
    • Follow the onscreen instructions to install the software.

  2. Company Creation:

    • Launch Tally and select "Create Company."
    • Enter your company's details, including company name, address, and currency. You can also customize your company logo and set up multiple companies if required.

  3. Auto Backup:

    • Enable auto backup to protect your data in case of any unexpected events.
    • Choose the backup location and frequency, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.

  4. Keyboard Shortcuts:

    • Master keyboard shortcuts for faster navigation and data entry.
    • Refer to the Tally documentation or use the "Help" menu to discover helpful shortcuts.

Creating Ledgers

  1. Purpose and Classification:

    • Ledgers are used to track transactions for each account in your business.
    • Classify ledgers based on their nature, such as assets, liabilities, equity, income, and expenses.

  2. Specific Ledger Creation:

    • Create specific ledgers for different account types, such as:
      • Cash ledger for cash transactions.
      • Bank ledger for bank account transactions.
      • Accounts receivable ledger for money owed to you by customers.
      • Accounts payable ledger for money owed by you to suppliers.
      • Inventory ledger for tracking stock levels.

  3. Ledger Grouping:

    • Group ledgers based on their function for easier management and reporting.
    • For example, create a "Sales" ledger group for all ledgers related to sales activities.

Creating Vouchers

  1. Transaction Documentation:

    • Vouchers serve as supporting documents for financial transactions. They provide detailed information about the transaction.

  2. Voucher Types:

    • Choose the appropriate voucher type based on the transaction:
      • Contra voucher: Used for non-cash transactions like bank deposits or internal transfers.
      • Payment voucher: Used to record expenses or payments made to suppliers.
      • Receipt voucher: Used to record income or payments received from customers.
      • Sales voucher: Used to record sales of products or services.

  3. Voucher Details:

    • Enter necessary information in the voucher, including:
      • Date of transaction.
      • Parties involved (e.g., supplier, customer).
      • Description of transaction.
      • Amount.
      • Payment mode.
    • Tally automatically calculates subtotals, sales tax, and total amounts based on the entered details.
