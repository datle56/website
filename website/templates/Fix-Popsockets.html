Reattaching a PopSocket: A Hassle-Free Guide for Swapped and Classic Models

Popping your PopSocket out of its base can be a frustrating experience, but fear not! With our comprehensive guide, you'll have your trusty phone grip back in action in no time. Whether you've got a swappable or classic model, we've got you covered with detailed instructions and troubleshooting tips. Grab your pliers and let's get that PopSocket reattached!


Reattaching a PopSocket

PopSockets, the handy phone accessories that provide a secure grip and multiple uses, can occasionally come loose or break. Fortunately, it's often possible to reattach them yourself with a few simple steps.

Swappable Models

  • Remove the Top: Gently pull the top portion of the PopSocket straight up to detach it from the base.
  • Open the Funnel Hole: Using needle-nose pliers, carefully widen the funnel-shaped hole on the broken base.
  • Insert Connector: Take the connector piece that came with the PopSocket and align it with the widened hole. Insert it into the hole until it fits snugly.
  • Attach Top: Place the top portion of the PopSocket over the connector piece and turn it 90 degrees clockwise to secure it.

Classic Models

Cap Replacement:

  • Identify Slots: On the underside of the funnel, you'll find two small slots.
  • Align Tabs: The new cap has two corresponding tabs that fit into these slots. Align the tabs with the slots on the funnel.
  • Snap into Place: Press down firmly on the cap until you hear a satisfying "pop." This indicates that the cap has been securely attached.

Funnel Reassembly:

  • Prepare Sticky Pad: Locate the small, sticky pad on the surface of your phone or phone case where you want to reattach the PopSocket.
  • Place Funnel: Align the funnel with the sticky pad and press down gently.
  • Rotate Funnel: Turn the funnel slowly clockwise until it snaps into place.
  • Install Cap: Fit the tabs of the new cap into the corresponding holes on the funnel. Press down until the cap is securely attached.

Loose Base:

If the sticky base of your PopSocket has become loose, you can try the following steps:

  • Clean the Base: Run warm water over the PopSocket's base and gently rub it to remove any dirt or debris.
  • Remove Debris: Use a soft cloth or cotton swab to gently scrub the sticky base, removing any remaining residue.
  • Dry Thoroughly: Place the PopSocket on a clean towel and let it air dry for approximately 10 minutes.
  • Reattach: Peel off any protective backing from the sticky base and reattach the PopSocket to your phone case. Press down firmly to ensure a secure connection.

By following these detailed instructions, you should be able to successfully reattach your PopSocket and restore its functionality. If you encounter any difficulties or the PopSocket becomes damaged beyond repair, you can contact the manufacturer for assistance or purchase a replacement.


Conclusion

Reattaching a PopSocket is a breeze with these simple steps. Whether you're working with a classic or swappable model, you'll have your phone accessorized and ready to use in no time. If you encounter any resistance, don't force the pieces together. Apply gentle pressure and adjust the angles as necessary. With a little care, you'll have your PopSocket securely reattached to enhance your phone's functionality and style.
