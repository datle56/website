Update Your Current City on Facebook: A Detailed Guide

Introduction:

Keeping your Facebook profile up-to-date is essential for staying connected with friends and family. One important aspect of your profile is your current city, which allows others to know where you're based. Whether you've recently moved or simply want to update your location, this comprehensive guide will provide detailed instructions on how to update your current city on Facebook using both the mobile app and a computer.

Using the Mobile App:

  1. Launch the Facebook App: Begin by opening the Facebook app on your mobile device.

  2. Access Your Profile: Tap on your profile picture located in the bottom right corner of the screen.

  3. Edit Your Profile Information: Tap the pencil icon that appears below your profile picture.

  4. Navigate to Details: Scroll down and locate the "Details" section. Tap on "Edit" next to it.

  5. Update Current City: Find the "Current City" field and tap on the pencil icon next to it.

  6. Search for Your New Location: Enter the name of your new city or town in the search bar.

  7. Select Your City: From the list of suggested locations, tap on the correct one to select it.

  8. Control Visibility: Adjust the privacy settings for your current city by tapping on "Who can see your city" and choosing the appropriate option (e.g., friends, only you).

  9. Save Changes: Tap the "Save" button to confirm your updates.

Using a Computer:

  1. Visit Facebook.com: Open your web browser and go to the Facebook website (www.facebook.com).

  2. Access Your Profile: Click on your name in the top right corner of the Facebook homepage.

  3. Edit Your Profile: From your profile page, click on the "Edit Profile" button located under your cover photo.

  4. Manage Your About Information: Scroll down and click on the "Edit Your About Info" link in the left-hand menu.

  5. Locate Places Lived: Under the "Basic Information" section, click on "Places Lived."

  6. Edit Current City: Next to the "Lives in" field, click on the three dots (...) and select "Edit Current City."

  7. Update Your City: Type the name of your new location in the box provided.

  8. Save Changes: Click on the "Save" button to confirm your updates.

  9. Adjust Privacy Settings: To control who can see your current city, click on the privacy icon next to your city's name and select the appropriate option.

Conclusion:

Updating your current city on Facebook is a simple and quick process that ensures your profile is accurate and current. By following the detailed instructions outlined in this guide, you can effortlessly keep your Facebook information up-to-date and continue to connect with others seamlessly.
