How to Find the Area of a Semicircle

Step 1: Understanding the Semicircle

A semicircle is a shape formed by slicing a circle in half along a diameter. It consists of two sections: an arc and a straight line.

Step 2: Measuring the Radius

The radius of a semicircle is the distance from the center of the circle to the midpoint of the straight line. It is essentially half the length of the circle's diameter. To determine the radius:

  • If the diameter is given: Divide the diameter by 2.
  • If the diameter is not given:
    • Measure the distance from the center of the arc to the midpoint of the straight line.
    • This distance represents the radius.

Step 3: Calculating the Area

The formula for calculating the area of a semicircle is:

Area = (1/2) * (π * Radius²)

where:

  • π (pi) is a mathematical constant approximately equal to 3.14.
  • Radius is the measured radius obtained in Step 2.

Step 4: Plugging in Values

Once you have measured the radius and identified the value of π, substitute them into the formula and solve:

  • Plug the radius value into the "Radius" term.
  • Multiply the radius by itself (square it).
  • Multiply the squared radius by π.
  • Divide the result by 2.

Step 5: Interpreting the Result

The calculated value represents the area of the semicircle in square units. Ensure that you include the appropriate unit in your answer. For example, if the radius was measured in centimeters, the area would be expressed in square centimeters.

Example Calculation:

Suppose you have a semicircle with a radius of 5 centimeters.

  • Area = (1/2) * (3.14 * 5²)
  • Area = (1/2) * (3.14 * 25)
  • Area = 39.25 square centimeters

Therefore, the area of the given semicircle is 39.25 square centimeters.
