Uncover the Secret to Mastering Your Stance: A Step-by-Step Guide to Identifying Your Regular or Goofy Foot Stance


Master the Art of Skateboarding: A Guide to Finding Your Perfect Stance

Embark on a thrilling skateboarding journey with our comprehensive guide to finding your natural stance. Discover the secrets to achieving balance, stability, and effortless maneuvering on the board. From determining your dominant foot to perfecting your stance, this guide will empower you to ride with confidence and style. Let's delve into the essential elements of finding the ideal skateboarding stance, tailored specifically for your body and preferences.


Part 1: Finding Your Natural Stance

1. Regular vs. Goofy:

  • Regular: Your left foot is your lead foot (the one that goes forward when walking, running, or kicking).
  • Goofy: Your right foot is your lead foot.

2. Determine Your Dominant Foot:

  • Standing Test: Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and your arms relaxed at your sides. Notice which foot naturally steps forward or shifts over to support your balance.
  • Running Test: Run a short distance and pay attention to which foot you naturally push off with more force.
  • Sliding Test: Stand on a slippery surface (e.g., ice or tile) and perform a controlled slide. The foot that instinctively goes out to stop the slide is your dominant foot.

3. Test Your Stance:

  • Find a skateboard or snowboard and place it on a flat surface.
  • Mount the board with your suspected dominant foot in the back (lead foot in front).
  • Have someone gently push you from behind to see which foot you instinctively use to balance and steer.

Part 2: Confirming Your Stance

1. Mount the Board:

  • Once you've identified your suspected lead foot, stand on the board with that foot firmly planted in the front of the board.

2. Push Off and Ride:

  • Using your back foot, gently push off and start to glide forward.
  • Observe how you feel and how the board responds.

3. Switch Feet:

  • Once you've completed a short ride with one foot forward, stop and switch feet.
  • Push off with the other foot and ride again.

4. Choose Your Comfortable Stance:

  • Compare how it feels to ride with each foot forward.
  • Choose the stance that feels most natural and comfortable, as that is likely your true stance.

Part 3: Perfecting Your Stance

1. Stable Riding Stance:

  • Practice pushing off and maintaining balance with your chosen lead foot forward.
  • Focus on keeping your weight centered and your body aligned with the board.
  • Bend your knees slightly to absorb shock and improve maneuverability.

2. Smooth Pushing:

  • Develop a smooth and confident push-off technique using your back foot.
  • Use your calf muscles to extend your back leg and generate momentum.
  • Avoid using excessive force or "stomping" on the ground.

3. Avoid "Mongo Foot":

  • Mongo foot refers to pushing off with your lead foot.
  • While it may seem counterintuitive, mongo foot is not an effective or stable technique.
  • Learn to push off with your back foot to improve control and balance.

Conclusion:

Finding your natural stance on a skateboard is an essential first step to mastering this exhilarating sport. By following these simple steps, you can determine your regular or goofy stance, confirm your choice, and gradually perfect your technique. Remember, it takes time and practice to become comfortable and proficient on a skateboard. With patience and dedication, you'll find your natural rhythm and experience the pure joy and adrenaline rush that skateboarding offers. So, strap on your board and embrace the adventure!
