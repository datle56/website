Escape the Clutches of iTunes: A Guide to Uninstall It from Windows and Mac

Are you tired of iTunes hogging your computer's space and slowing down your system? It's time to break free from the digital shackles and reclaim your technological freedom. In this comprehensive guide, we'll reveal the simple steps to remove iTunes from your Windows 10 or Mac computer, empowering you to bid farewell to the software that once held you captive.

Whether you've found a more user-friendly media player or you're simply decluttering your digital life, this guide will provide you with the clear and concise instructions you need. So, let's embark on this liberating journey together and conquer the dreaded iTunes!


Removing iTunes from Windows 10:

  1. Go to "Settings" and click "Apps."
  • Click the Start button and select "Settings" from the menu.
  • In the Settings window, navigate to "Apps" and click on it.
  1. Find iTunes in the list and click "Uninstall."
  • In the list of installed apps, scroll down until you find iTunes.
  • Select iTunes and click on the "Uninstall" button that appears.
  1. Follow the prompts to complete the uninstallation.
  • Windows will display a confirmation message asking if you want to uninstall iTunes. Click "Uninstall" to proceed.
  • The uninstallation process will begin. Follow the on-screen prompts to complete it.
  1. Restart your computer.
  • Once the uninstallation is complete, restart your computer to ensure that the changes take effect.

Removing iTunes from Mac (not recommended):

Caution: Removing iTunes from a Mac is not generally recommended as it can affect the playback of music and entertainment content. Consider using an alternative media player like VLC Media Player instead.

  1. Disable Apple's security feature (SIP) by restarting your Mac and holding down "Control" + "R."
  • Turn off your Mac completely.
  • Press the power button and immediately hold down the "Control" and "R" keys together.
  • Continue holding down the keys until you see the Apple logo or a progress bar appear.
  1. Navigate to Terminal and type "csrutil disable."
  • When the macOS Utilities window appears, select "Utilities" from the menu bar at the top.
  • Choose "Terminal" from the drop-down menu.
  • In the Terminal window, type the following command: csrutil disable
  • Press Enter to execute the command.
  1. Restart your Mac and log in as an administrator.
  • Click the Apple menu and select "Restart."
  • When your Mac restarts, hold down the "Command" and "R" keys to enter Recovery Mode.
  • Select "Utilities" from the menu bar and choose "Terminal."
  • In the Terminal window, type the following command: resetpassword
  • Press Enter and follow the prompts to reset your administrator password.
  • Once you have reset your password, log in to your Mac as an administrator.
  1. Open Terminal and navigate to the Applications folder.
  • In the Terminal window, type the following command: cd /Applications
  • Press Enter to execute the command.
  1. Type "sudo rm -rf iTunes.app/" to delete iTunes.
  • In the Terminal window, type the following command: sudo rm -rf iTunes.app/
  • Press Enter to execute the command.
  • You will be prompted to enter your administrator password. Enter it and press Enter.
  1. Re-enable SIP by restarting your Mac, holding down "Control" + "R," and typing "csrutil enable" in Terminal.
  • Once iTunes is deleted, restart your Mac.
  • Hold down the "Control" and "R" keys to enter Recovery Mode.
  • Select "Utilities" from the menu bar and choose "Terminal."
  • In the Terminal window, type the following command: csrutil enable
  • Press Enter to execute the command.
  • Your Mac will restart and SIP will be re-enabled.

Conclusion:

Uninstalling iTunes can be a straightforward process on Windows 10, requiring only a few clicks. However, it's crucial to consider the potential consequences on a Mac. While it's possible to remove iTunes by disabling SIP, this is not recommended as it can disrupt music and entertainment playback. For Mac users, it's advisable to explore alternative media players like VLC Media Player to minimize compatibility issues and ensure a seamless experience.
