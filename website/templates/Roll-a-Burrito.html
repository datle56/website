Master the Art of Burrito Rolling: Two Foolproof Methods

Prepare yourself for burrito bliss! Embrace the culinary challenge of rolling the perfect burrito with our expert guide. Whether you're a seasoned burrito connoisseur or a novice ready to embark on this culinary adventure, we'll walk you through two foolproof methods that will transform your tortillas into mouthwatering marvels.

From warming the tortillas to tucking and rolling the fillings just right, we'll cover every step in meticulous detail. Whether you prefer the classic large burrito or a petite version for quick and easy snacking, our instructions will empower you to create burritos that will tantalize your taste buds with fillings that dance harmoniously within their tortilla embrace.

So, grab your favorite ingredients, a dash of patience, and let's dive headfirst into the art of crafting the perfect burrito!


Method 1: Rolling a Large Burrito

Step 1: Prepare the Tortilla

  • Place the tortilla in a microwave-safe container and microwave for 15-20 seconds, or until warm and pliable.
  • Alternatively, wrap the tortilla in a damp paper towel and microwave for 10-15 seconds.
  • If using a skillet or griddle, heat it over medium heat and place the tortilla on it for 30-60 seconds per side, or until warm and flexible.

Step 2: Assemble the Fillings

  • Spread a layer of your desired fillings down the center of the tortilla, leaving about 2 inches of empty space at the top and bottom.
  • Common fillings include rice, beans, meat, vegetables, cheese, salsa, and guacamole.
  • Layer the fillings in a way that will create a cylindrical shape when rolled.

Step 3: Fold the Bottom and Front Flaps

  • Fold the bottom flap of the tortilla over the fillings.
  • Fold the front flap of the tortilla down over the bottom flap, overlapping it by about 1 inch.

Step 4: Fold the Left and Right Flaps

  • Fold the left flap of the tortilla over the fillings from left to right.
  • Fold the right flap of the tortilla over the fillings from right to left, but do not overlap the left flap yet.

Step 5: Tuck the Top Flap

  • Fold the top flap of the tortilla down over the fillings.
  • Tuck this flap underneath the rolled-up fillings, ensuring it is well sealed.

Step 6: Roll the Burrito

  • Hold the tortilla firmly and start rolling it forward, away from you.
  • Apply gentle pressure as you roll to keep the burrito compact and cylindrical.
  • Continue rolling until the burrito is completely sealed.

Step 7: Secure the Burrito

  • Wrap the burrito tightly in aluminum foil.
  • Fold the edges of the foil up over the ends of the burrito to keep it warm and prevent the fillings from leaking out.

Method 2: Rolling a Small Burrito

Step 1: Prepare the Tortilla

  • Follow the same steps as in Method 1 to warm and soften the tortilla.

Step 2: Assemble the Fillings

  • Place a small amount of your desired fillings in the center of the tortilla, creating a small, cylindrical heap.

Step 3: Fold the Sides and Bottom

  • Fold both the left and right sides of the tortilla towards the center, overlapping them slightly.
  • Fold the bottom flap of the tortilla up and over the fillings, overlapping the sides.

Step 4: Roll the Burrito

  • Continue rolling the tortilla tightly into a cylindrical shape, starting from the bottom.
  • Apply gentle pressure to keep the burrito compact.

Step 5: Secure the Burrito

  • Wrap the burrito tightly in aluminum foil.
  • Fold the edges of the foil up over the ends of the burrito to keep it warm and prevent the fillings from spilling out.
