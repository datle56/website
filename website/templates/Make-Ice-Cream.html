How to Make Homemade Ice Cream

Custard Base Ice Cream

Ingredients:

  • 4 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 5 large egg yolks
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

Steps:

  1. Simmer the Milk: In a medium saucepan, heat the milk over medium heat until it just starts to simmer.

  2. Combine Egg Yolks, Sugar, and Salt: In a large bowl, whisk together the egg yolks, sugar, and salt until well combined.

  3. Temper the Egg Mixture: Gradually whisk about 1 cup of the hot milk into the egg mixture until well incorporated.

  4. Return to Heat: Pour the tempered egg mixture back into the saucepan with the remaining milk.

  5. Cook the Custard: Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens and reaches 170°F (77°C) on a candy thermometer.

  6. Strain the Custard: Pour the custard through a fine-mesh strainer into a clean bowl to remove any lumps.

  7. Add Vanilla: Stir in the vanilla extract.

  8. Chill the Mixture: Cover the bowl and refrigerate the custard base for at least 30 minutes or up to overnight.

Churning the Ice Cream

With an Electric Ice Cream Maker:

  1. Freeze the Ice Cream Maker Bowl: Place the ice cream maker bowl in the freezer overnight or for at least 12 hours.

  2. Set Up the Machine: Assemble the ice cream maker according to the manufacturer's instructions.

  3. Pour in the Chilled Custard: Pour the chilled custard base into the frozen ice cream maker bowl.

  4. Churn the Ice Cream: Turn on the machine and churn the ice cream according to the manufacturer's instructions. This typically takes around 20-30 minutes.

  5. Transfer to Freezer-Safe Container: Once the ice cream is churned, transfer it to a freezer-safe container, cover, and freeze for at least 4 hours before serving.

By Hand:

  1. Freeze a Freezer-Safe Container: Place a freezer-safe container in the freezer for at least 30 minutes.

  2. Pour in the Chilled Custard: Pour the chilled custard base into the frozen container.

  3. Freeze for 30 Minutes: Place the container in the freezer and freeze for 30 minutes.

  4. Beat the Ice Cream: Remove the container from the freezer and beat the ice cream with a hand mixer until it lightens in color and texture.

  5. Repeat Freezing and Beating: Repeat steps 3 and 4 several times, freezing for 30 minutes and beating until the ice cream is firm.

No-Churn Ice Cream

Ingredients:

  • 1 (14-ounce) can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 2 cups heavy cream

Steps:

  1. Chill a Freezer-Safe Pan: Place a freezer-safe pan in the freezer for at least 30 minutes.

  2. Combine Ingredients: In a large bowl, combine the sweetened condensed milk, vanilla extract, and heavy cream.

  3. Whip the Heavy Cream: Using a hand mixer or stand mixer, beat the heavy cream on high speed until stiff peaks form.

  4. Fold in Whipped Cream: Gently fold the whipped cream into the sweetened condensed milk mixture until combined.

  5. Pour into Chilled Pan: Pour the mixture into the chilled freezer-safe pan and cover.

  6. Freeze for 2 Hours: Place the pan in the freezer and freeze for at least 2 hours.

  7. Add Mix-Ins (Optional): If desired, stir in any desired mix-ins, such as fruit, nuts, or chocolate chips.

  8. Freeze for an Additional 3 Hours: Freeze the ice cream for another 3 hours or until it is firm before serving.
