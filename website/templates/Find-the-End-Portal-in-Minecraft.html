How to Find the End Portal in Minecraft: A Comprehensive Guide

Materials:

  • Blaze rods: Obtained from killing blazes.
  • Ender pearls: Obtained from killing endermen.
  • Flint and steel: Used to craft fire.
  • Obsidian: Obtained by pouring water over lava.
  • Eyes of Ender: Crafted using blaze powders and ender pearls.

Steps:

1. Craft Eyes of Ender:

  • Open the crafting grid.
  • Place a blaze rod in the center slot.
  • This will craft blaze powder.
  • Remove the blaze powder and place it in the crafting grid again.
  • Add an ender pearl to the slot below the blaze powder.
  • This will create an Eye of Ender.

2. Activate Eyes of Ender:

  • Equip an Eye of Ender.
  • Hold down the "Use" button (right-click by default).
  • The Eye of Ender will float up into the air and move horizontally.

3. Follow the Eyes:

  • Keep following the path of the Eye of Ender.
  • It will lead you towards the nearest stronghold.
  • If the Eye of Ender starts to descend, it means the stronghold is underground.

4. Find the Underground Stronghold:

  • Keep using Eyes of Ender until you find one that travels downward.
  • This indicates the location of an underground stronghold.
  • Dig out the ground to reveal the stronghold.

5. Locate the Portal Room:

  • The stronghold is a labyrinthine structure. Navigate through the rooms until you find a staircase leading to a platform over a lava pool.
  • The end portal is located on top of this platform, surrounded by 12 green squares.

6. Activate the Portal:

  • Place an Eye of Ender in each of the green squares around the portal.
  • Once all the squares are filled, the portal will activate, turning purple.

7. Enter the End:

  • Jump into the portal to enter the End dimension.
  • You will be transported to an eerie and barren wasteland, where you must face the formidable Ender Dragon.

Tips:

  • It can take several Eyes of Ender to find the stronghold. Don't be discouraged if the first few eyes lead you astray.
  • Be prepared for a fierce battle with the Ender Dragon. Bring your best weapons and armor.
  • If you die in the End, you will lose all your items. Consider storing valuable possessions in a chest before entering the portal.
  • The End Portal is a one-time structure. Once you jump into it, it will be destroyed. However, you can return to the End by activating the End Crystal, which requires defeating the Ender Dragon first.
