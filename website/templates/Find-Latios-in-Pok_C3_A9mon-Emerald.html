How to Get Latios: A Comprehensive Guide to Capturing the Eon Pokémon

Latios, the elusive Eon Pokémon, is a formidable addition to any trainer's arsenal. Its immense speed, potent psychic attacks, and graceful design make it a highly sought-after Pokémon. Acquiring this legendary Pokémon requires patience, strategy, and a bit of luck. Here is a detailed guide to help you get your hands on Latios:

1. Conquer the Elite Four:

Embark on an epic journey to defeat the Elite Four, the final challenge of the Pokémon League. Once you have triumphed over these formidable trainers, you will unlock the path to encountering Latios.

2. A Mother's Request:

Return home to your mother, who will engage you in conversation. During the dialogue, she will mention a news report discussing a mysterious blue Pokémon. This is the first indication of Latios' presence in the Hoenn region.

3. The Color of Destiny:

In response to your mother's question about the Pokémon's color, select "Blue." This decisive choice sets the stage for Latios' appearance in the game.

4. Preparation is Key:

As you prepare for your encounter with Latios, meticulous planning is crucial. Consider the following strategies to ensure a successful capture:

  • Master Ball Readiness: If you have previously acquired a Master Ball, keep it in reserve. This legendary item guarantees the capture of any Pokémon, making it an invaluable tool for this endeavor.

  • Speed and Control: Train a Pokémon with high speed and the move "Mean Look." This move prevents the target Pokémon from fleeing, significantly increasing your chances of capturing Latios.

  • Shadow Tag Support: Alternatively, you can utilize a Wobbuffet with the "Shadow Tag" ability. This ability also prevents the target Pokémon from escaping, providing you with an extra layer of control.

  • Party Strength: Fill your party with strong Pokémon to aid you in weakening Latios. Stock up on Ultra Balls as well, as they have a higher capture rate than ordinary Poké Balls.

5. Locating the Elusive Latios:

To find Latios, employ the following techniques:

  • Super Repel Trick: Equip a Pokémon with the move "Super Repel." This move deterrents wild Pokémon encounters, making it easier to locate Latios.

  • Safari Zone Exploration: Fly to the Safari Zone and roam the grassy areas. Latios may appear in any patch of grass within the zone.

  • Location Reshuffle: If you are unable to find Latios, enter and exit the Safari Zone. This action reshuffles the locations of all Pokémon, potentially bringing Latios closer to your encounter.

6. The Moment of Truth: Capturing Latios:

When you finally encounter Latios, engage it with the following tactics:

  • Trapping Maneuver: Use "Mean Look" or "Shadow Tag" to trap Latios, preventing it from escaping.

  • Health Reduction: Utilize "False Swipe" or other moves to weaken Latios' HP without knocking it out.

  • Ultra Ball Barrage: Throw Ultra Balls relentlessly at Latios until it is successfully captured.

7. Latias Release Scenario:

If you have previously released Latias, the counterpart Eon Pokémon, there are alternative methods to obtain Latios:

  • Trades: Trade with a friend or online player who possesses Latios.

  • Gameshark Codes: Utilize Gameshark codes to unlock the Eon Ticket, an item that allows you to encounter Latios or Latias at their respective islands.

With patience, perseverance, and a touch of luck, you will be able to add Latios to your Pokémon team. Its immense power and majestic presence will undoubtedly enhance your journey through the Hoenn region.


Conclusion:

Congratulations, you have now successfully captured Latios! This legendary dragon-type Pokémon is a powerful addition to any team and will make you a formidable opponent.
