Seek out a Coca-Cola vending machine adorned with an LED display that scrolls an enigmatic text. This digital billboard holds the key to the secret menu's entrance.

Step 2: The Gateway to Knowledge

Ascend to the vending machine's control panel, where an array of buttons awaits your command. Determine the method for entering numbers using these buttons. Consult the machine's instructions or observe the typical sequence for product selection.

Step 3: Unlocking the Portal

With the numerical gateway unlocked, enter the hallowed code: 4 3 2 1. Each digit represents a pivotal step towards unveiling the machine's secrets.

Step 4: Confirmation and Revelation

Summon your resolve and press the final button (typically 4), serving as your affirmation of the code's veracity. Await the machine's response, for it now holds the power to reveal its inner workings.

Options Unveiled: A Cornucopia of Information

Upon entering the secret menu, you will be greeted with a tapestry of options, each offering a tantalizing glimpse into the machine's enigmatic soul:

  • SALE: Unveils the lifetime sales statistics, whether measured in units dispensed or the sweet nectar of dollars earned.

  • VER/CASH: Unveils the vending machine's software version, a testament to its technological prowess. It may also reveal the cash balance, a glimpse into its financial well-being.

  • EROR: Provides a candid account of errors encountered by the machine, shedding light on its struggles and tribulations.

  • RTN: Offers a graceful exit from the secret menu, returning you to the familiar realm of beverage selection.

  • Temp: (Available on select models) Reveals the machine's internal temperature, a critical parameter for preserving the delicate balance of carbonated bliss.
