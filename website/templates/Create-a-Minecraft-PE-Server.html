Discover the Step-by-Step Guide to Establishing Your Own Minecraft Bedrock Server, Illustrated with Clear Images


Explore the Limitless Realms of Minecraft PE Multiplayer!

Prepare to embark on an unforgettable multiplayer adventure in Minecraft Pocket Edition! Whether you're seeking a thrilling journey with friends or an immersive world to call your own, we've got you covered. In this comprehensive guide, we'll delve into two foolproof methods to set up multiplayer servers: using Realms and Aternos. So, grab your virtual tools and let's unlock the secrets to creating an unforgettable multiplayer experience in the blocky world of Minecraft PE!


Method 1: Using Realms

Step 1: Establish Connectivity and Launch Minecraft

First, ensure that your device has a stable Wi-Fi connection. Then, launch Minecraft on your chosen platform.

Step 2: Sign in to Xbox Live Account

If you haven't already done so, sign in to your Xbox Live account. This is necessary to access the Realms feature.

Step 3: Create a New World and Select "New Realm" Tab

Click on the "Create New World" option. Once the world creation interface appears, navigate to the "Multiplayer" tab and select "New Realm."

Step 4: Name Your Realm and Choose Hosting Level

Provide a unique and memorable name for your Realm. Next, choose the hosting level that meets your requirements. The "10 Players" level allows for up to ten concurrent players, while the "2 Players" level is designed for smaller groups.

Step 5: Create and Subscribe to Host Your Realm

Finalize the creation of your Realm by clicking on the "Create Realm" button. You will then be prompted to subscribe to a hosting plan. Select the plan that aligns with your budget and usage needs. Once you have subscribed, your Realm will begin hosting and become accessible to you and your invited players.

Method 2: Using Aternos

Step 1: Create an Aternos Account

Visit the Aternos website and click on the "Create Account" button. Provide a username, email address, and password to complete the account creation process.

Step 2: Create a Server and Choose a Username

Click on the "Create Server" button and select a username that will be associated with your Minecraft server.

Step 3: Agree to Terms and Conditions

Carefully read and agree to the terms and conditions by clicking the "I agree" checkbox. Then, click on the "Next" button.

Step 4: Provide Email and Password

Enter your email address and create a password for your Aternos account. This information will be used to manage and access your server.

Step 5: Install PocketMine for Server

Aternos will guide you through the process of installing PocketMine, which is the software required to run your Minecraft server. Follow the instructions provided by Aternos carefully.

Step 6: Start Server and Note Address and Port

Once PocketMine is installed, start the server. Aternos will provide you with the server address and port number, which you will need to connect to your server from Minecraft PE.

Step 7: Open Minecraft PE and Add Server

Launch Minecraft PE on your device. Navigate to the "Servers" tab and click on the "Add Server" button. Enter the server address and port number you obtained from Aternos. Your Minecraft PE client will now connect to your server, allowing you to enjoy multiplayer gameplay.


Conclusion:

Whether you prefer the convenience of realms or the customization options offered by Aternos, both methods provide reliable and accessible ways to enjoy multiplayer Minecraft on your mobile device. With just a few simple steps, you can join forces with friends or explore the world alone, making your Minecraft experience truly immersive and unforgettable. So embrace the possibilities and unlock the boundless adventures that await you in the vast Minecraft universe!
