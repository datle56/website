Exercising for a Fuller Butt:

  • Bridges:
    • Lie on your back with your knees bent and feet flat on the floor.
    • Lift your hips into a bridge pose, squeezing your glutes at the top.
    • Slowly lower your hips back down to the starting position.
    • Aim for 10-15 repetitions per set, completing 3-4 sets.
  • Squats:
    • Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and toes slightly turned out.
    • Lower your body by bending your knees and hips, as if sitting back into a chair.
    • Keep your chest up and your knees in line with your toes.
    • Rise back to the starting position, engaging your glutes and thighs.
    • Aim for 12-15 repetitions per set, completing 3-4 sets.
  • Lunges:
    • Stand with your feet hip-width apart.
    • Step forward with one leg and lower your body until your front thigh is parallel to the floor.
    • Push back to the starting position and repeat on the other side.
    • Aim for 10-15 repetitions per leg per set, completing 3-4 sets.
  • Core Exercises:
    • Planks: Hold a plank position with your forearms on the ground and your body in a straight line from head to heels.
    • Supermans: Lie on your stomach and simultaneously lift your arms and legs off the ground, engaging your back and glutes.
    • Bird Dogs: Start on your hands and knees and extend your opposite arm and leg simultaneously, keeping your core engaged.
    • Aim for 30-60 seconds of hold time for planks and 10-15 repetitions per side for Supermans and Bird Dogs.
  • Strength Training:
    • Perform exercises such as barbell hip thrusts, Romanian deadlifts, or glute bridges with weights to build muscle mass in your glutes.
    • Aim for 8-12 repetitions per set, completing 3-4 sets with a challenging weight.
