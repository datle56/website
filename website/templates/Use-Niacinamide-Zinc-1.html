Incorporating Niacinamide 10% Zinc 1% into Your Skincare Routine

Step 1: Cleanse and Dry Your Face

Begin by thoroughly cleansing your face to remove any dirt, oil, or makeup residue. Use a gentle cleanser suitable for your skin type and pat your skin dry with a clean towel.

Step 2: Apply Niacinamide 10% Zinc 1%

Dispense 2 drops of the Niacinamide 10% Zinc 1% serum onto your palm. Use your fingers to gently rub the serum onto your entire face, paying special attention to areas of concern such as redness or acne scars. Massage the serum into your skin until it is fully absorbed.

Step 3: Follow with Oil-Based Serums

If desired, you can layer additional oil-based serums over the niacinamide serum. Oil-based serums can provide extra hydration and nourishment to the skin. Choose serums that complement the benefits of niacinamide, such as those containing hyaluronic acid, ceramides, or retinoids.

Step 4: Finish with Heavy Creams or Moisturizers

To seal in the benefits of the niacinamide and oil-based serums, apply a heavy cream or moisturizer. This will help to lock in moisture and protect your skin from environmental stressors. Choose a moisturizer that suits your skin type and addresses your specific skincare concerns.

When to Use Niacinamide 10% Zinc 1%

  • Twice Daily: Use the serum twice daily, in the morning and evening, for optimal results.
  • All Skin Types: Niacinamide 10% Zinc 1% is suitable for all skin types, including sensitive and acne-prone skin.
  • Red, Oily Skin: The anti-inflammatory properties of niacinamide make it particularly beneficial for red, oily skin. It can help to reduce redness, control oil production, and minimize the appearance of enlarged pores.
  • Acne Scars: Niacinamide has been shown to improve the appearance of acne scars by promoting collagen production and reducing hyperpigmentation.
  • Uneven Skin Tone: Niacinamide can also help to even out skin tone and reduce the appearance of dark spots by inhibiting melanin synthesis.

Cautions

  • Avoid with Vitamin C: Niacinamide should not be used in conjunction with vitamin C products, as this can lead to the formation of irritating compounds.
  • Discontinue Use: If you experience any irritation or discomfort while using niacinamide, discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional.

By following the simple steps outlined above, you can unlock the transformative benefits of these essential ingredients.
