Flying the Hydra

The Hydra, a versatile fighter jet known for its impressive firepower and agility, is a formidable aerial asset in Grand Theft Auto: Vice City. Mastering the controls of this aircraft is essential for dominating the skies and unleashing its destructive potential.

Instructions for Consoles (PlayStation 2 and Xbox)

  1. Locate the Hydra: The Hydra can be found at various locations throughout Vice City, including the rooftops of police stations and military bases.

  2. Enter the Hydra: Approach the Hydra and press the Triangle (PS2) or Y (Xbox) button to enter the cockpit.

  3. Control Flight: Once inside, hold down the R2 (PS2) or RT (Xbox) button to accelerate. Use the left analog stick to control the aircraft's pitch, roll, and yaw.

  4. Landing: To land the Hydra, release the R2 or RT button, pull down on the left analog stick, and press the Square (PS2) or X (Xbox) button to activate the landing gear.

  5. Shooting: Press the Circle (PS2) or A (Xbox) button to fire the Hydra's machine guns. Hold down the button for sustained fire.

Instructions for Computers

  1. Locate the Hydra: The Hydra can be found at the same locations as on consoles.

  2. Enter the Hydra: Approach the Hydra and press the Enter key or F to enter the cockpit.

  3. Control Flight: Use the numeric keypad to control flight. Here's a breakdown of the keys:

  • 8: Accelerate
  • 2: Deccelerate
  • 4: Roll left
  • 6: Roll right
  • 5: Pitch down
  • 0: Pitch up
  • 9: Yaw left
  • 3: Yaw right
  • Insert: Hover mode

  1. Landing: To land the Hydra, release the 8 key, hold down the 5 key, and press the L key to activate the landing gear.

  2. Shooting: Press the Spacebar to fire the Hydra's machine guns. Hold down the Ctrl key for sustained fire.

Cheat Codes
