How to Find Surface Area of a Rectangular Prism

Introduction

A rectangular prism is a three-dimensional shape with six rectangular faces. Finding the surface area of a rectangular prism is crucial in various fields, including geometry, engineering, and architecture. Here's a detailed explanation of the steps involved in calculating the surface area of a rectangular prism:

Step 1: Labeling the Prism

Before you can calculate the surface area, it's essential to label the prism's dimensions:

  • Length (l): The distance along the longest side of the prism parallel to the base.
  • Width (w): The distance along the shorter side of the prism parallel to the base.
  • Height (h): The distance from the base to the top of the prism, perpendicular to the base.

Step 2: Finding the Area of Each Face

The surface area of a rectangular prism comprises the areas of all six faces. Here's how to calculate the area of each face:

  • Bottom and top faces: The area of the bottom and top faces is equal to the length (l) multiplied by the width (w). Area = l x w.
  • Front and back faces: The area of the front and back faces is equal to the width (w) multiplied by the height (h). Area = w x h.
  • Left and right faces: The area of the left and right faces is equal to the length (l) multiplied by the height (h). Area = l x h.

Step 3: Adding the Areas Together

Once you have calculated the area of each face, add them together to obtain the total surface area of the rectangular prism:

Total surface area = 2lw + 2wh + 2lh

Shorter Formula

Using algebraic simplification, the formula for the surface area of a rectangular prism can be shortened to:

Total surface area = 2(lw + wh + lh)

Example

Let's consider a rectangular prism with the following dimensions: length (l) = 4 inches, width (w) = 3 inches, and height (h) = 5 inches.

  • Bottom and top faces area: 4 x 3 = 12 square inches
  • Front and back faces area: 3 x 5 = 15 square inches
  • Left and right faces area: 4 x 5 = 20 square inches

Total surface area = 2(12 + 15 + 20) = 2(47) = 94 square inches

Therefore, the surface area of the rectangular prism is 94 square inches.

Applications of Surface Area

The surface area of a rectangular prism has various applications in real-world scenarios:

  • Painting: Determining the amount of paint required to cover the surfaces of a room or object.
  • Construction: Calculating the total surface area of walls, roofs, and other structural components.
  • Packaging: Designing boxes and containers with the appropriate surface area to accommodate specific objects.
  • Heating and cooling: Estimating the surface area of windows, doors, and walls to determine the energy efficiency of a building.
