Part 1: Connecting Your PSP to a Wi-Fi Network

  1. Activate the Wi-Fi Switch on Your PSP:

    • Slide the Wi-Fi switch located on the side of your PSP (usually found near the power switch) to the "On" position.

  2. Update Your PSP Firmware:

    • Navigate to the "Settings" menu on your PSP and select "System Settings."
    • Choose "System Update" and follow the on-screen prompts to download and install the latest firmware update. This step ensures that your PSP is compatible with the latest Wi-Fi protocols.

  3. Access the Network Settings Menu:

    • Return to the "Settings" menu and select "Network Settings." This will open a submenu for configuring the network connection.

  4. Configure Infrastructure Mode:

    • Choose "Infrastructure Mode" from the menu options. Infrastructure mode allows your PSP to connect to an existing wireless access point (router).

  5. Create a New Connection:

    • Select "New Connection" from the menu options. This will initiate a scan for available Wi-Fi networks in your vicinity.

  6. Select and Connect to Your Network:

    • Wait for the PSP to scan and display a list of available networks.
    • Choose the name (SSID) of the network you want to connect to.

  7. Enter the Network Password:

    • If the network you selected is password-protected, a prompt will appear asking for the password.
    • Enter the correct password for the network.

  8. Save Your Settings:

    • Once you have entered the password, select "Save" to complete the connection process.

  9. Test Your Connection:

    • After saving the settings, you can test the connection by selecting "Connection Test" from the Network Settings menu. This will verify that your PSP can access the internet and other online resources through the newly established connection.

Part 2: Troubleshooting Connection Issues

If you encounter any problems connecting your PSP to a Wi-Fi network, consider the following troubleshooting tips:

  1. Verify Router Status:

    • Ensure that your router is powered on and broadcasting a wireless signal. Check the router's lights or indicators to confirm this.

  2. Configure Router Settings:

    • Access your router's settings (usually through a web browser) and verify that the security type is set to "WPA-PSK [TKIP] + WPA2-PSK [AES]." This security setting is compatible with PSP devices.

  3. Disable MAC Address Filtering:

    • MAC address filtering restricts access to your network based on the MAC address of connected devices. Disable this feature or add your PSP's MAC address to the list of allowed devices.

  4. Reset Your Router:

    • If the above troubleshooting steps do not resolve the issue, consider resetting your router to its factory defaults. Refer to your router's user manual for specific instructions on how to perform a reset.
