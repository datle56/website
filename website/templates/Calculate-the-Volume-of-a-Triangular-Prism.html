Calculating the Surface Area and Volume of a Triangular Prism

Surface Area (Triangular Base)

To calculate the surface area of the triangular base of a triangular prism, you will need to measure its width (denoted as "b") and height (denoted as "h"). Once you have these measurements, apply the following formula:

Area of Triangle = (1/2) x b x h

Volume of the Prism

The volume of a triangular prism is calculated using the surface area of the triangular base and the prism's height (denoted as "l"). Here's the formula:

Volume of Triangular Prism = Base Area x Prism Height = (1/2) x b x h x l

Step-by-Step Instructions:

1. Measure the Triangle's Base and Height:

Using a ruler or measuring tape, determine the width (b) and height (h) of the triangular base. Record these measurements.

2. Calculate the Base Area:

Plug the base width and height into the formula for the area of a triangle:

Area of Triangle = (1/2) x b x h

Evaluate this expression to obtain the surface area of the triangular base.

3. Measure the Prism's Height:

Locate and measure the height of the triangular prism perpendicular to the base. Let's denote this height as "l".

4. Calculate the Prism's Volume:

Now, substitute the base area and prism height into the formula for the volume of a triangular prism:

Volume of Triangular Prism = Base Area x Prism Height = (1/2) x b x h x l

Multiply the base area by the prism's height to determine the volume of the triangular prism.

Example:

Let's say you have a triangular prism with a base width of 5 cm, a base height of 3 cm, and a prism height of 6 cm.

Step 1: Calculate the Base Area

Area of Triangle = (1/2) x 5 cm x 3 cm = 7.5 cm²

Step 2: Calculate the Volume

Volume of Triangular Prism = 7.5 cm² x 6 cm = 45 cm³

Therefore, the surface area of the triangular base is 7.5 cm² and the volume of the triangular prism is 45 cm³.
