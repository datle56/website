DIY Florida Water:

Crafting your own Florida water is a simple and rewarding endeavor. Here's a basic recipe:

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup vodka
  • 1/2 cup distilled water
  • 1/4 cup dried herbs (such as rosemary, sage, and lavender)
  • 10 drops bergamot essential oil
  • 5 drops cinnamon essential oil
  • 3 drops lemon essential oil

Instructions:

  1. Combine the vodka and distilled water in a glass jar.
  2. Add the dried herbs, essential oils, and any other desired ingredients.
  3. Seal the jar and shake vigorously.
  4. Store the jar in a cool, dark place for 40 days, shaking it occasionally.
  5. After 40 days, strain the liquid into a dark bottle using a cheesecloth or fine-mesh sieve.
