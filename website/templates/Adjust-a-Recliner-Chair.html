Recline with Ease: A Step-by-Step Guide to Adjusting Recliner Tension and Pitch

Indulge in the ultimate relaxation experience with a perfectly tuned recliner. Whether your recliner is proving too stiff or its seat is slouching, we've got you covered with comprehensive solutions. Say goodbye to discomfort and embark on a journey towards effortless reclining bliss!


Method 1: Adjusting Recliner Tension

Assessing the Tension:

Begin by reclining in the chair and assessing the ease of movement. An overly firm recline indicates high tension, while a loose recline suggests low tension.

Locating the Tension Adjustment Mechanisms:

Inspect the underside of the seat for thumb wheels or wing nuts. These mechanisms are typically located in pairs, one on each side of the seat.

Adjusting the Tension:

Rotate the adjustment mechanisms counterclockwise to decrease tension and clockwise to increase it. Always make small, incremental adjustments and test the tension after each turn. Avoid overtightening the mechanisms, as this can damage the recliner.

Method 2: Replacing Tension Springs

Identifying Damaged Springs:

Flip the recliner upside down and locate the springs that are attached to the frame. Inspect the springs for signs of damage, such as rust, stretching, or breakage.

Removing Damaged Springs:

Use a pair of pliers to carefully remove the damaged springs from their attachment points. Grip the spring firmly and pull it straight out, taking care not to damage the frame.

Installing New Springs:

Replace the damaged springs with new springs of the same size and strength. Before installing, slightly stretch the springs to reduce the tension. This will make it easier to attach them to the frame.

Method 3: Changing Recliner Pitch

Evaluating the Pitch:

Sit upright in the recliner and assess its pitch, or the angle at which the backrest reclines. An upright pitch means the backrest is close to vertical, while a laid-back pitch means it reclines at a greater angle.

Locating the Cam Bolts:

Flip the recliner upside down and identify the cam bolts that secure the frame to the base. These bolts are typically located at the front and back of the frame.

Adjusting the Frame:

Loosen the back bolts just enough to allow for adjustment of the frame. Do not loosen the bolts too much, as this can compromise the recliner's stability.

Changing the Pitch:

Move the frame up to make the pitch more upright or down to make it more laid-back. Ensure that the adjustment is even on both sides to maintain the recliner's balance.

Tightening the Bolts:

Insert the front bolts into the frame and tighten all four bolts securely. Return the recliner to the upright position and test the pitch. If necessary, make further adjustments until the desired pitch is achieved.


Conclusion:

By implementing these methods, namely adjusting tension, replacing springs, and changing pitch, you can restore the comfort and functionality of your recliner. Remember to make gradual adjustments and thoroughly test the results after each step. With a bit of care and attention, you can enjoy a rejuvenated recliner that perfectly aligns with your relaxation preferences. Whether you prefer a firm and upright position for reading or a laid-back and cozy experience for movie nights, you now possess the knowledge to customize your recliner for maximum comfort and enjoyment.
