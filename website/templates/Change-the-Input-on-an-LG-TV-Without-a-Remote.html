Unlocking the Hidden Gems: Changing Input on LG TVs Without a Remote

In the vast world of home entertainment, sometimes the simplest tasks can become daunting when the trusty remote control goes missing. Not to worry, LG TV users! We've got your back with a trio of ingenious methods to conquer this remote-less conundrum.

From the untapped potential of TV buttons to the wizardry of smartphones and the precision of computer mice, this guide will empower you to navigate your entertainment landscape with effortless ease. So, let's dive right in and unlock the hidden gems of changing input on your LG TV, all without the remote.


Method 1: Using TV Buttons

Navigate to your LG TV and locate the physical buttons situated beneath the LG logo. These buttons typically include a "Power" button, which doubles as the "Input" button on modern LG TV models.

Press the "Power" button repeatedly to cycle through the available input options. Each press will advance you through the input sources, allowing you to select the desired input for your viewing experience.

Method 2: Using the Smartphone App

To change the input on your LG TV using a smartphone app, follow these steps:

  1. Install the LG ThinQ App: Download the official LG ThinQ app from the Google Play Store (for Android devices) or the App Store (for iOS devices) and install it on your smartphone.

  2. Establish Network Connection: Ensure that your LG TV and smartphone are connected to the same Wi-Fi network. This will allow the app to communicate with and control your TV.

  3. Create an Account: If you haven't already, create an account within the LG ThinQ app. This will enable you to register your TV and access its features.

  4. Add Your TV: Follow the prompts within the app to add your LG TV to your account. This involves entering the TV's model number or scanning the QR code displayed on the TV screen.

  5. Access Input Controls: Once your TV is added, tap on "Input" within the LG ThinQ app. This will display a list of available input options, allowing you to select the desired input source.

Method 3: Using a Computer Mouse

Connect a wireless computer mouse to the USB port located on the back of your LG TV.

Turn on the TV and press the "Input" button either on the TV buttons (as described in Method 1) or using the remote (if available).

Once the input selection menu appears on the TV screen, use the wireless mouse to navigate and select the desired input source. The mouse cursor will function as a pointer, allowing you to select the input option of your choice.


Conclusion:

With the methods outlined above, you can easily change the input on your LG TV even without a remote. Whether you prefer the tactile approach of using TV buttons, the convenience of a smartphone app, or the precision of a computer mouse, there's an option that caters to your needs. By following these simple steps, you can enjoy seamless access to your favorite content, effortlessly navigate between devices, and enhance your overall TV experience.
