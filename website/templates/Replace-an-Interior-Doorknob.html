Step-by-Step Guide with Visuals: Replacing an Interior Doorknob in 15 Easy Steps


Unlock the Secrets of Doorknob Mastery

From the mundane to the intricate, doorknobs play a pivotal role in our daily lives. They control access to rooms, secure our homes, and add a touch of style to our décor. But when it comes to replacing or upgrading these ubiquitous fixtures, the task can seem daunting.

Fear not, savvy homeowner! In this comprehensive guide, we'll demystify the art of doorknob removal and installation, empowering you with the knowledge to tackle this essential maintenance task like a pro. Whether you're replacing a worn-out knob or simply want to update the look of your door, we'll guide you through each step with precision and clarity. Get ready to unlock the secrets of doorknob mastery!


Removing the Doorknob:

For Doorknobs with Visible Faceplates:

  1. Locate the two or three screws securing the faceplate to the door.
  2. Using a screwdriver, carefully remove the screws, being mindful not to strip the screw heads.
  3. Once the screws are removed, gently lift off the faceplate to expose the doorknob mechanism.

For Doorknobs Without Faceplates:

  1. If there are no visible screws, look for a small hole on the doorknob face where the latch mechanism engages.
  2. Insert a sharp object (paperclip or nail) into the latch hole.
  3. Gently pull the inner knob away from the door while pushing inward on the sharp object. This will release the latch mechanism and allow you to remove the inner knob.
  4. Once the inner knob is removed, the outer doorknob should slide off easily.

Optional Step (for Doorknobs with Faceplates):

  1. If the doorknob has a faceplate, gently pry it off using a flathead screwdriver or putty knife.
  2. Locate and unscrew the screws securing the faceplate to the outer door.

Removing the Outer Doorknob:

  1. Unscrew the screws connecting the outer doorknob to the knob bars that extend through the door.
  2. Gently pull the outer doorknob off the knob bars.

Removing the Latch:

  1. Locate the latch assembly inside the door. It will be a metal mechanism with a spring-loaded latch bolt.
  2. Unscrew the screws securing the latch assembly to the door.
  3. Carefully pull the latch assembly out of the door.

Installing a New Latch:

  1. Insert the new latch assembly into the hole in the door, ensuring that the latch bolt is facing inward.
  2. Align the faceplate of the latch with the corresponding screw holes on the door.
  3. Screw the latch faceplate into the door to secure the latch assembly.

Installing a Doorknob:

For Doorknobs with Faceplates:

  1. Insert the knob bars of the new doorknob through the holes in the latch faceplate.
  2. Attach the faceplate to the outer door using the supplied screws.
  3. Connect the inner knob to the other end of the knob bars.
  4. Slide the inner knob onto the threaded stem on the door.

For Doorknobs Without Faceplates:

  1. Connect the outer doorknob to the knob bars that extend through the door.
  2. Slide the outer knob onto the threaded stem on the door.
  3. Screw the inner knob into the door, ensuring that it engages with the latch mechanism.

Final Step (for Doorknobs with Faceplates):

  1. If you used a faceplate, slide the outer knob onto the threaded stem protruding from the faceplate.

Conclusion:

Congratulations! You have successfully removed and replaced your doorknob. Whether you needed to upgrade the style, repair a broken latch, or simply want to refresh the look of your home, this guide has provided you with the step-by-step instructions to tackle the task with ease. Remember to double-check the fit and function of the new hardware by opening and closing the door several times. With this newfound knowledge, you can now approach any future doorknob replacements with confidence and precision.
