Simplify Two-Step Algebra Equations in Three Easy Steps

Method 1: Solving Equations with One Variable

Step 1: Isolate the Variable Term

  • Subtract/Add:
    • If the constant (a number without a variable) is on the same side as the variable term, subtract it from both sides.
    • If the constant is on the opposite side, add it to both sides.
  • Multiply/Divide:
    • If the variable term has a coefficient (a number before the variable), divide both sides by the coefficient.
    • If the variable term is multiplied by a fraction, multiply both sides by the denominator (the number below the fraction line).

Example:

Solve for x in the equation: 2x + 5 = 13

  1. Subtract 5 from both sides: 2x = 8
  2. Divide both sides by 2: x = 4

Method 2: Solving Equations with Variables on Both Sides

Step 1: Move Constants

  • Add or subtract the same number to both sides of the equation to move all constants to one side.

Step 2: Move Variables

  • Subtract the same variable term from both sides to isolate all variables on one side.

Step 3: Solve for Variable

  • Divide or multiply both sides by the coefficient of the variable you want to solve for.

Example:

Solve for y in the equation: 3y - 2 = y + 4

  1. Add 2 to both sides: 3y = y + 6
  2. Subtract y from both sides: 2y = 6
  3. Divide both sides by 2: y = 3

Other Methods

Keep Variable on Right Side:

  • Follow the same steps as in Method 1, but do not move the variable term to the left side.
  • Instead, keep the variable on the right side and isolate it by subtracting and dividing.

Multiply Instead of Divide:

  • Follow the same steps as in Method 1, but instead of dividing both sides by the coefficient, multiply both sides by the denominator.
  • This method is useful when the coefficient is a fraction.

Example (Keep Variable on Right Side):

Solve for x in the equation: 2x + 5 = 13

  1. Subtract 5 from both sides: 2x = 8
  2. Subtract 2x from both sides: -8 = 5

This method does not result in a solution because the constant term on the left side is negative.

Example (Multiply Instead of Divide):

Solve for y in the equation: 1/2y - 3 = 5

  1. Add 3 to both sides: 1/2y = 8
  2. Multiply both sides by 2: y = 16
