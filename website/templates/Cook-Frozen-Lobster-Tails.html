Lobster Tail Cooking Guide

Defrosting:

  • Advance Preparation: Remove lobster tails from the freezer 24 hours in advance. This allows ample time for the tails to thaw thoroughly without damaging their delicate texture.
  • Refrigerated Thawing: Place the lobster tails on a plate and cover them loosely with plastic wrap. Refrigerate them until fully thawed, which typically takes 24 hours. The tails will be flexible and easily bendable when they are ready.

Cooking Methods:

1. Broiled Lobster Tails:

  • Preheat Oven: Heat your oven to a blistering 500°F (260°C). This high temperature will create a flavorful char on the lobster while keeping the meat tender and juicy.
  • Shell Cutting: Using sharp kitchen shears or a knife, carefully cut open the shells of the lobster tails lengthwise. Leave the tails intact to preserve their shape and prevent the meat from falling apart.
  • Seasoning: Season the lobster tails generously with a tantalizing blend of garlic powder, paprika, and white pepper. Don't forget to add a touch of melted butter to enhance their richness.
  • Broiling: Place the seasoned tails on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Broil them in the preheated oven for approximately 5-10 minutes. Keep a watchful eye on the tails, as the cooking time may vary depending on their size.
  • Tenderness Test: The lobster tails are cooked when the meat turns opaque and has a delicate white color. Insert a toothpick or skewer into the thickest part of the tail to confirm that it is cooked through.

2. Grilled Lobster Tails:

  • Fire up the Grill: Heat your grill to a medium-high temperature. This will create the perfect sear while ensuring that the lobsters cook evenly.
  • Skewering: Skewer each lobster tail through the center using a sturdy metal skewer. This will prevent the tails from curling while cooking and help them stay straight.
  • Oiling Up: Brush the lobster tails with olive oil to prevent them from sticking to the grill grates.
  • Grilling: Grill the lobster tails for 9-10 minutes, flipping once halfway through the cooking process.
  • Herb Butter Basting: Towards the end of the grilling time, baste the lobster tails liberally with a flavorful herb butter. This will add extra moisture and enhance their savory notes.

3. Boiled Lobster Tails:

  • Salty Brew: Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. The salt will help season the lobster tails while cooking.
  • Submerging: Carefully submerge the lobster tails into the boiling water.
  • Cooking Duration: Boil the lobster tails for 5 minutes per pound (16 ounces). This ensures that the lobsters are cooked through without overcooking and becoming tough.
  • Bright Red Indication: The lobster tails are done cooking when they turn a vibrant red color.
  • Melted Butter and Lemon: Serve the boiled lobster tails immediately with melted butter and lemon wedges for a classic accompaniment.

Tips for Success:

  • Multiple Tails: If you're cooking multiple lobster tails, double or triple the recipes to accommodate larger quantities.
  • Straighten Up: To keep the lobster tails straight while cooking, insert a skewer into each tail before placing them in the cooking device.
  • Microwave Defrosting: While it's not the recommended method, you can defrost lobster tails in the microwave on the "defrost" setting. Be sure to carefully monitor the defrosting process to prevent overcooking.
