Gameplay:

  1. Deal: Three cards are dealt to each player from a standard 52-card deck. The remaining cards are placed facedown in a pile known as the "stockpile."

  2. Flip-Over Card: The top card from the stockpile is flipped over and placed face-up in the center of the playing area. This card establishes the suit and number that subsequent players must match.

  3. Dealer's Announcement: The dealer initiates the game by declaring, "The name of the game is Mao." This serves as a reminder of the game's objective and unspoken rules.

  4. Gameplay: Players take turns playing cards from their hands that match either the suit or the number of the face-up card. If a player cannot play a matching card, they must draw a new card from the stockpile.

  5. Clockwise Rotation: Gameplay proceeds in a clockwise direction around the table. The player to the left of the dealer takes their turn first, followed by the player to their left, and so on.

  6. Card Play and Special Rules:

    • Aces: When an ace is played, the next player is skipped.
    • Twos: When a 2 is played, the player who played it takes an extra turn.
    • Sevens: When a 7 is played, the player immediately to the left of the player who played the 7 must pick up a card from the stockpile.
    • Eights: When an 8 is played, the direction of play is reversed.
    • Jacks: When a jack is played, the player who played it can declare a new suit that all subsequent players must adhere to.
    • Spades: When a spade card is played, players must state the full name of the card (e.g., "ace of spades" instead of "ace").
    • One Card: When a player has only one card remaining, they must declare, "one card."
    • Identical Cards: Identical cards (e.g., two aces) can be discarded at any time, without having to match the face-up card or follow any special rules.

Unspoken Rules:

  • Players should not hesitate or take excessive time when playing their cards.
  • Players should not assist or hint to other players about what cards to play.
