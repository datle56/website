Part 1: Creating the "Open A" Position

To begin crafting the "Open A" position, which forms the foundation for Jacob's Ladder, follow these steps:

  1. Measure and Cut the String: Determine the optimal length of the string by measuring it from one shoulder to the other, then doubling that measurement. This ensures you have sufficient string to comfortably manipulate the ladder without it being too bulky.
  2. Tie the Ends Together: Create a continuous loop by securely tying the two ends of the string together. This connection point becomes the starting and ending point for the ladder.
  3. Loop the String around Thumb and Pinky: Slip the knotted loop around your dominant hand's (usually the right hand) thumb and pinky. Position the loop so that the thumb rests on the top of the loop and the pinky is below it.
  4. Hook Index Fingers: Cross your index fingers in front of each other and hook them over the string that is above your thumb. Your thumbs should remain on top of the string, while your index fingers should be underneath.

Part 2: Making Cat's Whiskers

To create the distinctive "Cat's Whiskers" section of Jacob's Ladder, follow these steps:

  1. Drop Strings from Thumbs: Gently release the strings from around your thumbs, allowing them to hang loosely.
  2. Hook Thumbs Under Strings: Extend your thumbs underneath the strings and hook them under the first and then the second string on your index fingers. This creates a "V" shape with your thumbs supporting the strings.
  3. Drop Strings from Pinkies: Similarly, release the strings from your pinkies, letting them hang freely.
  4. Hook Pinkies Under Strings: Extend your pinkies underneath the last string and hook them under it. Return to the starting position by looping the string around your pinkies again.

Part 3: Finishing Jacob's Ladder

To complete the Jacob's Ladder, follow these final steps:

  1. Hook Thumbs Under Third Strings: Position your thumbs underneath the third strings from the top of the ladder, creating a support point.
  2. Move Right Index Finger Loop Over Thumb: With your right hand, bring the loop on your index finger over your thumb and pull it through the space. This creates a knot on the right side of the ladder.
  3. Pull Up Lower String on Right Thumb: Continue to hold the right loop with your index finger and pull up the lower string on your right thumb. This forms a diagonal strand that intersects the previous knot.
  4. Repeat with Left Hand: Mirror the actions you performed with your right hand on the left side of the ladder, creating a symmetrical pattern.
  5. Flip Hands Around: Rotate your hands so that your palms face each other.
  6. Remove Strings from Pinkies: Release the strings from around your pinkies.
  7. Pull Strings Through Small Triangles: Use your thumbs and index fingers to gently pull the strings through the small triangles that have formed between the knots and supports. This action completes the Jacob's Ladder pattern.
