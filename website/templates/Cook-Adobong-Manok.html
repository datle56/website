Easy Adobo Chicken Recipes

Introduction

Adobo, a classic Filipino dish, is renowned for its savory and tangy flavor. It's a beloved staple in Filipino cuisine, with its aromatic blend of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and other seasonings. Whether you prefer the convenience of a stovetop preparation or the depth of flavor gained from marinating, this guide will provide you with step-by-step instructions for both methods.

Stovetop Method

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup vinegar (white or apple cider)
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 cups water
  • 3 bay leaves

Instructions:

  1. Heat the oil: Heat 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat.
  2. Sauté aromatics: Add the garlic and onion to the hot oil and cook until fragrant and slightly browned.
  3. Add chicken and seasonings: Add the chicken pieces to the skillet and season with the soy sauce, vinegar, brown sugar, salt, black pepper, and bay leaves. Stir to combine.
  4. Add water: Pour in the water and bring the mixture to a boil.
  5. Reduce heat and simmer: Once boiling, reduce the heat to low, cover the skillet, and simmer for 20-30 minutes, or until the chicken is tender and the sauce has thickened.

Marinade Method

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup vinegar (white or apple cider)
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 3 bay leaves

Instructions:

  1. Combine marinade ingredients: In a large bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, onion, brown sugar, salt, black pepper, and bay leaves.
  2. Marinate chicken: Add the chicken pieces to the marinade, ensuring they are submerged. Cover and refrigerate for several hours or overnight, allowing the flavors to penetrate.
  3. Drain marinade: Remove the chicken from the marinade and discard the marinade.
  4. Cook chicken: Heat 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the chicken pieces and cook until browned on all sides.
  5. Add water: Pour in 2 cups of water and bring to a boil.
  6. Reduce heat and simmer: Once boiling, reduce the heat to low, cover the skillet, and simmer for 20-30 minutes, or until the chicken is tender and the sauce has thickened.

Tips

  • Serve with sides: Adobo chicken pairs perfectly with rice, potatoes, or bread.
  • Customize flavors: Experiment with different spices to suit your taste preferences. Add a touch of chili flakes for a spicy kick, paprika for a smoky flavor, or cumin for an earthy aroma.
  • Adjust seasonings: The amount of vinegar and soy sauce can be adjusted to your liking. If you prefer a more tangy flavor, add more vinegar. For a richer soy flavor, add more soy sauce.
