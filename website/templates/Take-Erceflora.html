How to Use Erceflora: A Comprehensive Guide

Erceflora is a brand of Bacillus clausii, a beneficial bacteria that plays a vital role in maintaining gut health. This probiotic supplement is available in both liquid and capsule form and can be used by individuals of all ages. Here's a detailed guide on how to use Erceflora effectively:

Who Can Take Erceflora?

Erceflora is safe and suitable for individuals of all ages, including:

  • Infants (0-3 years)
  • Children (4-15 years)
  • Teenagers (16+ years)
  • Adults (16+ years)

Dosage and Administration

The recommended dosage of Erceflora varies depending on age and condition:

Infants (0-3 years):

  • 1-2 vials per day

Children (4-15 years):

  • 1-2 vials per day or 1-2 capsules per day

Teenagers/Adults (16+ years):

  • 2-3 vials per day or 2-3 capsules per day

For adults and children over 3 years old, Erceflora can be taken in either liquid or capsule form. For infants, only the liquid form is recommended.

How to Take Erceflora:

  1. Shake Liquid Erceflora: Before each use, gently shake the liquid Erceflora vial to evenly distribute the spores.
  2. Mix with a Drink: Empty the contents of the vial into a small amount of water, milk, tea, or orange juice to improve the taste and make it easier to swallow.
  3. Drink the Entire Mixture: Drink the entire mixture to ensure you receive the full dose of beneficial bacteria.
  4. Take at Regular Intervals: As directed by your doctor, take Erceflora at regular intervals throughout the day, such as morning, afternoon, and evening.

Storage

Proper storage is essential to maintain the potency and effectiveness of Erceflora.

  • Keep sealed Erceflora at room temperature in a dry location.
  • Avoid exposing Erceflora to temperatures over 30°C (86°F). Do not freeze the product.

Safety Precautions

Before using Erceflora, it's essential to take the following safety precautions:

  • Inform your doctor if you experience any side effects from taking Erceflora.
  • If you have a weakened immune system or are taking any immunosuppressant medications, it's crucial to consult your doctor before using Erceflora.
  • Provide your doctor with a list of all other supplements or medications you are using to avoid potential interactions.

Caution: Do not inject or administer Erceflora in any other way than as directed. Improper use of Erceflora may cause allergic reactions or other adverse effects.
