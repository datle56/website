Getting Arceus: A Comprehensive Guide

Legitimate Methods

Complete the National Pokédex

To unlock Arceus without resorting to cheats, you'll need to complete the National Pokédex, which involves catching or registering all 721 Pokémon in the game. Completing the National Pokédex is a daunting task that requires patience, dedication, and access to various trading and transfer methods. However, it is the only surefire way to obtain Arceus without relying on cheats.

Once you have completed the National Pokédex, you can receive Arceus in one of the following ways:

  • Defeat the Sinnoh Champion: After completing the National Pokédex, talk to Professor Rowan in Sandgem Town. He will give you the Azure Flute, which allows you to summon Arceus in the Hall of Origin atop Mt. Coronet.

Trade for Arceus

Arceus has been distributed through various special events in the past. If you missed these events, you can only obtain Arceus by trading with someone who received it through an event. You can use online forums, trading websites, or your personal connections to find trading partners.

Transfer Arceus from Another Game

If you have Arceus in Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold, or SoulSilver, you can transfer it to Pokémon X or Y using the PokéTransporter and PokéBank services. These services allow you to move Pokémon from previous games to newer ones.

Using Cheats (Action Replay)

If you are not averse to using cheats, there are several Action Replay codes that can grant you Arceus. However, these cheats may have unintended consequences and can corrupt your game data. Use cheats at your own risk.

Diamond, Pearl, Platinum

  • Add the Azure Flute code to your Action Replay.
  • Enter any PokéMart and talk to the green man to receive the Azure Flute.
  • Climb Mt. Coronet with the flute to access the Hall of Origin and battle Arceus.

HeartGold, SoulSilver

  • Add the Arceus code to your Action Replay.
  • Enter any PokéMart and talk to the green man to receive the Toy R' Us Event Arceus.
  • Take Arceus to the Ruins of Alph to trigger a special event.

Black, White, Black 2, White 2

  • Add the Arceus code to your Action Replay.
  • Put an expendable Pokémon at the front of your party.
  • Press Select to activate the cheat and receive a random-level Arceus.

Additional Tips

  • Make sure to save your game before using cheats.
  • Consider using a backup memory card or save file to mitigate the risk of data corruption.
  • Check online forums and resources for the latest cheat codes and instructions.
  • Use cheats with caution and at your own discretion.
