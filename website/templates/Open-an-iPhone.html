How to Open an iPhone 6s or 7: A Comprehensive Guide

Materials:

  • Pentalobe screwdriver (0.8mm for iPhone 6s, 1.2mm for iPhone 7)
  • Phillips #000 screwdriver (iPhone 6s only)
  • Tripoint Y000 screwdriver (iPhone 7 only)
  • Spudger (a thin, flat plastic tool)
  • Heat source (such as a gel bag or microwaveable item)
  • Suction cup
  • Plastic bags or containers

Safety Precautions:

  • Turn off the iPhone before opening it.
  • Ground yourself by touching a metal surface to prevent static electricity damage.

Step-by-Step Instructions:

iPhone 7:

  1. Remove the Pentalobe Screws: Using a pentalobe screwdriver, remove the two pentalobe screws located at the bottom of the iPhone, next to the Lightning port.

  2. Heat the Bottom: Place the iPhone on a flat surface. Apply heat to the bottom half of the phone using a heat source for about 30 seconds to soften the adhesive holding the screen in place.

  3. Attach the Suction Cup: Securely attach the suction cup to the lower half of the screen, slightly above the Home button.

  4. Create a Gap: Pull up on the suction cup to create a small gap between the screen and the frame.

  5. Insert the Spudger: Gently slide the spudger into the gap on the left side of the iPhone.

  6. Slide the Spudger: Slide the spudger around the edges of the screen, carefully separating the adhesive holding the screen to the frame. Repeat on the right side.

  7. Use a Credit Card: Use a credit card or another thin object to help separate the screen at the top, where the screen clips are located.

  8. Pull Down and Disconnect: Gently pull down the screen assembly slightly to disconnect the screen clips.

  9. Open the Screen: Open the screen to the right like a book. Be careful not to pull too hard, as the cables connecting the screen to the logic board are still attached.

  10. Remove the L-Shaped Bracket: Use a spudger to remove the L-shaped bracket covering the battery and screen connectors.

  11. Disconnect Battery and Screen: Pry up the battery connector and the screen connector using the tip of a spudger.

  12. Remove the Last Connector Bracket: Locate the bracket covering the last connector, which is at the bottom of the logic board. Remove this bracket and pry up the connector.

  13. Remove the Screen: Carefully lift the screen off the frame. Avoid touching the delicate display cables.

iPhone 6s:

  1. Remove the Pentalobe Screws: Using a pentalobe screwdriver, remove the two pentalobe screws located at the bottom of the iPhone, next to the Lightning port.

  2. Heat the Bottom: Apply heat to the bottom half of the phone using a heat source for about 30 seconds to soften the adhesive.

  3. Attach the Suction Cup: Securely attach the suction cup to the lower half of the screen, slightly above the Home button.

  4. Pull Up and Insert Spudger: Pull up on the suction cup to create a small gap between the screen and the frame. Gently insert the spudger into the gap.

  5. Slide the Spudger: Slide the spudger around the edges of the screen, carefully separating the adhesive holding the screen to the frame. Repeat on the right side.

  6. Swing Up the Screen: Swing the screen up to a 90-degree angle, being mindful of the cables connected to the logic board.

  7. Remove Battery Connector Bracket: Use a spudger to remove the bracket covering the battery connector.

  8. Disconnect Battery: Pry up the battery connector using the tip of a spudger.

  9. Remove Display Cable Bracket: Use a spudger to remove the bracket covering the display cable connector.

  10. Disconnect Camera and Display Cables: Pry up the camera connector and the display connector using the tip of a spudger.

  11. Remove the Screen: Carefully lift the screen off the frame. Avoid touching the display cables.

Tips:

  • Once the iPhone is open, you can replace parts or apply new adhesive.
  • Be cautious when applying pressure to avoid damaging the delicate components inside the iPhone.
  • Opening the iPhone will void its warranty.

Conclusion:

Congratulations! You have now successfully opened your iPhone 6s or 7. Remember to handle the components with care, and be sure to reassemble the device in the reverse order of disassembly. If you encounter any difficulties, refer to the step-by-step instructions again or seek professional assistance.
