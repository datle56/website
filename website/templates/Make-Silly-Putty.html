Create Your Own Playful Putty: A Step-by-Step Guide to 3 Silly Putty Recipes


Unleash Your Inner Scientist: Creating Silly Putty with Household Goodies

Are you ready for some squish-tastic fun? This guide will show you how to transform everyday items into the iconic bouncy, moldable substance known as silly putty. Get ready to create an endless supply of ooey-gooey happiness with these easy methods using glue, borax, starch, and cornstarch!


Method 1: Using Glue and Borax

Materials:

  • Clear craft glue (also known as PVA glue)
  • Borax powder
  • Warm water
  • Bowl
  • Spoon for stirring

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Prepare the Glue Mixture: In a bowl, combine clear glue and water in a ratio of 2 parts glue to 1 part water. Stir until well mixed and a smooth, even consistency is achieved.

  2. Create the Borax Solution: In a separate bowl, dissolve borax powder in warm water at a ratio of 1 teaspoon of borax per 1 cup of water. Stir vigorously until the borax is completely dissolved.

  3. Combine the Mixtures: Slowly pour the borax solution into the glue mixture while stirring constantly. The mixture will begin to thicken and form a gel-like consistency. Continue stirring until it reaches a desired consistency.

  4. Knead the Gel: Remove the gel from the bowl and knead it with your hands for 5-10 minutes. As you knead, the gel will gradually transform into silly putty. Continue kneading until it forms a smooth and pliable ball.

Method 2: Using Glue and Liquid Starch

Materials:

  • Clear craft glue
  • Food coloring or glitter (optional)
  • Liquid starch
  • Spoon for stirring

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Prepare the Glue Mixture: In a bowl, mix clear glue with food coloring or glitter, if desired. Stir until well combined.

  2. Add Liquid Starch: Gradually add liquid starch to the glue mixture while stirring constantly. Begin with small amounts and add more as needed until the mixture begins to form a putty.

  3. Knead the Putty: Once the mixture has reached a putty-like consistency, remove it from the bowl and knead it with your hands until it becomes solid and less sticky. Continue kneading until it forms a cohesive ball.

Method 3: Using Cornstarch and Dish Soap

Materials:

  • Dish soap
  • Glitter (optional)
  • Cornstarch
  • Bowl
  • Spoon for mixing

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Prepare the Dish Soap Mixture: In a bowl, combine dish soap with glitter, if desired. Stir until well mixed.

  2. Add Cornstarch: Gradually add cornstarch to the dish soap mixture while mixing constantly. Begin with small amounts and add more as needed until the mixture starts to thicken and form a gel.

  3. Knead the Gel: Remove the gel from the bowl and knead it with your hands for several minutes. As you knead, it will gradually transform into silly putty. Continue kneading until it becomes smooth and pliable.


Conclusion:

With these three simple methods, you now have the power to create your own squishy, moldable silly putty at home. Whether you prefer the traditional glue and borax combination or opt for the cornstarch and liquid starch alternatives, the process is straightforward and mess-free. Embrace your creativity and experiment with different colors and textures to personalize your very own fidget toy. From science experiments to sensory play, silly putty provides endless hours of entertainment and stress relief, making it a perfect activity for kids and adults alike.
