Unleash Personalized Productivity: Explore 4 Ways to Tailor Your Windows Taskbar's Location


Unlock the Secrets of Windows Taskbar: Move and Customize for a Tailored Experience

Ready to unleash the potential of your Windows taskbar? Whether you're a tech enthusiast seeking a more efficient workflow or simply want to add a personal touch to your computing experience, you'll find a treasure trove of customization options in this comprehensive guide.

We'll delve into the simple steps to effortlessly move your taskbar to the side of your screen, the top, or even the bottom. But that's just the beginning! You'll also discover a world of customization options, from changing the color to adding toolbars and features that suit your specific needs.

Follow us on this journey to unlock the full potential of your Windows taskbar. Get ready to make it work for you, not against you!


How to Move or Customize the Windows Taskbar

For Windows 10

  1. Unlock the Taskbar: Right-click on an empty area of the taskbar and uncheck the "Lock the taskbar" option.
  2. Open Taskbar Settings: Click on "Taskbar settings" from the right-click menu.
  3. Change Taskbar Location: Under the "Taskbar location on screen" section, select the desired location for the taskbar: "Left," "Right," or "Top."
  4. Adjust Taskbar Size: Hover the mouse over the edge of the taskbar until a double arrow cursor appears. Drag the edge to resize the taskbar vertically.

For Windows 8

  1. Unlock the Taskbar: Right-click on the taskbar and uncheck "Lock the taskbar."
  2. Open Taskbar Properties: Click on "Properties" from the right-click menu.
  3. Change Taskbar Location: In the "Taskbar location on screen" dropdown, select the desired location for the taskbar.
  4. Adjust Taskbar Size: Drag the top or bottom edge of the taskbar to resize it vertically.

For Windows 7

  1. Unlock the Taskbar: Left-click and hold on an empty area of the taskbar.
  2. Move the Taskbar: Drag and drop the taskbar to the desired location on the screen (top, left, or right).
  3. Adjust Taskbar Size: Right-click on the taskbar and select "Properties." In the "Taskbar" tab, adjust the "Taskbar height" value to change its vertical size.

Customization Options

Change Taskbar Color:

  • Open "Settings."
  • Go to "Personalization" > "Colors."
  • Select the desired color for the taskbar from the "Highlight color" section.

Add or Remove Features:

  • Right-click on the taskbar and select "Properties."
  • Under the "Toolbars" tab, check or uncheck boxes to add or remove features such as the clock, search box, or virtual desktops.

Add Toolbars:

  • Right-click on the taskbar and select "Properties."
  • Click on the "Toolbars" tab and select a toolbar from the list to add it to the taskbar.

Hide or Show Taskbar:

  • Right-click on the taskbar and select "Properties."
  • In the "Taskbar" tab, check or uncheck the "Automatically hide the taskbar in desktop mode" box to control whether the taskbar is hidden or not.
