Homemade KFC-Style Chicken: A Culinary Adventure

Introduction:

Indulge in the irresistible flavors of iconic fried chicken without leaving the comfort of your kitchen. With this step-by-step recipe, you'll create homemade KFC-style chicken that rivals the beloved original.

Ingredients:

Seasoning Blend:

  • 1/3 cup Kosher salt
  • 2 teaspoons dried thyme
  • 2 teaspoons dried basil
  • 2 teaspoons dried oregano
  • 2 teaspoons celery salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon dried mustard
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/4 teaspoon white pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon Accent Seasoning (optional, but highly recommended)

Chicken:

  • 1 whole chicken (about 3-4 pounds), cut into 10 pieces

Marinade:

  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 large egg

Breading:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour

Frying:

  • 4-6 cups vegetable oil (for deep frying)

Instructions:

1. Preparing the Seasoning Blend:

  • Gather all the seasoning ingredients and place them in a food processor or spice grinder.
  • Process until the mixture is a uniform, fine powder.
  • Transfer the seasoning blend to a large bowl.

2. Marinating the Chicken:

  • Place the chicken pieces in a large bowl and pour the buttermilk over them.
  • Use your hands to gently massage the buttermilk into the chicken, ensuring all pieces are coated.
  • Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 3 hours, or overnight for best results.

3. Breading the Chicken:

  • In a separate bowl, whisk together the all-purpose flour and the prepared seasoning blend.
  • Remove the chicken pieces from the buttermilk marinade and shake off any excess.
  • 1 egg in a shallow dish and whisk until beaten.
  • Dip each chicken piece in the egg wash, then gently shake off any excess.
  • Immediately coat the chicken in the seasoned flour, pressing gently to adhere.

4. Frying the Chicken:

  • Heat the vegetable oil in a large pot, deep fryer, or electric skillet to 350°F (177°C).
  • Carefully place the chicken pieces in the hot oil, taking care not to overcrowd the pot.
  • Fry for 12-18 minutes, depending on the size and cut of the chicken.
  • Use a meat thermometer to ensure the internal temperature of the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C) to ensure it is safely cooked.

Tips:

  • For a more intense flavor, double the amount of seasoning blend and save half to use later.
  • Serve the homemade KFC-style chicken with classic sides like mashed potatoes, corn on the cob, coleslaw, or potato salad for a complete and satisfying meal.
  • Cast iron skillets or deep fryers provide optimal heat distribution and result in crispy, golden-brown fried chicken.

Conclusion:

With this easy-to-follow recipe, you can now savor the irresistible flavors of KFC-style chicken in your own home. Gather your ingredients, embark on this culinary adventure, and tantalize your taste buds with a delicious and satisfying meal.
