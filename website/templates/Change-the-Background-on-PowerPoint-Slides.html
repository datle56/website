How to Customize PowerPoint Backgrounds

Using Microsoft PowerPoint

Step 1: Select the Slide

  • Open your PowerPoint presentation.
  • Click on the slide you wish to modify the background for.

Step 2: Access the Design Tab

  • In the top toolbar, locate the "Design" tab. Click on it to open the design options.

Step 3: Format Background

  • In the "Design" tab, click on the "Format Background" button. A drop-down menu will appear.
  • Select the "Fill" option from the drop-down menu.

Step 4: Choose Background Type

  • The "Fill" pane will provide various options for customizing the background:
    • Solid Color: Choose from a wide range of solid colors to fill the entire slide.
    • Gradient: Create a gradual blend of colors for a dynamic effect.
    • Picture or Texture: Insert an image or a texture pattern as the background. You can browse your computer or use the built-in options.
    • Pattern: Select from a gallery of pre-defined patterns to add visual interest to the background.

Step 5: Apply Background to All Slides

  • After customizing the background for the selected slide, you can apply it to all slides in the presentation by clicking the "Apply to All" button at the bottom of the "Fill" pane.

Using Google Slides

Step 1: Open Presentation

  • Access Google Drive and open the presentation you wish to edit.

Step 2: Select Slide

  • Click on the slide you wish to modify the background for.

Step 3: Access Slide Menu

  • In the top toolbar, click on the "Slide" menu.

Step 4: Change Background

  • From the "Slide" menu, select the "Change background" option.

Step 5: Choose Background Type

  • The "Change background" window will provide two options:
    • Solid Color: Choose from a range of solid colors to fill the slide.
    • Image: Select an image from your computer or Google Drive to use as the background.

Step 6: Apply Background to Theme

  • After selecting a background, click the "Apply to theme" button to apply the changes to all slides in the presentation.
