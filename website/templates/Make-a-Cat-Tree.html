Craft a Custom Cat Tree in 15 Easy Steps with our Visual Guide


Calling all cat-loving architects!

Tired of your feline friend climbing the curtains and knocking over the bookcases? It's time to build them a magnificent cat tree of their own—and it doesn't have to be a daunting task.

Get ready to explore two foolproof methods for creating the ultimate cat paradise:

Method 1: Building from Scratch with Wood and Carpet

Transform a few simple materials into a towering feline fortress. This method gives you complete control over the size, design, and coziness of your masterpiece.

Method 2: Repurposing an Old Ladder

Give a forgotten ladder a new lease on life as a glamorous cat playground. With a little creativity, you can turn a sturdy old frame into a fun and functional haven for your furry companion.


Method 1: Building a Cat Tree from Wood and Carpet

Step 1: Planning

  • Determine the optimal size and layout for your space and cat's preferences. Consider their climbing and lounging habits.
  • Sketch out a detailed design plan that outlines the base, supports, platforms, and any additional features like perches or tunnels.

Step 2: Materials Gathering

  • Wood: Plywood for the base and platforms, wooden beams or posts for the supports
  • Carpet: Plush carpet or artificial turf to cover the wood surfaces and provide a comfortable grip for your cat
  • Tools: Drill, saw, stapler gun, measuring tape, level

Step 3: Cutting and Assembly

  • Cut the plywood base and platform pieces according to your design plan.
  • Construct the base by securely attaching the plywood base to the wooden supports using wood screws or nails.
  • Build the platforms by joining the plywood pieces to the supports with wood glue and screws.
  • Reinforce the structure by attaching cross-members between the supports for added stability.

Step 4: Carpeting

  • Staple the carpet over the wooden surfaces, ensuring it is taut and free from creases.
  • Use a staple gun with strong staples long enough to secure the carpet firmly to the wood.
  • Trim any excess carpet around the edges.

Step 5: Perch Installation

  • Cut additional plywood pieces to create perches for your cat to climb and rest on.
  • Screw the perches securely to the supports at strategic heights.
  • Cover the perches with carpet for a comfortable and non-slip surface.

Step 6: Building Extensions

  • Continue adding platforms, perches, and other features to your cat tree as desired.
  • Adjust the design as needed to cater to your cat's specific needs and preferences.
  • Secure all components safely using screws, nails, or wood glue.

Method 2: Creating a Cat Tree from a Ladder

Step 1: Ladder Selection

  • Locate an old wooden ladder that is sturdy and has a suitable height and width for your cat.
  • Inspect the ladder for any damage or rot, ensuring it is safe for use.

Step 2: Piece Preparation

  • Sand the ladder to remove any rough edges or splinters.
  • Paint or stain the ladder in a color that complements your decor.
  • Attach a plywood platform to the top of the ladder for additional lounging space.

Step 3: Hammock Creation

  • Cut a piece of sturdy fabric large enough to create a hammock between the bottom rungs of the ladder.
  • Sew the fabric securely to create a comfortable sleeping spot for your cat.

Step 4: Scratching Post Enhancement

  • Wrap the legs of the ladder with sisal rope, starting from the base and working your way upwards.
  • Secure the rope tightly to create a scratching post that your cat will enjoy using.

Step 5: Finishing Touches

  • Hang a toy at the top of the cat tree to provide entertainment and stimulation.
  • Add any additional features or decorations that your cat will appreciate, such as scratching posts, tunnels, or hiding boxes.

Conclusion: Empowering Cat Owners with Custom Cat Tree Creations

Building a cat tree from scratch can be a rewarding and cost-effective endeavor for feline enthusiasts. Whether you choose to construct a wooden and carpet masterpiece or repurpose an old ladder, the possibilities are limitless. By embracing the DIY spirit, you can create a personalized haven that caters to your cat's individual needs and aesthetic preferences.

Remember, the most important aspect is to ensure the safety and comfort of your furry friend. Carefully plan the design, secure all components, and use comfortable materials. Your cat will surely appreciate the opportunity to climb, perch, and cozy up on a cat tree made with love and tailored to their unique personality.
