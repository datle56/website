How to Master the Art of Catching Water Pokémon in Pokémon Emerald

Introduction

The vast oceans and tranquil rivers of Pokémon Emerald house an array of elusive Water-type Pokémon, each possessing unique abilities and intriguing characteristics. To successfully add these aquatic marvels to your team, you must embark on a fishing adventure armed with the right tools and techniques.

Choosing the Right Fishing Rod

Three types of fishing rods are available to anglers in Pokémon Emerald, each with its own capabilities:

  • Old Rod: This basic rod allows you to catch Pokémon up to Level 15. It is found early in the game and is suitable for catching common species.
  • Good Rod: An upgrade from the Old Rod, the Good Rod enables you to reel in Pokémon up to Level 30. It provides access to a wider variety of Water-types, including Magikarp.
  • Super Rod: The ultimate fishing tool, the Super Rod can catch any swimming Pokémon regardless of level. It unlocks the potential to encounter rare and powerful Water-types, such as Kyogre.

Choosing a Fishing Spot

Not all bodies of water are conducive to catching Water-type Pokémon. The type of Pokémon you encounter is primarily determined by the location you choose to fish. Explore different areas to discover the ideal spots for specific species.

One Notable Exception: Feebas

Feebas is a peculiar Water-type Pokémon that can only be caught on specific tiles within Route 119. Unlike other Pokémon, its location is fixed and requires patience and persistence to locate.

The Fishing Process

Once you have selected your fishing rod and found a promising location, the fishing process unfolds in three distinct stages:

  1. Casting the Line: Stand at the water's edge and press the "A" button to cast your line. Patience is key as it may take some time for a Pokémon to bite.
  2. Hooking the Pokémon: When you see the message "Oh! A bite!" on the screen, press "A" immediately to hook the Pokémon.
  3. Reeling in the Pokémon: A series of bite sounds will follow. Press "A" with each bite to successfully reel in the Pokémon, initiating a battle.

Tips for Success

  • Timing is Critical: The fishing mechanic in Pokémon Emerald requires faster reflexes than in previous versions. Practice your timing to improve your chances of hooking the Pokémon.
  • Patience and Perseverance: Don't be discouraged if you don't catch a Pokémon right away. Fishing can be a time-consuming process that requires patience and perseverance.
  • Experiment with Locations: Different fishing spots yield different types of Pokémon. Explore various areas to increase your chances of encountering the Water-type you seek.
  • Optimize Your Team: Having Pokémon on your team that can use moves like Dive or Waterfall can expand your fishing options and increase the number of Water-types you can encounter.

Conclusion

Catching Water-type Pokémon in Pokémon Emerald is a rewarding and challenging endeavor. By choosing the right fishing rod, exploring diverse locations, and mastering the fishing process, you can expand your collection and add these aquatic wonders to your team. Embark on your fishing adventure today and discover the treasures hidden within the watery depths of Pokémon Emerald!
