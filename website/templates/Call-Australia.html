Calling Australia from Anywhere: A Detailed Guide

Step 1: Dial Your Country's Exit Code

This initial code indicates that you are making an international call. It varies depending on your location, so it's essential to check the specific exit code for your country. For reference, here are a few examples:

  • UK and New Zealand: 00
  • US and Canada: 011
  • Most European countries: 00
  • Australia: 0011

Step 2: Enter Australia's Country Code

The country code for Australia is 61. This number indicates that you are calling a number within Australia.

Step 3: Dial the Australian Area Code

Area codes are 1-2 digits in length and represent the specific city or region within Australia that you are calling. Here are some examples:

  • Sydney: 02
  • Melbourne: 03
  • Brisbane: 07
  • Perth: 08

Step 4: Omit the Leading Zero from the Area Code

When dialing from overseas, it's important to remember to omit the leading zero from the area code. For example, if you are calling a number in Sydney, you would dial 02 instead of 002.

Step 5: Dial the 8-Digit Australian Phone Number

The final step is to dial the actual phone number you wish to reach. This is typically an 8-digit number.

Example:

To call a number in Sydney from the UK, you would follow these steps:

  • Dial 00 (UK exit code)
  • Dial 61 (Australia country code)
  • Omit the leading zero from the area code and dial 02 (Sydney area code)
  • Dial the 8-digit phone number (e.g., 12345678)

Total Number Sequence: 00 61 02 12345678

Alternative Calling Methods:

In addition to traditional phone calls, there are several alternative methods you can use to contact someone in Australia:

  • Video Messaging Apps (e.g., Skype, FaceTime, Google Meet): These apps facilitate free video calls over the internet. They are a great option for staying connected face-to-face without incurring phone charges.

  • Audio Messaging Apps (e.g., WhatsApp, Viber): Similar to video messaging apps, these platforms allow you to make audio calls over the internet, but without the video component. They are often used for casual conversations or when video quality is not optimal.

  • VOIP (Voice Over Internet Protocol): VOIP services like Skype and Google Voice enable you to make international calls at significantly lower rates than traditional phone companies. They work by using your internet connection to transmit calls, bypassing the need for expensive calling plans.
