Creating Custom T-Shirts with Iron-on Transfers: A Detailed Guide

Materials:

  • Iron-on transfer paper: Choose the type of paper suitable for the color of your fabric (light or dark).
  • Image you want to transfer: This can be a photo, design, or text that you create or find online.
  • Hard, flat surface: Such as a Formica countertop or cutting board.
  • Iron: Set to its hottest dry setting. Do not use steam.
  • Cardboard or brown paper bag: This will act as a protective barrier inside the garment.

Steps:

1. Creating the Transfer:

  • Design the image: Use a computer program to design or import your desired image.
  • Print the image: Ensure that you print the image on the correct side of the transfer paper. Refer to the manufacturer's instructions for details.

2. Applying the Transfer:

  • Prepare the fabric: Lay out the fabric on the flat surface and insert the cardboard or paper barrier inside the garment to prevent the transfer from adhering to the other side.
  • Trim and place the transfer: Cut the transfer to the desired size and carefully place it on the fabric, image side down.
  • Iron the transfer: Use firm, even pressure and move the iron in large circular motions over the transfer for approximately 3 minutes. Do not slide the iron back and forth.

3. Removing the Backing:

  • Cool and peel: Allow the transfer to cool for a few minutes before gently peeling off the backing paper.
  • Check for bubbles: If any bubbles appear, reapply the iron to that area until the bubble disappears.

4. Care for Transferred Clothes:

  • Wait before washing: Allow 24 hours before washing the garment to ensure proper bonding of the transfer.
  • Gentle washing: Wash and dry the garment on cool and gentle cycles, preferably turning it inside out.
  • Protective border: For added durability, consider sewing a border around the transferred image.

Tips:

  • Test the transfer: Before transferring the final image to a garment, test it on a scrap piece of fabric to ensure optimal results.
  • Mirror the image: If transferring an image to light-colored fabric, be sure to mirror the image horizontally before printing.
  • Avoid steam: Steam can cause the transfer to smudge or distort. Use the iron's dry setting only.
  • Iron evenly: Apply even pressure and avoid moving the iron too quickly over the transfer.
  • Multiple transfers: Allow ample time between transfers to avoid creating overlapping or smudged images.
  • Additional protection: To further protect the transferred image, consider using a spray-on fabric sealant or covering the image with a protective sheet during ironing.

Conclusion:
