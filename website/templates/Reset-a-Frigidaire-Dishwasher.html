Step 3: Reset Your Appliance (if needed)

For certain appliance issues, a simple reset can often resolve the problem. Here are two common reset methods:

  • Cancel Button Reset: Locate the Cancel button on your appliance's control panel. Press and hold it for approximately 3 seconds until the display resets.
  • Circuit Breaker Reset: Locate the circuit breaker that powers your appliance. Turn it off for 5 minutes and then turn it back on.
