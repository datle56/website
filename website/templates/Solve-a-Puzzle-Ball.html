Unlock the Secrets of the Tangram Puzzle: A Guide for Beginners

Welcome, puzzle enthusiasts! Dive into the fascinating world of tangrams, where six simple shapes can form an infinite number of intricate designs. Join us as we embark on a three-part exploration of this timeless classic.

In Part 1, we'll identify the seven unique pieces that make up the tangram. From umbrella-shaped sections to enigmatic alphabet-like shapes, each piece holds a key to assembling the puzzle. With our step-by-step instructions, you'll master the art of connecting these pieces into breathtaking designs. So, prepare to unleash your creativity and discover the captivating world of tangram puzzles!


Part 1: Identifying the Pieces

Imagine an umbrella cut in half transversely. The two resulting pieces resemble umbrellas:

  • Piece A: This piece forms the base of the umbrella. It has three symmetrical notches along one edge and a thick, sturdy handle that extends from the center.

  • Piece C: This piece forms the canopy of the umbrella. It has two notches along one edge and a thin, hook-shaped handle that projects from the center.

In addition to these umbrella-shaped pieces, there are three other components that do not resemble umbrellas:

  • Piece B: This piece resembles the numeral "3" or the letter "E" when held upright. It has a T-shaped indentation along one side and a square cutout on the other.

  • Pieces D and E: These pieces have a rounded dome shape with a square hole cut through them. Piece D has one notch along the edge and two differently sized bumps on the back. Piece E has a longer notch along the edge and two identically sized bumps on the back.

  • Piece F: This piece looks like the numeral "1" when held upright. It has a long, thin shape with a square cutout at the top and a rounded end on the bottom.

Part 2: Assembling the Ball

  1. Connect Piece A to Piece B: Slide Piece A into the T-shaped indentation on Piece B, making sure that the notches on Piece A align with the corners of the square cutout on Piece B.

  2. Attach Piece C: Insert the stem of Piece C into the notch on the combined Piece A and B. Rotate Piece C until the hooks on its handle engage with the notches on Piece A.

  3. Fit Piece D onto Piece A: Align the square cutouts and notches on Piece D with those on Piece A. Slide Piece D onto Piece A until it fits snugly.

  4. Slide Piece E into Place: Line up the square cutout and notch on Piece E with the corresponding features on the other pieces. Slide Piece E into place until it fits securely.

  5. Insert Piece F: Pass the square cutout on Piece F through the hole in the assembled ball. Push Piece F through until it snaps into place, connecting the ball together.

Part 3: Disassembling the Ball

  1. Remove Piece F: Push inward on one of the four squares on the ball until the piece pops outward. This is Piece F.

  2. Extract Piece E: Insert your finger into the square opening where Piece F was removed and push against the inner wall of the ball. Piece E will come loose and can be removed.

  3. Separate Pieces B and C: Press on the remaining squares of the ball until two pieces come free: Pieces B and C.

  4. Detach Pieces A and D: Push on the final square of the ball to detach the last two pieces: Pieces A and D.
