Making Marijuana Butter: A Comprehensive Guide

Introduction:

Marijuana butter, also known as cannabutter, is a versatile ingredient that allows you to enjoy the psychoactive effects of cannabis without the need for smoking. It forms the base for various delectable edibles, such as cookies, brownies, and tea, offering a convenient and discreet way to medicate or simply unwind.

Step 1: Decarboxylation - Unlocking the Potency

Before using cannabis in butter, it must undergo decarboxylation, a process where heat activates its psychoactive compounds. Without this step, the butter will remain inactive and fail to produce the desired effects.

To decarboxylate:

  • Preheat your oven to 240°F (115°C).
  • Grind the cannabis buds or flower into small pieces.
  • Spread the ground cannabis evenly on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  • Bake for 40 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent burning.
  • Allow the decarboxylated cannabis to cool thoroughly before proceeding.

Step 2: Grinding - Enhancing Infusibility

Finely grinding the decarboxylated cannabis increases its surface area, allowing for better absorption into the butter. Use a grinder to break down the cannabis into a fine powder.

Step 3: Butter Preparation - A Solid Base

In a large slow cooker or crock pot, combine unsalted butter and water in a 1:1 ratio. The butter acts as a solvent for extracting the cannabinoids from the cannabis, while the water prevents the butter from burning.

Step 4: Cannabis Addition - Infusing the Butter

Stir in the ground decarboxylated cannabis into the melted butter mixture. Ensure it is evenly distributed throughout the liquid.

Step 5: The Art of Cooking - Time and Heat for Potency

The cooking time determines the potency of the butter. For milder effects suitable for beginners or those with low tolerance, cook for 2-4 hours. For stronger butter, cook for up to 24 hours, stirring occasionally.

Low Heat: The ideal temperature is between 180-200°F (82-93°C). Avoid boiling or overheating, as these extreme temperatures can degrade the cannabinoids.

Step 6: Straining - Separating the Solids

Once the cooking time is complete, remove the slow cooker from heat. Pour the mixture through a cheesecloth-lined strainer into a clean bowl. This step filters out any excess plant material.

Step 7: Cooling and Storage - Preserving the Cannabinoids

Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and refrigerate the strained butter for at least 3 hours. This allows the butter to solidify and separate from any remaining water.

  • Refrigeration: Store the solidified butter in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 2 weeks.
  • Freezing: For longer storage, wrap the butter securely and freeze for up to 3 months.

Benefits of Marijuana Butter:

  • Smokeless Ingestion: Enjoy the benefits of cannabis without the harmful effects of smoking.
  • Versatile Culinary Uses: Create a wide range of delicious and potent edibles, including cookies, brownies, tea, and much more.
  • Beginner-Friendly: A suitable option for those new to cannabis or seeking milder effects.
  • Extended Shelf Life: Properly stored butter can maintain its potency for extended periods, offering convenience and longevity.

Dosage:

The potency of marijuana butter can vary depending on the cannabis strain, decarboxylation time, and cooking duration. Start with a small amount and gradually increase the dosage until you achieve the desired effects. It is recommended to wait at least 1-2 hours after ingestion before consuming more, as the effects take some time to manifest.
