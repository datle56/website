Master the Art of Signing Out of Viber on PC or Mac: A Comprehensive Guide with Step-by-Step Visual Aids


Unplug from Viber on Your Desktop: A Two-Step Guide

Whether you're craving a digital detox or need to remove the temptation of constant notifications, deactivating Viber on your computer is a simple yet effective solution. Follow our two-method approach, tailored for both Mac and Windows users, to swiftly disconnect and find respite from the digital hubbub.


Method 1: Deactivating Viber on Mac

  1. Launch Viber on your Mac: Begin the process by opening the Viber application from your desktop or through the Applications folder.

  2. Access Settings: In the top right corner of the Viber window, you will find a small gear icon. Click on this icon to open the Settings menu.

  3. Select Privacy: Within the Settings menu, navigate to the left-hand sidebar and select the "Privacy" tab. This tab contains settings related to your privacy and data management within Viber.

  4. Locate Deactivation Button: Scroll down to the bottom of the Privacy tab until you find the section labeled "Deactivate Viber on Desktop." Below this label, you will see a purple "Deactivate" button.

  5. Confirm Deactivation: Clicking the "Deactivate" button will display a pop-up window asking you to confirm your choice. Carefully read the message and ensure you wish to proceed with deactivating Viber on your Mac.

  6. Complete Deactivation: Once you have confirmed your choice, click the "OK" button in the pop-up window. Viber will now proceed to deactivate, and once complete, it will display a message indicating that the account has been successfully deactivated.

Method 2: Deactivating Viber on Windows

  1. Open Viber on Windows: To start, open the Viber desktop application on your Windows computer. You can do this by clicking on the Viber icon in your Start Menu or searching for it in the search bar.

  2. Access Menu: In the top left corner of the Viber window, you will find three horizontal lines forming an icon. Click on this icon to open the main menu of the application.

  3. Select Privacy Settings: Within the menu, hover over the "Tools" option and select "Privacy settings" from the drop-down menu. This will open a separate window containing privacy-related settings.

  4. Locate Deactivation Button: At the bottom of the Privacy settings window, you will find a section labeled "Account." Within this section, click on the purple "Deactivate" button to initiate the deactivation process.

  5. Confirm Deactivation: Clicking the "Deactivate" button will trigger a pop-up window asking you to verify your intent. Read the message carefully and ensure you are ready to deactivate Viber on your Windows computer.

  6. Complete Deactivation: Once you have confirmed your decision, click the "OK" button in the pop-up window. Viber will initiate the deactivation process, and upon completion, you will see a message confirming that the account has been deactivated successfully.

Important Note:

It is essential to remember that deactivating Viber on your computer only removes data and settings from the desktop application. Your Viber conversations, contacts, and other information will still be accessible and active on your mobile devices. If you wish to deactivate Viber completely, you will need to do so from your mobile device by following the corresponding steps for iOS or Android.


Conclusion:

Now that you know how to deactivate Viber on both Mac and Windows, you can easily manage your Viber presence on all devices. This can be useful for maintaining privacy or if you're experiencing issues with the desktop app. Remember that deactivating Viber only affects the desktop version, so your conversations and data will remain accessible on your mobile devices. Whether you're temporarily stepping away or permanently leaving the platform, deactivating Viber is a quick and convenient way to clear your digital footprint and streamline your communication experience.
