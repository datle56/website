Unveiling the Art of Crafting a Delicate Kusudama Flower: An Illustrated Guide


Unfold the Art of Origami with an Enchanting Kusudama Flower

Step into the magical realm of paper artistry with our comprehensive guide to crafting a captivating Origami Kusudama flower. Materials:

  • 5 square pieces of origami paper (approx. 6x6 inches)
  • Glue
  • Paperclips

Step-by-Step Instructions:

Step 1: Crease the Paper

  • Begin with a square piece of origami paper.
  • Fold the paper diagonally in half from one corner to the opposite corner, forming a triangle.
  • Unfold the paper and repeat the diagonal fold from the other two corners to create four intersecting creases.

Step 2: Form the Basic Cube

  • Fold the bottom two corners of the triangle up to meet the center point, forming a square.
  • Unfold the previous folds halfway, aligning the edges to create a diamond shape with two triangular flaps on either side.

Step 3: Tuck the Triangles

  • Fold the triangular point on one side into the shape below, tucking it under the overlapping flap.
  • Repeat on the other side, tucking the opposite triangular point inward.

Step 4: Refold the Cube

  • Fold the corners inward to form a square shape again.
  • Glue the top edges of the flaps that were previously tucked inward.

Step 5: Connect the Flaps

  • Bring the two opposite flaps together and secure them with glue and paperclips.
  • Repeat with the remaining two opposite flaps.

Step 6: Form the Petals

  • Create four more basic cube units following steps 1-5.
  • Apply a thin layer of glue to the seams of each unit.

Step 7: Assemble the Flower

  • Join the five units together in a circular pattern, with the glued edges facing outwards.
  • Form the petals by connecting the corners of each unit to create a star shape.
  • Secure the petals with paperclips.

Step 8: Secure and Dry

  • Allow the glue to dry completely before removing the paperclips.
  • If desired, you can add embellishments such as beads or glitter to customize your kusudama flower.
