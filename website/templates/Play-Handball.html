Part 1: Team Handball

Court and Field Players:

  • Team handball is played on a rectangular court measuring 40 x 20 meters (131.2 x 65.6 feet). The court is divided into two halves by a center line, and a goal is positioned at each end.
  • The goal area is a trapezoidal zone 6 meters (19.7 feet) in the center and 3 meters (9.8 feet) wide at the sides, where only the goalkeeper may stand.
  • Field players are not allowed to touch the ball below the knee or hold it for more than three seconds without dribbling or passing.
  • Players can dribble the ball between their legs and take up to three steps without dribbling.

Goal and Scoring:

  • The objective of the game is to score goals by throwing the ball into the opposing team's goal.
  • A goal is scored when the entire ball crosses the goal line between the goalposts and below the crossbar.
  • Teams score a point for each goal scored.

Gameplay:

  • A game of team handball consists of two 30-minute halves, with a 10-minute break in between.
  • The game begins with a throw-off by the referee, who tosses the ball in the air at the center line.
  • The ball is in play when a player from either team touches it.
  • The ball goes out of play if it leaves the playing area, in which case a throw-in is awarded to the team that did not touch it last.
  • If the ball goes out of play over the side or end lines, the team that scored the last goal receives possession.

Penalties and Violations:

  • Violations in team handball include holding, tripping, pushing, and unsportsmanlike conduct.
  • Penalties for violations can range from warnings to suspensions or even exclusion from the game.
  • Free throws are awarded for minor violations, while penalty throws are given for more serious offenses.

Part 2: Singles or Doubles Handball

Game Variations:

  • There are two main variations of handball: Chinese and American.
  • Chinese handball, also known as "wall handball," involves bouncing the ball before hitting the wall, while American handball, also known as "four-wall handball," requires one continuous hit.

Rules and Scoring:

  • Games in both Chinese and American handball are typically played to 7 points.
  • In Chinese handball, players serve by hitting the ball with a single bounce (known as "fa die") before it touches the wall.
  • In American handball, players serve by hitting the ball without a bounce directly at the front wall.
  • The player who fails to return the ball after two bounces (or one bounce in "King's Handball") loses their turn.
  • In Chinese handball, players must hit the ball into the front wall on their first bounce, while in American handball, players can hit the ball off any wall.
  • Points are scored when an opponent fails to return the ball or commits a fault, such as hitting the ball out of bounds or hitting the ball with their feet or legs.
