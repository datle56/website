How to Download YouTube Videos on Android Without Play Store

Want to save your favorite YouTube videos on your Android device for offline viewing? But what if you don't want to use Google Play Store? Don't worry, we've got you covered! In this guide, we'll show you two easy methods to download YouTube videos using third-party apps that you can install without the Play Store. Get ready to effortlessly save your online video favorites on your Android device!


Method 1: InsTube

Step 1: Enable App Installation from Unknown Sources

  • Open your Android device's "Settings" and navigate to "Security" or "Privacy."
  • Look for an option labeled "Install Unknown Apps" or "Allow App Installation from Unknown Sources."
  • Toggle the switch to "Allow" or "Enable." This allows your device to install apps from sources other than the Google Play Store.

Step 2: Download InsTube from the Official Website

  • Visit InsTube's official website (https://www.instube.com/) using your device's browser.
  • Click on the "Download" button.

Step 3: Install InsTube

  • Once the APK file has downloaded, locate it in your device's "Downloads" folder.
  • Tap on the APK file to start the installation process.
  • Follow the on-screen prompts to complete the installation.

Step 4: Find the Video to Download

  • Open InsTube on your device.
  • Use the search bar or browse through the categories to find the video you want to download.

Step 5: Download the Video

  • When you find the desired video, tap on it.
  • Click the "Download" button below the video.
  • Select the "M4A" format and choose the desired quality from the drop-down list.
  • The video will now start downloading.

Method 2: Tubemate

Step 1: Enable App Installation from Unknown Sources

  • Open your Android device's "Settings" and navigate to "Security" or "Privacy."
  • Look for an option labeled "Install Unknown Apps" or "Allow App Installation from Unknown Sources."
  • Toggle the switch to "Allow" or "Enable." This allows your device to install apps from sources other than the Google Play Store.

Step 2: Download Tubemate from the Official Website

  • Visit Tubemate's official website (https://www.tubemate.net/) using your device's browser.
  • Click on the "Download Tubemate" button.

Step 3: Install Tubemate

  • Once the APK file has downloaded, locate it in your device's "Downloads" folder.
  • Tap on the APK file to start the installation process.
  • Follow the on-screen prompts to complete the installation.

Step 4: Find the Video on YouTube

  • Open the YouTube app on your device.
  • Search for the video you want to download.

Step 5: Download the Video

  • Once you find the desired video, tap on the "Share" button below it.
  • Select "Download" from the list of options.
  • Tubemate will automatically open and start downloading the video.
  • Select the desired resolution from the options provided.

Conclusion:

With these two user-friendly methods, you can effortlessly download YouTube videos in MP3 or MP4 formats. InsTube provides a seamless process with its direct download button, while Tubemate allows you to select from a range of resolutions to suit your needs. Whether you prefer high-quality downloads for offline viewing or lightweight audio files for background listening, these tools empower you to capture your favorite YouTube content with just a few simple steps.
