How to Play Nintendo Switch Games on PC

The Nintendo Switch is a fantastic console for portable gaming, but what if you want to enjoy your Switch games on a larger screen or with a more comfortable setup? There are three main methods for playing Nintendo Switch games on PC: using a capture card, using a PC monitor as a TV, or transferring files from your Switch to your PC.

Method 1: Using a Capture Card

Materials:

  • Capture card
  • HDMI cables
  • Video capture software

Steps:

  1. Connect your Switch to the capture card: Connect one end of an HDMI cable to the HDMI output of your Switch dock and the other end to the input of the capture card.
  2. Connect the capture card to your PC: Connect the capture card to your PC using an HDMI cable.
  3. Install video capture software: Install video capture software on your PC, such as OBS Studio or XSplit Gamecaster. This software will allow you to display the Switch screen on your PC monitor.

Method 2: Using PC Monitor as TV

Materials:

  • HDMI cable

Steps:

  1. Connect your Switch to your PC monitor: Connect an HDMI cable from the HDMI output of your Switch dock to the HDMI input of your PC monitor.
  2. Turn on your Switch: Turn on your Nintendo Switch and select the input source on your PC monitor that corresponds to your HDMI connection.
  3. Use your controller: You can now use your wireless Switch controller to control your Switch on the monitor.

Method 3: Transferring Files from Switch to PC

Materials:

  • USB-C cable

Steps:

  1. Connect your Switch to your PC: Connect your Switch to your PC using a USB-C cable.
  2. Go to Switch Settings: On your Nintendo Switch, navigate to the "System Settings" menu.
  3. Select Data Management: Select "Data Management" from the System Settings menu.
  4. Choose Screenshots or Videos: Select either "Screenshots" or "Videos" to transfer.
  5. Transfer Files: Your PC will detect the Switch and allow you to copy or move the files to your computer.

Additional Notes:

  • Method 1 (using a capture card) is the best option for high-quality gameplay with minimal latency.
  • Method 2 (using a PC monitor as a TV) is a convenient option for playing Switch games on a larger screen without the need for additional hardware.
  • Method 3 (transferring files) is useful for sharing screenshots or videos from your Switch to your PC.
  • Keep in mind that you cannot actually play Switch games on your PC using these methods. The methods described here allow you to display the Switch screen on your PC or transfer files from the Switch to your PC.
