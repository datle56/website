Step-by-Step Guide to Successful Pig Farming: 14 Essential Steps Illustrated


Securing a Haven for Your Precious Porkers

Welcome, pig enthusiasts and aspiring homesteaders! Embark on an adventure into the world of pigkeeping, where you'll uncover the secrets to providing a cozy and nurturing environment for your prized porkers. From crafting a secure pigpen to raising healthy and happy pigs, this comprehensive guide will arm you with the knowledge you need to embark on a fulfilling journey as a pig parent.


Housing Your Pigs

Creating a Safe and Comfortable Pigpen

Pigs require a dedicated space to thrive, known as a pigpen. This enclosure should provide a safe, dry, and comfortable environment that meets their specific needs.

  • Size: Plan for approximately 50 square feet of space per pig to ensure ample room for movement and comfort. A larger pen is always preferable.
  • Fencing: Surround the pen with sturdy wire fencing of at least 4 feet in height. The fencing should be securely buried to prevent pigs from digging underneath and escaping.
  • Shelter: Install a covered shelter that provides protection from the elements. Provide around 15-20 square feet of shade per pig for hot and rainy conditions. Ensure the shelter is well-ventilated and has a solid floor to keep pigs dry and clean.
  • Mud Wallow: Pigs enjoy wallowing in mud to cool down and remove parasites. Consider creating a designated mud wallow area within the pen, especially in warmer climates.

Managing Your Pig Herd

Acquiring Healthy Piglets

  • Source piglets from reputable breeders with a proven track record of health and quality.
  • Choose piglets that are active, have a good appetite, and show no signs of illness.

Water and Nutrition

  • Provide access to clean, fresh water at all times. Pigs require approximately 2-4 gallons of water per pig per day.
  • Feed pigs a balanced diet consisting of commercially prepared feeds specifically formulated for their age and weight.
  • Supplementation with fruits, vegetables, meat scraps, and garden clippings can enhance nutrition, but avoid using these as the primary food source.

Health and Parasite Control

  • Prevent internal parasites by worming pigs regularly with anthelmintics prescribed by a veterinarian.
  • Monitor pigs for signs of illness and seek veterinary attention promptly if any issues arise.

Marketing and Sale

  • Determine the target market weight for your pigs, typically between 200-250 pounds, based on their breed and health.
  • Sell pigs to local markets, processors, or other farmers.
  • Handle pigs with caution during sorting and transportation. Use sorting boards to gently guide them back into pens, avoiding forceful movements.

Safety Precautions

  • While pigs are generally docile animals, they can become aggressive or injured if mishandled.
  • Always approach pigs with caution and use appropriate handling techniques.
  • Be aware that pigs can carry diseases, so practice good hygiene and wear protective clothing when working with them.

Conclusion

Providing a comfortable and safe environment for your pigs is essential for their well-being and productivity. By adhering to the guidelines outlined above, you can create an optimal housing and raising system that will ensure the health and happiness of your pigs while maximizing their growth potential. Remember to prioritize sanitation, proper nutrition, and regular veterinary care to maintain a thriving piggery.
