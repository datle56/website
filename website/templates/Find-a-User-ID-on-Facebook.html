How to Find Your Facebook Profile and ID

Understanding your Facebook profile and ID is crucial for managing your account, sharing content, and maintaining privacy. Let's delve into the steps to find both your username and User ID on desktop and mobile devices.

Finding Your Username (Profile URL)

Desktop:

  1. Click on your profile picture located in the top right corner of the Facebook homepage.
  2. Navigate to the 'Settings & Privacy' menu and select 'Settings'.
  3. Click on 'Accounts Center' and choose 'Profiles'.
  4. Select 'Your Facebook Account' and then 'Username'.

Mobile:

  1. Tap the menu icon (three horizontal lines) located at the bottom-right corner of the Facebook app (for iPhone/iPad users) or the top-right corner (Android users).
  2. Scroll down and select 'Settings & Privacy', followed by 'Settings'.
  3. Tap on 'Accounts Center' and choose 'Your Facebook Account'.
  4. Select 'Username'.

Finding Your User ID (for Apps and Games)

Desktop:

  1. Visit https://www.facebook.com in your browser.
  2. Click on your profile icon.
  3. Go to the 'Settings & Privacy' menu and select 'Settings'.
  4. Choose 'Apps and Websites' and click on 'View and Edit'.
  5. Locate your User ID at the bottom of the page.

Mobile:

  1. Open the Facebook app on your mobile device.
  2. Tap the menu icon (three horizontal lines) and select 'Settings & Privacy'.
  3. Go to 'Settings' and choose 'Apps and Websites'.
  4. Select an app you're connected to.
  5. Scroll down to the bottom of the page to find your User ID.

Considerations

  • Username: The username is the customizable component of your Facebook profile URL. It allows you to create a more personalized and identifiable URL for sharing.
  • User ID: The User ID is a unique numerical identifier assigned to your account. It's primarily used by apps and games connected to your profile.
  • Privacy: It's important to exercise caution when sharing your User ID, as it can be used to access certain publicly available information on your profile.

Conclusion:

With these simple steps, you can easily locate your Facebook profile URL and User ID. Your username serves as an accessible and memorable way for others to find your profile, while your User ID plays a vital role in app integrations and account security.

Remember, your User ID is a sensitive piece of information that should be used with caution. Keep it secure to safeguard the privacy of your Facebook account and personal data.
