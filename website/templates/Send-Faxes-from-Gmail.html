Discover the Effortless Way to Fax Documents Directly from Your Gmail Inbox


Faxing in the Digital Age: Unlocking Convenience with Online Fax Services

In today's fast-paced world, where technology reigns supreme, the ability to send and receive faxes has taken an innovative leap forward. Gone are the days of bulky fax machines and convoluted processes. Enter online fax services – the seamless and cost-effective solution for your faxing needs. Dive into this comprehensive guide as we unveil two foolproof methods to send faxes online using WiseFax and Gmail. Let's embrace the convenience and power of digital faxing!


Method 1: WiseFax

WiseFax is a convenient and reliable online fax service that allows you to send faxes directly from your Google Chrome browser. Here's a detailed guide on how to use WiseFax:

1. Install the WiseFax Extension:

  • Visit the Google Chrome Web Store and search for "WiseFax."
  • Click the "Install" button and follow the on-screen prompts.

2. Upload Your Document:

  • Once the extension is installed, open Google Chrome and click the WiseFax icon in the browser toolbar.
  • Select "Fax Document" and choose the file you want to fax.
  • Supported file formats include PDF, DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX, PPT, PPTX, TXT, and JPG.

3. Enter the Recipient's Fax Number:

  • In the "Recipient Fax Number" field, enter the fax number of the recipient, including the country and area code.

4. Sign in with Google and Purchase Fax Credits:

  • Click the "Sign In" button and choose "Google" as your sign-in method.
  • Follow the Google account authorization process.
  • Select the number of fax credits you need to purchase. Fax credits are used to pay for sending faxes.

5. Enter Your Payment Information:

  • Enter your credit card or PayPal information to complete the purchase.

6. Send Your Fax:

  • Click the "Send Fax" button.
  • WiseFax will process your fax and send it to the recipient.
  • You will receive an email notification once the fax has been sent successfully.

Method 2: Gmail

Gmail, the popular email service from Google, also offers an online faxing service. While you need to create a separate fax account with a third-party provider, you can send faxes directly from your Gmail inbox.

1. Create an Online Fax Account:

  • Choose a reputable online fax service provider that offers free trials.
  • Sign up for an account and provide your personal information and payment details.

2. Open Gmail on a Computer:

  • Access your Gmail account through a web browser on a computer. Using the Gmail mobile app is not supported for faxing.

3. Enter the Fax Number and Extension:

  • In the "To" field, enter the recipient's fax number followed by the appropriate extension.
  • Different fax service providers use different extensions. Check with your provider for the correct extension to use.

4. Add a Cover Page (Optional):

  • If you want to include a cover page, click the "Attach files" icon and select "Compose and attach cover page."
  • Enter your cover page text and format it as desired.

5. Upload Your Document(s):

  • Click the "Attach files" icon and select the documents you want to fax.
  • You can attach multiple documents as one fax.

6. Send Your Fax:

  • Review the details of your fax, including the recipient's fax number, cover page (if any), and attached documents.
  • Click the "Send" button to transmit your fax.
  • You will receive an email notification once the fax has been sent successfully.

Conclusion: Choosing the Right Method

When deciding between WiseFax and Gmail for your faxing needs, consider the following:

  • Ease of Use: WiseFax offers a user-friendly interface and a simple setup process, making it a great option for beginners. Gmail requires setting up an external fax account, which may require additional steps.

  • Cost: WiseFax requires purchasing fax credits, while Gmail's faxing feature may be included in some paid plans or require a separate purchase. Determine the frequency and volume of your faxing needs to find the most cost-effective solution.

  • Features: WiseFax offers additional features such as scheduled faxing, fax receipts, and international faxing. Gmail's faxing feature is more limited but may suffice for basic faxing needs.

Ultimately, the best method for you will depend on your specific requirements. If you value simplicity and a seamless experience, WiseFax is a great choice. If you prefer the integration of your faxing service with your email and have less need for advanced features, Gmail's faxing feature may be sufficient.
