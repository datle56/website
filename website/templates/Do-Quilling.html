Get started with quilling using this comprehensive guide that provides step-by-step instructions (with helpful images) for creating beautiful paper art.


Embark on a paper-crafting adventure with the enchanting art of quilling! In this comprehensive guide, we'll demystify the tools and techniques to help you create stunning, intricate designs that will leave you mesmerized. Get ready to transform ordinary paper into extraordinary art as we delve into the captivating world of quilling.


Part 1: Getting Started

Paper Curling Tools:

  • Slotted Tool:

    • A beginner-friendly tool with a slotted opening.
    • Creates a small crease in the paper when rolled, making it ideal for simple curves.

  • Needle Tool:

    • A more advanced tool, requiring some dexterity.
    • Creates precise and intricate spirals without any creases.
    • Allows for a wide range of curves and designs.

Quilling Strips:

  • Cutting Strips:
    • Cut strips from colorful, acid-free paper using a sharp craft knife or scissors.
    • Paper weight between 80 and 120 gsm is suitable for quilling.
  • Purchasing Strips:
    • Prefabricated quilling strips are available in various widths (1/16 inch to 1/8 inch) and colors.
    • These strips are typically easier to handle and provide consistent results.

Part 2: Creating Your Design

Coiling:

  • Slotted Tool Coiling:
    • Insert the end of a paper strip into the slot and roll it tightly against the ridge.
    • Continue rolling until you reach the desired size.
  • Needle Tool Coiling:
    • Wrap the paper strip around the needle, keeping it taut.
    • Roll the paper over the needle, creating a tight coil.

Gluing:

  • Applying Glue:
    • Use a small amount of acid-free liquid glue or a glue stick.
    • Apply the glue only to the end of the coil where it overlaps.
  • Holding:
    • Hold the coil together gently for about 20 seconds to allow the glue to set.

Shaping:

  • Simple Shapes:
    • Pinch the coil gently to create basic shapes like teardrops, triangles, and squares.
  • Complex Shapes:
    • Use your fingers, tweezers, or a shaping tool to mold intricate shapes (e.g., flower petals, animal heads).

Assembly:

  • Component Assembly:
    • Arrange the shaped coils according to your design pattern.
  • Gluing:
    • Apply a small amount of glue to the base of the coils and carefully attach them together.
    • Use a light touch to prevent warping or distortion.
  • Finishing:
    • Allow the assembly to dry completely before handling or displaying it.

Tips:

  • Experiment with different quilling techniques and designs to explore your creativity.
  • Use a variety of tools and materials, such as beads, sequins, and wire, to enhance your quilling creations.
  • Practice regularly to improve your accuracy and efficiency in quilling.
  • Be patient and enjoy the process of creating beautiful and intricate paper art.
