Reinvigorate Your Blinds: A Step-by-Step Guide to Replace Worn Strings

Tired of malfunctioning blinds and unsightly tangled strings? Our comprehensive guide will empower you to restore the functionality and aesthetic appeal of your window coverings with ease. Dive into this step-by-step journey and learn the techniques to revitalize your blinds in just a few simple steps. Follow our precise instructions on measuring, purchasing, replacing, and securing the strings, ensuring a seamless and satisfying outcome that will brighten up your home.


Part 1: Measuring and Buying String

  • Measure the blind's length: Use a measuring tape or ruler to determine the exact length of the blind from the top of the headrail (the bar that holds the blind fabric) to the bottom of the hem.
  • Measure the blind's width: Measure the width of the blind from side to side.
  • Add excess: To ensure ample string for threading and securing, add 20-40 inches to both the length and width measurements. For example, if the blind measures 36 inches in length and 24 inches in width, purchase a blind cord kit with at least 56 inches of string in length and 44 inches of string in width.
  • Purchase a blind cord kit: Select a cord kit that includes enough string to cover the calculated measurements and contains all necessary components, such as safety washers and tassels. The string should be made of a durable material like polyester or nylon for longevity.

Part 2: Replacing the Old String

  • Remove the safety washers: Locate the safety washers at the bottom of the blind, where the lift strings exit. Use a pair of pliers or your fingers to carefully remove the washers.
  • Pull out the lift strings: Gently pull out the old lift strings from the blind.
  • Melt the old and new strings: Use a lighter to gently melt the ends of the old and new strings together. Be careful not to burn yourself or melt too much string.
  • Thread the new string: Use the old string as a guide to thread the new string through the holes or channels in the blind. Start by threading one end of the string through the top hole and continue threading it through the remaining holes in a serpentine pattern.
  • Secure the new string: Once the new string is threaded through, secure it by tying a knot at the end to prevent it from sliding out. Ensure the knot is small enough to fit through the holes.

Part 3: Securing the Cords

  • Reinstall the safety washers: Place the safety washers back onto the bottom of the blind, securing the strings. This will prevent them from slipping out of the holes.
  • Cut the lift cords: Cut the lift cords to the desired length, ensuring they are long enough to reach the tassels comfortably.
  • Add tassels: Attach tassels to the ends of the lift cords for ease of use. Tassels provide a grippable handle and enhance the aesthetic appeal of the blind.

Conclusion:

With these simple steps, you have successfully replaced the strings on your blinds, restoring their functionality and aesthetic appeal. By taking the time to properly measure, purchase, and install the strings, you have ensured that your blinds will operate smoothly and last for years to come. Remember, regular maintenance, such as cleaning and inspecting the strings, can further prolong the lifespan of your blinds. Congratulations on completing this DIY project and adding a touch of convenience and style to your home!
