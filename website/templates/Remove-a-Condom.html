How to Remove a Condom Safely and Effectively

Condoms are an essential part of safe sex and play a crucial role in preventing both pregnancy and sexually transmitted infections (STIs). Removing a condom properly is just as important as putting it on correctly to ensure effectiveness and minimize the risk of mishap.

Step-by-Step Guide to Condom Removal

For External (Male) Condoms:

  1. Maintain Erection: Remove the condom while your penis is still erect to prevent it from slipping off or tearing.

  2. Secure the Base: Grasp the base of the condom firmly between your thumb and forefinger to prevent it from coming off prematurely.

  3. Roll Downward: Carefully roll the condom down towards the base of your penis, avoiding pulling on the tip which may cause it to tear.

  4. Inspect and Dispose: Once the condom is completely removed, inspect it for any tears or holes. Dispose of the used condom in a trash receptacle, preferably wrapped in a tissue or paper towel for discretion.

For Internal (Female) Condoms:

  1. Recline: Stay in a lying or reclining position to facilitate condom removal.

  2. Twist and Remove: Hold the outer ring of the female condom and gently twist it several times to loosen it. Carefully remove the condom by slowly pulling it out while maintaining a twist.

  3. Check for Integrity: Inspect the condom for any holes or tears. If any abnormalities are detected, discard the condom immediately.

  4. Dispose Properly: Dispose of the used female condom in a trash bin, preferably wrapped in a tissue or paper towel for privacy.

Additional Important Tips:

  1. Hand Hygiene: Always wash your hands thoroughly with soap and water before and after handling condoms.

  2. Avoid Reuse: Never attempt to reuse a condom, even if it appears intact. Each condom is designed for single-use only.

  3. Emergency Measures: In the event of a condom breaking or falling off during sexual activity, seek emergency contraception (such as Plan B) as soon as possible. Additionally, get tested for STIs to rule out any potential infections.

  4. Stuck Condom: If a female condom becomes stuck inside the body, try to remain calm and carefully push down as if having a bowel movement to create pressure. Avoid using excessive force or sharp objects to retrieve the condom. If you are unable to remove it yourself, seek medical assistance promptly.
