Bananagrams: Simplified Rules

Traditional Rules:

  • Distribution of Tiles: Each player receives a number of tiles based on the number of players:

    • 2 players: 21 tiles
    • 3 players: 15 tiles
    • 4 players: 12 tiles
    • 5-6 players: 11 tiles
    • 7-8 players: 10 tiles

  • Game Objective: Players simultaneously create a grid of interlocking words using all of their tiles.

  • Word Formation:

    • Words can be formed in any direction, horizontal, vertical, or diagonal.
    • Words must interlock with at least one other word on the board.
    • Interlocking words must share at least one letter.

  • Tile Exchange:

    • Peeling: If a player cannot form a word on their turn, they can say "Peel" and draw a new tile from the central pile.
    • Dumping: Players can exchange one tile for three new tiles by saying "Dump." This can only be done once per game.

  • End of Game and Victory:

    • The game ends when the central pile has fewer tiles than the number of players.
    • The first player to use all of their tiles wins by shouting "Bananas!"

Alternate Rules:

  • All Tiles Distributed:

    • Instead of distributing tiles based on the number of players, all tiles are divided equally among players.
    • The first player to use all of their tiles wins.

  • No Peeling:

    • Players are given 21 tiles and must use them all without peeling.
    • The first player to complete their grid wins.

  • Solitaire:

    • Play the game alone and race to complete a grid using all tiles within a time limit.

  • Cooperative:

    • Instead of playing competitively, players work together to create a single interlocking grid using all tiles.
