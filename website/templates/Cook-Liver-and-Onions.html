Easy Liver and Onions Recipe: A Savory Delight

Indulge in the timeless classic of liver and onions, a culinary masterpiece that combines the richness of liver with the aromatic sweetness of onions. This simple yet satisfying dish is a testament to the power of simple ingredients when combined with culinary expertise.

Ingredients:

  • Flour: 1/2 cup, used to dredge the liver and prevent sticking
  • Black pepper: 1 teaspoon, adding a touch of spice and warmth
  • Salt: 1/2 teaspoon, enhancing the flavors of the liver and onions
  • Olive oil: 3 tablespoons, providing a medium for browning and flavorful sautéing
  • Liver: 1 1/2 pounds of your preferred choice (beef, chicken, or lamb), sliced into 1/2-inch thick slices
  • Onions: 2 medium-sized onions, thinly sliced, adding sweetness and aroma
  • Beef or chicken broth: 1 cup, creating a savory and flavorful gravy

Instructions:

  1. Prepare the liver: Remove the outer membrane from the liver and slice it into 1/2-inch thick pieces. This thickness ensures even cooking and a tender texture.

  2. Season and dredge the liver: In a shallow dish, combine the flour, black pepper, and salt. Toss the liver slices in the seasoned flour, ensuring they are evenly coated. This step helps to tenderize the liver and prevent it from sticking to the pan during cooking.

  3. Brown the liver: Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Once the oil is shimmering, carefully place the seasoned liver slices into the pan. Cook for approximately 2-4 minutes per side, until they have browned nicely. Avoid overcooking at this stage, as the liver should remain tender.

  4. Sauté the onions: Remove the browned liver slices from the pan and set them aside on a plate. In the same skillet, add the sliced onions and stir occasionally until they have softened and become translucent. This process can take about 5-7 minutes.

  5. Reintroduce the liver and add broth: Return the browned liver slices to the skillet with the onions. Pour in the beef or chicken broth, ensuring it covers the bottom of the pan. Bring the mixture to a simmer.

  6. Simmer until tender: Cover the skillet and reduce the heat to low. Simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until the onions are tender and a flavorful gravy has formed. Stir occasionally to ensure even cooking.

  7. Serve and enjoy: Once cooked, transfer the liver and onions to a serving dish. Garnish with fresh parsley or chives for added color and flavor. Serve immediately, with or without mashed potatoes. The creamy texture of mashed potatoes complements the savory richness of the liver and onions perfectly.

Tips:

  • Choosing your liver: Opt for fresh, high-quality liver for the best results. Choose liver that is free of any bruising or discoloration.
  • Using a sharp knife: Employ a sharp knife to cut the liver slices to avoid tearing. Clean cuts ensure even cooking and a more appetizing presentation.
  • Dredging the liver: Make sure the liver is evenly coated with the seasoned flour. This helps to tenderize the meat and prevent it from sticking to the pan during cooking.
  • Simmering time: Adjust the simmering time depending on the desired tenderness of the liver. For a softer texture, cook for a longer duration.
  • Serving suggestions: Liver and onions can be paired with a variety of side dishes. Mashed potatoes are a traditional accompaniment, but you can also try other options such as roasted vegetables, rice pilaf, or crusty bread.
