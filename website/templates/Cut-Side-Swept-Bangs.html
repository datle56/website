Master the Art of Side-Swept Bang Trim: An At-Home Guide with 3 Simple Methods


Unlock a New You with Perfect Side Bangs: A Step-by-Step Guide

Whether you're craving a chic makeover or simply seeking to freshen up your look, side bangs are an effortlessly stylish choice. With a few simple snips, you can transform your appearance and unleash your inner fashion icon.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll walk you through the art of cutting side bangs with scissors or a razor, tailored to suit different hair textures. Whether your tresses are straight and sleek, curly and voluminous, or somewhere in between, we'll provide expert tips and step-by-step instructions to guide you every step of the way.

From strategic sectioning to skillful scissorwork, we'll reveal the secrets to creating perfectly angled bangs that complement your facial features and enhance your overall style. So, grab your hair tools and let's embark on a journey to embrace the transformative power of side bangs!


Cutting Side Bangs with Scissors

Step 1: Sectioning the Hair

  • Begin by wetting your hair and combing it through to remove any tangles.
  • Using a comb or brush, create a triangle-shaped section at the front of your hair. This triangle should extend from the center of your forehead to the outer edges of your eyebrows.
  • Divide the triangle into two sections: the upper section and the lower section. Secure the upper section with a hair clip or elastic band.

Step 2: Cutting the Lower Section

  • Take the lower section of hair and pinch it between your thumb and index finger.
  • Pull the hair down to the desired length, using a reference point on your face, such as the bottom of your nose or your chin.
  • Hold your fingers in place and sweep the bangs to the opposite side of your face.
  • Using sharp scissors, carefully cut the bangs just below your fingers.

Step 3: Cutting the Upper Section

  • Release the upper section of hair and repeat the same process as above.
  • Pinch the hair, pull it down to the desired length, and sweep it to the opposite side of your face.
  • Cut the hair just below your fingers, ensuring that the two sections are approximately the same length.

Step 4: Blending the Sections

  • Take both sections of bangs and hold them together between two fingers.
  • Using the tips of your scissors, make small vertical clips in the hair. This will remove bulk without altering the overall length, creating a softer and more blended look.

Cutting Side Swept Bangs with a Razor

Step 1: Sectioning and Cutting the Larger Section

  • Section your hair into a triangle as described in step 1 for cutting with scissors.
  • Divide the triangle in the middle, creating two sections.
  • Take the larger section and cut it at a slant using a sharp, single-blade razor.
  • Start from the bottom of your nose and work your way upwards, cutting towards the outer corner of your eye.

Step 2: Thinning the Larger Section

  • To remove excess bulk, hold the razor blade at a slight angle and lightly graze it over small sections of hair.
  • Continue thinning until the hair is less dense and flowy.

Step 3: Cutting the Uncut Section

  • Take the uncut section and cut it to match the length and angle of the first section.

Step 4: Cutting the Smaller Section

  • Trim the remaining smaller section at the same downward angle as the other two sections.

Cutting Naturally Curly Side Bangs

Step 1: Preparing the Hair

  • Allow your hair to dry naturally or use a diffuser to gently enhance your curls.
  • Part your hair from ear-to-ear and tie the rest of your hair back.

Step 2: Cutting the Shortest Ringlet

  • Finger-comb your curls to separate them.
  • Identify the shortest ringlet and cut it off. This will serve as the base length for your bangs.

Step 3: Cutting the Remaining Ringlets

  • Continue cutting curl groupings at a downward slant, with the longest ringlet being on the opposite side of your face.
  • Work your way up, cutting each ringlet slightly longer than the previous one.

Step 4: Adjusting the Length

  • Wear your new bangs for a few days and then wash your hair. Once your hair is dry, check the length of your bangs and make any necessary adjustments before cutting further.

Conclusion:

Congratulations! You've successfully trimmed your side bangs using scissors or a razor. Whether you prefer a choppy, layered look or sleek curtain bangs, these techniques empower you to achieve your desired result. Remember to approach the task with precision and patience, and don't hesitate to consult tutorials or seek professional guidance if needed. By following these steps, you can confidently cut and style your side bangs at home, framing your face with a chic and fashionable flair.
