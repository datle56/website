How to Copy and Paste Images

Copying and pasting images is a fundamental task in many digital workflows. Whether you're working on a presentation, creating a document, or simply sharing an image with someone, knowing how to copy and paste effectively can save you time and effort.

Copying and Pasting Images on Different Platforms

Windows and Mac

  1. Right-click the image you want to copy.
  2. Select Copy Image from the context menu.
  3. Right-click where you want to paste the image.
  4. Select Paste from the context menu.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Long-press the image you want to copy.
  2. Tap Copy from the pop-up menu.
  3. Long-press where you want to paste the image.
  4. Tap Paste from the pop-up menu.

Android

  1. Long-press the image you want to copy.
  2. Tap Copy from the pop-up menu.
  3. Long-press where you want to paste the image.
  4. Tap Paste from the pop-up menu.

Microsoft Office

  1. Click the image you want to copy.
  2. Click the Copy button on the Home tab.
  3. Click where you want to paste the image.
  4. Click the Paste button on the Home tab.

Google Workspace

  1. Click the image you want to copy.
  2. Press Ctrl+C (Windows) or Cmd+C (Mac) to copy it.
  3. Click where you want to paste the image.
  4. Press Ctrl+V (Windows) or Cmd+V (Mac) to paste it.

Additional Notes:

  • When copying and pasting images from the web, you may need to right-click and select Save Image As to download the image onto your computer first.
  • The format of the pasted image will depend on the application and settings you're using.
  • You can also use keyboard shortcuts to copy and paste images in many applications:
    • Windows: Ctrl+C (copy), Ctrl+V (paste)
    • Mac: Cmd+C (copy), Cmd+V (paste)
  • When pasting images into documents or presentations, you may have formatting options to adjust the size, alignment, and other properties of the image.
  • Be aware of copyright laws and always ensure you have permission to use images that you copy and paste.
