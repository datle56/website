Create Stunning Hairstyles with Jaw Clips

Jaw clips, with their versatile hold and chic designs, are an effortless way to elevate any hairstyle. Follow these step-by-step guides to master the art of jaw clip styling and create a variety of stylish looks.

Method 1: Elegant French Twist

Step 1: Smooth and Tangle-Free Base

Begin by ensuring your hair is tangle-free and smooth. Brush it gently to remove any knots or tangles that may interfere with the styling process.

Step 2: Gather and Position

Bring your hair towards the back of your head and loosely gather it into a ball shape. Keep the hair slightly off-center to enhance the visual appeal of the final twist.

Step 3: Twist and Secure

Start twisting the hair upwards, maintaining a gentle tension. As you twist, gradually tighten your grip to create a secure hold.

Step 4: Repeat and Refine

Continue twisting the hair, rotating it slightly as you go. Once you reach the desired height and fullness, secure the twist with a jaw clip.

Step 5: Finishing Touches

Smooth any stray hairs around the twist and adjust the position of the jaw clip to ensure it sits comfortably and securely.

Method 2: Effortless Ponytail Twist

Step 1: Create a Stable Ponytail

Brush your hair and gather it together at the desired height on the back of your head. Secure the ponytail with a hair elastic to create a firm base.

Step 2: Twist the Tail

Grip the ponytail and begin twisting it into a tight spiral. Continue twisting until you reach its full length.

Step 3: Tuck and Secure

Take the end of the twisted ponytail and tuck it under the base of the twist. Conceal the tuck and secure it with a jaw clip to keep the spiral intact.

Method 3: Quick Jaw Clip Style

Step 1: Gather Side Sections

Divide your hair into two sections, taking hair from both the left and right sides of your face. Pull the sections back towards the back of your head.

Step 2: Lift and Clip

Lift the hair slightly and secure it with a jaw clip at an angle. Position the clip to gather both sections and hold them firmly in place.

Method 4: Sleek Two Clip Style

Step 1: Secure the Lower Half

Gather the back section of your hair and secure it with a jaw clip near the base of your neck. This will create the lower layer of the style.

Step 2: Gather Top Section

Take the rest of your hair, including any hair from the sides, and gather it towards the back of your head.

Step 3: Clip Beneath the First

Position the second jaw clip directly above the first one, but slightly off-center. Secure the gathered hair beneath the lower clip to create a layered look.

Step 4: Adjust and Perfect

Smooth any flyaways and adjust the clips as needed to ensure both layers are held securely and the hairstyle is polished and polished.
