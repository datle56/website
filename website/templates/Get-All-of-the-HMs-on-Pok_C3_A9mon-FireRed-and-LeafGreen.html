Uncover the Secrets: A Comprehensive Guide to Acquiring All HMs in Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen


Unlock the Secrets of Pokémon's Hidden Moves!

Prepare yourself for an unforgettable adventure, Trainers! Discover the secrets behind Pokémon's elusive hidden moves (HMs) and unlock a world of possibilities in your quest to become a true Pokémon Master.

From the essential Cut, which allows you to traverse overgrown paths, to the majestic Fly, that grants your Pokémon the power of aerial travel, each HM holds the key to unlocking hidden areas and overcoming formidable challenges.

Embark on a journey filled with puzzles, encounters, and the unwavering determination to collect all seven HMs. From the bustling streets of Vermilion City to the serene shores of Four Island, your quest will lead you to hidden treasures and empower your Pokémon beyond their limits.


HM01 Cut

To acquire HM01 Cut, embark on the following journey:

  1. Navigate to Vermilion City, a bustling port city renowned for its brightly lit lighthouse.
  2. Ascend the iconic S.S. Anne, a luxurious cruise liner docked at the harbor.
  3. Venture into the ship's lower decks, where you'll encounter Bill, a renowned Pokémon researcher.
  4. Lend Bill a helping hand by freeing him from an amusing Pokémon costume predicament.
  5. Express your gratitude for your assistance, and Bill will reward you with a coveted ticket.
  6. Seek out the ship's Captain, a seasoned mariner with a wealth of knowledge.
  7. Engage in a cordial conversation with the Captain, and he shall bestow upon you the coveted "HM01 Cut."

HM02 Fly

Your quest for HM02 Fly begins in Celadon City, a sprawling urban hub renowned for its advanced technology.

  1. Depart from Celadon City and venture onto Route 16, a verdant path that meanders through scenic hills.
  2. Locate a modest tree near the perimeter fence that encloses the path.
  3. Utilize the newly acquired "HM01 Cut" to effortlessly fell the tree, clearing a path forward.
  4. Follow the newly revealed path behind the fence until you reach a quaint cottage nestled amidst the greenery.
  5. Politely knock on the door, and a young girl will cordially greet you.
  6. Strike up a friendly conversation with the girl, and she will graciously bestow upon you the invaluable "HM02 Fly."

HM03 Surf

Embark on a thrilling expedition to Fuchsia City, a coastal haven known for its abundance of aquatic Pokémon.

  1. Upon arrival in Fuchsia City, venture into the expansive Safari Zone, a vast preserve teeming with diverse wildlife.
  2. Journey to Zone Four, a verdant oasis where towering trees and serene lakes create a picturesque landscape.
  3. Enter the enigmatic building located within Zone Four, where adventure awaits.
  4. Approach the friendly attendant stationed within the building and engage in a cordial conversation.
  5. Express your desire for aquatic mastery, and the attendant will present you with the coveted "HM03 Surf."

HM04 Strength

Your quest for HM04 Strength leads you back to Fuchsia City, a city renowned for its thriving Safari Zone.

  1. Return to the Safari Zone and navigate to Zone Four, a verdant expanse where hidden treasures lie in wait.
  2. Seek out the Gold Teeth, a jovial Pokémon enthusiast adorned with gleaming dentures.
  3. Venture to the Warden's house, a humble abode nestled amidst the sprawling preserve.
  4. Knock politely on the door, and the Warden will emerge, ready to test your worthiness.
  5. Prove your mettle by successfully navigating his challenges, and he will grant you the coveted "HM04 Strength."

HM05 Flash

To acquire HM05 Flash, embark on a journey to Vermilion City, a port city illuminated by the iconic lighthouse.

  1. Depart from Vermilion City and venture southeast along Route 11, a winding path that leads to a hidden treasure.
  2. Enter the enigmatic Diglett's Cave, a subterranean labyrinth renowned for its mischievous inhabitants.
  3. Utilize the "HM01 Cut" to remove the pesky tree obstructing your path.
  4. Continue your journey into the cave until you stumble upon a modest building.
  5. Approach the enigmatic man inside, a figure cloaked in darkness, save for his glowing eyes.
  6. Express your admiration for his obscurity, and he will reward you with the invaluable "HM05 Flash."

HM06 Rock Smash

Your quest for HM06 Rock Smash begins on the remote One Island, a secluded haven nestled amidst the vast expanse of the ocean.

  1. Navigate to the island's easternmost point, known as Kindle Road, a winding path illuminated by sporadic flames.
  2. Surf northward along the coastline until you encounter a cavernous entrance.
  3. Venture into the depths of the cave, where darkness envelops you.
  4. As you progress through the cave, you will encounter a solitary man.
  5. Engage in a cordial conversation with the man, and he will impart upon you the coveted "HM06 Rock Smash."

HM07 Waterfall

Your journey for HM07 Waterfall leads you to Four Island, an enigmatic land shrouded in mystery.

  1. Ascend the heights of Icefall Cave, a frigid labyrinth renowned for its treacherous ice patches.
  2. Utilize "HM03 Surf" to navigate through the cave's icy waters.
  3. Carefully maneuver over the treacherous ice patches, guiding your way through the labyrinth.
  4. Descend two treacherous holes, delving deeper into the cave's icy depths.
  5. Ascend the ladder that awaits you, and you will be rewarded with the coveted "HM07 Waterfall."

Conclusion:

With all six HMs safely in your possession, you're now equipped to explore the vast world of Pokémon to its fullest extent. These powerful techniques will allow you to cut through thickets, traverse bodies of water, and even soar through the skies. Use them wisely and you'll unlock countless new possibilities in your Pokémon adventure. Remember, the true measure of a Pokémon Trainer lies not only in the strength of their team but also in their mastery of the HM techniques that empower them.
