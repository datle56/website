Method 1: Backcombing

Step-by-Step Guide to Backcombing Method:

Materials:

  • Regular hairbrush
  • Special backcombing comb with fine, closely spaced teeth
  • Crochet needle (optional)
  • Rubber bands
  • Dread cream or dread wax

Instructions:

  1. Section the Hair: Divide your hair into small, distinct sections. The size of the sections will determine the thickness of your future dreads.
  2. Backcomb: Using the special backcombing comb, gently tease the hair towards the scalp in short, upward strokes. Continue combing until the hair becomes noticeably knotty and looped.
  3. Tighten with Crochet Needle: If desired, use a crochet needle to further tighten the knots created by backcombing. Insert the needle into the knot and pull the hair through to secure it.
  4. Secure with Rubber Bands: Tie rubber bands at both the ends and base of the dread to hold it in place.
  5. Apply Dread Cream: To prevent dryness and promote dread formation, apply a small amount of dread cream or wax to the dread.

Method 2: Twist and Rip

Step-by-Step Guide to Twist and Rip Method:

Materials:

  • Hairbrush (optional)
  • Rubber bands

Instructions:

  1. Section the Hair: Similar to the backcombing method, divide your hair into small sections.
  2. Twist: Holding a section of hair tightly at the ends, twist it clockwise or counterclockwise. Continue twisting until the hair becomes coiled and compressed.
  3. Rip: Release the ends and gently pull them apart to separate the twisted hair. Repeat the twisting and ripping process several times until the hair begins to knot.
  4. Secure with Rubber Bands: As with the backcombing method, secure the dread with rubber bands at both ends.

Method 3: Natural

Step-by-Step Guide to Natural Method:

Materials:

  • Salt water spray (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Grow Your Hair: Allow your hair to grow to a length of at least 10 inches. This will provide ample hair to form dreads.
  2. Avoid Combing and Brushing: Cease brushing or combing your hair to prevent the hair from untangling.
  3. Discontinue Washing: Stop washing your hair for several months. The natural oils and residue on your hair will help to mat and dread.
  4. Avoid Conditioner: Do not use conditioner, as it can soften the hair and make it less prone to forming dreads.
  5. Promote Knotting: Spray your hair with a salt water solution to promote the formation of knots.
  6. Be Patient: Natural dreads take time to form, with the process typically taking 1-3 years to complete.

Conclusion:
