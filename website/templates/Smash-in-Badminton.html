Unleash Your Power on the Court: Essential Techniques for Demolishing Opponents in Badminton


Master the Art of the Smash: Elevate Your Badminton Skills to New Heights

Prepare to launch into the stratosphere of badminton with this comprehensive guide to the smash, the most devastating weapon in the arsenal of any seasoned player. Whether you're a seasoned pro or an aspiring beginner, this definitive breakdown of the forehand, jumping, and backhand smashes will elevate your game to unprecedented heights. Get ready to unleash a torrent of unstoppable shots that will leave your opponents dazed and defeated.


Forehand Smash

Approach the shuttle with swift steps, feet shoulder-width apart and knees slightly bent. Position yourself at an optimal distance to allow for a powerful swing.

Hold the racket firmly in your dominant hand, slightly behind your head and pointing upwards. Your non-racket hand should be raised to chin level, serving as a counterbalance.

Initiate the swing by dropping the racket head slightly. As you reach the back of the swing, quickly snap your wrist and drive the racket forward towards the shuttle. Aim to make contact with the shuttle at its highest point for maximum power.

Release the non-racket hand upon contact, allowing the racket to follow through. Finish the swing with your racket pointing downwards and your body leaning forward.

Jumping Smash

Similar to the forehand smash, the jumping smash requires a more dynamic approach. Intercept the shuttle at its highest point by timing your jump accurately.

As you jump, swing the racket back and reach the intercept point at the peak of your jump. Bend your legs backwards to generate power and stability.

With your racket extended towards the shuttle, make contact with the shuttle at the highest point. Snap your wrist and use your full arm extension to deliver a powerful smash.

Release the shuttle and follow through with your swing, landing with your feet shoulder-width apart to maintain balance.

Backhand Smash

The backhand smash is employed when facing short, high returns. It involves a different grip and technique compared to the forehand smash.

Switch to a backhand grip, turning your wrist so that the palm faces upwards. Keep the racket close to your body, with the racket head positioned behind your non-racket shoulder.

Step forward and swing the racket smoothly, extending your arm fully towards the shuttle. Snap your wrist at contact, using your non-racket arm to stabilize your body against the momentum.

Aim to hit the shuttle at its highest point and finish the swing with your racket facing downwards. Recover to a balanced position and be ready for the next shot.


Mastering the forehand, jumping, and backhand smashes empowers you to unleash an arsenal of devastating shots in badminton. By executing these techniques with precision and power, you can dominate the court and crush your opponents. Remember to approach the shuttle with speed and agility, time your swing perfectly, and deliver a forceful strike. Whether you're a seasoned veteran or a budding player, incorporating these smashes into your repertoire will elevate your badminton game and leave your opponents in awe.
