Step-by-Step Guide to Effortlessly Migrate from Hotmail to Gmail in 11 Simple Steps


Unlock the Seamless Switch from Hotmail to Gmail

Are you ready to bid farewell to Hotmail and embrace the power of Gmail? Whether you seek a simpler way to manage your contacts or a comprehensive migration of your entire Hotmail account, this guide has you covered. Let's embark on a hassle-free transition that will leave you wondering why you didn't make the switch sooner.


Method 1: Transferring Just Contacts

  1. Export Contacts from Hotmail:

    • Log in to your Hotmail account.
    • Click on the "Contacts" icon.
    • Select "Manage" from the top menu bar.
    • Choose "Export contacts."
    • Select "CSV" as the export format.
    • Click "Export" and save the CSV file to your computer.

  2. Import Contacts to Gmail:

    • Log in to your Gmail account.
    • Click on the "Contacts" icon in the left side panel.
    • Click on the "More" drop-down menu and select "Import."
    • Select "CSV or vCard file" and browse for the exported CSV file from Hotmail.
    • Click "Open" and follow the prompts to import the contacts.

  3. Notify Contacts:

    • Compose an email to your contacts, informing them of your new Gmail address and asking them to update their contact lists.

Method 2: Transferring Everything

  1. Access Gmail Settings:

    • Log in to your Gmail account.
    • Click on the gear icon in the top right corner.
    • Select "Settings."

  2. Import Mail and Contacts:

    • In the "Accounts and Import" tab, locate the "Import mail and contacts" section.
    • Click on "Import mail and contacts."

  3. Enter Hotmail Account Details:

    • Enter your Hotmail email address and password in the provided fields.
    • Select the "Import contacts" checkbox if you want to transfer your contacts as well.

  4. Choose Import Options:

    • You can choose to import emails from a specific time period or leave it blank to import all emails.
    • You can also choose to label the imported emails with a specific tag.

  5. Complete the Import:

    • Click on "Start import."
    • The import process will begin and may take some time, depending on the amount of data being transferred.

Important Notes:

  • It's crucial to notify your contacts of your new email address, as they may not automatically receive an update from Gmail.
  • You can continue to access your Hotmail address for any emails you may miss during the transition.
  • Consider updating any subscriptions or services linked to your Hotmail account to use your new Gmail address.

Conclusion:

By following these two methods, you can effortlessly transfer your valuable contacts or your entire Hotmail account to Gmail. Both methods are straightforward and user-friendly, ensuring a seamless transition. Whether you prefer to transfer just contacts or all your Hotmail content, the steps outlined above will guide you through the process. Remember to inform your contacts about your new Gmail address, ensuring uninterrupted communication. By utilizing this guide, you can rest assured that your contacts and emails will be safely transferred from Hotmail to Gmail, enabling you to continue your online communication effortlessly.
