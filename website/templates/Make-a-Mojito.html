Classic Mojito: A Refreshing Cuban Delight

Ingredients:

  • 10-12 fresh mint leaves, plus one for garnish
  • 2 teaspoons granulated sugar or simple syrup
  • 1 ounce freshly squeezed lime juice
  • 2 ounces white rum
  • Club soda, for topping
  • Lime wedge, for garnish

Steps:

  1. Muddle the mint, sugar, and lime juice: In a tall glass or muddler, gently muddle the mint leaves, sugar, and lime juice until the mint is fragrant and the sugar has dissolved.
  2. Add rum, ice, and stir: Pour in the white rum and fill the glass with crushed ice. Stir gently to combine.
  3. Top with club soda: Add club soda until the glass is full.
  4. Garnish and serve: Garnish with a lime wedge and a sprig of fresh mint. Enjoy your classic Mojito!

Strawberry Mojito: A Fruity Twist on the Classic

Ingredients:

  • 5-6 large strawberries, quartered
  • 10-12 fresh mint leaves
  • 2 teaspoons granulated sugar or simple syrup
  • 1 ounce freshly squeezed lime juice
  • 2 ounces white rum
  • Club soda, for topping
  • Lime wedge, for garnish
  • Strawberry slice, for garnish

Steps:

  1. Muddle the strawberries, mint, sugar, and lime juice: In a tall glass or muddler, gently muddle the strawberries, mint leaves, sugar, and lime juice until the strawberries are crushed and the sugar has dissolved.
  2. Add rum, ice, and stir: Pour in the white rum and fill the glass with crushed ice. Stir gently to combine.
  3. Top with club soda: Add club soda until the glass is full.
  4. Garnish and serve: Garnish with a lime wedge, a strawberry slice, and a sprig of fresh mint. Indulge in your refreshing Strawberry Mojito!

Coconut Mojito: A Tropical Paradise in a Glass

Ingredients:

  • 10-12 fresh mint leaves
  • 2 teaspoons granulated sugar or simple syrup
  • 1 ounce freshly squeezed lime juice
  • 1 ounce cream of coconut
  • 2 ounces coconut-flavored rum
  • Club soda, for topping
  • Lime wedge, for garnish
  • Cherry, for garnish

Steps:

  1. Muddle the mint, sugar, lime juice, and cream of coconut: In a tall glass or muddler, gently muddle the mint leaves, sugar, lime juice, and cream of coconut until the mint is fragrant and the sugar has dissolved.
  2. Add rum, ice, and stir: Pour in the coconut-flavored rum and fill the glass with crushed ice. Stir gently to combine.
  3. Top with club soda: Add club soda until the glass is full.
  4. Garnish and serve: Garnish with a lime wedge, a cherry, and a sprig of fresh mint. Sip on your tropical Coconut Mojito and escape to paradise!

Tips for All Mojitos:

  • Use a sturdy highball or Collins glass to prevent spills.
  • Muddle the mint gently to release its flavor without making it bitter.
  • Opt for a white or silver rum for a light and refreshing taste that complements the lime and mint.
  • Garnish your Mojito with fresh mint or lime wedges for an extra touch of zest.
  • Serve Mojitos immediately for optimal freshness and flavor.
