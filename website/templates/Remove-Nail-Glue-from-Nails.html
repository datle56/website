Say Goodbye to Fake Nails: A Gentle Removal Guide

Are you rocking fabulous false nails but dreading the inevitable removal process? Banish your worries! We've got three methods to help you bid adieu to your artificial talons without the hassle or damage. Whether you prefer a gentle soak or a targeted approach, we've got you covered. Let's dive in and uncover the secrets of a seamless false nail removal!


Method 1: Removing False Nails without Acetone

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Soak your nails in warm, soapy water: Prepare a bowl of warm water and add a few drops of mild dish soap. Submerge your hands and soak for 15 minutes. The warm water and soap will help loosen the adhesive bond between the false nails and your natural nails.
  2. Gently pry off the nails: Once the adhesive has softened, gently pry off the false nails using an orange stick or similar tool. Be careful not to pull or tug at the nails, as this could damage your natural nails.
  3. Use a nail buffer to remove excess glue: If there is any leftover glue on your natural nails, use a nail buffer to gently remove it. Rub the buffer gently in a circular motion until all the glue is gone.
  4. Wipe away any remaining glue with acetone: If any glue residue still remains, you can use a small amount of acetone on a cotton ball to gently wipe it away. Be sure to only use a small amount of acetone and avoid getting it on your skin.

Method 2: Removing False Nails with Acetone Soak

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Trim the false nails down: Before soaking your nails in acetone, trim the false nails down as much as possible using nail clippers. This will reduce the amount of time needed to dissolve the adhesive.
  2. File off the glossy surface: Use a nail file to file off the glossy surface of the false nails. This will help the acetone penetrate the adhesive more easily.
  3. Wrap each nail in a cotton ball soaked in acetone: Soak cotton balls in pure acetone and wrap them around each false nail. Secure the cotton balls with aluminum foil to prevent the acetone from evaporating.
  4. Remove the foil and pads after 20 minutes: After 20 minutes, remove the aluminum foil and cotton balls. The adhesive should be dissolved by now.
  5. Push off the softened false nails: Gently push off the softened false nails using an orange stick or similar tool. Be careful not to apply too much pressure, as this could damage your natural nails.
  6. Buff away any remaining glue: Use a nail buffer to gently remove any remaining glue from your natural nails. Rub the buffer gently in a circular motion until all the glue is gone.

Method 3: Treating Your Nails After Glue Removal

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Wash your hands with warm, soapy water: Wash your hands thoroughly with warm, soapy water to remove any glue residue or acetone.
  2. Rub a natural oil over your nails and hands: Apply a few drops of a natural oil, such as coconut oil or olive oil, to your nails and hands. Massage the oil into your nails and cuticles to hydrate and nourish them.

Conclusion:

Removing false nails without damaging your natural nails can be achieved with care and the right techniques. By opting for acetone-free methods or following the acetone soak approach diligently, you can preserve the health of your nails while achieving a natural look.
