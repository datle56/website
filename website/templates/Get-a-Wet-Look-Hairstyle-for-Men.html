Creating a Wet Look Hairstyle: A Comprehensive Guide

Method 1: Using a Wet Look Hair Product

  1. Prepare Your Hair: Thoroughly wash your hair with shampoo and conditioner. Towel dry your hair until it is slightly damp but not dripping wet.

  2. Choose Your Product: For a wet look hairstyle, you will need a hair product that provides a strong hold and a wet, glossy finish. Mousse, gel, pomade, or wax are all suitable options.

  3. Apply the Product:

    • Mousse or Gel: Dispense a small amount of mousse or gel onto your fingers. Rub them together to evenly distribute the product. Massage the product into your hair, starting from the roots and working your way to the tips.
    • Pomade or Wax: Take a dime-sized amount of pomade or wax and warm it up in your hands. Apply the product to your fingers, then finger-comb it into your hair.

  4. Style Your Hair: Once the product is evenly distributed, comb or style your hair as desired. Use a wide-toothed comb for less defined curls or a fine-toothed comb for more defined curls.

Method 2: Applying Hairspray for Extra Hold

  1. Finish with Hairspray (Optional): To enhance the hold of your wet look hairstyle and prevent frizz, spray a light mist of hairspray over your hair. Keep the can 6-8 inches away from your head to avoid a stiff or helmet-like finish.

Choosing the Right Hair Products

The type of hair product you choose will depend on your desired look and hair type. Here's a breakdown:

  • Pomade: Pomade provides a sleek, slicked-back or pompadour look. It is ideal for medium to thick hair.
  • Hair Wax: Wax creates a textured, bedhead or messy look. It works well for shorter hairstyles or to define individual strands.
  • Gel: Gel gives a classic sleek, shiny wet look. It is suitable for all hair types, but it can make fine hair appear limp.
  • Cream: Cream tames frizz and flyaways while adding a subtle shine. It is best for fine or damaged hair.
  • Mousse: Mousse adds volume to thin hair and creates a soft, natural-looking wet look.
  • Hairspray: Hairspray provides extra hold to keep your wet look hairstyle in place throughout the day.

Tips for a Flawless Wet Look

  • Use a blow dryer to dry your hair on a cool setting to avoid frizz.
  • Apply the product to slightly damp hair, not soaking wet hair.
  • Start with a small amount of product and gradually add more as needed.
  • Comb your hair while the product is still wet to ensure even distribution.
  • Use a microfiber towel to blot excess moisture from your hair.
  • Avoid touching or running your fingers through your hair after styling, as this can disrupt the wet look.
