How to Type the Squared Symbol (²)

The squared symbol (²) is a mathematical operator that indicates that a number or expression is raised to the second power. It is often used in equations, formulas, and scientific calculations to denote the area of a square or the volume of a cube. Here's how to type the squared symbol on various devices and operating systems:

For Android and iOS Devices:

  1. Download the Gboard Keyboard App: If you don't have it already, download and install the Gboard keyboard app from the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store. This feature-rich keyboard provides a wide range of symbols and special characters, including the squared symbol.

  2. Hold Down the '2' Key: Once Gboard is installed, open any text or document editing app and activate the Gboard keyboard. Hold down the "2" key on the keyboard until you see a pop-up menu appear.

  3. Tap the '²' Key: In the pop-up menu, you'll see the superscript ² symbol. Tap on this symbol to insert it into your text.

For Chromebook Devices:

  1. Use the Keyboard Shortcut in Google Docs: Open Google Docs on your Chromebook. Place the cursor where you want to insert the squared symbol. Press and hold the "Ctrl" key and then press the period (.) key twice, followed by the number "2."

  2. Turn Off Superscript (Optional): After inserting the squared symbol, you may want to turn off the superscript formatting. To do this, press and hold the "Ctrl" key and then press the period (.) key again. This will return the font to its normal size.

For Windows PC:

  1. Use the Alt Code: Place the cursor where you want to insert the squared symbol. Press and hold the "Alt" key on your keyboard, then type "253" using the numeric keypad on the right side of your keyboard. Release the "Alt" key, and the ² symbol will appear.

For Mac Devices:

  1. Open the Character Menu: Place the cursor where you want to insert the squared symbol. Press and hold the "Ctrl" key, the "Cmd" key (Command key), and the "Space" key simultaneously to open the Character Menu.

  2. Navigate to 'Digits - All': In the Character Menu, click on the "Digits - All" category from the menu on the left-hand side.

  3. Select the Superscripted ² Symbol: Scroll down until you find the superscripted ² symbol. Click on it to select it.

  4. Insert the Symbol: Once the symbol is selected, click on the "Insert" button to insert it into your text.

By following these steps, you can easily type the squared symbol (²) on various devices and operating systems, enabling you to accurately express mathematical equations and calculations in your written content.
