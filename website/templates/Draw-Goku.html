How to Draw Goku (Simplified)

Goku, the iconic protagonist of the Dragon Ball franchise, is a beloved character recognized worldwide. If you're an aspiring artist or simply a fan who wants to capture the essence of Goku on paper, this simplified guide will provide you with step-by-step instructions to create a recognizable and stylized Goku drawing.

Part 1: Face

  1. Frown Wrinkle: Begin by drawing a horizontal line slightly above the center of the page. This line represents the frown wrinkle between Goku's eyes.
  2. Eyebrows: From each end of the frown wrinkle, draw two angled lines extending upward to create his signature eyebrows.
  3. Pupils: Directly below the eyebrows, draw two circular pupils.
  4. Nose: Create a small nose below the frown wrinkle by drawing a vertical line and a curved line that meet at the tip.
  5. Smile: Sketch a crooked smile below the nose using a curved line.

Part 2: Head and Hair

  1. Cheekbones and Chin: Add two curved lines to indicate the cheekbones and a short, curved line for the chin indent.
  2. Head Outline: Starting from the temples, draw a line that outlines the head shape, curving inward towards the chin.
  3. Ears: Create two large, pointed ears on each side of the head.
  4. Hair: Draw triangular-shaped hair over the head, starting with the forehead and extending to the sides and top. Ensure that the hair has a distinctive spiky appearance.

Part 3: Upper Body

  1. Neck and Shoulders: Outline the neck and shoulders, with the shoulders being slightly wider than the head.
  2. Chest Muscles: Sketch two semi-circular lines in the center of the chest to represent Goku's well-defined muscles.
  3. Vest Straps: Draw thick straps over the shoulders, extending down to the chest muscles.
  4. Shirt: Curve the ends of the shoulder line downward and draw a horizontal line below the chest muscles to indicate the top of Goku's shirt.

Additional Tips for Enhancing Your Goku Drawing:

  • Use a light touch and avoid applying too much pressure, as this can result in smudging or tearing the paper.
  • Take your time and pay attention to the proportions and angles of each feature.
  • Refer to images or screenshots of Goku for accurate details and expressions.
  • Experiment with different poses and angles to capture the dynamic nature of the character.
  • Practice regularly to improve your skills and develop a more distinctive style.

By following these simple steps and incorporating these additional tips, you can create a recognizable and expressive portrayal of Goku, a true testament to your artistic abilities and passion for the beloved anime character.
