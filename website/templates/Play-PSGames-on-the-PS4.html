How to Get Started with PlayStation Now: A Comprehensive Guide

Step 1: Connect Your PS4

Before you can start streaming games with PlayStation Now, you need to ensure that your PS4 console is connected to the internet. Use an Ethernet cable for a wired connection, providing a more stable network connection.

Step 2: Create a PlayStation Network Account

If you don't have a PlayStation Network (PSN) account, you'll need to create one. This account will be used to access PlayStation Now and other online features. Visit playstation.com to sign up for a free account.

Step 3: Access the PlayStation Store

On the PS4 home screen, select the shopping bag icon to access the PlayStation Store. This digital marketplace offers a wide range of games, add-ons, and subscriptions, including PlayStation Now.

Step 4: Find PlayStation Now

Once you're in the PlayStation Store, scroll down the left-hand menu and click on "PS Now." This will take you to the PlayStation Now page, where you can browse games and start your subscription.

Step 5: Start Free Trial

PlayStation Now offers a free 7-day trial for new subscribers. To start your trial, click on "Start 7-day free trial." After the trial period ends, you can continue your subscription for $19.99 per month.

Step 6: Confirm Purchase

Enter your PSN password to complete the purchase. This will activate your PlayStation Now subscription and allow you to start streaming games.

How to Play PS3 Games on PS Now

Step 1: Open PS Now App

Once your PlayStation Now subscription is active, select the PS Now icon on the PS4 home screen. This will launch the PlayStation Now app, where you can browse and play games.

Step 2: Browse Games

Click on "Browse" to see the catalog of games available on PlayStation Now. You can sort games by genre, release date, popularity, and more.

Step 3: Select Game

Choose a game you want to play from the list. Click on the game's title to view more details, including screenshots, trailers, and reviews.

Step 4: Start Streaming

Once you've selected a game, click "Stream Now" to start playing. The game will begin downloading in the background, and you'll be prompted to connect a controller when it's ready.

Troubleshooting

If you encounter any issues while using PlayStation Now, try the following:

  • Use a Wired Connection: Switch from Wi-Fi to a wired connection for a more stable internet connection. This will reduce latency and improve streaming quality.
  • Close Other Services: Suspend any active large downloads or streaming services on other devices connected to your network. This will free up bandwidth for PlayStation Now.
  • Check Internet Speed: Ensure that your internet connection meets the minimum requirements for streaming. Visit the PlayStation support website for more information on recommended internet speeds.
  • Restart the Game: If you experience any freezing or lagging, restart the game from the PlayStation Now app. This may resolve temporary issues.
  • Contact PlayStation Support: If you continue to encounter problems, contact PlayStation support for assistance. They can help diagnose and resolve any issues you may be facing.
