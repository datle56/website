How to Hide Mutual Friends on Facebook on Android: A Comprehensive Guide

Introduction

Facebook's mutual friends feature allows users to view connections they share with other individuals. While this can be a useful way to engage with friends and discover new contacts, some users may prefer to keep their mutual friends hidden for privacy reasons. While it's not possible to completely conceal mutual friends, there are steps you can take to limit their visibility on Facebook for Android.

Method 1: Hide Your Entire Friend List

Step 1: Open the Facebook app on your Android device.

Step 2: Tap the menu icon (three horizontal lines) in the bottom right corner.

Step 3: Select "Settings & Privacy" > "Settings."

Step 4: Scroll down and tap "Profile Settings."

Step 5: Under "Privacy," tap "Who can see your friends list?"

Step 6: Select "Only Me."

Method 2: Ask Your Friends to Hide Their Friend Lists

If you don't want to hide your entire friend list, you can ask your friends to hide their friend lists. This will make your mutual friends less visible on your profile.

Step 1: Contact your friends and ask them to access their Facebook settings.

Step 2: Have them follow the same steps outlined in Method 1 to set their friend list visibility to "Only Me."

Additional Considerations

  • Hiding your friend list or asking your friends to hide theirs affects only your Facebook profile. Mutual friends may still appear in group chats, event invitations, or other situations where multiple friends are involved.

  • Hiding mutual friends can make it more difficult for others to discover potential connections or make introductions through you.

  • If you decide to hide your mutual friends, it's important to do so discreetly. Requesting multiple friends to hide their friend lists simultaneously can raise suspicions and may not be met with enthusiasm.

  • Remember that privacy settings are subject to change. Check your settings periodically to ensure they align with your preferences.

Conclusion

By implementing these methods, you can effectively reduce the visibility of your mutual friends on Facebook while maintaining control over your privacy. While it may not be possible to completely hide mutual friends, these steps provide a reasonable balance between privacy and social engagement.


Conclusion:

While it's not possible to fully erase mutual friends from your Facebook profile, the above methods provide a workaround to minimize their visibility. Remember, however, that these measures may not be foolproof, and certain situations may still reveal mutual acquaintances. Ultimately, the best way to control who sees your friend list is to carefully curate who you add and maintain a strong awareness of your privacy settings.
