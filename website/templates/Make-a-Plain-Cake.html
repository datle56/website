Easy No-Frills Plain Cake: A Baking Odyssey for Beginners

Introduction:

Embark on a culinary adventure with this no-nonsense recipe that will yield a delectable plain cake. Perfect for those seeking a straightforward and crowd-pleasing treat, this cake will satisfy your sweet cravings without any fuss or elaborate decorations. Whether you're a novice baker or an experienced chef looking for a simple yet satisfying recipe, this plain cake is sure to impress.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup sugar: This essential ingredient provides the backbone of sweetness to the cake, ensuring a tantalizingly sugary treat.
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter: Unsalted butter plays a crucial role in creating a moist and tender cake. Its creamy richness imparts a melt-in-your-mouth texture that elevates the overall experience.
  • 2 large eggs: Eggs serve as binding agents, helping to hold the cake together and contribute to its fluffy texture.
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract: This aromatic extract adds a subtle yet distinct flavor that enhances the natural sweetness of the cake, providing a touch of sophistication.
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour: All-purpose flour provides the necessary structure and volume to the cake, resulting in a well-balanced and cohesive bake.
  • 1 3/4 teaspoons baking powder: Baking powder is a leavening agent that helps the cake rise, creating a light and airy texture that melts in your mouth.
  • 1/2 cup milk: Milk adds moisture to the cake, preventing it from becoming dry and crumbly. It also helps to activate the baking powder, ensuring a perfectly risen cake.

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the Oven: Ensure your oven is preheated to 350°F (177°C) before starting the batter. This step is crucial as the temperature of the oven directly affects the baking process.

  2. Prepare the Baking Pan: Grease and flour a 9-inch square baking pan thoroughly. This step prevents the cake from sticking to the pan and promotes even baking.

  3. Cream Together Wet Ingredients: In a large bowl, use a hand mixer or electric beaters to cream together the unsalted butter, eggs, and vanilla extract until the mixture is light and fluffy. The creaming process incorporates air into the batter, contributing to the cake's tenderness and volume.

  4. Whisk Dry Ingredients: In a separate bowl, whisk together the all-purpose flour and baking powder until well combined. Whisking the dry ingredients ensures an even distribution throughout the cake batter, resulting in a uniform texture and preventing lumps.

  5. Combine Dry and Wet Ingredients: Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing until just combined. Overmixing can develop the gluten in the flour, resulting in a tough and dense cake.

  6. Stir in Milk: Slowly stir in the milk until the batter reaches a smooth and pourable consistency. The milk adds moisture and helps activate the baking powder, ensuring a well-risen cake.

  7. Pour into Baking Pan: Transfer the cake batter into the prepared 9-inch square baking pan, ensuring an even distribution throughout the pan.

  8. Bake: Place the cake in the preheated oven and bake for 30-40 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Baking time may vary slightly depending on the specific oven, so keep an eye on the cake to prevent overbaking.

  9. Cool Before Serving: Once baked, remove the cake from the oven and let it cool in the pan for 1 hour before slicing and serving. This cooling period allows the cake to settle and develop its full flavor and texture.

Tips:

  • For a richer flavor: Use brown sugar instead of granulated sugar. Brown sugar imparts a deeper, slightly caramelized flavor to the cake.
  • For a taller cake: Double the recipe and bake in a 13x9-inch baking pan. This will yield a taller, more impressive cake that is perfect for larger gatherings.
  • For a glossy finish: Brush the top of the cake with a simple syrup (equal parts sugar and water, heated until the sugar dissolves) before it cools completely. This will give the cake a shiny, professional-looking finish.
