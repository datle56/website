Discover the Art of Origami Envelopes: A Step-by-Step Guide with Visual Aids


Unveiling the Art of Paper Enchantments

Prepare to be mesmerized as we embark on a journey into the captivating world of paper crafts. In this comprehensive guide, we will unlock the secrets of two extraordinary methods that will transform ordinary paper into objects of intrigue and delight.

First, we delve into the intricate art of creating a charming "Gift Package" envelope. With its elegant origami-inspired design, this package will elevate any special occasion or surprise someone with a touch of thoughtfulness.

Next, we explore the enigmatic "Hidden Message" technique. Learn how to conceal heartfelt sentiments or secret notes within a discreet and ingenious envelope. Whether used for love letters, cherished keepsakes, or simply as a playful way to communicate, this method is sure to captivate hearts and minds.


Method 1: Gift Package

  1. Prepare the Paper: Begin with a square piece of paper, preferably decorative or of a size suitable for the gift you wish to place inside.

  2. Create the Initial Fold: Take the square paper and fold it diagonally in half, forming a triangle. Ensure the edges and corners are aligned precisely.

  3. Fold the Top Corner: Take the top corner of the triangle and fold it down to the opposite bottom edge. This will create a rectangular shape with a diagonal crease running through it.

  4. Fold the Right Corner Inward: Fold the right corner of the rectangle inward towards the center, approximately one-third of the way towards the left edge. Create a clean fold along the diagonal crease.

  5. Fold the Left Corner Over: Subsequently, fold the left corner of the rectangle over to meet the right corner, completely covering it. This will form a smaller triangle on the top layer.

  6. Fold the Flap Tip: Fold the tip of the triangular flap created in step 5 over to the left edge, aligning it with the edge of the paper.

  7. Create a Crease: Take the tip of the flap that you just folded and fold it upward, creating a small crease. Unfold it afterward.

  8. Turn the Envelope: Turn the paper over so that the folded flap is on the bottom.

  9. Fold the Top Point Down: Take the top point of the triangle that you created in step 7 and fold it downward, aligning it with the bottom edge.

  10. Insert the Flap: Carefully push the folded flap (step 6) into the pocket created in step 9. Ensure it fits snugly and is secure.

  11. Flatten the Envelope: Once the flap is inserted, flatten the envelope to give it its final shape. The gift package is now ready to be decorated or filled with a small gift.

Method 2: Hidden Message

  1. Prepare the Message: Write or type your secret message on a rectangular piece of paper. Fold it in half lengthwise to create a long, thin strip.

  2. Unfold and Fold the Sides In: Unfold the paper and fold both sides inward at right angles to meet the center crease. This will create a narrower strip with two open spaces on each side.

  3. Fold Corners to Center: Take the corners of the open spaces on each side and fold them inward to meet the center crease. This will result in four triangular flaps along the sides.

  4. Fold Sides Inward Again: Fold both sides inward again at right angles, covering the triangular flaps you created in step 3.

  5. Tuck Triangle Tips: Take the tips of the triangles (created in step 4) and tuck them under the flaps on the opposite side. This will secure the envelope closed.

  6. Flatten and Conceal: Flatten the envelope to make it compact and inconspicuous. The hidden message is now concealed within the folded envelope. To open it, simply unfold the sides and reveal the secret message.


Conclusion:

With these two simple methods, you can now create your own unique and meaningful envelopes. Whether you're wrapping up a thoughtful gift or sending a secret message, these envelope-folding techniques offer a creative and personal touch. So grab some paper and let your imagination soar. The possibilities are endless!
