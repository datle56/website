Reconnect Candy Crush Saga to Facebook: A Comprehensive Guide

Candy Crush Saga, the beloved mobile puzzle game, allows players to connect their progress to their Facebook account for a seamless gaming experience across multiple devices. However, sometimes, due to various reasons, the Facebook connection might become disconnected. To ensure you can continue your Candy Crush journey without losing any precious progress, here is a detailed guide on how to reconnect Candy Crush Saga to Facebook:

Method 1: Reinstalling the App

This method involves uninstalling and then reinstalling the Candy Crush Saga app. This process will reset the app's settings and may resolve connection issues.

  1. Uninstall Candy Crush Saga: Remove the app from your device as you would with any other application.
  2. Download and Reinstall: Visit the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android) to download and install the latest version of Candy Crush Saga.
  3. Retrieve Your Progress: Open the newly installed app and tap on the "Retrieve My Progress" option. A prompt will appear asking you to connect to Facebook. Tap on "Connect" to restore your game data.

Method 2: Fixing Permissions

If the Facebook connection has expired or if the permissions have changed, you can reconnect by updating them within the Facebook app itself.

  1. Open Facebook App: Launch the official Facebook app on your device.
  2. Go to Settings: Tap on the menu icon (three horizontal lines) and select "Settings & Privacy" followed by "Settings".
  3. Find Candy Crush Saga: Under the "Apps and Websites" section, locate Candy Crush Saga from the list.
  4. Renew Connection: If you find that the connection has expired, tap on the "Renew" button and follow the on-screen instructions to reconnect.

Method 3: Clearing the Cache (Android)

Clearing the app cache can eliminate corrupted data that may interfere with the Facebook connection.

  1. Access App Settings: Go to your device's "Settings" menu and select "Apps & notifications".
  2. Find Candy Crush Saga: Scroll through the list of installed apps and tap on Candy Crush Saga.
  3. Clear Cache: In the app's settings, locate the "Storage" section and tap on "Clear Cache".

Method 4: Clearing the Cache (iPhone & iPad)

Similar to Android, clearing the cache on iOS devices can resolve connection issues.

  1. Open Settings App: Launch the "Settings" app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Find Candy Crush Saga: Scroll down and locate Candy Crush Saga within the list of installed apps under the "General" or "iPhone/iPad Storage" section.
  3. Clear Cache: Tap on Candy Crush Saga and choose either "Offload App" or "Delete App" to clear the cache.

Method 5: Other Fixes

In addition to the previous methods, consider the following additional troubleshooting steps:

  • Update Apps: Ensure that both the Facebook and Candy Crush Saga apps are updated to their latest versions.
  • Stable Internet Connection: Use a reliable Wi-Fi or cellular data connection to establish a stable connection between the apps.
  • Check Facebook Settings: Verify that your Facebook account settings allow Candy Crush Saga to access your information.
  • King Account: If you experience persistent connection issues, consider creating a King account and linking it to your Candy Crush Saga progress.

Note:

  • If you change your Facebook password or acquire a new device, you will need to reconnect Candy Crush Saga using one of the aforementioned methods.
  • Contacting Candy Crush Saga Support: If you continue to encounter problems reconnecting, do not hesitate to reach out to the Candy Crush Saga support team for assistance.
