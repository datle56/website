Stop Unwanted Calls on Your Phone: A Comprehensive Guide

In today's digital age, unwanted phone calls have become an increasingly common nuisance. They disrupt us at inconvenient times, invade our privacy, and can even lead to fraud or scams. However, there are various effective ways to put an end to these unwanted intrusions.

Utilizing Service Provider Features

Most major service providers offer built-in call blocking features. Here's a detailed breakdown:

  • AT&T:

    • Download the Call Protect app from the App Store or Google Play.
    • The app allows you to manually block numbers, report spam, and receive alerts about potential fraud.
    • This service is available for free to all AT&T subscribers.

  • Verizon:

    • Verizon offers a free call blocking service that allows you to block up to 20 numbers.
    • For more advanced blocking capabilities, you can subscribe to Verizon's Usage Controls service for $5 per month.
    • This service lets you block unlimited numbers, manage blacklists and whitelists, and even block text messages.

  • T-Mobile:

    • Contact T-Mobile customer service at 611 or visit a T-Mobile store to request manual number blocking.
    • The availability of this service may vary depending on your plan and device.

  • Sprint:

    • Log into your My Sprint account online or via the mobile app.
    • Navigate to "Call Blocking" and enter the numbers you wish to block.
    • This service is available for free to all Sprint customers.

Blocking Directly on Smart Phones

In addition to service provider features, you can also block unwanted calls directly on your smart phone.

  • iPhone:

    • Open the Phone app and tap on "Recents."
    • Find the number you wish to block and tap on the "i" icon.
    • Scroll down and select "Block this Caller."

  • Android:

    • Go to "Settings" > "Call" > "Call Rejection."
    • Tap on "Auto Reject List" and enter the number you want to block.
    • You can also block unknown numbers or callers not in your contacts list.

  • Samsung:

    • Go to "Call Settings" on your Samsung device.
    • Enable the "Call Blocking Mode" option.
    • This will block all incoming calls, except those from numbers in your contacts list or specified exceptions.

Additional Blocking Methods

  • National Do Not Call Registry:

    • Register your phone number on the Federal Trade Commission's National Do Not Call Registry at donotcall.gov.
    • This will reduce the number of sales and telemarketing calls you receive.
    • Please note that this registry does not prevent calls from political organizations, charities, and certain other entities.

  • Google Voice:

    • If you have a Google Voice account, you can block numbers from all devices linked to your account.
    • To do this, log into your Google Voice account and go to "Settings" > "Calls" > "Blocked numbers."
    • Enter the numbers you wish to block and they will be automatically applied to all your linked devices.

  • Third-Party Phone Apps:

    • For Android users, there are a variety of phone apps available in the Google Play Store that can help block unwanted calls.
    • Popular options include Mr. Caller, Truecaller, and Call Blocker.
    • These apps allow you to block specific numbers, unknown callers, spam calls, and even robocalls.
